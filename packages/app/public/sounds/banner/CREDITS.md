# Banner sounds

The fighting-game banners over the board. Swap a file for another with the same name to change it. (Until Oct 9, 2026
"QUEEN DOWN!" played `boss-roar.mp3`, a pitched-down male yell, and "QUEEN SLAIN!" played `start.mp3`; both are now
the queen's gasp. The roar is in git history.)

| File | When | Source (CC0) |
|---|---|---|
| start.mp3 | "START!" opens a boss battle | A synthesised whoosh with Juhani Junkala's `sfx_wpn_punch3` and `sfx_exp_short_hard3` (*The Essential Retro Video Game Sound Effects Collection [512 sounds]*) |
| queen-gasp.mp3 | a queen taken, either way: "QUEEN SLAIN!" and "QUEEN DOWN!" | wintuh, *65-GaspSeñora.wav* ("A woman gasping"), Freesound sound 471875, CC0 1.0: trimmed to the gasp, high- and low-passed, a light hall echo, levelled under a move (peak −7.7 dBFS, mean −22.8 dBFS) |
