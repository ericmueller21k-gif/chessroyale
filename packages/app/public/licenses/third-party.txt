HunChess: third-party software, fonts and sounds in the app

The app's own code is HunChess's. It includes the following third-party software and fonts, each under its license,
whose text follows. (The chess pieces have their own notice: /licenses/pieces.txt. The chess engines, Stockfish and
Maia-3, are separate programs with their own notices: /engine/COPYING.txt, /engine/maia/NOTICE.txt and
/engine/maia/LICENSE.txt. Their complete source is on this site too, to download: Stockfish's (GPL-3.0) at
/engine/stockfish-source.tar.gz, our Maia box's (AGPL-3.0) at /engine/maia/maia-box-source.tar.gz. The classic piece
sounds and their variations are PyChess's, under the GPL-3.0: see "Piece sounds" below. The other sounds are CC0 or
the game's own: no notice is needed.)

- Preact (https://preactjs.com): MIT license.
- chess.js (https://github.com/jhlywa/chess.js): BSD 2-Clause license.
- Vite's small runtime helper (https://vite.dev), compiled into the app: MIT license.
- Fonts: Archivo, Archivo Black (Omnibus-Type) and Press Start 2P (CodeMan38), from Google Fonts: SIL Open Font
  License 1.1.
- Piece sounds: the classic knocks and their variations, from PyChess: GNU General Public License, version 3.

Piece sounds: the classic knocks and their variations (PyChess, GNU GPL version 3)
================================================================================================================
These sound files are from PyChess (https://github.com/pychess/pychess, its sounds/ folder:
https://github.com/pychess/pychess/tree/master/sounds), by the PyChess authors, and are licensed under the GNU
General Public License, version 3. They are separate data files the game plays, not part of the game's code.

- /sounds/move.mp3 ("Classic knock"): PyChess's sounds/mov2.wav, converted to mp3.
- /sounds/capture.mp3 ("Classic double knock"): PyChess's sounds/capture2.wav, converted to mp3.
- /sounds/castle.mp3 (castling): PyChess's sounds/castle.wav, converted to mp3.
- /sounds/pieces/move-classic-light.mp3, move-classic-deep.mp3, move-classic-soft.mp3 and
  /sounds/pieces/capture-classic-light.mp3, capture-classic-deep.mp3, capture-classic-soft.mp3: variations of
  PyChess's sounds, made by our script. Modified by HunChess on October 12, 2026, from mov2.wav and capture2.wav:
  a small pitch shift, a gentle EQ, a slightly shorter or longer decay, and leveled to the classic's loudness.
  Exactly how each was made: /sounds/SOURCES.md.

Their complete source, served with the game: /sounds/pychess-sounds-source.tar.gz (PyChess's original WAV files,
the script that made the variations, and how to run it). The full text of the GNU General Public License, version 3:
/engine/COPYING.txt.
