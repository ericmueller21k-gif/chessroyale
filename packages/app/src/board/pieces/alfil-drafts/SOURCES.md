# Alfil's elephant piece: design A (not in the game yet)

Eric picked design A (Oct 17, 2026), without its forehead cap. Nothing in the game draws it yet: no CSS, no wiring.
Each is a 45 x 45 SVG in the cburnett files' format (same outline weight 1.5, white `#ffffff` or black `#000000` fill,
black outline, details black on white and `#ececec` on black), so it can drop in beside them.

- `elephant-A-white.svg`, `elephant-A-black.svg`: an elephant's (mammoth's) head in profile, drawn for HunChess. Its
  base ribbon and collar are the cburnett bishop's own (`../cburnett/Chess_blt45.svg`, Colin M.L. Burnett, BSD: see
  `../LICENSE.txt`), so it stands like the bishop it moves like; the head, ear, trunk and tusk are new. Not traced
  from any other icon. The forehead cap (a nettipattam) is gone (Eric: "without it, it'll actually look better"), and
  the trunk's two wrinkles are thinner and shorter, inset from its outline, so they stay clean at 40 px.
