# The zebra boss: Eric's words (Nov 10, 2026, by voice; lightly cleaned)

"I want to make this one a zebra, but I want the design of it to be like the giraffes, how they kind of stand, like upright, you
know, or like a human kind of stand." (The giraffes: the shady dealer giraffe, `characters/sidekicks/dealer.ts`, and Jefferson's
giraffe kin.) "The zebras are enemies of the giraffes."

**A bakery, run like a mob.** "They hold giant [bread] in like the same way you would hold like a gun ... it's silly enough. It'll
work ... like assault rifle style with like two hands, like the two guards by the door." "Instead of the deli, let's make it a bakery.
That way we can do nice French rolls, and then it's easy to like put those marks along the bread. So it clearly looks like a big roll
of bread that they're holding." The guards look like mafia members ("I'll have to come up with something funny").

**The front room.** "When you walk in to the deli [bakery], there's always two guards like looking at you." "One of the big animated
pieces will be like a giant mixing bowl that's like mixing dough. The counter ... there's bread for sale behind the counter." "The
whole vibe of this place is like, it's a bakery, but they keep like making kind of jokes, like being like, nothing here but a bakery,
you know, move along ... clearly there's more going on, but it's just a bakery. That's the joke."

**The back room.** "The total size will be like the other ones [Big Boy's home size, or whatever the default is], but it's smaller on
the inside because there's actually going to be a door, like when you walk in and back into the left, that you can go through. And then
it loads into another room." The back room is about 1/3 to 1/4 of the total. "A big table, and there's a TV in this room that you can
see, and on the TV let's put what looks like to be a chess stream playing, and this big table, there's a bunch of scattered chess
pieces, and there's just like four guys in there." "On the wall too, there's just like kind of scribbles and stuff and game theory ...
what the actual underground operation is like, they're just chess lovers, and they're just obsessed over studying chess." "Puffing a
little bit" [smoking; keep it to a cartoon puff or skip it].

**The chatter (loops).** "Have a conversation going between them, and it can loop ... 4 zebras. Make one of them have like a big pudgy
belly ... kind of the kingpin of the whole operation ... a conversation being had between the 3 guys and then this dude. And he's just
arguing ... go look at some chess theory lines for some different things ... he's always right. If they talk about, like a Queen's
Gambit or whatever, like we want to have different openings ... he basically always one-ups, he corrects all their wrong information.
But not just basic chess stuff ... he would say things like 'rook b1 takes, check', that sort of talk. And then they would say
something, and he'd be like, no, that's not how it works. We don't want to have him say 'are you stupid?' but something to that effect,
a little bit lighter, a little bit more fun."

**The boss is the kingpin, in the back room** (not out in the town). "When you go in there, and if you walk up to him and talk to him,
they all of a sudden all stand guard and draw their breadsticks from their side and hold it and point them at you. And then he tells
them all to relax. And then they put them down, and then it's like, oh, you're looking to play a game of chess ... fun, silly. And
then he goes onto the battle arena." He says a line, they say a line, and it starts.

**The battle.** "Loaves of bread as the splash screen." His startup bit: "This guy, he pulls out his bread and holds it like a rifle,
and he just pretends to shoot it at you, and you just hear like a little pop, and then he just starts laughing." "Same animation for the
God King save as well. He comes down, and it looks like he's just firing off a bunch of shots with this loaf of bread. Keep it light,
funny, but still a good animation."

**Gameplay:** Eric will decide ("I will come up with the actual thing about him as far as how he plays. Feel free to give me some
suggestions"). Start on the artwork now.

## Eric, Nov 11 (by voice): name and passive
- **Name: Don Dough.** "The Don Dough is good, and the rising dough is a good passive for him."
- **Passive: Rising Dough.** "He drops early on in the game. He'll do this a few times ... randomly like a piece of dough." "It's better
  that you do know what it is" (the dough shows what it will become). The God King says something like "squash it before it rises".
  - **Times:** "if it's a pawn, it'll take maybe 4 turns. A knight or bishop is 7, and a rook is 10, and a queen is 15." (Elsewhere: "it'll
    probably end up coming up after 5 turns, aka 5 minutes"; "12 turns is probably too little for a queen"; "we'll just experiment with
    the numbers and the placement".)
  - **Odds and places:** "he should rarely get the queen, the rook. Those can be put near his side somewhere, but somewhere that's gettable
    for the pawn." "He gets about a pawn and a half to maybe 1 knight sometimes per game. On occasion, he may throw out a queen." "You're not
    going to have too much trouble trying to squander [squash] his pieces."
  - **The oven timer, not a meter:** "a little timer, like an oven timer, like a digital timer" on his side, counting down in "minutes"
    that are turns: "it shows 12 minutes. When you play your turn, the timer drops to 11 minutes, but it does a cool millisecond drop down
    where it looks like all of a sudden a minute passed. And then when he plays, it drops again." (So one minute per move, yours and his.)
  - Not a real-time timer ("we already kind of do that with Babur").
- **Ultimate:** not decided: "something with the black and white cookie ... I'll think on that while I'm away."
