# Eric's ideas, Nov 26 (noted; not started unless marked)

1. **Settings vs the Handbook.** Settings (main menu) should be short: "here are the settings, here's what you can adjust". Everything
   that explains (rules, legal, how bosses work, how each mode works, how ranking works) lives in the HunChess Handbook (already
   searchable). Put the Handbook in the computer's left side menu as its own tab. Eric will look at Settings at home before we change it.
2. **How we describe the engines** (Handbook/marketing): don't name engines in that text; say we use multiple chess engines plus our own
   system, three working in tandem, to make the bosses play the way they do; a selling point and hard to copy. NOTE: the license
   notices (Stockfish GPL, Maia AGPL) must still name them and offer their source where the licenses require it; that stays on the
   licenses page, separate from the description.
3. **Chess integrity statement.** Somewhere (maybe on Boss mode: an icon or a first-time pop-up), say plainly that the integrity of
   chess comes first and has from day one: what makes Boss mode and the other modes fun is deliberate, made to feel new without
   weakening chess, making it stop feeling like chess, or taking away what makes skillful play skillful. Wording and place: Eric
   hasn't decided; director offered a draft.
