# iPhone Home Screen app: keep content out of iOS 26's top blur band (Nov 21). Opus, high. Light/targeted lane (CSS at the root).
**Eric, Nov 21:** "If you think that the fix for it would not mess with any visual, anything, I would say we could try it. But that's obviously my concern, is that we end up making a disaster out of trying to convert ... the banners at the top. ... if you think it's doable and it's not going to mess with the UI at all, then we can give it a shot."
Background: docs/decisions/2026-11-20-ios-standalone-top.md (#849 made the status bar solid). Still, iOS 26 frosts the first ~20-25 pt of the page under the status bar in the installed Home Screen app only (Safari is fine). Other projects' fix: reserve that band (move content down) in standalone mode.
- ONE root-level change, nothing per screen: in the installed iOS app only (`@media (display-mode: standalone)` plus an iOS-only gate, e.g. a class set once at boot from `navigator.standalone === true`), reserve a blank band (the page background color, light and dark) of a tunable height (start 24 px; in settings or the one CSS var) at the very top, and make every screen's height math see a shorter screen (e.g. the root's app height var / one wrapper), so nothing overflows, nothing scrolls, and every screen looks exactly as now, just that much lower (the board may be up to that much smaller where height bounds it).
- No edits to the ~20 places that use env(safe-area-inset-top) one by one; no change at all in Safari, Android, or computers. If it can't be done at the root, STOP and write `.question` explaining why.
- Prove: emulate standalone (a test-only switch to force the class) on an iPhone-sized viewport: home, a boss battle, the tower floor, Chess Town, settings: no scroll, nothing clipped, header below the band; and the same screens without the switch pixel-identical to main. One focused e2e check in an existing spec. Stills: the forced-standalone home + boss battle (phone). Short decision note. Easy revert = set the band to 0.
