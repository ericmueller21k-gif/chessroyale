a broken alt ... The other possibility is if your piece stays on one, it just gets charred, but it has to happen twice, and that might be
the better way to go. It's just like if a piece ... is there when the tile starts burning, that it just becomes charred, and then if it
does it twice, then it disintegrates." (The sim: Jefferson the hardest boss, 44% of the floors met lost.)

  the match) and **burns** (gone) the second time. His own pieces stay fireproof, the waves' share on his half unchanged. The Sparkler
  (his passive) unchanged. A charred piece that moves stays charred.
- Art: a charred look for a piece (reuse an existing darkening/soot treatment where one exists before drawing new; Eric's art rules: no
  hand-drawn sprites; a code tint/overlay is fine). Banner/line: the first hit "Charred! One more burns it".
