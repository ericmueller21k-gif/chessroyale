Replaces the bar-props A/B mockup (stills/tower-bar-props, the .mockup note on claude/tower-bar-props): that question goes away.
- The split bar (`screens/TowerBar.tsx`, tower.css) on every screen size: the boss to move (the view's focus) gets ~75% of the bar's width, the others share the rest equally. Each boss's card is ALWAYS laid out at its full (expanded) width; a folded part is just a narrower window (overflow hidden) onto the card's LEFT side (his sprite, maybe cut off; the name may be hidden). Expanding = the window widening to reveal more of the same card; never a relayout of the card's insides. The card holds everything a lone bar shows, his bar prop/count included (Jefferson's candles, Hollow's bulbs, Timber's workshop, Dash's tally, Don Dough's timers; Babur's pump as now).
- On a turn change the windows slide (~300 ms, CSS transition of the parts' widths/flex-grow; reduced motion: instant). Tap a folded part: it opens (the others fold) until the turn changes or it's tapped again. The bar's height never changes, so the board's size math doesn't change.
- Eric's standing rule "nothing shifts" (docs/rules/eric.md): this bar is his named exception (record it in the decision note); nothing else moves.
