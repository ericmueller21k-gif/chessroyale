- Ranks: placement matches yes; queuing with friends counts. Squads: RP entirely from the squad's placement.
- 50 v 50: new pre-game vote replaces the endings vote: Strong (top 3 played moves; close in score -> random among them; one clearly
  best -> it), Majority (most votes), Chaos (totally random). Default end: play till the game ends, maybe a 60-move limit. No more
  1v1/2v2 endings.
- Eliminations: each round removes equally from each side, by score relative to own team; down to a 1v1 that plays normal chess to the end.
- End screen: Olympic podium (top 3 pawns on pedestals of different heights), the full chart of players and scores below.
- "Winner" = the player with the highest rating even if they lost.
- RP: factor both where you finished and your engine score; 100 strong players and an early cut shouldn't cost much; survived long but
  blundered; standing within your elimination round (ahead of the other 9: lose fewer).
- (later) The top 4 on each side (the last 8) play a bit longer: a few moves taken from the 1v1's share go to the 4 v 4.
- (later) A cut player gets a Spectate option to watch how it ends (late cuts especially).
