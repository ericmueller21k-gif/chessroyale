# Hollow's challenge intro: Eric's design (Nov 3), for the intro round after the cave ships

Longer than the others' ~12 s is fine ("his startup time can be a few seconds longer").
1. Hollow walks on to his mark on the pond (as the others).
2. The darkness ball in his belly/abdomen powers up: material builds up around it, spins, and it expands a little.
3. It **sucks in the light**: the lanterns go out **one by one**, the bulb string's lights are drawn in toward him, light from
   everything around, even from your own chest piece (the player's pawn).
4. Everything goes black.
5. It does NOT all switch back on: darkness everywhere, and a **dim, steady light** around you, the board and him (not flickering),
   enough to see everything. Then the battle starts.
Check: the lanterns going out must be visible from the actual intro camera (the cave round checks; add lanterns in view if needed).
