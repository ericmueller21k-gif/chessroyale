# Crate tier stings: audio for Eric (Nov 24)

Audio only, rendered offline with the build's own pieces (`renderCues`, `timeline()`, `clickTimes`, `cues`; the script is
`scripts/render-crate-stings.ts` on `claude/crate-tier-stings`). Spin and box are unchanged (C's ticks, A's ending, no swell or charge);
only the ending after the lid bursts changes per tier. MP3s for listening; WAVs are the same, lossless.

- `opening-common.mp3`: Common and Uncommon (one shared sting, today's).
- `opening-rare.mp3`: Rare: a step brighter, the chord's top notes held an octave up and trembling (a shimmer).
- `opening-epic.mp3`: Epic: fuller (a bass octave under the chord) and a rising arpeggio after the burst.
- `opening-legendary.mp3`: Legendary: the big chord, a choir under it and a bell tolling.
- `opening-mythic.mp3`: Mythic: the biggest, its own color: a fanfare's chord change (flat six, flat seven, home), the choir and the
  bell landing on home, and a cascade of sparkles falling over it.
- `transcendent-alone.mp3`: the Transcendent moment (the strip landing on today's Fischer Random tile): a quick harp sweep up onto a
  held, trembling "magic" (Lydian) chord, a soft deep gong under it and a crystal ting.
- `opening-transcendent-mythic.mp3`: the whole Transcendent path as the game runs it: the first strip, the Transcendent moment as it
  lands, the banner's wait, the second strip and a Mythic's sting (no box on that strip).
