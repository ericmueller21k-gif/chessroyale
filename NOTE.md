# Birdie: no tail; his train smooth (Nov 23, 2026)

Branch `claude/birdie-tail-smooth`. Captured on an emulated iPhone 13 (390 × 664) and a 1280 × 800 computer, in the built app on the local server.

- `panel-before-after-phone.png`: the boss panel with Armored Birdie, before (left: the tail hanging between his legs to our left) and after (right).
- `panel-figure-before-after-phone.png`: his figure alone from the same panel, 3× (before left, after right).
- `panel-after-desktop.png`: the computer's screen after his fall, no tail.
- `cutscene-armored-before-after.png`: the cutscene's armored frames (reveal's last, hold's first), before (left pair) and after (right pair), 2×.
- `train-before-phone.png`: 8 consecutive display frames of the train's ride before (Chrome's screencast, about 16.7 ms apart): about 12 px jumps a
  frame, as it ran at twice its speed (it then stood off the board for the second half of the ride). A rank.
- `train-after-phone.png`, `train-after-desktop.png`: 8 consecutive display frames after: an even 5.9 px a frame (phone), the wheels and smoke on
  their 12-frame loop. A file (the warned line is the game's pick).

Measured every animation frame through the whole ride (phone): before, moving for 0.65 s, then 38 of 77 frames still; the sprite 4 frames at 9.4 fps.
After: 77 of 77 frames moving 5.82 to 5.92 px each; the sprite 12 frames at 14.8 fps. Computer: 10.6 px a frame, none still.
