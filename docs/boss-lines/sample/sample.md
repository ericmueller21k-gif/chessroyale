## Ginger: character sheet

**A furious gingerbread cookie, left in a bakery freezer over a long weekend, who now ices everything she touches.**

Ginger was baked to smile in a bakery's holiday window. Then someone shut her in the walk-in freezer over the long weekend. She came out frosted solid, gripping a candy cane snapped off the display, with a cold streak that never thawed. Now anyone who gets close gets frozen. She wants to be feared, not nibbled, and she'll never be anybody's cute decoration again. To her, the other bosses are half-baked. She grudgingly respects anyone who runs cold. Anything warm, glowing or fiery is a natural enemy, and she says so loudly.

- **Voice:** Short, clipped bursts with lots of exclamation marks. She opens or closes with "Snap!" or a grumpy "Hmph." Baking and frost puns arrive back to back. Trailing "…" only when she's plotting or cracking.
- **Words:** snap, crumb, stale, half-baked, icing, frosty, chill, gumdrops, freezer, crumble
- **Catchphrases:** "Crunchy outside. Frozen inside. Hmph!" · "Nobody touches the cane!" · "Snap to it, or snap in half!" · "I've been colder than you since the long weekend."
- **Never says:** Let's all warm up by the oven!; Aww, group hug?; No rush, take your time, sweetie.
- **Loves:** The hum of a walk-in freezer, which is her lullaby; Her candy cane, which she swears she only borrowed; Perfectly piped icing with crisp, sharp edges; The crunch of frost underfoot; Snow days, when nobody bakes anything
- **Hates:** Ovens, which she won't even look at; Warm hands; Being dunked in milk, the soggiest fate there is; Being called cute; Long weekends
- **Flaw or secret:** She still keeps the little price tag from the bakery window tucked behind her bow tie. She gets very touchy if anyone asks what she was worth.
- **Brags:** "Your best piece? Iced. Frozen solid. Hmph!" · "Snap! That one's not going anywhere for a while." · "Blizzard! One piece wiggles. The rest? Snowed in!"
- **Gripes:** "I can't ice the king. Something about manners. Bah!" · "My blizzard thaws out by the end. Drip, drip… rude." · "Check melts my ice? Who made ice so soft?!"
- **New details (not in the sources):** The freezer's hum is her lullaby; She keeps the bakery price tag behind her bow tie and hates being asked her price; She calls the snapped-off candy cane "borrowed"; She won't look at ovens; Being dunked in milk is her worst fear (soggy); She hates being called cute; She hates warm hands; She hates long weekends; She loves crisp icing edges and crunchy frost; She loves snow days because nobody bakes; She respects cold bosses and picks fights with warm or fiery ones; Pronouns: this sheet uses she/her per the lore, FAQ and area pages; the sprite comments' "his" is still for Eric to settle

## Jefferson: character sheet

**A cocky, fire-happy giraffe who spent years sure he was a dinosaur and now works as a fireworks stand's mascot, lighting things he really shouldn't.**

Jefferson grew up certain he was a dinosaur and answered to G-REX for years. Nobody had the heart to tell him otherwise, so he roared at everything, and the roars still slip out. Being a dinosaur never paid, so he took the mascot job at the fireworks stand, and he has been lighting things he shouldn't ever since. He wants the biggest 'ooh' and the loudest 'aah' in town, plus a good view of both, which his neck provides. He sizes up the other bosses by height first. He is the tallest boss around, and the God King only matches him with his wings spread, which Jefferson calls cheating. He teases any ice or water type as a born enemy of a good show. Anyone who likes light and sparkle is a potential opening act.

- **Voice:** Short, loud bursts with lots of exclamation marks. He drops his g's (sizzlin', smokin') and leans on cooking puns: toast, crispy, well done, simmer. When he gets excited he roars like a dinosaur, then catches himself with an awkward '…I mean.' He brags in threes and counts down a lot.
- **Words:** sizzlin', toasty, crispy, spark, fuse, boom, rawr, neck, hot stuff, fireworks
- **Catchphrases:** "Stand back. Further. Little further. Perfect!" · "Mascot by day, menace by also day." · "Rawr! …Okay, that one was on purpose." · "Three, two, one… whoops, started early!"
- **Never says:** Maybe we should keep this low-key.; I'm just a regular giraffe.; Let's put that out before it spreads.
- **Loves:** The quiet half-second between the 'ooh' and the 'aah'; Being the tallest one in every group picture; Toasting marshmallows on his own horn tips; The dinosaur museum, especially the gift shop; Birthday cakes with way too many candles
- **Hates:** Rain, drizzle, and anyone who brings an umbrella to a show; 'NO FIREWORKS' signs, which he takes personally; Being asked how the weather is up there; Fire extinguishers, which he calls 'the red party poopers'; Cakes with only one candle
- **Flaw or secret:** He's afraid of the dark. That's the real reason everything around him is always lit, and he sleeps with both sparklers on.
- **Brags:** "One square, three stages, tick, tick… toast! Better scoot!" · "Your half, my campfire. Don't get comfy on it." · "Some fireballs land on my side. My crew never burns. Ha!"
- **Gripes:** "My sparkler won't touch a king. Not even a little singe!" · "They just step off the fire. Every time. Where's the fun?" · "The shadows show up early! It spoils the whole surprise!"
- **New details (not in the sources):** He's the tallest boss around, and he calls the God King's spread wings 'cheating' in height contests; He teases ice and water bosses as born enemies of a good show; He's afraid of the dark and sleeps with both sparklers lit; He toasts marshmallows on his flaming horn tips; He loves the dinosaur museum and its gift shop; He loves cakes with too many candles and hates cakes with just one; He hates rain, umbrellas, 'NO FIREWORKS' signs and fire extinguishers ('the red party poopers'); He gets annoyed when people ask how the weather is up there; His roars still slip out by accident, and he follows them with an awkward '…I mean'; He counts down a lot and sometimes starts early

## Ginger and Jefferson: their relationship

Loud frenemies. Ginger thinks Jefferson is an oven on legs, and Jefferson thinks Ginger is a walking 'NO FIREWORKS' sign. In the town square, she stays a few steps away so his flames can't sweat her gumdrops, and he follows her around trying to toast a marshmallow 'near' her. On the same team, the bickering turns into competitive pride. Her ice pins a piece in place and his fire burns the square under it, so a frozen piece can't step off the fire. Each insists the combo is their own doing. They never admit they like working together, but they high-five through a cloud of steam.

- **Opposites:** Ice vs. fire: she freezes everything she touches, he sets fire to everything he can reach, and each calls the other a natural enemy out loud.; Size: a cookie you could hold in one hand vs. the tallest boss in town, who sizes everyone up by height first.; Dark vs. light: she naps to the hum of a dark walk-in freezer, while he keeps everything lit and sleeps with both sparklers on.; Baking: ovens are her worst nightmare, but he loves birthday cakes with way too many candles and calls a good hit 'well done.'
- **In common:** Both were hired to be cute: she was a holiday window decoration, he's a fireworks stand mascot, and both hate being treated like decor.; Both talk in short, loud bursts with lots of exclamation marks, and both say 'Let it simmer…' when they're thinking.; Both carry a stick into battle (her 'borrowed' candy cane, his two sparklers), and neither power can touch a king, which bugs them both.; Both hide a soft spot: her price tag behind the bow tie, his fear of the dark.
- **Silly angles:** The weather war: Ginger asks 'How's the weather up there?' on purpose because she knows he hates it, and he fires back about the weather 'down there.'; Steam: whenever they stand too close, the steam clouds the view and both blame the other for ruining the show or the icing.; Jefferson is secretly fascinated and terrified by the freezer ('how dark does it get in there?'), and Ginger realizes she can use it against him.; Jefferson loves cakes and baked goods a little too much, and Ginger is a baked good. She keeps her cane between them at all times.; Prehistoric jokes: Ginger calls his dinosaur phase 'stale' and asks if he's a fossil yet; he says she's the one who got kept on ice.
- **Ginger asks Jefferson:** "How's the weather up there? Frosty, I hope! Hmph!" · "Snap! Why do your horns smoke? Is your brain baking?" · "You were a dinosaur? Which kind? The half-baked kind?"
- **Jefferson asks Ginger:** "How's the weather down there? Not fun, huh?! Rawr! …I mean, ha!" · "Is it dark in that freezer? Like, really dark? …Asking for a pal." · "You were in a holiday window? How much were they askin' for ya?"
- **Running joke:** They both say 'Let it simmer…' and keep fighting over whose line it is. Jefferson says simmering is a fire thing and she stole it. Ginger snaps that it's a baking word, she had it first, and her simmer is 'the cold kind.' Every time one of them says it, the other mutters 'Thief!'
- **New details:** Ginger asks Jefferson 'How's the weather up there?' on purpose, knowing he hates it; Jefferson answers by asking about the weather 'down there'; They both use 'Let it simmer…' and argue over who said it first; Ginger claims hers is 'the cold kind'; Ginger calls Jefferson 'an oven on legs'; Jefferson calls Ginger 'shorty' and 'cookie' and thinks of her as a walking 'NO FIREWORKS' sign; Ginger keeps a few steps away from Jefferson in the town square so his flames won't sweat her gumdrops; Jefferson keeps trying to toast a marshmallow near Ginger, and she keeps her candy cane between them; Jefferson is curious about and scared of how dark the walk-in freezer gets; Standing close makes steam, and each blames the other for it; On a team, each claims the freeze-plus-fire combo as their own idea and won't admit they like it; They high-five through a cloud of steam when the combo works; Jefferson asks how much Ginger was priced at in the window, which touches her price-tag sore spot
