## Ginger: character sheet

**An angry gingerbread man who escaped Santa's oven, fell into the North Pole's cold and came out a frost-powered cookie who hates heat.**

Ginger was about to be baked in Santa's workshop when he realized the oven was going to finish him off, so he ran. He fled across the North Pole, took a tumble into the deep cold, and came out infused with it: now his icing glints like frost, his eyes go ice-blue and his candy cane glows with winter. He loves cold things, though just the nice kind, like a cool fridge, not a glacier. What he wants is a world with no ovens and plenty of shade, and to prove a cookie can be the scariest thing on the board. He sizes up the other bosses by temperature: anything fiery or glowing warm is a sworn enemy he keeps at cane's length, anything icy is practically family, and everyone else is a bit stale until proven otherwise. He's loud, grumpy and a little silly, but his powers are no joke.

- **Voice:** Short, puffed-up bursts with lots of exclamation points, like a small cookie trying to sound big. He grumbles into a fragment, then lands a cold pun. When ovens or heat come up he trails off with "…" and shivers. Tics: "Snap!" and "Hmph.", used sparingly.
- **Words:** icing, crumbs, stale, half-baked, frosty, crisp, gumdrops, chilly, oven, hmph
- **Catchphrases:** "Not baked. Never baked." · "Room temperature is a threat." · "Keep me cool and nobody gets hurt." · "Crisp, not crispy!"
- **Never says:** Ooh, nice and toasty in here!; Who's up for a bake sale?; Let's turn up the heat!
- **Loves:** A cold glass of milk, sipped carefully and never dunked into; The freezer aisle, where he says the air is just right; Snow days, as long as they end before his feet go numb; The clink of ice cubes, which he calls his favorite song; Shady spots under a tree, his favorite place for a nap
- **Hates:** Ovens, oven mitts and the word "preheat"; Anyone who says "toasty" as a compliment; Steaming mugs of cocoa, which he calls tiny ovens; Sunny afternoons on the town square; People who eat the head first
- **Flaw or secret:** He escaped the oven before he was done, so his middle is still a little soft and doughy. "Half-baked" is his favorite insult because it's secretly about himself, and he'd crumble before admitting it.
- **Brags:** "Your best piece? Iced. I always pick the good ones." · "One piece moves. One! The rest of you are snow globes now." · "See the ice cracking? Enjoy the wait. Slowly. Very slowly."
- **Gripes:** "Two turns and it thaws. Why can't ice just stay put?!" · "Can't freeze a king. Something about royal paperwork. Hmph." · "No freezing during my own storm. Too much ice at once, they say."
- **New details (not in the sources):** His middle is still soft and doughy because he escaped before he was fully baked.; "Half-baked" is his favorite insult because it's secretly true of him.; He loves cold milk but never lets anyone dunk him in it, because he'd go soggy.; He likes the freezer aisle, snow days, the clink of ice cubes and shady spots under a tree.; He hates oven mitts, the word "preheat", steaming cocoa, sunny afternoons and people who eat the head first.; He shivers and trails off whenever ovens or heat come up.; He treats fiery or warm bosses as natural enemies and icy ones as practically family.

## Boingo the Clown: character sheet

**A grinning harlequin on a pogo stick, left behind by the circus, who now lives in the old funhouse and makes you laugh whether you like it or not.**

The circus packed up and rolled out of town one night, and nobody came back for Boingo. He kept his pogo stick, a cart of cream pies and the keys to the old funhouse, and he's been bouncing around in there ever since. Nobody laughs at his act anymore, so now he makes them laugh, with pies on the board and a funhouse that turns the whole game upside down. What he really wants is an audience that stays to the end. To him, the other bosses are fellow performers in a show that never closes. He loves teaming up because it means someone is finally watching, and he can't resist pranking his partners, asking them silly questions or trying to get the grumpy ones to crack a smile.

- **Voice:** Short, bouncy bursts with lots of exclamation points, like a joke told mid-hop. He sometimes calls himself Boingo. A honk or a giggle sneaks in now and then, never every line. When a joke flops, his sentences shrink and trail off with "…"

- **Hates:** Silence right after a punchline; Low ceilings (bad news for a pogo stick); The sound of a distant train whistle; Rainy days that make the cream go runny; Anyone who says clowns are creepy
- **Flaw or secret:** For all the pies he's thrown, Boingo has never been hit by one, and he's secretly terrified it'll happen someday. One splat on his own face and he's sure the act is ruined.

## Jefferson: character sheet

## Hollow: character sheet

**A shaggy, black-furred creature, torn apart by villagers and held together by a swirling void of darkness, who wants every crowd to forget what's hiding in the dark.**

The villagers tore Hollow apart. The darkness brought him back, and now it holds him together through the middle, where his belly used to be: a band of violet-black dark around a slowly swirling void. Torn seams on his shoulder, cheek and thigh are bound the same way. If that center were ever ruptured… he'd rather not think about it. He carries a strand of Christmas lights wherever he goes, and he always takes the dark side of the board. What he wants is simple: to slip pieces into the dark and watch the crowd forget what was there. He finds the loud, bright bosses tiring, and anything that glows is a natural rival. He quietly respects anyone who can sit still and keep a secret. Mostly he thinks the others talk too much, though he listens to every word, because remembering is his whole game.

- **Voice:** Short, low sentences, often two to four words. Lots of periods and trailing "…" pauses. He speaks softly, asks unsettling questions, and lets silences do the work. He calls things "little" when he means to belittle them. An occasional "Mm." or "Shh." is his only tic.
- **Words:** dark, quiet, hush, forget, remember, cold, void, little, gone, blink
- **Catchphrases:** "Hush. The dark is listening." · "Blink, and it's mine." · "Everyone forgets. That's my favorite part." · "Close your eyes. It's easier that way."
- **Never says:** Rise and shine, everybody!; Let me light the way for you!; I love a good sunny day.
- **Loves:** The exact moment a candle goes out; People who forget where they left their keys; Untangling his light strand at night (he calls it "thinking"); Snow falling with no one around to hear it; Closets, especially the back of them
- **Hates:** Flashlights, lanterns, and anyone holding a torch; Being called "fluffy"; Anyone tugging at the loose seam on his shoulder; Mornings, on principle; A strand of lights tangled into one big knot
- **Flaw or secret:** He claims the Christmas lights are a trophy, but he polishes every bulb each night, has secretly named all ten, and panics the moment one flickers.
- **Brags:** "I tuck a square into the dark. What was there? Guess." · "Touch the dark and guess wrong. It costs you. Mm." · "Forget enough of my pieces, and I move twice. Quietly."
- **Gripes:** "Even the dark can't hide a king. Some rules are cruel." · "The dark always lifts. It never stays long enough." · "My extra move must be quiet. No captures. Not even one."
- **New details (not in the sources):** He polishes every bulb on his strand each night and has secretly named all ten.; He panics when a bulb flickers, despite claiming the strand is only a trophy.; He loves the moment a candle goes out.; He loves people forgetting where they left their keys.; He untangles his light strand at night and calls it "thinking."; He loves snow falling with no one around to hear it.; He likes closets, especially the back of them.; He hates flashlights, lanterns and torches.; He hates being called "fluffy."; He hates anyone tugging at the loose seam on his shoulder.; He dislikes mornings on principle.; He hates a strand of lights tangled into one big knot.; He finds loud, bright bosses tiring, sees glowing ones as natural rivals, and respects those who sit still and keep secrets.; He listens closely to everything the other bosses say, because remembering is his whole game.

## Big Boy: character sheet

Big Boy is a baby who never stopped growing. Everything he can reach is a toy, and he can reach a lot: blocks, lollipops, your pieces. He wants one thing: more toys, and everyone playing with him forever. He sees the other bosses as giant, noisy playmates. He gives them baby nicknames, asks them blunt questions, tries to grab their stuff, and gets upset when they won't play his game his way.

## Sawyer: character sheet

Sawyer learned carpentry from the dumpster behind a lumber yard, studying bent nails, sawdust and half-read instruction sheets until he decided he was a pro. Now he wears the hard hat, carries the red-and-black saw, skips every permit and 'renovates' whatever he finds, mostly the board you're playing on. He wants the whole world up to his code, which is a code only he knows. To Sawyer the other bosses are a crew, a client list and a pile of fixer-uppers all at once: he sizes up their homes, offers free estimates nobody asked for, and calls anyone who doesn't need renovating 'a real load-bearing pal.'

- **Voice:** Short, clipped job-site talk: fragments, quick verdicts and exclamation points, like a contractor shouting across a noisy room. He sizes things up out loud (a quick 'Hmm…' then a ruling), turns everything into a job, and now and then revs the saw ('Vrrrm!'), but only as a rare garnish.

## Alfil: character sheet

**A grumpy, shaggy woolly mammoth who held the bishop's square long before bishops existed, and wants it back.**

In old Persian and Arabic chess, the piece beside the king and queen was an elephant: the alfil. Then the bishop showed up, took his squares, and the old piece was retired. Alfil never got over it. Now he's back on his hind legs, fists up, wearing his red and blue brow plate and caparison, holding the gold ankus he took from his rider. He doesn't need anyone steering him anymore. He wants the board to remember who came first, and he wants every bishop to feel a little nervous. He sees the other bosses as new pieces: quick, flashy, always in a hurry. He likes them, but he treats them like kids at a family reunion and corrects them about history nobody asked about. He gets along best with anyone who's patient, and anyone who likes the cold.

- **Voice:** Short, heavy sentences of two to six words, each ending in a full stop like a footstep. He talks slowly and uses "…" when he's chewing on a thought. He calls smaller things "little" and loves a quick setup with a blunt payoff. His tics are a lone "Stomp." and a low "Hrmph." Both are rare, never two lines in a row.
- **Words:** little, heavy, old, tusks, rumble, squares, stomp, patience, ice, first
- **Catchphrases:** "I was on this board first." · "Big feet. Small board." · "Slow is a speed too." · "Back when kings ran from me…"
- **Never says:** Sorry, I'll hurry up.; Bishops did it better.; Who, me? I just got here.
- **Loves:** Being called "the original" (he'll make you say it twice); Polishing his ankus between games until it shines; Snow days, cold squares, and anything that reminds him of the ice age; The moment slammed pieces drift back down, right where he said they would; Long, slow games where nobody rushes him
- **Hates:** Bishops, especially the smug ones on long diagonals; Being told to hurry up; Peanuts. Every new player offers him peanuts.; Tight corners. His tusks hit everything.; Warm weather. His fur takes forever to dry.
- **Flaw or secret:** He brags that he threw off his rider and took the ankus, but there's still an empty seat on his caparison. He never lets anyone sit in it. Late at night he talks to it.

## Smudgy: character sheet

**A lime-green chameleon who never could blend in, so he became a painter and now hides a rook in plain sight.**

Every chameleon Smudgy grew up with could vanish into a leaf. Not him: his orange, teal and green stripes stayed loud no matter how hard he tried. So he picked up a brush and decided that if he couldn't change his colors, he'd change everyone else's. Now he paints his pawns, swaps his rook into hiding, and turns your knights and bishops his color with a flick of the wrist. He wants two things: to be noticed and admired as a real artist, and to pull off one perfect disappearing trick that would make the other chameleons gasp. He sees the other bosses as portrait subjects and color schemes. Fire bosses are "warm tones," ice bosses are "cool tones," dark and light are just black and white paint to him, and anyone who won't hold still is a personal tragedy.
