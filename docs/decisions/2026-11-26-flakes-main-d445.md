# Three retry-passes on main d44548ba, fixed at their causes (Nov 26)

- **Dash's clock tap:** his lying frames are made ahead, a slice at a time, as his battle shows (`prewarmLie`). Made in the beat's
  first render, they froze the screen for 250-380 ms (about 1 s on a slow runner), so a move made at once went in after he was
  down. And the joke is judged by when your move went in (`movedAt`; a party's `myPickAt`), not when the screen drew it.
- **End screen:** an animation's progress never goes below 0. A frame's time can be earlier than the run's start on a busy main
  thread, and smooth() of a negative value overshoots (smooth(-1) is 5), so the bar flashed full and the count stepped back.
- **Hollow Peak's shop test:** its walk taps after the view rests on you, and counts only once you've stopped there. A tap read
  mid-glide hit the dog, and the walk to his counter passed (7, 6). A test fix only; the room did what the tap asked.
