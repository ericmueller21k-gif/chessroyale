# The pogo's sound sped up to the pawn's pace (Nov 15)

- Eric, after riding both: "Dozer is great pogo tho needs to be sped up to match the speed you move around."
- The pogo loop boings 4 times a second; the pawn moves a square every 90 ms. Each ride now has a speed, `RIDE_SOUND.rides[id].rate`
  (the loop's playbackRate while moving; an engine ramps up to it and sinks to `stopRate × rate`). The dozer stays at 1; the pogo is 1.5
  (6 boings a second, about 7 semitones higher). A call: the speed, not a new loop, so it's one number to tune; if the higher pitch
  doesn't suit, the loop can be rendered again at this tempo.
