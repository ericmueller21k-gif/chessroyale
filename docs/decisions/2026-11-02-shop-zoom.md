# Inside a shop: two zooms (Nov 2, 2026)

Eric: "I wanna have the zoomed in be the default with another zoom in option like the town has but this one is super zoomed
resembling normal 3rd person sizing so effectively about a 300% zoom of the current render you did, or honestly just use my
description if 300% math isn't correct."

- **Default inside the tent:** the town's zoomed-in camera (zoom 0, `TOWN_FEEL.camTall` / `camWide`), not the zoomed-out view
  the town opens on.
- **A second zoom, "Close":** a camera that comes down and in behind you and looks more ahead than down: `h` 3.2, `back`
  2.4, pitch 24°, `cy` 0.3 (in place of the phone's 9.3, 5.7, 42°, 0.36). Its scale at your square is 3.0x the default's
  (49 to 146 px a square on a 390 x 844 phone). The call: a lower, flatter camera reads as third person; a plain 3x lens on the
  same camera would only crop the view. The same numbers on the computer for now.
- The − / + buttons switch between the two, as in the town. The mockup is `scripts/boss-art/shop-view.ts … close`, with
  stills in `scripts/boss-art/out/house-shop-babur/view-close-*.png`. These numbers go into settings when the code lands.
