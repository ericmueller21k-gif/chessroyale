# Moo, the cow and his friend the Moon: three stills for Eric's review (Oct 20, 2026)

**Eric:** the boss is **Moo**, a normal cow, with the **Moon** as his friend. Lore: he was a lonely cow who always wanted
a friend; the moon was always looking down, curious about the farm animals, and now they're buddies. The cow doesn't talk
(he's a cow); the moon does, and Moo answers with moos. He's called Moo because that's what the moon always heard him
say. This step is the look only (three reference pictures); his powers and his animations come later.

## What was built

`packages/app/src/characters/moo.ts`: `MOO` with `MOO_PALETTE`, `MOO_POWER_PALETTE`, three one-frame animations and
`MOO_PORTRAIT`. He isn't in `CHARACTERS`, `BOSS_KITS` or the roster: nothing in the game draws him yet. The preview draws
him through its `extra=` option
(`npm run preview:characters -- <dir> extra=packages/app/src/characters/moo.ts only=moo`).

- `idle`: standing on his hind legs, hugging the moon, his cheek on its tip (reference 1).
- `sit`: sitting on the moon, which floats under him like a little boat with its face on the front, his hooves together
  on his lap (reference 2).
- `power`: the idle powered-up (reference 3): `mooLayers(POWERED)` with `pal: MOO_POWER_PALETTE`.

Parts are painted once and the poses are numbers (`MooPose`, `mooLayers(pose, "hug" | "sit")`), as the other bosses':

- **the moon is a part of its own** (`moonBody`: a crescent from three numbers, held or a boat), and its **eyes, mouth and
  cheeks are parts of their own** (it talks and he moos back): `moonEyes` and `moonMouth` (`smile` or `open`);
- **his head** moves as one (`head`: it nuzzles the moon), with his eyes (`eyes`: `open`, `shut`, `glow`), brows
  (`brows`: `none`, `angry`), horns, hair (the cream tuft or the powered-up crest) and the roots of his ears riding on it;
- **his ears** (`earN`, `earF`: the angle each flops at, negative standing up), **his tail** (`tail`: its swing) and
  **his front legs** (`elbowN`, `handN` for the near one, `handF` for the far hoof) from numbers;
- **the aura** is a part of its own (`aura`: three flicker variants, each from its own seed), behind everything.

**The powered-up colors are a palette swap**, not a `look`: `MOO_POWER_PALETTE` is seven keys (the cream's four tones
become a lemon gold, the eyes' two become glowing white, the outline a dark gold), so any frame of any pose can be drawn
powered-up with `pal`, and a transformation can flash it. The shapes that go with it (the crest in place of the tuft,
the angry brows, the glowing eyes, the raised ears, the aura) are `POWERED`, a set of pose numbers. The preview's
auto-looks would have drawn a plain gold cow with none of those, so there is no `looks` entry.

## Calls made

- **Facing:** three-quarters on, facing our left, as Alfil, Sawyer, Chub & Chirp and Smudgy (the references are front-on):
  the snout and the far eye toward the moon, the near eye (the one under the big patch) toward us, the tail on our right.
  The moon is on his left side, toward the board, its face toward us.
- **Layering:** his head is drawn over the moon, so his snout rests on its upper arc (as in reference 1). The other way
  round showed the moon's top tip but hid half the snout, so it stayed this way. His far leg is only a hoof beside the
  near one (the rest is behind it): both forelegs drawn whole read as a cage of bars across his chest.
- **Size:** a 70 x 80 frame. The idle is 68 px from the halo over his horns to his feet, the sit 67 and the powered-up
  one 69 (the aura's tips; he is 67 inside it): all inside the select stage's 70 px (`TALLEST / PX` in `BossSelect.tsx`),
  so he is drawn at full size (0.8), as Alfil.
- **The phone's boss bar: he does not need the `wide` box for the idle or the sit.** Measured from his feet (the halo's
  edge): idle 24 left, 23 right; sit 24, 24; the usual box holds 24 each side. The powered-up still's aura reaches 25 left
  and 28 right, past the usual box on the right and inside the `wide` one (29): if the powered-up state is drawn in the bar,
  give it `wide`, or let the aura be clipped. To check on a phone when he's wired in.
- **The sit floats:** the boat's bottom edge sits a couple of pixels above the ground, over a smaller shadow.
- **Colors:** a warm cream in four tones, a dark chocolate brown in four for his patches, ears, hooves and tail tuft, pink
  in four (snout, inside his ears) with its own nostril key, tan horns, a brown eye with a lighter ring, the moon's yellow
  in four tones with a darker yellow-orange rim on its lower edge and a brown for its face and two pinks for its cheeks;
  the crest (three keys) and the aura (five keys, gold with an orange rim) have keys no other part uses. Outline `#25140d`,
  halo `#2c2624` (the roster's), light from the top left, so he reads on light and dark grounds. The powered-up yellow is a
  lemon gold, cooler than the moon's yellow, so the moon still stands out against it, and its outline is a dark gold
  (`#4a2f06`): on the light ground the gold has a dark edge round it, and the flames' orange rim holds the aura.

## Simplified for the pixel size

- **Fur and lashes:** no fur strokes on his cream, no eyelashes, no ear hair; his eyes are a dark ring, a pupil, a lighter
  lower iris and a glint.
- **Patches:** the references have about nine on the body and legs; he has four on his torso (one over the flank and
  hips, one on his back, one on his chest, one on his belly), a brown elbow on the near foreleg, a patch over the near
  thigh and knee, one at the top of the far leg, the big one over his near eye and his brown ears. In the sit: three on
  his torso and one on the haunch.
- **His face:** two nostrils and a one-pixel smile under the snout, no mouth line; the horns are plain tan, lit on one side; the cream tuft is a bump of five rows with a curl, not separate strands; the hooves are one dark piece each,
  no cloven split.
- **The moon:** the crescent's top tip is behind his cheek (it shows as the moon's top edge under his snout), and its
  lower tip is a thin wedge under his arm; its shine is the lit top-left edge, no sparkle. Its face is two arches, a
  four-pixel smile and 2 x 2 cheeks.
- **The powered-up look:** the glow is a white block with a pale rim, no bloom; the brows are two dark bars; the crest is
  three spikes on a band of hair, not the reference's tousled swoosh; the aura is fifteen flames from the shared `flame`
  shape in five keys, with no haze round them and no loose sparks (a frame's `specks` can add those when it flickers).
- **The sit:** his hind legs are one folded shin and hoof in front of the haunch, with the far hoof peeking out behind it.
