# Coin boost and Crate Bonus (Nov 26, payments PR 4)

Eric, Nov 18: "2.5 times coin boost for your next certain hours of play", from a crate that "occasionally drops from a daily quest"; a crate bonus: "your next 5 level-ups give double crates". Built as the design's section 7 (`COIN_BOOST`, `LEVEL_CRATE_BONUS`; art is placeholder). Calls:
1. The drop is rolled from the quest op's key, which holds the hidden ledger id: the same on every retry, checkable from the ledger, unguessable from a quest's id (no reroll farming).
2. The boost runs only in games that paid play coins: past the day's 300 it waits, so its minutes always buy boosted play coins. A game uses the minutes that earned its play coins (its counted minutes, but for the game reaching the day's 300 only its play coins over 1.8, rounded up: from 290, a 30-minute game uses 6; the review's M1), at least 5 (call 5). A game partly in it gets that share.
3. "Level-ups" are the paying ones (every 2nd level); keys stay 1.
4. Stored as three wallet counts moved only by the ledger. The FAQ says hours (its XP section never says minutes).
5. **At least 5 boost minutes a game** (`COIN_BOOST.minGameMinutes`; the director's call on the review's m1, Nov 26): a boosted game's coins (bonus too) are multiplied by its share of the minutes, so without a floor a 40-second win (1 play coin, 15 bonus) cost 1 minute for 24 extra coins, and short wins farmed a crate to about 810. A crate's worth (extra coins, a fresh day, from core's `boostFor`): about **320** for 30-minute games with no bonus, **420** for the design's 15-minute games with the average bonus (8), **500** for 15-minute wins. Short games, with the floor: 40-second wins about 430 (685 before), 1-minute wins about 460 (815 before). Left open: 5-minute wins all crate long (24 straight) reach about **715**, and the top is about **785** (the day's whole 300 bonus inside one crate, e.g. 24 straight 5-minute Squads 1sts, which no real Squads game is); the bonus steps bound it. A floor of 10 would hold 5-minute wins to about 410 (15: about 290, and 15-minute play unchanged): one setting, if playtests show farming. The FAQ still says no minutes (Eric, Nov 9).
6. **Boss picks are never doubled** (the review's n2): the Crate Bonus doubles a paying level's series crates (`lvlbonus:` op) but never its boss crate pick or its keys. "Double crates" means the level's crates.
7. **The drop's roll reads the odds live** (the review's n2): `questDrop` takes `COIN_BOOST.dropChance` and `LEVEL_CRATE_BONUS.dropChance` as they are when the op is built. A replay after a tuning builds different legs, which is harmless (the op's first leg is already in, so it pays nothing more), but "checkable from the ledger alone" holds with the odds of the day the op was written.
8. **Tests choose the drop** (the review's B1): the test switch deals each quest with a drop (`none` by default | `boost` | `bonus`; `?quests=warm-up:2:boost`) by drawing its id until its op's key rolls that, so no test that finishes a daily is 8% flaky. Production deals and rolls as before.
