# Chandy's Dodge when you checkmate him (Nov 26, 2026)

**Eric, Nov 26:** "he'll only do it if you put him in checkmate. And then instead of the game ending right away, it pauses and he jumps onto the center of the board and collapses, and then he reaches his arm out though towards your king, and that's when he zaps it or whatever, and he's says something like, I'm not I've got one last trick up my sleeve, but something that's not that line, and then it starts. ... if you fail that, you lose, and then if you succeed, then he's checkmated and it goes to the end game screen. ... if you do succeed ... something a little extra too right before it ends, just clearly showing that you win." **A minute later:** "it also triggers still from his 5 upgrades tho".

- **Two ways in, once a battle:** his meter (5 upgrades, then full), or your mate. A mate doesn't decide the battle: his opening plays, then the Dodge. Survived, the win beat plays, then the crowd wins by mate. Used already, a mate is a mate.
- **The opening** is 6.1 s: pause 1.0 s, leap 1.2 s, reach 0.8 s, zap 0.7 s, then his line. The server and solo start the Dodge after it.
- **On a floor of several bosses, a mate is a mate**, so one boss's second life doesn't decide a shared floor.
- **Warned by his meter, then mated:** one Dodge, brought by the mate's opening.
- **Until the art lands,** the poses are stand-ins. The dock reads "Checkmate?" then "Checkmate!", and the God King's CHECKMATE! banner marks the win.
- **His line** is Eric's pick: "Mate? Negative. One shot left in the chamber." It's paced in two sentences, so a phone's bar fits it.
- **The God King stays quiet** through the opening, so no stale warning sits over your mate.

Full design and measurements: PR #973.
