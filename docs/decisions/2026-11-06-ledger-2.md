# The ledger, part II (Nov 6, 2026)

Eric, Nov 6: "things like an audit trail for items does make sense … all these things are worth doing." (Audit of Nov 6, §1.)

- **Keys:** the app sends one request id per buy, open and grant, and its retry sends the same one (`<kind>:<ledger id>:<request>`); an online drop is `drop:<code>:<match start>:<ledger id>`, a Squads reward `squads:<code>:<lobby made>:<ledger id>`. Per account, as the audit says: one account in two seats of one lobby gets one drop and one reward.
- **Squads, at least once:** a reward stays in the lobby's record until it's written, retried every minute; the lobby keeps its code up to 24 hours for it. Its results row goes in once per account and lobby, so a retry adds none.
- **Backfill:** an admin's POST, one transaction, a leg per account and asset, so a rerun adds nothing. The daily check runs in the existing 15-minute cron (09:00 UTC): no new service.
- **The claw** isn't on main: `clawOp` in ledger.ts is its adapter, and the guard test fails on `claw.ts` until it uses it.
