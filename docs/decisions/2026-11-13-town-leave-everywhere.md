# Leaving Chess Town asks from inside buildings too (Nov 13)

Eric: "even if you're in a building, I don't want somebody to click out … There should always be 'are you sure you want to exit' if
you're in the town." Every way out asks "Leave Chess Town?", street or building. Three ways out didn't: the back gesture with nothing
of the app's before the town (the town opens with replaceState, so back left the site: the town now pushes a step of its own,
`TOWN_STEP`), an invite toast's Join, and a profile's Challenge over the town. Calls made: Stay on an invite keeps it (the toast stays);
a town left another way leaves its history step behind, and a back onto it only puts the address back to `/` (one back that does
nothing visible, instead of popping it as the town closes, which races the screens' own address changes). Closing the tab or reloading
doesn't ask: only the browser's own generic box could, and it can't say "Leave Chess Town?". Unchanged (the director's call): out of a
building onto the street, boss challenges, the AFK drop and forced exits.
