# The Colosseum: the standard spelling (Oct 27, 2026)

## Eric's words
"i think we should spell it Colosseum that's the standard way"

## What changed
- Every name players see: "The Colosseum" on its card, on the gate's tablet (the game writes it there), on its chip, and
  in the FAQ.
- Comments, tests, `ROADMAP.md` and the area pages (`docs/areas/town-square.md`, `docs/areas/hub.md`) say Colosseum too.
- Code names stay as they were (`COLISEUM`, `onColiseum`, `houses/coliseum.ts`, the ids `coliseum`, `house-coliseum`,
  `chip-coliseum`): no player sees them, and renaming them would only churn. Earlier decision files keep the spelling
  they were written with.
