# Auto chess: the bot saves when you save (Nov 10, 2026)

Eric: "if you're saving a bunch of your gold, … it shouldn't spend a ton of its own gold necessarily." From round 2 the bot reads your gold at the
round's start and what you carried over (never this round's buys). You saving (10+ carried over or 30+ to start): it banks to your interest steps
(the tens beyond this round's income), 85% of the time, buying only what it needs (no reroll, XP or hold). Calls: "late or low" is round 7+ or
either side at 8 HP or less, and then it never banks; round 1 has nothing to read, so it keeps the old 30% coin flip. All in `AUTOCHESS.bot.follow`.
