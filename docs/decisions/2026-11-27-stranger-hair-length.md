# The Stranger's hair: collar length, nothing behind him (Nov 27, 2026)

Eric, cropping the hair to the face: "This is what it should look like. So get rid of all the hair behind him a bit. Like this is
basically the length that it should be." The long, wide fall behind him is gone: straight strand-by-strand locks frame his face to his
collar and the top of his chest, and the hair behind his head stays inside them. The call: his back hilts sit lower and steeper, their
guards tucked behind the hair at his shoulders, so they read as hilts and nothing pokes out sideways like a twig ("like two twigs").
