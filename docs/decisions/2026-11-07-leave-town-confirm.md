about to leave Chess Town, click yes and no… you should always have a confirm if you're going back out to a different menu or you're
trying to exit."

The call: leaving the town for another screen asks "Leave Chess Town?" / "You'll head back to <destination>." with Stay (focused,
Escape too, a tap outside) and Leave. Asked: the computer's side menu, the town's back button, the phone's or browser's back gesture
and a guest's chat sign-up. Not asked: a room's back (it goes out to the square), a boss's challenge into its battle, the claw machine,
the town's settings, map and chat, the AFK drop, the guest's time-up sheet and a bot game's return to its table.
