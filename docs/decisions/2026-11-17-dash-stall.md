# Dash's stall skit and lying-down clock tap (Nov 17, 2026)

Eric: "once a game if you take more than 30 seconds to move… at 20 seconds he smacks the shit out of the clock for 5 seconds… then at 30 s… he moves again… nahh jk bro and he moves his piece back"; "once per game he goes down laying down with his chess clock by your clock… tapping it"; "yes it should be in groups too".
- **Beats on the move's own clock** (`stallPlan`, `DASH_STALL`): a bank (Boss alone, a party) 20 s / 30 s; a move clock (a raid, a group battle) 50% / 75% of it (the director's call). The clock stops for both beats, for everyone, as for the King's strike (deadlines and bots moved back; `thinkTime` leaves them out), so 30 s means 30 s of your clock (35 s on the wall). *(mine)*
- **The slam and the fake move each once a battle, apart** (Eric, on the review, Nov 17: "this only fire at once 1 time per game", then "if you move in between like you said, then he still does the fake move after 30s but simply doesn't do the goofy crawl, so each still occur once per game"): the slam is spent as it starts (`dash.slam`), never a second. The fake move is spent only as it lands (`dash.stall`): after a move made between the two, it comes alone on a later move that reaches 30 s (75%), with no slam first (the plan's `slamAt` null; the round's `stall` message says `alone`). A King's strike before a beat leaves that beat for a later move. Never with another moment of his on the move, a scramble, his Rush, the Last Stand's re-pick, or in check; the strike is refused during a beat. A beat whose alarm fires late online (the round's deadline within `marginMs / 2`) is dropped: nobody's deadline moves back. *(mine)*
- **His fake move** is a seeded quiet move of his (no capture, castling, promotion, check) as if you'd passed: drawn, then slid back; nothing in the game changes (`dash.stall` records it). The board takes no move in its hold; a pick reaching the server then was made before it and counts (nobody's pick is lost).
- **The clock tap** is screen only (event `clockTap`, 0 ms): a seeded crowd turn 3–9, the first with nothing else of his; he lies in the dock's row beside your time (never over the squares or the digits), his figure in its spot hidden meanwhile; your clock runs. A move of yours cuts it short. Its moment has no kit (no animation or line), and it doesn't quiet his move's own line as that move lands (`quietMove` in `BossCharacter.tsx`).
- Art by the API runner (`claude/boss-art`): slam, fakeSmug, jkLaugh ($1.01) and the lying figure `dash-lie` ($2.37, its colors set to his palette in the game's copy). Lines are placeholders for Eric. Test switches: `&power=stall`, `&power=clocktap`.
