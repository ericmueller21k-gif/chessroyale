# Battle Tower polish from Eric's playtest (Nov 22, 2026)

1. **Moo's Full Moon glow in the accordion card.** Eric: "the cow didn't get his repaint ... it just didn't keep that persistently." Cause: the card's figure (`TowerBar.tsx` `Figure`, also a computer's `TowerSide`) played a fixed idle, never the kit's live state the lone bar's `BossCharacter` reads each frame. Now it draws the idle of `liveKit` (powered, staged) read as each frame is drawn (`SpriteAnim`'s new `pick`) and `lookOf`; the box stays his plain idle's. Audit of persistent looks the lone figure has: Moo's powered twins (fixed), Big Boy's black-pawn recolor and Chandy's stages (now carried; neither meets a tower floor: you play White, Chandy isn't in the pool), the Hidden Boss's suit (not in the tower), Hollow's bulbs (already: the art's swap). Props stay in the card's own `BossBarExtra`, as before.
2. **"Back to the Battle Tower".** Eric: "we need to change that so it says like back to the tower. Otherwise people might think that they're like exiting the battle tower". The floor's end button, the free-leave note and a party floor's leave note (which said "You'll go home") all name the Battle Tower. Elsewhere unchanged.
3. **No "entered the town" for a floor.** Eric: "it never says anything in the chat when you're going in and out of the different floors of the battle tower." Cause: the town is left for a floor's match (its leave drops the pawn) and a floor outlasts `enterRejoinMs`, so the return read as an arrival. The town opened back from a floor sends `floor: true` on its beats until one is answered, and `TownHere.beat` never calls that pawn fresh. Call: the device's word is trusted, as it can only keep its own line out. Going up to a floor never had a line.
