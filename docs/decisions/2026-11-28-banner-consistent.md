# One look for every power banner (Nov 28, 2026)

Eric, on the Stranger's ult banner: "All the words look different… his name looks weird… the text is hiding, and the worlds is,
like, bigger than the rest." The call: one face, the bundled Archivo (Arial Black, the old title face, isn't on an iPhone), for the
name, title and line; the name uppercase on a dark plate; the title on at most two lines of one size, fitted in render
(`fitBanner`), replacing the per-word fit and the `long`, `thaw` and `draw` variants; the line in sentence case with the title's
outline. Simple beats flashy: the same banner, fewer rules. Area page: [bosses.md](../areas/bosses.md).
