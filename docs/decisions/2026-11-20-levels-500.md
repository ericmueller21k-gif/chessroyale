# Levels of 500 XP, rewards every other level (Nov 20, 2026)

**Eric (Nov 20),** on the end-screen mockup, where a typical +150 XP game moved the 1,000 XP level bar only 15%: "Maybe smaller
levels and adjust the prizes less? Like double the levels needed? What do you think". **The director's call, told to Eric:** double
the levels, the same rewards per XP. It changes [2026-11-09-xp-phase2-levels.md](2026-11-09-xp-phase2-levels.md) (a crate and key
every level, a boss pick every 10th) and [2026-11-09-xp-rules.md](2026-11-09-xp-rules.md) (1,000 XP a level).

## The numbers (`XP` in settings.ts)

| | Before | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| XP a level (`perLevel`) | 1,000 | **500** (linear, no top) |
| A crate and a key (`levelCrates`, `levelKeys`) | every level | **every other level** (`levelRewardEvery: 2`: 2, 4, 6, …) |
| A boss crate to pick (`bossCrateEvery`) | every 10th level | **every 20th** |
| Most paying levels a game (`levelsPerGame`) | 20 | 20 (now counts paying levels only: 20 crates and keys a game, as before) |

The rewards per XP are the same: a crate and key every 1,000 XP (at 500, 1,500, 2,500, … now; at 1,000, 2,000, … before) and a
boss pick every 10,000 (at 9,500, 19,500, … now; 9,000, 19,000, … before). The level-up pop ("Level 7!") still plays on every level;
the reward toast shows only on a level that pays (an odd level's game has no rewards to show). A +150 XP game now moves the bar 30%.
The XP box's fill time (`xpFillMs`, 1.5 to 2.5 s by the gain's share of a level) is unchanged in code, so a typical game's fill is a
little longer (about 1.8 s instead of 1.65): more bar to cover.

## Paying safely: old-numbered levels are never paid again

Levels are paid by the server at a counted game, every level owed up to your level now, one ledger op each. Until today the op was
`level:<n>:<ledger id>` with n in levels of 1,000. Halving the level renumbers every account (2,700 XP: level 3 → 6), so the old ops
can't be read as today's levels: an account paid old levels 2 and 3 would have been paid today's 4 and 6 on top (or, with "treat every
level up to the old level as paid", today's 4 again: the XP from 1,000 to 2,000 paid twice).

**The call: count rewards, not level numbers.** The nth reward is the nth reward:

- Today's level ops are `lvl:<n>:<ledger id>` (a new prefix, so the two numberings never mix in the ledger; reason still "level").
- An old `level:<k>` op (k ≥ 2) paid the (k−1)th crate and key: it counts as today's (k−1)th, level (k−1) × 2 (old 2, 3, 4 → today's
  2, 4, 6). If it paid a boss pick (every 10th old level: read from its legs), it counts as today's (k/10)th pick, level 20 × k/10
  (old 10 → today's 20).
- A level owes only the parts not counted yet (`server/rewards.ts`: `paidLevels`, `levelOwed`, `owedLevels`). One case pays part
  of a level: an account paid old level 10 (its pick at 9,000 XP) gets today's level 20's crate and key but not a second pick.

So an account is never paid more crates, keys or picks than one that earned the same XP under today's numbers from the start, and
never misses one. No D1 schema change or migration: it's read from the ledger as it is (the same one read per counted game). Merged
guests' old ops count too (the same `PAID_LEVELS` read). Pre-launch, only Eric and testers hold old ops.

**A later change** to `perLevel`, `levelRewardEvery` or `bossCrateEvery` renumbers again and needs its own mapping in rewards.ts
(settings.ts says so beside them). A rollback of this change would read the `lvl:` ops as levels of 1,000: don't roll back past it
without a mapping.

old levels 2 and 3 at 2,700 XP paid 6 then 8 only; old level 10 with its pick: 20 pays a crate and key, no pick, and 40 a pick; old
levels past the XP now; a merged guest's old levels), `packages/core/test/xp.test.ts` (the numbers, the same rewards per XP).
