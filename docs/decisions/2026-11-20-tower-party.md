**Eric, Nov 20:** "there should be a solo mode, and then there have an invite friends, or another option, which is like basically like open party, and it maybe sends a message in the town that just says like player is ... looking for others to join ... if several people team up but then they like have to take a break, how can they all team up again later from the same starting spot? ... everyone would ... want to be the host then, because they get to keep the ... seed. **Eric, Nov 20:** "Yeah, just keep the join button. ... wouldn't it make more sense to just say like go to the battle tower or something? Because like you could have multiple. It's like you should be able to click on the clerk and see the parties and like which ones you could join or something. I think that makes more sense."

- **Every member keeps a copy** of the run. Teaming up again needs every copy climbed by that run.
- **Re-forming** starts from the lowest floor any member reached with the party. Higher copies go back, asked first.
- **Leaving mid-floor** counts the party's moves as they stood, so it never dodges a loss. Kick is greyed during a floor.
- **Open party:** one line per account, a minute apart, listed on the clerk's card. Its line says "Go to the tower".
- **After disbanding**, a copy goes solo.
- **Pay:** XP, and crates on floors 25, 50, 75 and 99.

No new table.
