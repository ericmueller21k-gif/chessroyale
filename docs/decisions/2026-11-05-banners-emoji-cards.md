# Power banners show your pawn; boss names, not emoji; town cards centered (Eric, Nov 5)

Eric's screenshots (Nov 5, iPhone): Wrangler's LONG PULL banner showed the God King on the right; the status line said
"🤠 Boss plays d4"; the challenge card's text was left-aligned while the homes' was centered.

Calls made:
- The right portrait of a power's banner is your own pawn (`Avatar`, dressed as your profile has it), at the God King's
  old size. The God King keeps his banner only for his save (LastStand).
- A boss's name is "[small portrait] Name" wherever his emoji stood (`BossTag`): the status line, the strike line, the
  results and the move list. Lines that were "Boss plays d4" now read "Wrangler plays d4". A boss with no drawing shows
  no emoji there (BossFace's fallback is nothing).
- The name is fitted to the words' share of the strip (as the big word is) and kept on one line. The strip of a power's
  banner is 44% of the board tall instead of 36%, so the name, the word and its line stack inside it.
- Every town card's text is centered; a person's row keeps its own left layout.

The "plays your move" line keeps its words ("Plays your move: Ke2") with only his small portrait in front: the name would push "Not scored." out of a phone's dock (one row, the first line keeps its width, the second is clipped).

Not changed: the boss-select screen's fallback and the public profile's boss face keep their emoji (not battles).
