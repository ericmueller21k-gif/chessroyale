# XP rules: the calls made building phase 1 (Nov 9, 2026)

Eric (Nov 9) approved the design (notes/briefs/design-xp-quests.md, v2): linear levels of 1,000 XP, 10 XP a minute up to 30, +50 a
win, 10 of your own moves to count, no XP for leaving, guests, test games or banned accounts. The rules are `XP` in settings.ts and
`packages/core/src/xp.ts`; the server's grant is `packages/server/src/xp.ts`. Calls made while building, each with its reason:

- **Whole minutes.** A game's time XP is 10 × its whole minutes (12 min 40 s: "12 min +120"), not round(minutes × 10): the box's
  "12 min +120" then always adds up, and the difference is under 10 XP a game.
- **Your time ends at your last move that went in** (online: the turn's lock; Squads: the half you acted in). "Watching after adds
  nothing" with no extra timestamp per knockout; it costs at most one turn's time.
- **A Squads win is your squad winning its lobby (1st of 8).** XP is once per game, and the Squads game is the lobby.
- **Solo games:** the time is the server's own clock between `POST /api/xp/solo/start` and the result (a new table `solo_games`, as
  boss tokens' `solo_battles`). The moves, the win, a resign and a test switch are the device's word, which can only lower the XP,
  except the win: so a solo win pays at most `XP.soloWinsPerDay` (5) times an XP day. A result with no server start counts 0 minutes.
- **Friends' lobbies (the director's call: once per opponent per day):** the win pays once an XP day per *set* of opponents (their
  ledger ids, never their account ids); the time always counts. A friends' lobby against bots only pays as a matchmade one.
- **The XP day** starts at 08:00 UTC (midnight Pacific), the design's daily-reset time, so phase 2 and 3 share it.
- **Guests** earn nothing only where sign-in is set up (as boss tokens): locally and on the e2e server everyone earns.
- **Not in phase 1:** auto chess (device only), and a 1 v 1 against a bot or a town table (they record no result today): no XP, and
  their result card has no box. The FAQ says so. Worth a follow-up if Eric wants them counted.
- **Banned** gets its own line ("No XP: this account is banned."), not in the design's list: it never shows a silent +0.
