# Building interiors: a battle circle and an animated showpiece in every one (Nov 2, 2026)

- "the center of each building should have a themed circle like the circus, where future battles will take place, Pokémon gym
  stylish in a sense";
- "every house will have an animated piece, the hippo will have a flower garden/pond. The circus add in the same 3 fire
  rings/hoops with the animation being the flame".

- **Every interior** (about 20 x 20 squares, the close third-person view by default: `2026-11-02-shop-zoom.md`) has:
  - a **battle circle** in its middle, in the home's theme: a bold arena floor with a center emblem and start marks on its
    north and south sides, drawn as floor tiles;
  - one **animated showpiece**.
- **Babur:** the battle circle is the ring. The showpiece is three burning hoops on stands, the hoops from his ultimate with
  their flames animated (`briefs/shop-babur-interior-2.md`). This overrides the first brief's "no fire" for that one piece.
- **Puddles:** a garden arena with a leaf emblem, and a pond garden as the showpiece: ripples, bobbing lily pads, nodding
  flowers (`briefs/shop-puddles-interior.md`).
- **Shopkeepers:** each shop's Shopkeeper is the boss's kid, reusing its existing sprite, so the counter is kept low (at most
  about 32 px) so the kid shows behind it. Babur's first counter (58 px) hid Stripes.
