# Birdie: heard every 3-4 moves, a slower train, a warning sign (Nov 23, 2026)

Eric: "the train sounds happen too often and the chirping in game for birdie, make it only like every 3-4 of his moves, you hear those. His attack train move should slow the train a bit down when it moves across the board. Also mark the piece required to move with a yellow train warning ⚠️ like this that's glowing over the tile but also a see through, make it look clean. This way the player knows which piece is in danger."

- **Sounds:** his ordinary move's whistle and the chirp over it play only on his 1st move, then 3, 4, 3, 4 of his moves apart (`BIRDIE_SOUND_EVERY`, from his move's number, so every device hears the same moves). His other moves make only the board's own move sound. *(director's call)* A capture or a check keeps its toot-long and chirp every time; his entrance, power, ride and fall sounds are unchanged. *(mine)* His idle chirps (the peek's chatter, the smug teeoo) also play only on your turn after one of his heard moves: Eric named "the chirping in game", and they sounded every 5.6 s while you thought.
- **Train:** `BIRDIE_RIDE.rideMs` goes from 1300 to 1950 ms. The smash, the pieces stepping aside, his return and the result's hold all follow it through `BIRDIE_BEATS` (solo and online; the server has no copy). *(mine)* His ride's run (`trainLong`) is paced 1.5x (`RIDE_PACE`), so its steam still comes as the train leaves; a unit test keeps the run and the ride together.
- **Sign:** *(mine)* a yellow ⚠️-style triangle drawn in code (SVG) over the piece's square. It sits over the piece, not under it, because under it the piece hides the "!". It is 60 to 80% opaque (36 to 50% read faint on the cream squares: the director, Nov 23), so the piece reads through it, and pulses on a 1.8 s cycle; only its opacity animates, and the glow is drawn once. It shows from the crossing's light until the piece moves (read from the drawn board) or the train reaches it, and it follows a flipped board.
