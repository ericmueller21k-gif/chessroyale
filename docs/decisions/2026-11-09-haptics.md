# Haptics: a tick on a tap and a move, a few boss moments (Nov 9, 2026)

Eric: "when you tap [a piece] … it should vibrate. And then when we drop it on a square … vibrate on that square … if you
just hold down on a piece, then no vibrate. … for phone, like when Big Boy bounces, it could do like 3 vibrates, or when
Babur's balloon pops, a quick one … it doesn't need to be for every boss, just where it makes sense … I could test them to
see how it feels."

**What changed.** One module, `packages/app/src/haptics.ts` (`haptic(kind)`, `hapticOnce`, `cueHaptic`); every pattern in
`HAPTICS` (settings.ts); Settings → Sound, "Vibration" (on; per device). The board ticks on a tap that picks up a piece
(`select`, 10 ms) and on a move you make (`move`, 15 ms). The two old buzzes (the 1 v 1 match found, a crate held) go
through it.

**The boss moments, for Eric to keep or drop** (each a line in `HAPTICS.cues` / `patterns`):

| Moment | Kind | Pattern (ms) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Big Boy's toy block lands | `bigboyBlock` | 35 |
| Big Boy's Big Bounce: the big crash that throws the pieces | `bigboyBounce` | 30, 60, 30, 60, 30 (three pulses) |
| Babur's balloon pops at the pump | `baburPop` | 25 |

**The iPhone, honestly.** iOS Safari (every iPhone browser is Safari underneath) has no Vibration API. The only way in is
the known trick: a hidden `<input type="checkbox" switch>` toggled through its label plays the system's switch tick
(iOS 18 and later; the switch attribute itself is iOS 17.4+). It plays only inside a user gesture, so:
- the tap that picks a piece up ticks (it fires as the finger lifts: a gesture everywhere);
- a drag let go on its square ticks (also as the finger lifts);
- a tap-tap move is made as the second press goes **down** (the board's feel, Oct 12). WebKit may not count a touch's
  press-down as a gesture; if Eric's iPhone doesn't tick on a tap-move, the fix is to tick that one as the finger lifts;
- the boss moments come by themselves (no tap), so on an iPhone they most likely do nothing. That's expected: `haptic()` is
  still called, does nothing and never throws;
- a pattern is one tick per pulse (no lengths), at most `iosTicks` (3), `iosTickGapMs` (90 ms) apart by timers, which may
  play only the first outside a gesture.

**Calls made.**
- A tap is a press let go on its own square within `HAPTICS.holdMs` (350 ms), not dragged, picking up a piece that wasn't
  already (`pickedUp`, logic.ts). Longer is a hold: no tick (Eric's "just hold down … no vibrate"). So `select` fires on
  the release, the only moment a tap is known from a hold.
- `move` covers every move your finger makes: tap-tap, a drop, a promotion picked, a premove placed, a try into Hollow's
  dark. Not a premove playing by itself when your turn comes, a move a power refuses, a drag let go on its own square or off
  the board, the opponent's, a replay's.
- Boss moments ride the art's own cue, beside its sound (`cueHaptic` next to `play` in BossCharacter, SpriteAnim and the
  board's effect batch): once per frame tag, only on the device that draws it, never for a quiet sprite. The town's boss
  intros reuse the same art, so a Big Boy intro that drops a block buzzes there too (fine: it's his moment).
- The bounce is one three-pulse pattern at the crash, not a pulse per hop (the hops' `boing` is in his other animations
  too).
- Left out: "a boss's strike taking your piece" (each boss's strike is its own art: a line per boss, if Eric wants it).
- The 1 v 1 "match found" buzz follows Vibration and still goes quiet with Sound off, as Oct 16 set it (its e2e check,
  `ranked-1v1-browse.spec.ts`, unchanged).
- The switch sits under Sound (a feel, like sound), greyed with "Not available on this device" where there's no way: a
  computer (desktop Chrome has `navigator.vibrate` but no motor, so the Vibration API counts only on a touch screen), an
  iPhone before the switch. Nothing on a hidden page.
- No FAQ line: the FAQ doesn't list Settings.
