# CI: 15 shards instead of 10 (Nov 9, 2026)

Eric (Nov 9), on making test runs faster: "Sure try it." Measured Nov 8: with two PRs' full runs at once (20 shards), every job
started within 3 s, so runners weren't the wait; each shard's 20 minutes of tests was. `MAX_SHARDS` 10 → 15 (e2e/ci-select.mjs):
main's full run and a full-lane PR spread over 15 shards (aim: about 15–17 minutes instead of ~22–28); a targeted PR still uses as
few as its specs need. The repo is public, so the minutes are free. Watch for a wait for runners once several runs overlap
(GitHub's free plan runs about 20 jobs at once); if it shows, go back to 10 or between.
