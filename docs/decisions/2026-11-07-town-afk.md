# Away in the town: the zzz, then home (Nov 7, 2026)

Eric: "I tab off for like hours on my phone and when I come back it's still loaded, shouldn't we be kicking players… After a few min put zzz with no text box… then boot after 10 min of not moving or in the background either one."

- **Any input counts** (a tap, a drag or looking around, a key, the wheel, walking with a held arrow): the simplest rule, and a player watching the screen isn't away. `TOWN_AFK`: zzz at 180 s, out at 600 s idle or 600 s hidden; the idle clock runs on while hidden (no input there either).
- **Not idle presence, so the clock holds:** a boss challenge from the fight to the battle, and the claw machine's controls. A card left open does count as idle (else one open card keeps you forever); matches and bot games leave the town anyway.
- **The server drops a pawn by the same numbers** (`town-here.ts`, in the live hub): each signed-in device beats every 5 s with its idle time; one beat as it hides; no beat for 20 s counts as hidden since the last. A dropped device's next beat brings it back; the app sends you home on its own clock, the moment it runs again.
- **Real players now walk in each other's town** (plain pawns, their names): the "zzz for everyone" needed a town everyone shares, which wasn't built. Signed-in, old enough (the town chat's rule); guests are in nobody's. Where the test switches are on, each test has its own town (`?townroom=`). `?townafk=off` or `zzz,boot,hidden` seconds.
- **Home's "You were away"**: the invite toast's look, 6 s of visible time, a tap closes it; one in-app redirect, no reload.
