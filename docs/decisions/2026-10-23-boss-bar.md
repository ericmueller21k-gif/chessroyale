# The boss bar, tidied for every boss, with room on its right and a level under the name (Oct 23, 2026)

Eric (Oct 23, with phone screenshots of Puddles' bar): "remove the skull shit, right below their name is now the ult bar if
they have one and below that their other move bar, then to the right is now free space"; "his text box very big"; "the meter
bars are not where I want them, and when placed appropriately will create space" (the next boss, a circus tiger, pumps up
balloons in that free space). Later the same day: each boss shows an RPG level right under his name, a number that is not
always the same ("randomly pick between say 40 and 60 even if it really means 1400 elo"), "LVL ???" for Fischer, and it follows
his strength ("For the cow it can also power up when he powers up... if you weaken their move their level should slash down
for a turn, don't forget about all those little things").

## What was wrong

- The battle bar drew five skulls (the boss's threat) in a row with the rage meter and a boss's extra meter, squeezed next to the
  name: at 320 px the row wrapped (Hollow's bulbs, Puddles' Seeds), the status sat in the middle of the bar's height, and there
  was no room for anything else.
- A phone's speech bubble (`.bc-bar-bubble`) was as tall as the bar's whole name room (`min-height: calc(100% + 4px)`) and as
  wide as the name's column, so a one-line "Mind the flower beds." covered the name, the skulls and the meters in a 204 × 85 box;
  Moo's 45-character line at 320 px made a five-line, 94 px box over the title.

## What changed

**The bar** (`BossBar`, `screens/Boss.tsx`; `styles.css`, "Boss battle"): a figure (a phone) and a grid of three columns by two rows.

- **No skulls.** The select screen and the menus keep theirs. The threat is in the bar's accessible label ("Puddles, threat 3 of 5"),
  as before it was in the skulls' label.
- **One column under the name, every row left aligned with it:** LVL, then the ultimate's rage meter (44 px wide as ever; Moo's
  moon beside it in the same row, now `BossUi.ultBar`, and no longer shortening the meter), then the passive's bar when the boss
  has one (`BossUi.bar`: Puddles' "Seeds N", Hollow's bulbs). A row with nothing in it takes no room. The Hidden Boss's phase 2
  has no meters, as before.
- **The status stays at the top right** ("Strikes in 3 moves", "58 moves left", the results, phase 2's "N of 4 won"), 12 px as before
  (it didn't need to shrink: it is 76 px for "60 moves left", which leaves the column and the room beside it).
- **The free room** is `.boss-prop`, the grid's two right columns in the second row: under the status and right of the bars.
  A boss draws into it with `BossUi.barProp` (new, nothing by default; `BossBarExtra part="prop"`): the slot is positioned and
  clips nothing, so a prop can reach past it, as the figure's box already reaches past the bar. The test link `?barslot=1`
  outlines it with a dashed line (`docs/areas/ops.md`, test switches). Its size, wide by high in px, from the e2e (`BAR_DEBUG=1`):
  Puddles 123 × 65 at 320, 193 × 65 at 390, 231 × 65 at 428; Moo & Moon 114 × 33, 184 × 33, 222 × 33; Hollow 165 × 36 at 320;
  a computer's bar (1280) is 504 px wide with no figure, so the slot is 22 px high for a boss with no passive bar (the column's
  height less the status's row; Moo 28, Hollow and Puddles 35) and 330 to 376 px wide.
  The width is what's left of the bar after the name column (the widest of the name, LVL, the bars), so a boss with a long
  name has less (Boingo the Clown 87 at 320); the height is the bar's own less the status row (33 px for a 60 px bar, 65 for a tall
  figure's 92 px bar). The e2e asks for at least 80 × 30 at 320 and 120 × 30 above on a phone, 20 high on a computer.
- **A smaller bubble.** Same font, a thinner frame (2 px border, 2 px shadow, 4 / 7 / 3 padding): as high as its lines and no
  higher, bottom aligned over the bar's bottom edge, from the name's left, and its width limited to the grid area of the first two
  columns, the room left of the status. So it can never reach the status, whatever the line, by the layout alone (no script):
  one line covers the meters' rows and leaves the name, two lines cover the name too, and a line that needs more than the
  bar's height grows upward over the title line. Sizes (wide × high, px), before → after:
  Puddles "Mind the flower beds." 428: 204 × 85 → 198 × 25; 320: 128 × 85 → 128 × 40.
  Moo & Moon "Hi! This is Moo. He's my best friend. (Moo!)" 428: 236 × 65 → 236 × 41; 320: 128 × 94 → 128 × 70.
  Hollow "Hollow is here." 428: 159 × 53 → 153 × 25; 320: 138 × 58 → 138 × 40.
  The computer's board is the same size (456 px at 1280 × 800, 408 at 1280 × 720, 456 at 1024 × 768) and its top moves down by the
  bar's growth (the column is three rows): 1 px for a boss with no passive bar, 7 for Moo, 13 for Puddles; no page scroll.
  The computer's bubble (by his figure beside the board) is unchanged; the bar there has the same column (no figure: his portrait).

**The level** (`BossLevel`; core `bossLevelBase`, `bossLevelNow`, `BOSS_LEVEL`; the runner's `bossView`).

- "LVL 54": the boss's rating over 28 (rounded), plus a random offset of -10 to +10, from 1 to 99. The number is the battle's own:
  drawn from the battle's seed (salted), so everyone in a raid sees the same and a reload shows it again; a new battle rolls
  again. Normal's 1400 gives 40 to 60; Hardest's 2200 gives 69 to 89.
- **Whose rating:** Boss alone's Maia rating (`bossAloneMaia`: what the Boss raid stage shows as "Rating 1400"), the one number
  the player already knows for this battle; otherwise (a raid online, a Solo raid, a test link's tier) the boss's strength in
  the battle. The level is computed where the battle runs (`MatchRunner.bossView`: solo on the phone, online on the server) and
  sent as `NetBoss.level` and `levelBase`, optional fields an older app ignores. The Elo itself still isn't on the wire: only
  this fuzzy level (28 Elo a level, ±10 levels).
- **"LVL ???"** for the Hidden Boss (`BossDef.levelUnknown`; screen readers: "Level unknown"), in both phases. His `level` is `null`.
- **The line is left out** from a server that sends no level (`level` missing).
- **Text:** "LVL 54", 10 px, bold, letter spaced, dimmed white; screen readers hear "Level 54" (a visually hidden text in the bar's
  live region, so the new level is read when it changes; the visible text is hidden from them).

**It follows his strength** (`MatchRunner.bossStrengthNow`): the level is the battle's level plus (the strength he plays his next
move at now - his rating) / 28, from 1 to 99. Up: gold with a small arrow, the number pops (scale and glow, 560 ms). Down: red
with a quick diagonal slash (480 ms flash, 420 ms stroke). Both stay as a color for as long as it lasts, and under reduced motion
that is all there is (no animation, no stroke).

## Calls I made

- **Rating basis for the level in a raid online and a Solo raid:** the boss's strength in the battle (`b.elo`), where Boss alone
  uses the stage's number; "the same number the select stage shows" exists only for Boss alone. The elo scale runs to 3190, so a
  strong raid boss shows 99 (the cap, 100 is Fischer's). The brief said to leave the line out for a battle with no rating
  to show; with a seeded level computed in the runner, every battle has one, and "everyone in a raid sees the same number" needs
  it on the wire. If Eric wants raids without it, it is one condition in `BossBar` (nothing drawn for a battle that isn't
  Boss alone).
- **`weakDrop` 700 and the Full Moon at the engine's top:** the divisor makes 700 Elo 25 levels: a clear slash from 50 to 25.
  Tunable in `BOSS_LEVEL`.
- **A slip isn't shown** (above). **The King's strike during the Full Moon** slashes that one move, as the rules play it ("stagger"
  comes before "best" in `bossMoveKind`).
- **The bubble is limited by the layout, not by measuring** (a first try measured the status and narrowed the box in script; the
  grid area does it with no code and the existing tests that measure a line's box in place still mean something).
- **At 320 px a long line takes more lines** (the room left of the status is 128 to 138 px there): not made smaller (the font as
  now, Eric's brief) and not given the status's room. Each boss's longest line (a copy of the live bubble holding it, measured
  in the bar; none reaches the status at either size), lines at 428 / at 320, and how far it rises over the bar's top edge at 320:
