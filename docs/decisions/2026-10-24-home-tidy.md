# Home tidy: RANKED centered, a shorter PLAY, Other modes with Auto chess, a bigger pawn with its tag above

Oct 24, 2026. Eric's desktop screenshot of home (and the tag he wanted). The calls the brief left open, and why:

- **RANKED** is centered over its three buttons (its label only; the row is unchanged).
- **PLAY** is a third shorter: 64 px to 43 px on a phone and a short window, 88 px to 59 px on a computer. Its width and
  text size stay. "Trim the tops and bottoms a little bit" read as the height shrinking by about a third.
- **No panel until a mode is picked.** "Tap a mode above to play" was the only thing in an empty box. The first pick
  brings the panel in above PLAY, so the RANKED row rises by the panel's room (68 px on a phone, about 70 px on a
  computer); PLAY, the cards and the line stay put. The room comes from the hero (phone) and the live window (computer),
  not from PLAY or the cards, which must stay on screen.
- **Other modes**: an 11 px muted label under PLAY (the RANKED label's style, its words as Eric wrote them), then the Boss
  raid card, then **Auto chess** (the Boss raid card's look and size; "Build an army, watch it fight"). Its picture is the
  board's own white knight (`autochess/pieceShapes.ts`), not a new drawing. Its tap opens `/autochess` as Settings' row
  does. This retires the Oct 19 "shelved" note for home; the Settings row stays as it was.
- **Boss raid title** is Archivo (800, as the other titles), not Archivo Black. Cause: Archivo Black's lowercase i has no
  dot in the font the app ships (`public/fonts/archivo-black.woff2`), so "raid" read as "raId".
- **The pawn** is 75% bigger than before on a phone (170 × 154 to 297 × 267) and on a computer from 1024 px (298 px wide).
  Where the room is narrower it is as big as the room allows: 340 × 308 at 1280 (a card 376 px wide) and 420 × 380 at
  1440 (a card 456 px wide). Widening the card would move the side menu or the live rail, which Eric did not ask for.
  On a phone the pawn is sized to the room the tag leaves (capped at 270 px tall). At 320 × 568 the pawn stays about
  its old size and the page scrolls.
- **The tag** is centered above the pawn's head: the icon (as tall as the two text lines: 38 px on a phone, 44 px on a
  computer), the name over "Weighty · 1580". The outfit line is gone (the helper that named it too). On home only, the tag
  is ordered first; the queue's small card keeps the pawn first.
- **A short card keeps the tag beside the pawn.** Above the head, the Auto chess card and the panel need about 80 px more
  than a 640 px phone has, so with a mode picked home would scroll. Eric's Oct 18 rule was that a short room puts the
  pawn beside the name instead, and the 360 × 640 "no scrolling in every mode" check stands, so the card switches back to
  that row under 190 px tall. Trade-off: at 320 × 568 the pawn is about 51 px (it was 76), because home fits there.
- **The bounce** never ran on a computer because its timer and the hop's CSS were both limited to widths under 900 px.
  Both limits are gone; reduced motion still stops it. Tested on the desktop project as well as the phone.
- **Fit**: home fits with no scrolling in every mode at 360 × 640, 375 × 667, 390 × 664, 390 × 844, 428 × 777, 320 × 568,
  and 1280 × 800 (checked by the specs and by measurement).
- **The first pick** brings the panel in, so the RANKED row rises by the panel's room (68 px on a phone, about 70 px on a
  computer). The keeps-still spec measures every later click against the page after that first pick.
