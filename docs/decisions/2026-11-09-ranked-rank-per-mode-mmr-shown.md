# Ranked: a rank per mode, and MMR shown (Eric, Nov 9, 2026)

Eric, answering docs/ideas/ranked-ladder.md's decisions 1 and 2: "Yes, one rank per mode is perfect." And on MMR: "in your
profile, you can see your rank and it should say in gray next to it, like MMR, and then show what your actual MMR is. There's no
reason for us to hide it from the players. I'm not a fan of hiding MMR."

- A separate rank per mode (1 v 1, Duo, Squads, 50 v 50); 1 v 1 is the main rank.
- MMR shown in gray beside each mode's rank on profiles (anyone's: "no reason to hide it"), rounded. The director's call: every
  profile, not only your own, as Eric's reason applies to all. Profiles today say "never the hidden rating": that rule is now
  reversed by Eric. The judge's "Rating" line gets a clearer label when MMR lands, so players don't see two "ratings".
- Leaving (decision 4), Eric: "Quitting during time control. Yeah, that's fine. Quitting mid-game. Yeah, full loss. Those are all
  fine." As written: a dodge −5 RP (−10 repeated) and a short lockout; a leave a full loss and −10 RP; 3+ leaves in the last 20
  ranked games, the delayed queue.
- Parties count for rank (Eric): "say you're playing with your friends in 50v50. You still get rating points. That's how it works in
  games like TFT ... play with up to 7 other people ... and have it count towards your rank ... they won't even be on the same side
  anyway." A 50 v 50 party (up to 8) queues into the public match, sides random, ranked as usual. Squads parties already queue with
  real people (bots only fill empty seats).
- Still open: seasons (decision 7).
