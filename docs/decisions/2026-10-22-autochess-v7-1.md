# Auto chess v7.1: benches on the board's edges, a bigger board, the clock in the gold row, calmer hops, the reroll price, the damage (Oct 22, 2026)

## What Eric asked

Oct 22, after playing v7 on his iPhone in Safari (his marked-up screenshot):

- **Layout:** "Still wrong spots for the bench, move them flush with the board as planned, also the whole board can be a bit bigger I think as a result of this because we have
  more edge space, the clock moves down between the income and odds."
- **Hops:** "The bouncing is too much, should be randomly once every 7-10 seconds."
- **Reroll:** "Rerolling lets make it cost 5+1 per roll up to 10 max per round. Just base on my testing it's too cheap relative to the total gold for how strong rolling is."
- **Damage:** "I also took WAY too much damage on round one it should be like TFT where each round is like say dmg+1 but we need to nerf the damage to where each game is about 7-10 rounds ish."
- **Shadows** (the director, later the same day): the hopping pawns show two shadows; keep the one that shrinks and fades as the pawn rises.

## What was wrong

**The benches.** The columns of v7 were as wide as the screen but the board is as wide as the room allows: on a phone whose height is the limit (Eric's) the board is narrower than the column, and the benches stayed at the
column's edges: theirs stuck out past the board's left edge and yours past its right edge, as in no mock.

**The phone's real size.** The brief measured Eric's screenshot as 393 x 730 CSS px. The screenshot is 1190 x 2576, which is a 3x phone shrunk to 0.92 of its size, so a phone 428 CSS px wide, not 393: his back button is
123 px wide in it (44 CSS px, so 2.79 px to the CSS px), his bench tiles 40 and his shop's Buy XP button 78 wide. At 428 wide the board's frame in his screenshot is about 300 px, which is what v7 gives at **428 x 777**
(frame 300.5, squares 292): his page is 777 high (Safari's bars take the rest). At 393 x 730 v7's board was 352 (nearly the column's width), not 279. So 428 x 777 is the size that reproduces his screenshot, and both are checked
and shown. It matters because v7's board only lost room to the height on his phone.

**The stale room.** v7's `--chrome` (everything on the page but the board and the shop) was a constant that no longer matched the rows: it overestimated by 33 px at 428 x 777 (the free space at the bottom was 41 px,
Eric's "every phone gets some free space" was meant to be about a dozen) and by 14 px on a short phone.

## What changed

**Layout (`styles.css`, `AutoChess.tsx`).**

1. **Benches on the board's edges.** Their bench's left edge is the board frame's left edge, your bench's right edge is its right edge, at every size: `--gut` (half of what is left of the column beside the frame) is a margin on each
   bench. The frame is the white edge of the board: the squares are 3 px inside it, and up to 8 device pixels narrower on the right (the board's own rounding); the test measures both. The pawns, the title row and the dock stay
   by the column's edges (see call 2).
2. **The clock moved into the gold row,** between "Income +N" and the odds pill: the time ("0:17") and the step's name under it ("Buy"), no box. Like the pill (a block of one inline-block) its baseline is its last line, so
   the step's name is on the baseline of the gold number and the pill's percents, and the time sits where the pill's icons are: the row is not taller for it. The green bars stay on the board. The old spot under the board has no clock.
3. **A bigger board: as big as fits.** Every row has a set height now but the board and the shop, and `--chrome` adds them all up exactly (the safe areas too, which the old constant ignored: on a notched phone in an installed app that is up to 80 px the constant did not know about). The board takes everything left but `--free` (12 px) at the bottom. Room came from: the stale free space, pawns 72 px instead of 82 (the hop's room 10 instead of 12, so a row is 110 instead of
   122) where the height is the limit (a phone as wide as the column anyway, 390 x 844 and up, keeps the approved mock's 82: `--pawn-fit`), the gaps (5 px, with the 4 px frame margin, so the bench is 9 px from the frame: the 6 px bar and 3 px of air, as 10 before), and the gold row's fixed height. The shop cards, Buy XP and Reroll are the same size.
   On a computer the same sums give a 512 px board (frame 520) at 1280 x 800 (was 484, frame 492).
4. **The result may use the 12 px.** The free room is room the board leaves, not padding, so a result that runs a few lines longer than the box reserved doesn't make the page scroll. At 320 the fight's rules are set a little smaller (11 px) so
   they fit the height the shop reserves.
5. **The gold row at 320.** It must hold gold, income, the clock, the odds pill and the streak. Under 415 wide the word "Income" is dropped (the "+140" stays), and the pill is a little tighter (icons 19, 22 px each); at 320 the coin and the
   numbers are a little smaller, the pill's icons 17 px (21 px each) and the streak pill's padding 6 px. Tested with the widest content a long match makes: 3 digits of gold, +200, a W10 streak, at 320, 360, 375, 393, 428 and 430.
6. **Messages and the Sell button.** The Sell button stays in the gap between your pawn and your bench, level with the bench, 44 px tall (as wide as the gap, up to 84). A message now shows under the bench's row, beside your pawn, on
   one or two lines (see call 3). The fight's counter is short in the clock ("12/60"; see the text changes).

**Hops.** Each pawn hops once, then waits a random 7 to 10 s (`hopEvery: [7, 10]`; its own draws, `Math.random` for each) before its next single hop; one hop lasts `hopSeconds` 1.8 s (the same crouch, rise, squash and settle,
once, not a loop). The 7 to 10 s are counted from the start of one hop to the start of the next ("once every 7-10 seconds"). The first hop of a pawn comes after a random wait of up to 7 s, so the two don't start together. Transforms
and opacity only, still under reduced motion (no timer at all).

**One shadow.** The dressed pawn's art for three skins (snowman, gingerbread, chimney) draws a ground shadow of its own; it stayed under the pawn as it hopped while `.ac-shadow` shrank. It is hidden on this screen only
(`.ac-pawn .skin-shadow`; the three ellipses got the class and nothing else changed, so home and the other screens keep theirs). The default pawn has no shadow of its own.

**Reroll.** `shop.reroll: { first: 5, step: 1, max: 10 }` replaces `rerollCost`: 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 10, ...; the first of each round is 5 (the shop's own reroll count starts at 0 with each round's shop). The button shows the price of the
next one, and "Need N more" says the gap to it. The bot pays the same: **`maxRerolls` 3 (was 4), `rerollGold` 70 (was 40), `rerollChance` 0.06 (was 0.15), `rerollChanceBare` 0.2 (was 0.4).** Measured over 3000 plans for each of rounds 1 to 10
(the plan's pure code, gold = the round's income and a little more): at 2 gold a roll the old bot rerolled in 63% of rounds (1.17 a round, 2.3 gold a round); the same habits at the new price would be 63% of rounds and 6.5 gold a
round; the new habits give 31% of rounds (0.40 rerolls a round) and about 2.1 gold a round, so it rerolls sometimes, not every round, and spends on it about what it did.
Two rerolls in a row had random times and could swap places in its list, so their prices are put in order after the times are drawn (the same prices, the same gold).

**Damage.** `hp: 24`, `damagePerRound: 1`, `piecesPerDamage: 3` replace `hp: 20`, `damageBase`, `damagePerPiece`: a lost round costs the round's number plus 1 for every 3 of their pieces left (not the king), rounded down. Round 1: 1 to 3
(1 + 0 to 8 pieces / 3); round 5: 5 to 7. A draw costs nothing, as before. The director's Monte Carlo (draws 15%, 2 to 8 survivors) is a unit test: median 8 rounds, 89% of games in 7 to 10 rounds when each side wins half the rounds
and 85% at 65%. The result's words ("You lose 3 HP.") are computed, so none states the formula; no how-to or FAQ line states the damage.

## Judgment calls

1. **The frame, not the squares, is "the board's edge".** Eric's lines in his screenshot run along the white curved edge; in the approved mock the benches are on it too. The squares are 3 px inside (and, at the right, up to 8 device
   pixels more from the board's rounding).
2. **The pawns stay by the column's edges** (and the title row with them), only the benches follow the board. Everything flush with the frame would put the pawn columns and the bench in less width than they need on a short phone
   (at 320 x 568 a bottom row of pawn, a gap for Sell and a bench needs 230 px; the frame there is 225). Where the board is as wide as the column (390 x 844 and up) they are all flush, as in the mock; at 428 x 777 the pawns are 17 px
   outside the frame's edges.
3. **A message goes under the bench's row, not in the gap.** At 320 x 568 and 360 x 640 the board is narrower than the column, so the gap between your pawn and the bench shrinks to 54 and 64 px: room for the Sell button (44
   wide) but not for words (the 25-character "Board full. Drag to bench" would take 5 lines). The area under the bench's row, beside your pawn, is empty and 190 to 280 px wide: every message is one line there. The Sell button
   keeps the gap, level with the bench.
4. **The fight's counter is short:** the clock has room for about 40 px at 320, so "Move 12 of 60 · twice as fast" became "12/60" (under "Fight"), with the full words as the clock's spoken label (`aria-label`). The "twice as fast"
   is no longer shown on screen. The result box has no clock (it takes the gold row's room), so "Round / Over" under the board is gone.
5. **The 12 px is reserved room, not padding** (see change 4), and pawns are 72 px on a tall phone: a 12% smaller pawn for a board of 348 px at 428 x 777 (with v7's 82 px pawns it would be about 325).
6. **Bench tiles 36 px wide under 415 wide** (were 40; 34 at 320 as before): the Sell button needs 44 px of gap at 360 and 375.
7. **The 12 px free at the bottom is kept in every height-limited case;** a phone that is limited by width (390 x 844 and up) keeps its larger free space, as v7 did.
8. **The hop's first wait, and the hop's length,** are mine: up to 7 s, and 1.8 s (v7's loop was 1.8 and 2.1 s with a rest in each; the two pawns have one hop now).
9. **The bot's reroll habits:** one setting, chosen from the plan simulation above, because the brief asked for "sometimes, not every round" and left the numbers open.

## Player-visible text changes

| Where | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reroll button | `2` (its price) | the price of the next reroll: `5`, then `6`, `7`, `8`, `9`, `10`, `10`, ... (back to `5` each round) |
| Reroll, short of gold | "Need 2 more" (any amount, to 2) | "Need N more" to the next price (e.g. "Need 5 more") |
| Reroll's spoken label | "Reroll the shop, 2 gold" | "Reroll the shop, N gold" (N: the next price) |
| HP of each side | 20 | 24 |
| Result box, a lost round | "You lose N HP." / "Your opponent loses N HP." with N = 2 + 1 per piece left | the same words with N = the round's number + 1 per 3 pieces left (round 1: 1 to 3) |
| The clock in the fight | "Fight" over "Move 12 of 60 · twice as fast" (under the board) | "Fight" over "12/60" (in the gold row); spoken label "Move 12 of 60 · twice as fast" |
| The clock after a round | "Round" over "Over" (under the board) | none (the result's box has the gold row's room) |
| Income, the word | "Income +N" down to 351 px wide ("+N" alone at 350 and under) | "Income +N" over 414 px wide; "+N" alone at 414 and under (the gold row needs the room) |

No other word changed. Messages ("Need 22 more", "Board full. Drag to bench", ...) say the same; they show in a different place.
