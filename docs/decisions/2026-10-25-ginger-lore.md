# Ginger is a he (Eric, Oct 25)

Eric (Oct 25, his words): "Ginger is a he. And he actually likes the cold because they tried to cook him in the oven and he realized that it was going to kill him. So he hates the heat. So that's why he likes the cold… he enjoys cold things. It doesn't have to be excessive though. And his powers are, because he escaped from being baked in Santa's workshop, and then he escaped through the North Pole, but fell down to the cold, but then infused with it, so now that's how he possesses the power of the cold. It's kind of basic, but I like it better. But he also is kind of silly clearly, but has these strong powers."

Ginger is he/him/his in the FAQ, the boss's code comments and the bosses area page. His lore replaces the Oct 21 sketch (the bakery-window story, PR #264, which had her as a she).
