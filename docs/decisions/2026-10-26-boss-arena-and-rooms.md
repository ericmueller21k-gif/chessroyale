# Boss raid becomes Boss Arena; the Coliseum; rooms (Eric, Oct 26, 2026)

Plan only: nothing in the game changes yet.

- **Name.** Eric: "we're going to change it from boss raid to call it like boss arena… that more implies… an arena or… a
  world." Boss Arena is the mode and its town square. The building in the middle, called the Boss Arena in the roadmap until
  now, is **the Coliseum** (the director's suggestion; Eric: "Yeah, the Coliseum works"). The game keeps "Boss raid" until
  Eric says to switch.
- **Rooms.** Eric: "we can't have 10,000 people in this little world… it automatically puts you in like a server or room…
  where you can only have like 100 actual people running around in there, or limit it to maybe 75, since you can't do a
  50 versus 50 in there anyway… they could still invite people into the one they're in, but we would just hard cap like
  the random joins." So: about 75 random joins per room, invites up to about 100, the busiest room with space first, new
  rooms as they fill, empty rooms closed. Maybe a challenge area in the square to queue other modes later.
- **How and what it costs** (the director's plan, explained to Eric, Oct 26): a Durable Object per room with WebSocket
  hibernation, as the lobbies, Squads and 1 v 1 already use; one message per tap; bosses and bots scripted on each phone.
  About $0.50 a month per full room, about $50 for 100 full rooms nonstop (Cloudflare's published prices). Eric: "nothing to
  worry about, this would be a good problem to have." A fresh estimate goes to Eric before rooms are built.
- **How the town square gets built:** one slice at a time on the hidden page `/town`, Eric testing each and sending small
  changes; slice 1 is in `2026-10-26-town-square-slice-1.md`.
