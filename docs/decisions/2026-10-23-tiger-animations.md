# The circus tiger: balloons, animation pack, hoops (Oct 23, 2026)

Lane: `characters`. How it works now: [bosses.md](../areas/bosses.md). Details: git history of this file.

**Eric (Oct 23):** "by the third, and then the final one where it's the actual balloon, it should be pretty clear, distinguishable as far as what the piece is".

**Decided:** a balloon pump in the boss bar inflates a limp balloon through four stages, then it flies to the board as one of his pieces or pops. Pixel style stays: sharper shapes, never smoothing. Balloon color is not tied to the piece. His attack on a king: ten balloons bonk and pop on the king's square. Hoops are steel squares fitted to a 2 x 2 block, fire reaching only outward.

**Reason:** a round hoop would cut into neighbors' squares; a color tied to the piece would promise a queen.
