# Chub & Chirp's Carrot Patch in the rules (Oct 18, 2026)

How it works now: [bosses.md](../areas/bosses.md); fair play: [fairplay.md](../areas/fairplay.md); tuning: [hopper-carrots-sim.md](../../reports/hopper-carrots-sim.md). Details: git history of this file.

**Eric's sheet:** "5 carrots wins him the game the moment he pulls the fifth."

**Decided:** the cast is his whole turn, once a battle: five carrots on empty squares, placed by the race lead. A piece claims a carrot by ending its move on it; the race runs 12 moves each. The spin rolls on his count and places the prize. His race brain and the crowd's judge both weigh the race.

**Fair play:** a race turn counts only when the engine's best move is also the race's best, and is then recorded with the engine's own numbers. Other race turns are left out.

**Reason:** a legitimate carrot move must never count against a player.
