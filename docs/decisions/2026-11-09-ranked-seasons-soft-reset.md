# Ranked seasons: a soft reset and 5 placement games (Eric, Nov 9, 2026)

Eric: "we would do like a soft reset on the ranks, and then you would have to play, you know, 5 more placement games to get your
new rating … look at … how other games do this."

- Three-month seasons. At a reset each mode's MMR is pulled a quarter of the way back to 1500 (`1500 + 0.75 × (old − 1500)`), its
  uncertainty widened, and the visible rank hidden until 5 placement games (Squads and 50 v 50: 3 matches). This replaces the
  design's earlier "drop one tier, no placements". The director's call: the 0.75 pull (Valorant, Rocket League and others keep
  most of your skill; a quarter back keeps a reset meaningful without making the first games lopsided). One setting.
- Rewards for rank: later (ROADMAP.md, "Ranked rewards"). Details: docs/ideas/ranked-ladder.md section 7 (S1).
