# Boss challenge: slower pacing, standing clear of the stools (Nov 2, 2026)

Eric, after Big Boy's live: "it happens so damn fast… slowly walk up to the chessboard, then say your line, then the tiles drop down,
then the seats drop down. Add a little delay between these things"; "Big Boy and the pawn need to be a little more away from the table…
Make sure they're not standing where the chair blocks are."

- Every beat is a number in `CHALLENGE_FEEL` (`introBeats`): a walk to the door at the town's walking pace (200 ms a square), a beat inside,
  a slow walk up (180 ms a square), facing a moment, the line alone (2.5 s), a pause, each build step with a gap, the table alone, then the
  attack swung 2 s until the cover. About 12 s from Fight to the cover for Big Boy and Alfil, 9 for the default (it was about 6).
- The line now comes before the build (it used to play over the pyramid's rise and the blocks' stacking): Eric's order.
- **A hop back, not moved marks:** you walk to and talk on the drawn marks; as the stools come in, both hop back to a clear place (you 2.2,
  the boss 2.6 squares from the table's middle). It's Eric's own second idea, it keeps the rooms' drawn marks meaningful (no art changes),
  and the hop gives the stools' arrival a beat. 2.6 is the widest need (Alfil's attack's feet); one number for every boss, for simplicity.
- **Every boss's attack is swung in place** where it stands (Alfil's and Big Boy's dropped in beside the table, onto their own stools).
- The room's camera across is wider (zoom 0.45 → 0.8, west 0.3 → 0.75) so the boss's step back stays in view on a phone.
