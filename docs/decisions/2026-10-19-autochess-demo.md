**Decided:** a client-only demo at `/autochess`, every number in `AUTOCHESS`. Nothing carries over between rounds. Pawns cashed in for +5 gold (Oct 21) instead of promoting. Quiet fights end after 10 moves each with nothing taken. Checks count for nothing (Oct 21; five in a row won on Oct 19).

**Reason:** a pawn on a thin board was three or four moves from a queen, so promotion made pawns beat everything.
