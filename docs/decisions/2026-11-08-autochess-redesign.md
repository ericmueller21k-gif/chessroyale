# Auto chess redesign (Nov 8, 2026)

Eric, Nov 7: "go ahead and start firing it off" (his v4 mockups and last changes: notes/briefs/brief-autochess-redesign.md).
- The left menu shifted because /autochess used the game frame (icons only, no right column); it now uses the front door's frame, so the menu and the right column stand where home has them. Its top slot says AUTO CHESS and the round and step; the title block and back button are gone on both (the gear's Leave is the way out: the phone mockup has no back button either).
- Cards are square at every size, so Buy XP and Reroll (stacked beside them, as the mockup) are half a card tall: about 26 px on a phone, 44 px wide or more. At 320 px the cards are 44 px tall and narrower (the one size not square).
- "Income +N" is everything earned this round (base, round bonus, interest, streak, the last fight's refund): the breakdown's total. The win-streak pill left the band; the streak is a line of the breakdown.
- The fight's rules may grow the box downward on a phone (never upward: the board doesn't move); the result's box takes the band too, as before.
- Messages show over the board's foot; Sell covers the cards (where a dragged piece is dropped to sell).
