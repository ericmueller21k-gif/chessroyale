# Town: a tap on or near a building's foot goes to its door (Nov 12)

Eric, Nov 12 (a screenshot of the foot of Alfil's Pyramid): "when you click any pixels that are at the bottom of the building, especially where the door is, even on the tiles that are not fully displayed that are underneath the building, that should trigger your guy to run to... one of the two tiles directly in front of these buildings, that would then always pull up the go inside message for both mobile and desktop."

What missed: the sidewalk row right in front of a home (a plain square), see-through pixels of its picture near its foot (beside the pyramid's steps, by its flagpole: the ground behind), and your own pawn standing at the door (a tap on yourself did nothing).

The rule (`world.ts homeNear`, `render.ts pick`, `Town.tsx pickTap`): a tap counts as a home's when it lands on (1) its picture, (2) a square of its plot (the half-hidden row under its front among them), (3) its foot row, the one row right in front of its front edge across the plot's width (the door square and the one beside it among them), (4) a see-through pixel of its picture's box over ground within one square of its plot that is no other home's, or (5) your own pawn over any of those. Never a corner square beside the foot row, nothing two rows out, and never a person, a boss, a table or a statue (those are picked first, as before). Of two homes, the nearer door wins. It walks you to its door square (or the square beside it if taken: `destFor`, unchanged) and opens the same card as a tap on the building ("Go in" for a shop). Both towns, phone and computer; the Colosseum keeps its own rule.
