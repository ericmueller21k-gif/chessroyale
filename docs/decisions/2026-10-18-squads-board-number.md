# Squads: which board you're on, at a glance

Date: 2026-10-18

## Asked

Eric, playing Relay on his phone: some players will lose track of which board they're on (and, with colors
alternating, which side). He suggested a faint number in the board's center, a number by the timer, or a color per
board with a thin frame around the big board, and then: "the mini boards above have their own colors already ... put a
thin border around the chessboard that denotes that", with an on/off switch in Squads' settings later.

## Decided

- **A frame:** the big board has a thin frame in the accent color, the same ring the strip draws around that board's
  tiny board. The ring and the frame match, so you can see which tiny board is the one on the big board.
- **A number:** the line above the board starts with the board's number (1-4 in Relay, 1-2 in Pairs; none in the
  final, which has one board), white with a dark number when you play White there, black with a white number when you
  play Black, neutral when you're watching a match you aren't in. It follows the big board (a jump, a tap, Back to my
  board).

## Why not the strip's colors

The colored bar above each match's tiny boards is the two squads in that match (left half one squad, right half the
other), not a color per board. A frame in those colors would say "Rooks v Knights", not "Board 2". A color per board
was also left out: the eight squads already use eight hues, so board colors would read as squad colors.

## Later

The on/off switch in Squads' settings (Eric: later), if players ask for it.
