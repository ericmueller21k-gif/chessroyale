# Eric's Nov 2 fixes, batch B: votes, boss crates in the inventory, shops (Nov 2, 2026)

Eric's words, then what changed and the calls made. Branch `claude/nov2-fixes-b`; stills in `docs/nov2-fixes-b/`.

1. **"Tapping on an augment area puts your piece there."** (The augments are 50 v 50's pre-game votes: `Settings.augments`.) I tried to
   make a tap on a zone reach the board under it, and couldn't: taps on the zones, before and after picking the pawn up, on a phone
   with real touches, through all three votes and into the game. The board under the vote screen takes no moves, and nothing was picked
   or played. **Call:** the sentence is worded like item 3's ("Buying a boss crate … adds the crate to your inventory"), which says what
   Eric wants, so it's built that way. A tap on a zone puts your pawn there and casts that vote, whether or not you picked the pawn up
   first. Until now a zone took a tap only after a tap on your pawn. The zone's tap area (`vote-zone-hit`) is there whenever you can
   vote, so the tap never reaches the board. It sits under your own pawn (z-index 2), so a pawn standing in a zone can still be dragged
   out where changing your vote is allowed. The e2e checks that no press reaches the board. 1 v 1's time-control vote (`DuelVote`, its
   own component) still needs the pawn tapped first: say if it should match.
2. **"Remove the emojis from the augment stages … delete them entirely."** The `icon` field is gone from `VoteOption`, its data and the
   vote screen (cards, zone labels, the winner's banner). **Call:** Squads' votes use the same screen and the same type, so theirs went
   too. Removed: 50 v 50: Team final 🛡️, Hidden Boss (his roster icon), Duel ⚔️, Normal ♟️, Random opening 🎲, Freestyle 🔀, Bullet ⚡,
   Blitz 🔥, Rapid ⏱️. Squads: Classic ♟️, Mercenary 💰, Coming soon 🔒, Normal ♟️, One opening 📖, Random openings 🎲, and the clocks
   (⚡ 🔥 ⏱️). The boss roster's own icons stay: other screens use them.
3. **Boss crates go into the inventory.** **Calls:**
   - Stored as **one row per crate held** (`boss_crates`: id, user, crate id, bought when), not a count. The buy's payment depends on
     the row the buy adds, and the open's item on the row it removes. That is the same all-or-nothing pattern the coin shop and the old
     buy already use, so there's no new kind of transaction. The count per boss is `COUNT(*)`, keyed by the crate's id ("boss-tiger"),
     one crate per boss today.
   - It's a new table, so older data reads as holding none. `/api/locker/open` sends a boss crate's id to `openBossCrate`. It never
     applies a test switch (forced Fischer Random or shiny): a boss crate is paid for.
   - The Crates tab lists every boss crate, under a **"Boss crates"** heading below the free ones. A crate you don't hold is greyed
     ("You have none"), shown rather than hidden. They share the Beta crate's look, so the boss's token sits on the crate's corner. The
     shop's own crate picture lives in the room's art chunk, which is too heavy to load for the Crates tab.
   - The shop card's answer is text only: "Babur's Boss Crate is in your inventory." Its row keeps its 40 px height.
   - Before a reload, an app still running the old code gets an answer with no `item` from a buy. The crate is safely held, but that
     card shows an error line. Pre-launch only Eric runs it.
4. **Chat text inside shops.** Each shop names its room's tone (`ShopDef.chatTone`): Babur's tent `night` (white), Puddles'
   greenhouse `day` (dark). TownChat uses it instead of the sky's tone while you're inside.
5. **Shop cards in the town.** A home with "Go in" shows only its name and "Go in", centered. Homes without a shop are unchanged: name,
   Hollow's line, "Shop" greyed, "Coming soon". **Call:** a shop home doesn't show a line either ("nothing else"). Only Hollow has lines
   today, and he has no shop.
6. **Drag inside a shop.** The room's rows run toward the camera, and the town's run away from it, so the same formula flipped the
   room's vertical drag. It now subtracts, as the town's drag does. The e2e checks that a point under the finger follows the finger on
   the town and in the room, with a finger (phone) and a mouse (computer).
