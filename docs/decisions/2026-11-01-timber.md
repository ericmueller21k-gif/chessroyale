# Timber the beaver: a new boss, admins first (Nov 1, 2026)

Eric's idea (Oct 20, `docs/ideas/beaver-builder.md`), his design approved Nov 1 and the art the same day ("Yes, both"). **Timber**
(id `timber`): a brown beaver standing upright, big buck teeth, an orange hard hat and vest, a yellow tool belt, planks on his back;
his sidekick a woodpecker foreman. How it works now: [bosses.md](../areas/bosses.md) (his row); the tuning:
[reports/timber-sim.md](../../reports/timber-sim.md).

## Eric's design, as built

- **Workshop strip** above the board, his side: pawn 0/2, knight 0/4, bishop 0/4, rook 0/6, queen 0/12; scraps land on them.
- **Scraps** (passive): 1 every 15 s (a real timer) plus 2 for each capture of his; each to one of the pieces at random. A finished
  piece drops onto an empty square of his half at the end of his turn; at most one a turn; a cap a battle (the sim's); its slot
  starts over at 0. A pawn never on a back rank; legal positions only.
- **Gnaw** (ultimate): after the usual warning he gnaws one of your pawns at random, eating it: 8 scraps. No pawn: nothing (said on
  screen).
- Not built here: the beaver tokens and hat (stored data, a later PR), boss shops.

## The calls made here, and why

1. **Admins first is one list in settings:** `BOSS_ADMIN_FIRST = ["timber"]` (settings.ts). `isPlayable` leaves him out (no random
   pick, no player's select or menu, no raid lobby's, no Boss Battle party's), `menuBosses` and the select give admins his tile
   like any other (`adminFirst`, `adminFirstBosses`), and Boss alone or a Solo raid meets him for an admin by the same path as the
   Hidden Boss (`bossHidden`). **To put him live for everyone: take `"timber"` out of `BOSS_ADMIN_FIRST`.** Nothing else changes;
   the FAQ block is written and shows once he's playable.
2. **The real-time meter is one block** (`ticksAt` in `bosses/base.ts`): ticks since `since` on `Battle.now` (the server's clock
   online, the page's visible time solo, the sims' own). Puddles' seeds now count with it; Timber's scraps do; Blitz's is next. The
   screens read it on a timer set for the next tick (the garden clock, `gardenNow`), never per frame.
3. **Where a scrap goes is public:** its roll is its index and the workshop's seed (`wseed`, drawn from the battle's), so every
   screen draws the same scrap landing on the same piece, from two numbers, with nothing sent as one lands. Nothing is hidden by it
   (which piece finishes next is on the strip anyway).
4. **Which finished piece drops: the first finished** (`done`), as Eric put it ("when one is finished … he drops it on his next
   turn"); one he can't have in a real game waits (a ninth pawn, more pieces than promotions could make: the engine refuses them).
   So **nothing drops while he has his whole army**; the workshop fills meanwhile. (The first build dropped the most valuable: the
   sim's first run gave him a queen in half his battles.)
5. **Where it drops:** his own two back ranks first, then the rest of his half, a seeded order, the first square Babur's landing
   guards allow (`landCheck`: not a square you attack, no check either way, no mate in one for either side, a move left for you, a
   pawn never on rank 1 or 8, not the en passant square). No engine is asked (simple beats flashy: no protocol, no host request).
   His back ranks first is the sim's call: a free piece in your face decided too many battles.
6. **The Gnaw comes after his move** on his turn after the warning (he keeps his turn), as Babur's hoops: a pawn eaten as your turn
   begins would change the board with no turn of his to show it. Never a pawn whose going adds an attack on your king (an impossible
   check) or leaves you no move; the en passant square kept only while it can still be taken. You have pawns but none allowed: it
   waits a turn. None at all: it comes and eats nothing ("None of your pawns left to gnaw"; his grumble).
7. **After the cap the workshop closes:** no more scraps, the strip greyed with "Closed" (nothing hidden). A scrap that rolls when
   every piece is finished is wasted.
8. **The strip's place:** the boss bar's prop slot (`barProp`), fixed: a phone's at its top right under the status (his speech
   bubble sits at the bar's foot, under it), a computer's left of "Move N" over the bar's height. Its size never changes.
9. **The sidekick** is on the strip: the woodpecker foreman's still at its end, pecking (a CSS nod) as each scrap lands. He isn't in
   the town square yet (the town's twenty-bot ladder is the town lane's; a follow-up).
10. **His art** is copied as drawn from `claude/boss-art` (`scripts/boss-art/out/boss-beaver`, `sidekick-beaver`; a line in each file
    says so; only the import paths and the figure's id changed). The game draws him in a frame with room round it
    (`timber-figure.ts`: 37 rows over his hat for his entrance's drop from above, a few at the sides): the art touched its 88 x 88
    frame's edge, which the character test forbids. No attack on a king (not drawn: the art run's open choice 1), so he keeps the
    usual Last Stand and mate. His new cues borrow other bosses' sounds until he has his own.
