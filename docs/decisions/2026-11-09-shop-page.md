# The Shop page, look first (Nov 9, 2026)

Eric: "we do want to follow basically the screenshot I sent you … Pretty much exactly how it is, but obviously we'll need our own
artwork inserted where the keys are and where those mystery crates are." Built from the director's brief
(`notes/briefs/brief-shop-page.md` on `claude/director-notes`). Look only: no purchase is wired.

Calls made:
- **A tapped price opens a small pop-up, "Buying opens soon." with OK** (not an inline note), so nothing on the page moves; the limited
  clock's words use the same pop-up. Escape or a tap outside close it.
- **The clock sits top-left** (as in the ribbon mockup), since the ribbon takes the top-right; it's a 44 px button with the gold
  corner drawn in it.
- **Card lines are the mockup's** ("A classic crate with a variety of rewards.", "A limited-time crate with special rewards.", "A
  limited-time crate for early supporters."), the Boss Crate's the brief's; the Crate Key's is "Opens one crate."
- **Rounding:** `shopPrice` gives whole coins, half up (`SHOP.discount` clamped to 0–1). At 0 the line under the tabs says "Buying opens
  soon." instead (the line keeps its place).
- **The bundle's crates** are the HunChess crate's black and gold with the Boss Crate's "?" seal; the keys are `KeyArt`'s drawing
  (shared as `KeyShape`). The Mythic Crate's shimmer reuses the HunChess crate's glint (`.hc-crate-glint`, still under reduced motion).
- **`BossCrateArt({ boss })`** is ported from the approved mock with a token seal, but the Crates tab keeps its current boss-crate rows
  (the brief leaves Crates and Locker unchanged).

**The Mythic Crate (defined here only; wired with purchases):** it holds what a Fischer Random pull holds: Legendary or Mythic items
only, at Fischer Random's odds (Legendary 80, Mythic 20). Each of the bundle's 3 random crates is a Mythic Crate with chance
`SHOP.bundleMythicChance` (0.05), else a random regular crate.
