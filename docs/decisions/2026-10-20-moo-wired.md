# Moo & Moon, wired in and playable (Oct 20, 2026)

The wiring lane for **Moo & Moon** (id `moo`; his rules: [2026-10-20-moo-rules.md](2026-10-20-moo-rules.md), his art:
[2026-10-20-moo-animations.md](2026-10-20-moo-animations.md) and [-still](2026-10-20-moo-still.md)). He is **live**: his
roster line has `kit: "Moo"`, so Boss alone, raids, the random pick and the Boss raid screen's eleventh tile all meet him.
How it works now: [docs/areas/bosses.md](../areas/bosses.md) (his row). The director reviews the stun on the board, the
powered look and the lines before this merges.

## What a player sees

| Moment | On screen | Length |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Moonbeam** (as the crowd's turn begins, after his 3rd move) | he lifts his snout and moos; at the `beam` cue (350 ms) a moonbeam falls from the top of the board onto the piece (220 ms), hits (240 ms), and holds: the beam's glow over the piece, a crescent moon burned into its square under it. Both stay for the crowd's whole turn and fade (0.34 s, `FADE_MS`) when the crowd's move lands. A tap on that piece's moves says, in the dock, "Moonlight holds that piece." Any other piece moves as usual | 1040 ms |
| **Full Moon** (as your move lands, while he thinks) | the Moon in his arms swells and he takes in its light; at the `swap` cue (480 ms) his colors go gold, his eyes glow white, his crest and ears stand and the aura flares on. From there **every animation of his is drawn powered up** (idle, thinking, move, capture, hurt, the king attack…), for the whole Full Moon | 1340 ms |
| **Flurry** (the Full Moon's first turn, in place of that turn's Moonbeam) | the Moon rises over his head and fires five beams, `beam1` to `beam5`, 120 ms apart, each falling on its own square in the order the rules give; all five hold together as the stunned marks, then through the crowd's turn | 1430 ms |
| **Dazed** (as the crowd's turn after his last powered move begins) | the aura fizzles; at `calm` (280 ms) his colors go back to normal | 1250 ms |
| **The Moon on his meter** | the rage meter's number as a 12 px moon, new to full in eight steps, beside the meter in his bar; the Moon says a line as the meter passes a quarter, a half and three quarters | |

No banners: the Moon talks (his lines are the moments' words) and the board shows the rest. The lengths are `MOO_BEATS` in
`bosses/moo.ts`, so the server waits exactly as long as the screen plays.

## The stunned mark: one rule, in one place

The view's `powers.stunned` is the truth (the squares that can't move this crowd turn). `components/Moo.tsx`'s `MOO_UI.board`
draws two layers from it and the moment's start, so every screen and every device shows the same thing:

- **Under the pieces**, one canvas (`BoardEffects`, `moonScorch`): each crescent burns in as its beam hits, holds, fades.
- **Over the pieces**, a column per held square (`.mo-sky`, `.mo-beam`, eight squares tall, its bottom square the target,
  clipped at the board's top, never taking a tap): fall, hit, hold, fade (`beamPhase`).
- **The fade** starts when the view empties `stunned` (the crowd's move landed). The screen that sees it remembers the squares
  it was holding (`heldMarks`, a battle at a time), so the marks fade out instead of vanishing, on whichever screen is up.
- **The refusal** is Ginger's frozen piece's: the board's `onRefused` shows `REFUSED.stunned`; nothing moves or snaps back,
  and the player picks again at no cost. The judge, the bots and the God King never offer a stunned piece's moves (the rules).
- **The held beam's veil.** The art's `hold` shaft is half see-through all the way up the file, which would veil every piece
  above the target for a whole turn (and five files at once in the flurry). One CSS mask on `.mo-beam[data-phase="hold"|"fade"]`
  fades it out three squares above the piece; the falling and hitting beam is whole. Delete the rule to get the art's shaft.

## Powered up: twins, not a recolor

The approved art is not touched. Each animation has a **`p_` twin** in `characters/moo.ts` (the same frames and cues, drawn with
`POWERED` and the palette swap), and `poweredKit(kit)` (`kits.ts`) maps every beat and loop to its twin while he is powered.
`mooPowered` (`characters/moo-state.ts`) says when, from state every player shares plus two times the screens note as their
moments play (the transformation's `swap`, the daze's `calm`):

- powered from the `swap` while the view's `fullMoon` is set (his whole Full Moon, his five moves at full strength);
- his last powered move and the start of the daze stay powered, until `calm`, on his own screen only; from the crowd's turn on
  the daze is over;
- `BossCharacter` asks each frame (`nowRef`), so a screen drawn mid-Full-Moon (a late joiner, a reload) is already powered.

## He hugs the Moon, then sits on it

Eric's idea (Oct 20): at rest he hugs the Moon, now and then lets go, sits on it, and hugs it again. `characters/stance.ts`
(pure, tested): the spell starts when he settles into his idle; `hugMs` 7000 hugging, `toSit`, `sitMs` 5000 sitting (a loop), `toHug`,
round again. **A moment never waits for it and is never cut by it:** the next moment starts the spell afresh, in the idle, so what
plays during a moment is always the moment's own animation. The switch's slip and plop are played once per showing. Powered, he
does not sit (`powerIdle` has no stance).

## Pixel icons in his words

His lines carry `{heart}`, `{question}` and the like (ten icons, the art's). `speech-icons.ts` parses a line into words and icons
(`parseLine`); `speech.tsx`'s `SpeechBox` draws each icon as a small image (cropped to its pixels, one image pixel to a screen
pixel, `.line-icon`) inline, in his bubble and wherever his lines show: the boss bar's bubble, the select screen's. A screen reader
gets `plainLine(text)`: the words with the icons, and the space before each, dropped ("(Moo! {heart})" reads "(Moo!)"). An icon
counts as **one character** everywhere a line is measured (`lineLength`: the typing speed, how long it stays up). A `{word}` that
isn't one of his ten stays as written.

## Drawn ahead, and the first move on a slow phone

Nothing of his is built when the app loads (his art file once took 6.6 s to import with every aura and glow built eagerly; they
are now `placedLazy`: registered, painted the first time a frame uses them; the import is about 340 ms and every frame is
identical: the hash of all frames is unchanged). When his battle starts (`prewarmMoo`) what takes no aura is drawn a slice a
frame, and the frames that each paint one (a hundred and fifty, most of them his powered twins) go **one every 200 ms**, in
the order they will be needed (moonbeam, dazed, Full Moon, powered idle, flurry, then the twins). `glow` (the aura's soft light)
uses squared distances and one square root (`Math.hypot` is slow): the same pixels (the hash of every frame is unchanged), about a
third less time building every frame.

Measured with `npm run perf:boss` on the phone profile (CPU 4x slower), the production build:

| | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| first move: long tasks (over 50 ms), slow frames | 44 (135 ms the longest), 37% | 17 to 19 (188 to 200 ms), 18 to 20% |
| later moves, the Full Moon's and the flurry's moments | flat, 16.7 ms | flat, 16.7 ms (no long task inside the Full Moon's or the flurry's moment, forced at move 3) |
| comparison, same script: Smudgy / Chub & Chirp, first move | | 8 long tasks, 10% / 3 long tasks, 9% |

His first turn is still about twice Smudgy's; every later move is flat and nothing grows with the match (25 moves). What
is left in the profile is mostly his frames drawn ahead during the intro (`glow`, `aura`, `frameKeys`); a real phone is Eric's check. The perf
script needed one fix on the way (`scripts/perf-boss.mjs` clicked "Boss alone" before opening the Boss raid card).

## The calls made here, and why

1. **Powered twins, not a palette applied at draw time.** A twin is a real animation, so the art tests, the contact sheets and
   the cue/sound tests cover it and its lengths are checked equal to the original's. A recolor at draw time would have missed
   the aura, the crest and the angry brows, which are parts.
2. **The Full Moon is a state, not an animation.** Powered is read from the shared view plus the two noted times, so a reload or
   a late joiner draws him right and no screen has to have seen the moment.
3. **The held beam is one CSS mask,** not a new animation. The art is approved; the mask is one rule to delete.
4. **dazed has CRITICAL priority.** The daze comes straight after the Moonbeam his last move may bring, and its line was being
   dropped as stale behind the Moonbeam's.
5. **No banner for any of his moments.** The brief says the Moon talks; a banner would cover the board during a turn that is
   about the board.
6. **The select tile is the eleventh boss, so thirteen tiles in all and "?" alone on the third row.** The alternative is a
   seven-and-six grid, which I did not try (a narrower tile than the six the Oct 19 call measured). Say if you would rather "?"
   sat beside the Hidden Boss's tile or the rows were different.
7. **The flurry's beams are quiet (no shimmer each)**; the Moon's `beam1` to `beam5` zaps are their sound. A single beam's shimmer
   plays as it hits.
8. **`MOO_CHANCE`:** Smudgy's, plus the wax, the ultimate, the flurry and the daze always speaking.
9. **The long lines wrap.** See below. Nothing was shortened.
10. **The FAQ block now says what the board shows** (the glow and crescent, his gold fur); the numbers are still from the settings.
11. **His strength offset is −240, not −250** (the coordinator's call after CI, Oct 20). Once he was playable, Big Boy's roster test
    ("weaker underneath than the others": `packages/chess/test/bigboy.test.ts`) saw a tie at −250 and failed. Moo plays at
    Stockfish's strength floor (about 1320 to 1350), so 10 points change nothing in play; the raid sim (`reports/moo-sim.md`) was
    run at −250 and stands. The Big Boy test is unchanged; the roster test in `packages/core/test/boss.test.ts` says −240; the
    FAQ block reads the offset from the roster ("play 240 under the usual strength").

director can shorten them without touching anything else.
