# Rides v2: every boss's ride for admins, front and back views, trails (Nov 1, 2026)

Eric (Oct 31 – Nov 1): a ride for every boss in the town, so he can "load them and test the feel"; "we're gonna have to change the
views so there is a run up and a run down"; trails "only appear when you run", and a ride's trail replaces the town's droplets
("Chirp's… definitely the way to go"); on sizes, after trying `?rideScale=`: "0.75 is good, I have a feeling most we will scale to that".

## What changed

- **Fourteen new rides** (16 in all), Mythic, slot `ride`, in no crate, shop or drop: only admins have them, as admin copies. Names and
  lines are each drawing's own (crates.ts). Hollow's Bulb is two-color (its glow is the second color, as drawn), so its admin copy is
  red with an emerald glow. Moon's Ride is as it was (side only, the droplets); the old cat (`hollow-steed`) isn't added.
- **The drawings** were copied from `claude/boss-art` (`scripts/boss-art/out/<ride>/`), Riding Chirp's replaced by its version there
  (the side unchanged; its views and footprints added). Only the import lines changed, plus the `!`s the app's stricter typecheck
  (`noUncheckedIndexedAccess`) asks for, put in by a script; **Hollow's Bulb's green cord was taken out by hand**, from the side and the
  back view (the front never showed it); nothing else of the bulb changed (its tile frame included). The brush's plum seat pad stays.
- **Views in the town**: walking left or right, the side (mirrored as before); up the screen, the back; down, the front; standing, the
  idle of the view you last walked in (`Walker.view`). The back and front are never mirrored. Others' riders the same way.
- **From behind, the pawn's face items don't show; its hat does** *(the director's call)*: the back view carries the pawn dressed
  without its face slot (`offFace`, you-look.ts).
- **Trails** *(the director's calls: seen only by you, like the droplets; scaled with the ride's size)*: on a ride with a trail, each
  square you leave gets its mark (one every `every` squares), on the mark's own times and fade, pointing the way you went (the side's
  mirrored going left), in place of the droplets. Only while running; standing, none come (the last ones fade). No ride, a ride not
  ready yet, or Moon's Ride: the droplets as before.
- **Cheetah Shoes walk twice as fast**: `RIDES.speed` in settings (one number a ride, default 1; the shoes 2). Your step takes
  `youStepMs / speed` (45 ms), another pawn's `botStepMs / speed` (100 ms). **The server doesn't relay town movement** (no real players
  walk in the town yet; nothing in `packages/server` reads a walk), so there is no speed check there to scale. When players' walks are
  relayed, the server must read the speed from the worn ride's catalog entry, never the client's word.
- **Sizes**: `RIDES.defaultScale` 0.75 for every ride (it was 1), `RIDES.scale` empty (an entry only where one differs). `?rideScale=`
  still tries others.

## The footprint (item 7): the town draws its riders from the drawings, no sheets

Today's way, WebP sheets baked per ride and color, was 2.0 MB for two rides; fifteen rides with three views would have been about
40 MB. **The choice: no baked pictures at all.** The town composes each rider's frames, once, from the ride's own drawing, the way it
already makes your dressed pawn (`dressedPawn`): each layer of each frame is rendered as SVG (`components/rides/draw.tsx`), made into an
image, and put down with the pawn on its seat, **one frame an animation frame** (as the sheets' composing was), then its trail marks.
The only thing that needs a browser ahead of time is each view's box, measured by `scripts/measure-rides.ts` into
`town/ride-cells.ts` (a few lines a ride; a hash test keeps it fresh).

- **Repo**: the 18 WebP sheets (2.0 MB) are gone; the 15 drawings add about 300 KB of source; `ride-cells.ts` 3 KB.
- **Download**: no sheets ever (a rider used to fetch 50–260 KB each). The drawings are in the app's main bundle as before (the
  locker, profiles and home draw them): main `index` 2,398 → 2,588 KB, **+55 KB gzip** (770 → 825 KB), the town's own chunk +2 KB.
  Splitting the drawings into a chunk a ride, loaded when worn, would take those 55 KB back off the first load; not done here (the
  locker, home and profiles draw rides synchronously, and making them wait would move boxes).
- **Frame rate**: composing costs no long tasks (0 on main and the branch, phone; below) and a set takes about 2 s on the phone (main's
  sheets, about 0.6 s; the person walks on foot meanwhile). After that the town draws one picture a rider, as before.
- **Bonus**: exact colors, second colors included (the sheets had one color), and nothing stale to rebake when a color changes.
- **Memory**: a rider set now holds three views (front and back cells are narrower), about 2–3× the old set; shared and freed as before.

## Main's flaky "beside you" (e2e/town.spec.ts, rides)

Main's run on 60975fa failed "beside you: 22,17" on the phone (and was flaky on the desktop). The cause, reproduced locally (9 of 10
tries with a 50 ms poll): **the camera trails your walk and keeps gliding a moment after you stop**. The test read the square beside you
the moment you'd arrived and clicked it; by the click the square had moved 5–12 px. On a phone the next square's middle is only about a
quarter of a square (8 px) clear of your own tap box, so the click landed on your own pawn and you didn't move. (#435's sizes weren't
it: the tap box is the pawn's at any size.) Fixed in the test: it reads a square once the camera has settled (`squareSettled`), and it
waits for walks already under way to finish after `__townCall(-1)` (which stops new ones only), so a bot can't claim the square as you
tap. The check itself is unchanged. 12 runs under 4–6 parallel workers passed.
