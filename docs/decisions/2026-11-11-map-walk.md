# Town map: tap a boss's home to walk there (Nov 11, 2026)

Eric: "When you open the mini map, if you tap on one of the bosses' buildings, it'll make your guy run there. And that's it. Simple."

- A tap on the large map's boss portrait, or on its home's block (within 2 squares of the home's middle; the nearest home wins),
  closes the map and walks you where a tap on that home in the town would (`destFor`: by its door), through `walkHome` in `Town.tsx`.
- Call: no card opens ("And that's it"); tap the home when you get there. Any other tap on the map still only closes it; the small
  minimap is unchanged.
