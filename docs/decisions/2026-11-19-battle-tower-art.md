- **Lot:** Colosseum Row's north side, columns 45-48 (rows 40-43), facing south, between Dash's Kicks and Seam Avenue (look-alikes 43-44 and 49-51). It was lot ground already, so no walk, street or the Colosseum's plaza (columns 13-32) changes; on the back line it rises over the back wall from most of the town. - **The clerk:** a penguin bellhop (black and white like the pieces, gold cap and bow tie, a slate jacket): nobody's kid; drawn at his art's size (`tall: 57`) so he shows over the counter.
- **Rooms:** 10 x 10 box rooms with one-surface side walls (as the Walk-up's). The floor number is on both side walls as asked and, since side walls are edge-on on a phone, also on the back wall either side of the elevator.
