# Dash the cheetah: a new boss, admins first (Nov 3, 2026)

Eric's design and art approved Nov 1; his name Nov 3: **Dash** ("Blitz" is a chess time control: confusing). A lanky, cocky
cheetah in a backwards orange cap and a teal and orange track jacket, a chess clock under his arm (ticks, never numerals); his
sidekick **Tick**, a sleepy tortoise with the clock on his shell. A bit silly, trying way too hard with the younger crowd's slang,
a sneaker hustler; friendly-cocky, never mean. How it works now: [bosses.md](../areas/bosses.md) (his row); the tuning:
[reports/dash-sim.md](../../reports/dash-sim.md). Round 1 of 3: his home and sneaker store (round 2) and his challenge intro (round 3)
aren't in; with no home, his challenge isn't offered.

## Eric's design, as built

- **Clock Slap** (passive): when he captures a piece he slaps the clock and steals some of your time.
- **Time Scramble** (ultimate): after the usual warning, your next 5 moves get 3 seconds each, a big countdown over the board.
- He never thinks: his moves come at once.

## The calls made here, and why (Eric asked for few questions)

1. **"Your time" is the clock you already have**, the dock's clock at the right of the box under the board (the same place on
   every boss screen, phone and computer; nothing new takes room, nothing shifts): **a time bank** where the battle has one (Boss
   alone and a Boss Battle party: BOSS_CLOCK's 10 minutes + 10 a move; a party's one team bank), **the move timer** where it
   hasn't (the Solo raid and a Boss raid: 20 s a move). One rule over both: the slap takes from it, the scramble caps it.
2. **How much a slap steals:** 30 s off the bank (`slapBankSeconds`), or your next move's timer 6 s shorter in a raid
   (`slapMoveSeconds`, never under `slapMoveMin` 8; one slap a move, they don't add up). A slap that would take nothing (an empty
   bank, a scrambled move next) doesn't come. In the sim about 10 slaps a battle took 5 minutes of the bank and nobody's bank ran
   out (the increment gives most of it back): pressure, readable at a glance (the "−30s" flying from your clock to him, Tick's
   tally "−5:00"), never the decider.
3. **The scramble's 3 seconds** cap the move's clock (`scrambleMoves` 5, `scrambleSeconds` 3, Eric's): on a bank they still come out
   of it and it still gets its increment; in a raid they're the move timer. The countdown is the app's own big 3-2-1 (the round
   start's `.cc-num`) in his orange, over the board, taking no taps; it stands in for the move's "Round end" count. The moves left
   are in Tick's tally ("3s × 4").
4. **Out of time is never a loss against him** (`noTimeLoss`): a move whose time runs out (a scrambled move's 3 s, or an empty
   bank) gets **a move played for you at random from the judge's top 4** (`flagTop`), seeded (the same on every device), his finger
   gun and his jab as your next turn begins ("Too slow, cuz! I picked one for you."), and the dock says "Out of time: a random move
   was played for you". The bank can sit at 0; each move then has its increment. In a raid it's only when nobody picked in time:
   the crowd's picks made in time are played as ever. **Why the top 4, not any legal move:** in the sim a random legal move hung a
   piece more often than not and decided the battle (under one a battle took the crowd from 0.25 to 0.03); from the top 4 it's a
   real cost (a weaker move than yours, your pick scored a miss) and he sits in the pack. Your pick, being empty, scores a miss.
5. **The same rules in Boss alone, the Solo raid, a Boss raid and a Boss Battle party:** the rules live in the runner
   (`dashMoveClock` behind `moveClock` and `allowedMsFor`, `outOfTime` in `resolveBoard`), which solo and the lobby share, so each
   mode's clock follows. **Online the server sets every deadline** (lobby.ts reads `allowedMsFor`): a scrambled move's 3 s, a
   slapped move's shorter timer and the bank are the server's, never a device's word, and a pick after the deadline (and its grace)
   is refused as any late pick; the flag's move is the server's too (`resolveBoard`, from the host's evaluation and the battle's
   seed).
6. **He never thinks:** his least wait before his move shows is `dashThinkMs` (350 ms) where every other boss waits 1200
   (`bossThinkMs`); a queen taken still waits for QUEEN SLAIN!. His `thinking` loop is the art's impatient drumming and yawn, with
   lines like "Bro, I already know my move. No cap."
7. **No `powerTurn`:** his powers take time, never a square or a move, so every turn counts for fair play and the God King's Last
   Stand works as ever (a flag's move, unscored by the judge, is never one he takes back).
8. **Admins first** as Timber: `"dash"` in `BOSS_ADMIN_FIRST` (settings.ts). **To put him live for everyone, take `"dash"` out of
   `BOSS_ADMIN_FIRST`.** His FAQ block is written and shows once he's playable.
9. **Names:** every word a player sees says Dash. The art keeps its own files and exports (`boss-blitz.ts`'s `BOSS_BLITZ`,
   `blitz.ts`'s `sidekickBlitz`, `token-blitz.ts`'s `TOKEN_BLITZ`), each with a source line; their figure's name is "Dash"
   (the game finds a boss's moments by it) and Tick's "Tick". No id stored for players used `blitz` (the boss tokens are keyed by
   boss id, Dash's new; the ride is `cheetah-shoes`): nothing to map. Cheetah Shoes' description now says "Dash says…". The Blitz
   time control in Duel and 1 v 1 is untouched.
10. **His figure** is the art's as drawn in a frame 33 px wider on the right (`dash-figure.ts`): his entrance's zip-in starts past
    the art's frame on purpose, and the game's frames never cut a drawing at their edge. His battle intro, added on the art branch
    Nov 3, is left out (round 3). No attack on a king was drawn: he keeps the usual Last Stand and mate.
11. **Every moment of his plays where he stands** (the director's review, Nov 3: the first build cast his Clock Slap at the board's
    corner, as most bosses cast, and on a phone his figure covered a8, b8 and the 7th rank). His slap, Time Scramble, his jab and the
    RAGE warning all play in his own figure (the boss bar's on a phone, beside the board on a computer: `BossUi.inPlace`, `pm-stay`),
    as the Hidden Boss's do; the stolen time flies from your clock to that figure. Every frame of every one of his animations stays
    within the bar's room (`boss-bar-same.test.ts` checks him as a playable boss) and none drops below his feet past the bar's padding.
12. **Tick** sits in the boss bar's prop slot (fixed, as Timber's strip): his still, asleep on the clock; he bobs awake at a slap and
    rattles through a scramble (CSS on a still, nothing per frame). He isn't in the town yet (round 2).
13. **His sounds** are his own, synthesized (`dash-sounds.ts`): sneaker squeaks, his zip-in, a snap, whooshes, the clock's click,
    tick, tick-tock, slam, whirr and flag, the time ticking away as he steals it, his little voice ("yeah!", "oof", a yawn, a slump's
    sigh), and Tick's ding, chomp and step; each under a move's loudness (about 4 dB under its mean, peaks under half its peak). His
    select sound: a sneaker squeak, a slap of the clock and his "yeah!" (`dashHello`).
14. **His lines** (`dash-lines.ts`): the director's draft merged with the art session's sneaker lines, two or three a moment, short
    enough for the bubble.

## The numbers (the sim)

`reports/dash-sim.md`: the raid sim with the player's clock (a 10 s median move, 2.2 s when scrambled). **Shipped: offset −250,
`rageSpeed` 1, `flagTop` 4, 30 s a slap: the crowd 0.15 (± 0.07) in 46 moves over 20 battles**, in the pack (Babur 0.12, Puddles
0.17, Timber 0.18, Moo 0.21); 1.9 scrambles a battle, 1.2 moves run out.
