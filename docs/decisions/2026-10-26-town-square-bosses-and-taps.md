# The town square: solo stays apart, homes are shops, the press and the trail (Eric, Oct 26, 2026)

- **Solo stays on its own screen.** Eric: "the solo boss mode will remain with the boss select screen we built, fully away
  from this whole thing… If you go to a boss house in the open world you can boss alone but it will simply say something
  like 'are you sure you want to leave'… letting you know it's going to close you from the server and take you to the boss
  selection solo thing." So in the square, a boss's "Challenge alone" asks "Leave the town square?" and opens Boss alone
  (with that boss picked: the director's suggestion). This replaces the Oct 25 idea of fighting in the square under a
  "[name] is battling [boss]" label.
- **Homes are shops.** "Each boss when tapped has their own shop (coming soon)… special equipables, animations, etc." For
  now: "tapping the building to shop, but it's grayed out with coming soon."
- **Idea, not built:** "Challenge at the Coliseum": "you go to Boingo and he will say 'I'm not bouncing anywhere without
  Babur'… then you have to go to Babur and request him and then he asks for a random 3rd boss, once you have all 3 you can
  go to the Coliseum and challenge them. Actually add this as a roadmap idea, leave this out of their menus for now and
  don't code it."
- **The press:** "Not a thin ring but a pulse/light up bold border of the tile… so pressing down feels like pressing down,
  lifting up releases." No sound and no vibration for moving: "other games have no sound when moving around, same for now
  with vibrate."
- **The trail:** "visible to you only… a little trail your guy leaves behind as he runs, maybe 3 or something white trailing
  fading dots… like you're leaving little droplets as you run."
- **One pawn per square, like chess**, with crowds ringing around a person (the director's proposal; Eric: "sounds
  perfect").
