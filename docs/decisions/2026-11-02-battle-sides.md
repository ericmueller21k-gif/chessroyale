# Boss on the left, you on the right (Nov 2, 2026)

Eric (relayed by the director): "I prefer the normal look is boss on left, you on right, not vertical."

- Every boss home's battle circle from now on puts its two start marks on the west side (the boss) and the east side
  (you), with the middle line running north to south.
- A boss's custom intro art assumes the table sits between them, left to right, with a stool on each side. The town's
  table already has its stools left and right.
- Only some bosses get a custom board-appearing animation (like Alfil's pyramid). The default is the boss's God King
  attack for a few seconds, so no intro art is drawn unless Eric asks.
- Already drawn with north and south marks: Babur's, Puddles' and Alfil's circles, and Big Boy's, which was drawing when
  this came in. They stay until Eric asks. The mark tiles can be turned and moved in each layout (`rot`) without
  redrawing.
