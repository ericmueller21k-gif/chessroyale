# The town square: the ordinary cursor, and three bosses smaller (Oct 26, 2026)

## Eric's words (testing on a computer)
- "It looks like a hand grabber is the default … I think just the normal default mouse probably works best."
- "Some of these bosses are a bit too big … Sawyer is literally bigger than his house … the hippo I think is actually fair
  size … Jefferson for example is totally correct size … but Sawyer the raccoon and the pig are massive. And the chameleon …
  getting them sliced down to about 65% of what they are now, or three-fourths would be a good move."

## What changed (director)
- **The cursor** over the town square is the ordinary arrow, also while you drag the view (`town.css`; it was the grab
  hand, and the grabbing hand while dragging).
- **Sawyer, Wrangler and Smudgy are drawn at 70%** of a boss's size in the town square (`TOWN_FEEL.bossScale`, by home id;
  70% is between Eric's 65% and three-fourths). Every other boss is unchanged. Their taps, names and speech follow the
  drawn picture. Their size in battles is unchanged.
