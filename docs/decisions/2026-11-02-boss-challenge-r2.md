# Boss challenge, round 2: layout, table and chairs, the default intro (Nov 2, 2026)

Eric, after the intro GIF: "For Alfil he shattered the pyramid, that's fine for him, but for other bosses they should just be
swinging for a few seconds like discussed. Also in the gif the chairs are not on the right side where the chess board is. I also
prefer the normal look is boss on left, you on right, not vertical, but I'm ok if it's some for some bosses and the opposite for
others; just be sure the table and chairs and pieces all are logically placed."

- **Alfil: across**, like the other two. Both were tried (stills: across `docs/boss-challenge/04-table-phone.png`, facing
  `r2-alfil-facing-phone.png`). Across shows you, the table, both stools and Alfil side by side with nothing hidden; facing hides
  his stool behind the table and stands him small, far behind it, with your back to the camera. It's also Eric's stated normal look. His (and Puddles') drawn
  start marks moved west and east of the emblem, and the middle line turned north to south, so the floor matches where you stand.
- **Where you stand:** a square and a half from the table's middle, just past your stool (the park table's stools are one square
  out). Two and a half (the circle's edge) left a gap between each player and their chair.
- **The table:** the park table as drawn is already "across" (its stools west and east); facing uses its middle and a stool
  north and south. The default intro uses the park's own stone table (no per-boss art needed).
- **Babur's ring** has a pedestal in its middle: the table stands 1.5 squares south of the middle, in front of it, rather than
  hiding his prop.
- **The default intro:** the line (1.8 s), the table pops in during it with a dust puff and a soft pop, then 2 s of the boss's
  God King attack swung in place (its lashes looping, no drop-in or leap off), then three white flashes and black bands wiping in
  from alternate sides (0.8 s), the boss's own whoosh. About 5.8 s from the door to black.
- **Placeholder lines** (until Gemini's): Babur, "Fine. One more show. Then I'm on break."; Puddles, "Wipe your feet. Then let's
  see what grows."
- **The boss's town figure** is turned to face the table: Babur's and Puddles' are drawn facing left (as their attack art is,
  `facesLeft`), so the figure is mirrored by the same flag.
