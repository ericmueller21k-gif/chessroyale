# Alfil's powers in the rules: Tusks and the Mammoth Slam (Oct 17, 2026)

Hidden until his art ships (`kit: null`). How it works now: [bosses.md](../areas/bosses.md); calibration: [alfil-sim.md](../../reports/alfil-sim.md). Details (tuning tables, review round, protocol): git history of this file.

**Eric (Oct 17), Tusks:** "he points his weapon at two of his pawns as if casting; they glow."

**Decided:** Tusks casts on his 3rd turn, then every 5 of his turns, at most two elephants; capturing a glowing pawn fails the fusion. The fusion is Alfil's whole turn (V1, the director's call). The Mammoth Slam lifts two pawns and a knight or bishop for three of your turns, never your queen, rook or king.

**Reason:** with the fusion sharing his move, the first build won all 26 sim battles (crowd score 0.00); V1 scored 0.23 (± 0.08), inside the pack.
