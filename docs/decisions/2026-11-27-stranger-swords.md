# The Stranger's swords (Nov 27, 2026)

Eric: "every few moves he draws his swords"; taking a piece that isn't a pawn, "he jumps down and does ... a flame strike ... or a literal strike with a sword"; his piece taken, "he ... blocks"; his shouts: the God King's four.

- His capture stays ordinary, shown first as a seeded finisher (flame, ice, cut, frostfire), held like Chandy's lock: solo and online match.
- A pawn: one of three slashes in place. His block plays before the hop (the hold grows to fit it). Flourish: every third quiet move.
- His shout is the God King's (`gkHyuah`): Eric's own exception to "a boss's sounds are its own".
