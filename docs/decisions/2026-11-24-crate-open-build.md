# The crate opening, built (Nov 24)

- **Eric's picks:** the sound, changed the same day (~18:55: "the spinning sound of number 3 a bit better, but the number 4 ending sound
  and everything else about number 4"): the spin is C's, the slot reel (its metal tick, the swell under the slowdown, the lock clunk), the
  ending A's (the glassy chord, A's bigger ending for Legendary and Mythic); the near-edge stop (the strip creeps just past the winning tile's near edge and
  settles); the winning box drops in and bursts open with a beam in the item's tier color, as in the approved mockup. Spin 4 s, the
  director's call from the mockup's recommendation, one setting (`CRATE_OPEN.spinMs` in core's settings).
- **Full screen (Eric: "the full phone screen takes over ... on desktop, it can take up that middle space"):** the whole opening is its own
  layer on the mockup's blue-black: on a phone the whole window (under a maintenance banner, safe areas kept), on a computer the frame's
  middle column (read from the frame's `--fd-menu`/`--fd-rail`), top to bottom. The page it was opened from stays mounted under it,
  unmoved; Done closes the layer and you're where you were. A short window scales the stage down whole rather than cutting it off.
- **My calls:** Fischer Random's second strip uses the same 4 s and its beam comes up out of the winning tile in the item's tier color
  (no box); the tier's light is CSS-colored masks of the Hidden Boss's light shapes (made once, not per color); the strip waits under
  "Opening…" up to 1.5 s for its first sounds from the sound worker, so none is made on its frames; the old reel's marimba is gone from
  crates only (Settings' "Reveal sound" still voices the move reveal).

- **No charge-up (Eric, Nov 24, after hearing the build):** "I don't want like build up right at the end"; "it kind of sounds like a charge". The
  swell under the slowdown and the rising charge under the box's shake are gone; the shake keeps its knocks. Approved from an audio-only clip.
