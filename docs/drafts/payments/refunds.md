# Refunds

**DRAFT. Not served, linked or wired. Final after payments PR 9 (refunds).**

We want refunds to be easy. This page says how they work.

## Unspent coins: a refund within 14 days

- You can get a refund for coins you bought and haven't spent, if the purchase was made in the last **14 days**.
- **Untouched pack:** you get the full price back. Your coins are taken back too.
- **Partly spent pack:** you get back the unspent part. The refund is the pack's price times the coins you still have, divided by the pack's coins, rounded down. Any tax is refunded in the same proportion.
- Coins are taken away first, then the money goes back to your card. The refund shows in Settings → ACCOUNT → Purchases, and on your profile's "Refundable" line.
- Past 14 days, or if the coins are all spent, the refund button is greyed out and says why. Email support@hunchess.com if you still need help.

## Items you've opened are yours to keep

- If you opened a crate or used a key bought with coins, those items **stay with you**. Coins you spent on items aren't refunded.
- We don't take items back. There is no item-return system.

## Tell us what went wrong (optional)

After a refund, you see this message:

> Sorry you weren't happy. Please keep your items as a thanks for trying HunChess. If there's something we could be doing better, tell us below (optional).

There is a text box. **Submit** is greyed until you type something. **Close** skips it. After Submit you see "Thanks! We read every one."

## Refunds for a claim you didn't authorize

If someone other than you (for example a parent) bought coins without your permission, email **support@hunchess.com**. We can refund every purchase in full at any age, and take back the coins from those purchases. Items you've opened stay with you.

## Chargebacks

If you dispute a charge with your bank or card company, we don't fight it. The coins from that purchase are taken back, and the dispute costs us a fee. Please ask us for a refund first. It's faster, and it's easier for both of us. The refund button is in Settings → ACCOUNT → Purchases.

- After the **first** chargeback, you get one message the next time you open the game.
- After the **second**, the account is closed. See the terms, section 7.

## Repeated refunds and chargebacks: a pause on buying

- If an account has **three** refunds or chargebacks in total, buying coins is paused for **90 days**. Each further refund or chargeback starts the 90 days again.
- While this pause is on, you can still earn coins, spend them, and get refunds. Only buying coins is paused.
- The buy buttons show greyed out. Tapping one says "Purchasing isn't available on this account." We don't give a reason on screen.
