# Payments: public page drafts

Drafts only. Nothing here is served, linked or wired into the app. Source: `docs/decisions/2026-11-17-payments-design.md`.

Drafts: final after payments PR 9 (refunds).

- `terms.md`: Terms of Service: coins and items are a license with no cash value, who may buy, closing an account, changes to the terms.
- `refunds.md`: the refund policy, as the design states it (14 days, opened items stay, the refund message, chargebacks, the 90-day pause on buying).
- `prices.md`: the coin packs and their dollar prices, copied from the design (`PAYMENTS` isn't in settings.ts yet).
- `privacy-purchases.md`: what purchase data we keep, how long, and how to download or delete it.
- `contact.md`: support@hunchess.com and what to include in an email.
