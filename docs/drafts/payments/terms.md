## 1. The game

HunChess is a free-to-play chess game. You can play, earn coins and open crates without paying anything. You can also buy coin packs. Only coin packs cost money.

## 2. Coins and items are a license, not money

- **Coins** are a virtual currency used only inside HunChess. You don't own them.
- **Items** (crates, keys, cosmetics, and anything else you get in the game) are a license for you to use them in HunChess. You don't own them either.
- Coins and items have **no cash value**. They can't be cashed out, sold, exchanged or traded for money, or for anything of value outside HunChess.

## 3. Earned and bought coins

- You earn coins by playing. Bought coins are kept apart from earned coins. When you spend, earned coins are spent first.
- Your profile shows your total coins and your refundable coins. Refundable coins are the bought coins that can still be refunded under our refund policy (see the refunds page).

## 4. Who may buy coins

- You must be signed in to buy coins.
- You must be **16 or older** to buy coins. If you're under 16, you can't buy coins, but you can keep earning them by playing.
- If you're **16 or 17**, you can spend up to **$50 a calendar month** on coin packs.
- You must be in the **United States or Canada** to buy. Coin packs aren't sold elsewhere yet.

## 6. Refunds and chargebacks

- Refunds follow the refund policy on the refunds page. That policy is part of these terms.
- **Chargebacks:** if you dispute a purchase with your bank or card company instead of asking us for a refund, the coins from that purchase are taken back. Your balance can go below zero. Earned coins pay it back before anything else can be spent.
- We don't fight chargebacks. We will send you one message about the first chargeback. After a second chargeback, we may close your account (see section 7).

## 7. Closing an account

- We may close an account, ending access to its coins and items, when it has repeated chargebacks. After the first chargeback you get a message. After the second, the account is closed.
- When an account is closed, you can't sign in to it, and the same Google sign-in can't create a new account.
- A closed account's data is kept for 90 days, so we can reopen it if we made a mistake. After 90 days it's erased, except for purchase and refund records we must keep for tax. Those records have no name attached.
- You can contact support@hunchess.com about a closed account.
