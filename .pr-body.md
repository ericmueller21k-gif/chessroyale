Crate opening: each rarity its own sting, all quieter than the ticks; "Fischer Random" renamed Transcendent with a new crate tile

**Lane:** targeted (sounds, a cue, player text, one SVG tile; no rules, odds or server). Decision:
`docs/decisions/2026-11-24-crate-tier-stings.md`.

## What changed
- **Each tier's own ending** (`crate-open/sounds.ts`): Common and Uncommon share today's; Rare a shimmer; Epic a rising arpeggio;
  Legendary bright and triumphant (a choir above, a chime run to a high ding: Eric found the first try "depressing"); Mythic a fanfare's
  chord change and a sparkle cascade. No build-up before any burst.
- **Transcendent's own sound** (a harp sweep onto a held Lydian chord) plays as its banner comes up, on the strip's audio clock
  (`T.bannerAt` in `timeline.ts`), in place of the banner's `bannerStart` whoosh.
- **Levels** (Eric: the endings "hurt my ears"): every ending now plays a few dB under the spin's ticks. Measured with EBU R128 (ticks
  -15.8 LUFS momentary; stings were 2 to 6 dB over, now 3 to 5 dB under; every ending's peak 5 to 9 dB under the ticks'). One table,
  `ENDING_DB`.
- **Rename (player text only):** the strip's tile, the banner ("TRANSCENDENT!"), the odds panel, the test switch's label and the FAQ.
  Code names (`FISCHER`, `.fischer-stage`, `?fischer=1`) stay. The chess variant "Fischer Random" in votes is untouched.
- **New tile:** `TranscendentArt` (Items.tsx), an SVG in SupplyBoxArt's flat style: a prismatic crate with a glowing star and light
  over its lid, on deep violet. The crown with the question mark (`FischerArt`) is removed.
- `scripts/render-crate-stings.ts` renders each tier's opening as audio (WAV and MP3) to listen to and re-measure.

## Player-visible
New sounds per rarity, quieter endings, "Transcendent" wherever crates said "Fischer Random", the new tile. Audio and stills:
