## What changed
- `town/keep-pictures.ts` (new) and `keep-pictures.worker.ts`: after a first visit's first frame, its painted pictures are packed into PNG pages, composed at most 3 ms a frame and encoded in a worker, then kept in IndexedDB with each picture's place and each roof's pieces. ## Fallbacks
No storage (private mode), a quota error, another build's or layout's entry, a malformed or partial one, a decode failure, a wrong page size or a read over 4 s: all paint as before, silently, and clear the entry. Only one entry is ever kept. Unit test: `packages/app/test/town-keep-pictures.test.ts`.
