# Chandy: his moves come in turn, the least fired first (Eric, Nov 18)

**Lane:** boss (rules). Brief: `notes/briefs/chandy-move-mix.md`. Decision: `docs/decisions/2026-11-18-chandy-move-mix.md`.

## The cause, in plain words
Measured first: Stockfish raid sim, before and after, every pick and landing traced. The dice were fine (20,000 seeds).
- **A plain random pick.** Once a move fired, the meter picked at random among the others. With six or seven moves a battle, one
  move often came three or four times and another never came: 3 battles of 10 had one move at half his moves.
- **The queen lock wasn't rare by its own rule.** It could come on 41% of turns (74% while you had a queen). It was one random
  pick among four or five every sixth move, so it came in 1 battle of 10.
- **A pick that couldn't land** (shields with only his king, rapid fire with only your king able to move) fell to a random other
  move, his last one included: in the stand-in engine's 80 battles, 19 of 30 repeats broke "not twice in a row".

## The fix
`meterOrder` (chandy.ts): the newest unlock first; never the one he fired last while another can come; then the least fired this
battle (`ChandyState.fired`), ties from the seed. The meter's pick, the queen lock's wait and a pick that can't land all use this one
order. The same code runs solo and online (the parity test passes). With only one move to fire it still repeats (Nov 16's rule).
