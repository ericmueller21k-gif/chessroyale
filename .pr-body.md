## Boss result v2: you vs. the boss, no God King

Lane: **boss** (targeted plus the boss kit). Brief: `notes/briefs/brief-boss-result-v2.md` (approved by Eric, Nov 9). Decision note: `docs/decisions/2026-11-09-boss-result-v2.md`.

### What changed
- **Boss result screens** (victory, defeat, draw; solo and raid; after a Last Stand too): no God King (no `GodKingEpilogue`, no God King line). One row (`components/ResultDuo.tsx`): the boss on the left (`BossCharacter`, place "results"), **your pawn** on the right (`YourPawn`, your look), facing each other.
- **Under your pawn:** your name, then "+N XP" and "Level N" over the bar, then the parts (`XpResult compact`: the same fill animation; a fixed 176 × 96 box). Other result screens keep the merged XP box.
- **A loss:** the boss plays his existing `kingAttack` (the one he plays on a king at his mate) where he stands, from his landing to his laugh (1.9 s); your pawn jolts back and flashes at each lash (the king's jolt and flash at a mate, in CSS). A win: his defeat pose as before. A draw: both stand.
- **No minutes anywhere in the XP parts** (`xpShownParts` in core): only "Win +50" (or "Win (paid once today) +0"), and "Daily solo limit" as words alone. The total still counts everything; the rules don't change. The FAQ's XP section now says "more for a longer game, plus a win bonus, at most N per game", with no minutes or per-minute numbers.
- `BossCharacter` no longer holds the result line for a God King reply (`RESULT_TALK.bossHoldMs`).
- New frames tool: `node scripts/frames-boss-result.mjs <dir> <boss> <win|loss|draw|resign> [width]` (gap, gutters and stills, frame by frame).

### Player-visible
- No God King on any boss result. Your pawn faces the boss with your XP under it. On a loss the boss attacks your pawn.
- The XP box never shows minutes ("12 min +120" is gone), on every result screen.

### For the director: one call on Eric's rule
The 16 px gap (nothing touches) holds **at rest**: before and after the attack, on a win and on a draw. Every boss was measured at 360 px: 20 px at worst (Smudgy), and every boss stays inside the 16 px gutters. **During the attack, the boss's blow lands on your pawn.** Nearly every attack is built to reach its king (pies, ink, a lasso, a hose), and Eric asked for the attack "on us"; keeping 16 px would mean blows stopping in the air. The tests check the blow never passes your pawn (Hollow's strand stops 23 px short of the far edge of the pawn's art box). Please confirm with Eric. If he says no, `.rd-attack` can be clipped 16 px short.
