# Quests: one free reroll per daily, not one per day

**Title:** Quests: each daily gets its own free reroll a day (Eric, Nov 10)

**What Eric saw:** reproduced on a local build with real quests (no `?quests=`): rerolling one daily swapped only that quest's name, but
the shared once-a-day reroll greyed **both** buttons. Nothing re-dealt the other quest; the cause was the single `rerollLeft` flag.

**What changed for players**
- Each unfinished daily has its own free reroll a day. Rerolling one swaps only that one and greys only its button; the other keeps its own.
  The new quest's reroll is used for that day and comes back tomorrow.
- New text: FAQ "**One free reroll a day for each daily:** the arrows on a daily you haven't finished swap it for another quest (only that
  one). The new quest's arrows come back the next day." Reroll button title: "Swap this quest (1 free a day per quest)" / "This quest's free
  reroll is used today". Privacy page: "…and the day a reroll gave it."

**How**
- Stored data: new column `quests.rerolled_day` (the XP day a reroll dealt the quest), added by `ensureSchema`'s COLUMNS (`QUESTS_COLUMNS`).
  `quest_days.reroll_day` is no longer written (kept, not dropped). Download-my-data includes the new column.
- The reroll is one batch: the replacement goes in only while the old quest is still there and not rerolled today; its row is the claim.
- API: per-quest `canReroll` on `QuestView`; `QuestsAnswer.rerollLeft` removed. Settings: `QUESTS.rerollsPerDay` → `rerollsPerDaily` (1).
