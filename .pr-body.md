## Birdie: no tail on Armored Birdie; his train smooth on the board

### What changed
- **The tail is gone** from Armored Birdie's figure (`characters/birdie-armored.ts`, every animation) and from his armored cutscene (`birdie-cutscene-armored.ts`; the sheet re-baked with `scripts/bake-birdie-cutscene.ts`). Nothing behind it shows a hole, and his violet backlight rim follows his outline. The portraits didn't change (regenerated: same bytes; his portrait is the plain dove). The plain dove's town sprite and his fall cutscene keep a bird's tail (yours to decide).
- **Why the train was choppy** (measured every display frame on a phone): his board layer redraws every frame and set the train's `animation-delay` from each frame's `now`. That retimes the running CSS animation, so the train ran at **twice its speed**: it crossed in 0.65 s in ~12 px jumps, then stood still past the board's edge for the other half of the ride (38 of 77 frames). The smash also no longer matched where its nose was.
- **The fix:** each animated element's delay is fixed when it's first drawn (`Timed` in `components/Birdie.tsx`). That covers the train and the line's pieces stepping aside, which had the same bug. The train now moves by `translate` instead of `left`/`top`, so the browser moves it every display frame off the page's thread: it kept moving with the page's thread blocked for 250 ms. Linear, so the smash lands as its nose reaches the piece (`trainReaches`).
- **The train's own loop** (`characters/effects/birdie.ts`): the art's code sampled at **12 frames of 65 ms** (14.8 fps) instead of 4 of 105 ms (9.4 fps). The town's Birdie's Train uses 10 of 65 ms. The vent's glow, the beams and his nod keep their old beat, and the smoke drifts at its old speed. No new drawing. The wheels' tread is a 4-pixel pattern, so a visibly richer turning wheel would need a paid art run (not ordered).
