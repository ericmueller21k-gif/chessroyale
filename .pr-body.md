Battle Tower PR 14: the climb sim (light lane: a sim script and a report).

**What changed**
- `packages/sim/scripts/tower-sim.ts`: bot climbers (Maia-3 at a rating) climb from floor 1 with the real deal (core `tower.ts`). Each floor is a real battle through the match runner, driven the way the boss kit drives one, with the bosses on Maia at the floor's rating. A run ends at a loss or a draw, or at floor 50.
- `reports/tower-sim.md` (raw runs in `reports/tower-sim/`): 10 runs each at 1000, 1500, 2000 and 2500, 161 floors.

**Findings**
- No run passed floor 8. A 2500 climber still loses about 1 floor in 5 against bosses at 700 to 1200. The powers and the one-loss rule decide how far a run gets, not the bosses' strength.
- `maiaStep` (100) isn't the lever yet: keep it. `rageScale` on 2-boss floors looks too generous. Proposal: `[1, 0.6, 0.5]`, then a rerun with the same seeds.
- Stockfish's floors 51 to 99 weren't simulated (no run got near them). The climber doesn't know the powers, so these numbers are a worst case.

**Player-visible changes:** none. **Test checks changed or removed:** none.
