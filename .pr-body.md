## Squads: your turn snaps the big board live

**Lane:** targeted (squads screens).

**Cause.** The match screen reset the watched board when a new half asked a move of you. But the board's history view
(`useHistoryView`, the « and » steps) lives in `MatchView`, keyed by match and board. When your turn came on the board
already in view (for example after "Jump to my next board" had landed there), the key didn't change, so the old ply stayed:
the board still showed "Move 5 · 9/11 · back to live" while the turn order said "Your turn · play now".

**Fix.** `MatchView` now sets its board back to live when your turn on it begins: a new half (keyed by match and half) with a
move for you there, not yet locked in. Every format. A board switch or the jump already remounts the board, so those start
live as before. Looking back outside your turn is unchanged.

**Player-visible change.** When your turn comes on the board you're looking at, the board goes live at once. "back to live"
goes away, and your move can be played on the position that's real.

**Changed checks.**
- Old → new: no existing check changed or removed.
- Added: one e2e check in `e2e/squads-three-boards.spec.ts`, "your turn snaps you live". It steps back with « on the board the
  jump lands on, waits for your turn, and expects the board live, "back to live" gone, the turn order on your board, and your move
  to play. It fails on the old screen (`expect … toHaveCount(0)`, received 1) and passes with the fix.

**Checks run locally.** `npm run typecheck` passes. The new e2e check passes (phone project), and fails on the pre-fix screen.
The rest of the squads specs were not run here; CI's targeted run covers them.

**Docs.** `docs/decisions/2026-11-09-squads-snap-live.md`; `docs/areas/squads.md` (Quick board jump paragraph).
