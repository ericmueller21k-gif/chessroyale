## Battle Tower PR 11b: the clerk, the elevator and the floor room are the tower

Eric, Nov 19: "you walk into the battle tower, you talk to the clerk, you start a match. After the round ends, it's like a victory or
defeat screen ... And then it takes you back. And then you stand there and wait in the room. And then the next, you go up whatever the
elevator, you go to the next floor and then you continue on." Lane: targeted (screens, town walking and animation).

**What changed**
- PR 10's `/tower` screen is out of the flow; its code (`screens/Tower.tsx`, its CSS) is kept unused at Eric's word (director, Nov 19).
  `/tower` is only a floor match's address now: a reload rejoins it.
- The clerk's card (`town/TowerDesk.tsx`): Start, or Continue: floor N with a small Start over that asks "Start over from floor 1?".
  Your best is written on the lobby's "Best floors" board.
- The elevator takes you up to the floor room (art from `claude/boss-art`, now `shops/tower-floor-art.ts`, plus open elevator doors).
  You come in at the bottom and walk to your mark. The floor's bosses come out of the elevator one after another and walk to their marks.
  The pieces come up, the room zooms into the board, and PR 10's battle starts (`shops/tower-floor.ts`, `tower-floor-scene.ts`).
- The usual result screen with the floor and the run in its line, and one button: Back to the tower. That puts you in the floor room on
  your mark. Won: the elevator is open ("Up to floor N+1"). Lost or drawn: it's shut and greyed. The way down (or Back) goes to the lobby.
- Reduced motion: nobody walks, everyone starts on their marks, and the floor number changes with a fade.
- ShopRoom hooks, all optional: `talkCard`, `passCard`, `at`, `RoomFx.hold/swap/signBody`, `ShopCtl.walkTo`, `loadTowerFloor`.
- Audit 2, findings 3 and 5 (director's ask): a start carries a device id kept on the device; the server keeps it in `fight` and
  answers it back, so a lost start answer is still this device's floor (server `tower.ts` start, app `floorPlan`). A 409 with `fight`
  on a result keeps it to send again (`settleKept`). Touches the server's start route (an optional field).
- Calls: `docs/decisions/2026-11-19-tower-flow.md`. Area pages: battle-tower.md, town-square.md.

**Player-visible:** the whole tower flow above. No tower screen, and no "Stop here (saved)" or Continue on the result.
