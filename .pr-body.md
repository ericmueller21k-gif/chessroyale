## What changed

The walk-up's rooms follow the town's time of day (Eric, Nov 14: "it's always light in that room... like a moon at night").

- `sky.ts`: `skyNow()` (the town's own weights: `?sky=` or the device's clock; chat's `townTone` now uses it too) and `roomLight()`
  (day / dusk / night; dawn shares dusk's warm look; night from half night).
- His room (`recluse-art.ts` `recluseLit`): the window and the floor's tiles drawn once per light, the rest shared. Dusk: orange-pink
  sky over the roofline, one window lit across the street, longer warmer dimmer panes on the floor, a light warm wash. Night: dark blue
  sky, the moon and a few stars, two windows lit across the street, pale cool moonlight panes, a gentle blue wash with the lights over it.
- The hall's street door glass and the landing's window darken the same way (`recluse-stairs-art.ts` `recluseStairsLit`); the sconces
  stay lit.
- `ShopRoom.tsx`: the walk-up's three rooms load with the current light (cached per light); RoomArt's `dim` wash takes a color (`dimRGB`).
- The dev preview (`/hidden-room-preview.html`) takes `?sky=` too.
- `docs/areas/town-square.md` (the Walk-up), `docs/decisions/2026-11-14-recluse-room-night.md`.

## What players see

The top floor's window shows dusk or a moonlit night by their clock, the room dimmer and warmer at dusk, blue and moonlit at night; the
hall and landing glass darkens to match. Day is drawn exactly as before. The light is picked as a room loads (a change mid-visit shows on
the next room).

Stills (phone and computer, `?sky=day|dusk|night`, the room, landing and hall; the summoning at night): branch `stills/recluse-room-night`.

## Frame rate

No town-room `@perf` spec exists, so the room was timed with its own hook (`__shopBench`, headless, ms per draw; day draws exactly as
main did, so day is the before):

| | at rest | view moving |
|---|---|---|
| phone, day (before) | 3.50 | 38.1 |
| phone, dusk | 4.89 | 36.6 |
| phone, night | 3.82 | 25.0 |
| computer, day (before) | 4.32 | 16.8 |
| computer, dusk | 3.02 | 19.8 |
| computer, night | 2.49 | 16.3 |

Within run-to-run noise (an earlier run: phone 3.39 / 4.24 / 3.30 at rest). The cost per frame is one full-room fill.
