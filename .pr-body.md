## Tower fixes from Eric's playtest (Nov 20)

Three things Eric hit playing the Battle Tower on his phone.

### 1. Sawyer's cut now sits over the square highlights
The last-move tint (and the selection and check tints) was drawn over the edge of Sawyer's board-saw gap and his cut grooves. Since #784, square highlights sit at z-index 2, above every boss's under-layer (1). Now, while Sawyer's cut or gap is on the board, those three tints drop to z-index 0, under his layer. The pieces (2) still draw on top of everything. Move dots stay at 2, so taps and other bosses' under-layers (Big Boy's tower) behave as before. The fix is CSS only (`styles.css`, using `:has()`), so solo, online, crowd and tower screens all match on phone and computer.

### 2. Wrangler: one Showdown a game everywhere; none on shared tower floors
Eric's first ask was "Disable wrangler ult plz in battle tower". He then refined it: "if wrangler is solo in battle tower he fires his ult off still, I noticed it comes early just make sure in all modes for him he can only ult once a game then it turns gray."

- **Once a game, every mode** (Boss alone, raids, 50 v 50, the tower): `showdownMax` goes from 2 to 1. Wrangler's rules now use the boss template's existing once-a-battle settings, the same way Chandy's do (`ultimateMax`, `spentMeterShown`, and `ultimateRunning` to keep the one counted). After his Showdown, his meter stays on the bar, empty and greyed, labelled "Rage: no more ultimates this battle". No more is asked for, and the admins' trigger refuses. Before this change the meter vanished after his last Showdown.
- **Shared tower floors (2 or 3 bosses):** no Showdown at all, via the new setting `BATTLE_TOWER.noUltimate: ["pig"]`. The runner marks his powers when a shared floor starts, so solo and the party's room agree. His meter is empty and greyed all floor, labelled "Rage: no ultimate in the Battle Tower".
- **A floor where he's the only boss:** he keeps his one Showdown, as everywhere.
- **"It comes early":** that's his designed pace. His meter is full after his 5th move (`showdownMeterTurns`), and Eric said on Oct 13 "after three or four moves". I didn't retune it; once a game limits how often it shows up.
- **FAQ:** Wrangler's entry now says one Showdown a battle, and none on a tower floor with other bosses.

### 3. Babur's balloon in the tower: what I found
**The power was running; the screen wasn't drawing it.** On a shared floor his balloons went on, grew, and landed or popped on the board as usual. The tower's split boss bar, though, never draws any boss's bar prop, and Babur's pump (where his balloon grows) is one of those. So there was nothing to watch until a balloon flew onto the board. Early in a game you also won't see landings: with all eight pawns, every balloon pops until he has lost a pawn or a piece.

**Fix:** his pump is now drawn on shared floors.
- **Phone:** in his part of the split bar, at the left of his figure, 1.25x.
- **Computer:** beside his figure by the board, 2x.

It's never dimmed with his part, because his balloon grows while other bosses are to move. Only one pump is ever on the page.

The other bosses' bar props are still not drawn on a shared floor: Hollow's bulbs, Timber's workshop, Dash's tally, Jefferson's candle row and Don Dough's timers. They need more room than a part has, so that's left for the director.

### Checks changed (old → new)
Two of Wrangler's own checks changed (below, old → new) for Eric's once-a-game rule. Nothing was removed, and none of Eric's guard tests were touched.
- `packages/chess/test/several-bosses.test.ts`: new test. Every boss in `BATTLE_TOWER.noUltimate` never casts on a floor of two, with the test switch on and the trigger pulled; the view's meter reads 0, paused, `noUlt`. Wrangler alone on a tower floor, and off the tower, gets his Showdown exactly once.
- `packages/chess/test/pig.test.ts`, "full, he fires…": the check changes from "the meter empties and fills again (rage 1/5 after the duel)" to "it stays empty and greyed (rage 0, paused, `ultAt` kept)".
- `packages/chess/test/pig.test.ts`, "at most showdownMax a battle": the check changes from "then no meter (rage null)" to "then the meter stays shown, empty and greyed (rage 0, paused), and the trigger refuses", plus a new check that `showdownMax` is 1.

- `e2e/sawyer.spec.ts` (board saw test): new checks that, with the gap up, the last-move tint has z-index 0, the gap 1 and the pieces 2, and that the selection is 0 while move dots stay 2. Plus an optional still when `STILLS` is set.

  - Babur's pump is in his part on a phone, or beside him and clear of the board on a computer, and there is exactly one pump.
