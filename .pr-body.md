## What changed

Eric's bonus crate (Nov 24: "like League of Legends does this ... 25% of the time ... every crate possible that exists, even ones that have been retired, just evenly spread ... if it requires a key ... give it a key as well"; and "flag if a crate came from another crate so ... you can't just keep crating into other crates"). Brief: `notes/briefs/bonus-crate.md`. Decision: `docs/decisions/2026-11-24-bonus-crate.md`.

- **The roll (server only):** every crate opening (a series crate's; a boss crate's, whether bought, picked at a level or won at the claw) has `CRATE_BONUS.chance` (0.25, settings.ts) of a bonus crate. It is rolled from the item's own crypto-seeded stream (the item first, then the bonus) and granted **in the same ledger op** as the item. The op is keyed by the open's request id, so a retry (even of the last crate, or three sent at once) answers the stored item and bonus (`openedBonus` reads the op's legs) and never grants or rolls again.
- **The pool** (core `bonus-crate.ts`, `bonusPool`): every `CRATES` and `BOSS_CRATES` entry, each as likely, built from the lists. Not the Welcome Crate. Not an admins-first boss's crate (Timber's, Dash's, Birdie's today), except for admins. The crate just opened can come up.
- **The grant:** the crate goes into the inventory unopened, plus one crate key when it opens with one (`opensWithKey`: series crates yes, boss crates no).
- **A bonus crate never rolls a bonus.** It is stored under its crate's id with a `bonus:` prefix: `series_crates` "bonus:winter-1", or a `boss_crates` row whose crate is "bonus:boss-tiger". **No new column, table or migration**: the prefixed id is the ledger's own asset, so reconciliation, a guest's sign-in, erasing and the account download carry it as they carry any crate (all tested). When you hold both kinds, the ordinary crates open first, so you keep your chance at a bonus; the bonus crates open after and never roll. If two opens at once both pick the last ordinary crate, the second takes the bonus one.
- **The locker** counts bonus crates in their crate's count (`seriesCrates`, `bossCrates`, so every count on screen is unchanged in meaning) and lists how many are bonus ones in a new `bonusCrates` field.
- **Shown:** a beat after the item's reveal card forms, a second card comes up over it on a short gold beam (`BonusStage`: the overhaul's beam and card, Web Animations on transform and opacity). It shows "BONUS!", the crate's art (a boss crate's with its token), its name, "+1 crate key" with the key when one came, "A bonus crate (and its key), in your inventory's Crates." and OK. The item's stage dims under it and takes no tap until OK, which goes back to the item's card. Because the open's answer carries the locker, the inventory updates at once.
- **Each crate's odds panel** (series and boss) has one plain line: "Every opening: 25% chance of a bonus crate (any crate, chosen evenly) and its key if it needs one. Opening a bonus crate never gives another." The FAQ's crates section quotes the same line (`bonusOddsLine`, from the setting) and adds what the pool leaves out. A crate's page says "You have 2 (1 bonus)…".
- **Test switch:** where `TEST_SWITCHES` is on (local, e2e, staging), the server gives a bonus **only when asked** (`bonus: true` or a crate's id; in the app `?bonus=1`, `?bonus=<crate>`, or the crate page's "Bonus crate" box), so every existing spec's counts stay as they were.
