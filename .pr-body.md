# Real screens: laptop sizes in the layout checks; ranked 1 v 1's end page fixed (Nov 25)

Targeted lane (screens and shared components: `EndScreen.tsx` is a shared foundation, so CI runs every spec). Brief: `notes/briefs/real-screens.md`, parts 1 and 2.
Decision: `docs/decisions/2026-11-25-real-screens.md`.

## The cause
Computer layouts were only built and checked at 1280×800, 1440×900 and 1920×1080. A 1080p laptop at Windows' recommended 125% (Eric's) has a window of about **1536×730**, shorter than all of them. The 1 v 1 end page was a column about 830 px tall, so on that laptop it scrolled down. On Windows, a page that scrolls down shows a real scrollbar, and our `100vw` widths count that scrollbar, so the page scrolled sideways too. Headless Chrome hides scrollbars, so no test could have seen the sideways scroll.

## Part 1: real screen sizes in the checks
- `REAL_SCREENS` (`e2e/helpers.ts`): **laptop 1536×730** (scale 1.25), **laptop 1280×600** (1.5), **laptop 1366×650** (1), **desktop 1920×960** (1). Each check opens its own window at that size and scale. They are not new Playwright projects: that would run the whole suite 4 more times.
- `realLayout` checks four things: no scrolling either way, nothing cut off at the top, every chat shows at least 120 px of messages, and the board is at least the window's height minus two 34 px name bars minus 40 px.
- `desktop-board.spec.ts` "real screens" runs these checks on every game screen: Boss alone, a raid, the 50 v 50, its vote, 1 v 1 and Squads. It also checks Boss alone's and the 50 v 50's end pages.
- `ranked-1v1.spec.ts` "real screens" checks a ranked game in play and its end page.

## Part 2: ranked 1 v 1's end page (stills on `stills/real-screens`, each labeled with its size)
- **On a computer (every real size):** the page is exactly the window and never scrolls. On the left half: the headline, then your rewards beside the chat. On the right: the move-by-move. Both halves start at the same top, and the gutters are even (the page's padding equals the gap between columns). The review's board reaches to within 40 px of the window's bottom. Every size is in design pixels (the window's height ÷ 800, between 0.75 and 1), so a laptop shows the desktop's layout, just smaller. Play again and Home are normal button size. The chat fills the height that's left, with at least 227 px of messages on laptop 1280×600 (Eric's laptop 1536×730: 398).
- **Content (phone and computer):** the placement card reads "1 of 5 games", and its slot is labeled "PLACEMENT" (was "RP EARNED", "1 RP"). The quests box lists your quests (dailies, then the weekly), with the ones this game moved lit. It never says "No quests moved this game". Other end screens are unchanged (screens are separate).
- **Phone 390:** still one column, with the new quests box and "1 of 5 games". Its chat had no message area before this change and still has none (unchanged: phone layout wasn't asked for).
- **1 v 1 in play, computer:** the chat now runs down to the board's foot. It was a fixed 320 px: on laptop 1536×730 that showed 104 px of messages, now 327.
