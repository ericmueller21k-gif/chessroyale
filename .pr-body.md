**Lane:** targeted (results screen and the phase 2 drivers; display only). The brief's first part, the scoreboard through phase 2, had

### Cause
- **"4th of 4" over "You beat the Hidden Boss on all 4 boards!"**: a boss result's headline is your place (core `finishBoss` ranks
  everyone at phase 1's end). In his room's fight everyone plays a board and the boards decide it, so the place read as a loss.
- **The review replayed the kid's match ("Move 1 of 11")**: the result's review is `match.moves`, every judged move of the match, the
  child form's first. Phase 2's boards were never in it.

### Fix
- `phase2Headline` (app `recluse-phase2.ts`): where everyone in the match played a board (players ≤ boards: his room's fight), the
  headline is the boards' verdict, **Victory!** or **Defeated** (a boss alone's words). Your place goes in the line under it: "3rd of 4
  in the crowd. Royal Fork Roy was the best." or "You were the best in the crowd: 1st of 4." A Crowd final keeps your place as the
  headline (crowd-hidden.spec.ts already asks for that; most of its 100 watched).
- **The review is the board you played** (`BoardReview` in `components/Breakdown.tsx`, positions from `boardSteps`): its Chess960 start
  to its end, opening on the end, ◀ ▶ and ← →, "You played Nf3." / "He played e5.", then "Your board is won." (or lost, lost on time,
  drawn). No scores: phase 2's moves aren't judged. Same stepper, board and text places as the judged review.
- The board comes from this device's phase 2 view as the result arrives: NetMatch `showResults` and SoloMatch `finishPhase2` put it on
  the results phase as `simulBoard`. Someone who watched (a Crowd final's others) or left their board has none and keeps phase 1's
  judged review. A page reloaded at the result has none too (same fallback).
