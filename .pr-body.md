## Town hitches: walking and zooming upload nothing

Lane: **targeted** (rendering: `render.ts`, `paint-gl.ts`, `sky-draw.ts`, `tags.ts`, `Town.tsx`). Specs: `e2e/town-street.spec.ts` (its loading-screen test, changed), plus the town's `@perf` specs run locally before and after.

Eric's laptop `/town?report` (Nov 25): 60 fps median, 20 at the 1st percentile, **1,063 texture uploads in 15.6 s of walking**, every worst frame with uploads, the time "outside our code".

**Cause:** labels were made and uploaded as they first showed at each size: home names (9 sizes), signs (each scale), tags (5 sizes), clouds, a park table clock's every second, a board's cached picture each time the camera came to rest. The speech bubble was baked into a new picture **every frame** its speaker moved a fraction of a pixel (it was keyed by its shape to a quarter pixel).

**Fix (the cause, not a checker):**
- The loading screen now makes and keeps every label once its fonts are in (`makeLabels`, `fontsIn`): all names at sizes 12–20, every sign once at its font's own pixels (drawn at whole multiples, nearest), everyone's tag at sizes 8–12, the current sky's clouds and sun, the clock's digits, and the bubble's tail.
- The bubble is now one picture per line (body, shadow and words), placed on whole device pixels.
- WebGL skips the board cache and draws the board directly, as it already does while you walk.
- A font finishing loading no longer redraws text that was already drawn in its own font (tags, names, WebGL text).
- Your name is set before loading, so your own tag is made there too.

**Measured (headless, 1220×915 at 1.25x):** 15 s of walking and zooming went from 116 uploads to 0, on both the first and second visit. A boss saying a new line costs one upload. Slowed-phone `@perf` runs are within run-to-run noise. First visit, first frame: 11.0 s after vs 11.4 s before (player's town); second visit: 6.2 s after vs 5.6 s before. Making the labels takes about 55 ms. The atlas grows by one 16 MB page at 1.25x and two at 3x.

**Buildings 111 ms / Placing & sorting 34 ms spikes:** those parts time the GL calls that a GPU stall lands in, so they are most likely the same uploads. This can't be measured headless (WebGL is software there), so Eric's next `?report` on the laptop will confirm.

**Player-visible changes:** none intended. Before/after stills match apart from animation. One exception: on a building front seen at a slant, the sign's letters now have unsmoothed (nearest) edges. Level signs show the same pixels as before.
