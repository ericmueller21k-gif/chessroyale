## Boingo's Pogo: a boing per square, on the hop (Eric, Nov 15)

**What changed**
- The pogo is no longer a loop. No fade in or out, no hold timer, no `rate`. Each step your pawn begins on it plays the next of its
  4 boings (a, b, c, d, a, …: low, mid, low, high) as its own one-shot, so they overlap freely. A tap (2 squares) gives 2 boings.
- The step event comes from your walker (`Walker.onStep`, fired in `update` inside the frame loop's tick, town and shop rooms). Each boing
  is scheduled on the audio clock at the step's take-off. The frames between steps do no extra work.
- When: on **take-off**, the first move frame off the ground. The pogo's 12 frames (880 ms of art) play once per 90 ms step, so the drawn
  hop is already one per square. Take-off is 22.5 ms into the step; landing is 65.5 ms in. Why take-off: a spring's boing is its release,
  its 245 ms ring covers the air time, and take-off is the jump moment nearest the press. The moment is read from the drawn frames
  (`rideHop`), so a redraw stays lined up. Timing strip: branch `stills/pogo-boings`, `pogo-hop-strip.png`.
- `boingo-pogo.wav` is now the 4 boings cut as sample A (0.245 s each from 2 ms before each start, a 6 ms end fade), back to back
  (0.98 s), at the loop's level (0.3). The dozer's loop is exactly as it was.

**Player-visible:** on the pogo, a boing on every square from the first, overlapping; stop and it stops (no fade). The dozer is unchanged.

**Settings** (`RIDE_SOUND.rides["boingo-pogo"]`): `kind: "steps"`, `level` 0.3, `boings` [0, 0.245, 0.49, 0.735], `lengthS` 0.245,
`on: "takeoff"` (or `"landing"`). The dozer gains `kind: "loop"`. Its other numbers are unchanged. The pogo's `fadeInMs`, `fadeOutMs`
and `rate` are removed.

**Checks, old → new**
- `ride-sound.test.ts`: removed the pogo-loop tests ("starts at its own speed", "fades out over its own fadeOutMs"). "The gain comes back
  up…" now uses the dozer. "Another ride: old loop goes…" became "the pogo's frames never start a loop; off the dozer onto it, the dozer
  fades". New "the pogo's boings" block (10 tests): the moment is the drawn take-off or landing; the first step plays at once at take-off
  with no fade and no timer; a late frame plays at once; one boing a step, cycling the 4; overlapping and let go on end; a tap on a real
  Walker gives 2 boings, standing gives none; on foot, the dozer and unlisted rides stay silent; mute or hidden plays none; the dozer is unchanged.
- `e2e/town.spec.ts` "rides (Oct 31; …)": the dozer's checks are unchanged. New at its end: wear the pogo, walk 4 squares, and expect
  exactly 4 boings, the 4 in turn, no loop source, all played out after.

**Run here:** typecheck ✓; app and core unit tests ✓ (209 files, 2858 tests); the rides e2e on phone ✓ (local Chromium override, since
the pinned headless shell isn't installed here). No frame-rate run: no per-frame code changed (the step callback runs only as a step begins).

**For the director:** if a tab is hidden mid-walk, its first frame back can catch up several steps at once, and their boings play
together. This is rare (a walk is 180 ms). It could skip a boing more than `lengthS` late if it ever shows up.
