## What changed

Two changes Eric asked for on Nov 24 (brief: `notes/briefs/quests-gap-boss-crates.md`). Targeted lane.

1. **Daily quests: at most one pop-up every 12 hours.** After you close the quests overlay (Got it, ×, Escape, a tap outside, or Chess Town's layer), it doesn't open by itself for 12 hours. That covers both kinds: the day's first visit and the pop-up after a game that moved a quest. The window is per account on this device (`hunchess.quests.closedAt`) and set by `QUESTS.autoOpenGapHours: 12` in settings.ts. The Quests button opens it any time. Anything held back is kept: the day stays due and moved counts stay unseen, so the next opening, including one from the button, slides them. The existing guards are unchanged (the walkthrough, the queue, other notices, `QUESTS_NO_POP_KEY`).
2. **Boss crates: "Show unowned".** There's now a checkbox next to the "Boss crates" title, unchecked by default. Unchecked, the list shows only the boss crates you hold. Checked, it shows every one, as before. The choice is remembered on this device (`hunchess.crates.showUnowned`, with try/catch). If you hold none and the box is unchecked, the list's place shows one line: "You have no boss crates yet. Check Show unowned to see them all." The whole label is the tap target (44 px tall).
