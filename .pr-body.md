Title: Birdie, part 1: his Train (warn a line, smash a piece left on it, your king ends the battle) and his Fall into Armored Birdie (admins first)

**Lane:** boss (targeted, plus the boss kit, plus the director's one quick review). It adds optional protocol fields (`NetBossPowers.birdie`,
the score job's `train`, as Jefferson's `burn`), a declared win (`trainWin`) and a fair-play hook (his warned turn), so the review may
want a look at `packages/chess/src/bosses/birdie.ts` (judge, `afterCrowdMove`), `runner.ts` (`trainJob`, `trainWon`) and the lobby's win
hold. Specs CI should run: what `e2e/areas.json`'s new "Birdie (birdie)" area selects (`e2e/birdie.spec.ts`, `e2e/boss-smoke.spec.ts`),
plus the shared paths the PR touches (the protocol, the runner, `packages/core/src/boss.ts`, settings), which run everything.

## What changed
- **Birdie in the roster** (`BOSS_ROSTER`, id `birdie`, offset −250), **admins first** (`BOSS_ADMIN_FIRST`): an admin's Boss raid has
  his tile; players don't meet him and the Battle Tower takes him once he leaves admins first. Test links: `?boss=birdie&power=train`
  (warned every turn from your second), `&power=fall` (his fall after your second move).
- **The Train** (`bosses/birdie.ts`; numbers in `BOSS_POWERS.birdie`): warned for your 2nd move, then every 3rd. A rank or file with
  exactly one of your pieces (your king too), that piece able to move and its smash a legal position; the seed picks among them. At the
  start of his turn: moved, saved; left, his train rides the line and smashes it (a base on the board); your king: the battle is his
  (`trainWin`), the board held for the ride before the results, solo and online (`powerWinShowMs`). Spared when the smash would leave a
  position the engine can't take. Resolved after the crowd's move (`afterCrowdMove`, like Jefferson's fire), so solo and online run the
  same rules (the kit's parity test).
- **Fair play and the judge:** a warned turn's score job names the square (`train`); every judge path (the server's, the devices',
  solo's, hints, the eval bar) counts a piece left there as lost (your king: a lost battle) and always scores the moves that save it.
  The Last Stand and fair play sit the warned turn out (`powerTurn`).
- **The Fall** (once a battle): his meter is the material he's lost, full at 9 (P1 N3 B3 R5 Q9, from the battle's start). At the start of
  his turn (after a train the same turn) he falls, his cutscene plays while he "thinks" (built like Moo's Full Moon: its moment, over the
  board, the game paused, skipped on a rejoin), and from its hit he's **Armored Birdie** for the rest of the battle; no train after.
  His armored power isn't designed: `armoredTurn` is its empty place; the FAQ only says what exists.
- **His art, moved in from `claude/boss-art`:** young Birdie (14 animations), Armored Birdie as his powered twin (`p_` loops; only his
  idle was drawn, so his one-shots are a breath of it), the train, the crossing and the smash, and the cutscene's art (it landed, so it's
  wired, not a placeholder), baked into one sheet (`birdie-cutscene.png`, `scripts/bake-birdie-cutscene.ts`: drawing it cost about 0.9 s on
  load). His sounds are his own (synthesized); his lines are placeholders for Eric's.
- **On screen** (`components/Birdie.tsx`, `BirdieFall.tsx`): the crossing on the warned line, the dock's "Move your knight!" through your
  turn; the ride: Birdie departs his spot, the train runs the line nose first, the line's pieces step aside and back, the smash on the
  piece it reaches, "His train hit your knight." in the dock; your king: "END OF THE LINE!". Found frame by frame and fixed here: the
  ride's layer read the line's pieces from the board as drawn, which had them off, so his own pieces on the line vanished for the ride
  (now `ridePieces`, from the rules' position; a unit test and an e2e check).
- Decisions: `docs/decisions/2026-11-23-birdie-rules.md`, `-birdie-fall.md`, `-birdie-art.md`. Area page: `docs/areas/bosses.md` (his
  row, his code, the hooks he uses). FAQ: his block. Sim: `reports/birdie-sim.md`.
