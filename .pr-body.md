## Levels of 500 XP: rewards every other level, a boss crate every 20th

Eric (Nov 20), on the end-screen mockup where a typical +150 XP game moved the 1,000 XP bar 15%: "Maybe smaller levels and adjust the
prizes less? Like double the levels needed? What do you think". The director's call: double the levels, the same rewards per XP.

**Lane: full** (rewards paid into wallets through the ledger). Needs the `full-e2e` label and the independent review. No D1 schema
change and no migration.

### What changed
- `XP.perLevel` 1000 → **500** (linear, no top). New `XP.levelRewardEvery: 2`: a level's crate and key come on levels 2, 4, 6, … only
  (core's `levelReward`); `XP.bossCrateEvery` 10 → **20**. `levelsPerGame` stays 20, now counting paying levels (20 crates and keys a
  game, as before). Same rewards per XP: a crate and key every 1,000 XP, a boss pick every 10,000.
- **Paying safely** (`packages/server/src/rewards.ts`): today's level ops are `lvl:<n>:<ledger id>`; the ledger's `level:<n>` ops are
  levels of 1,000. They're counted **by reward, not level number**: old level k = today's (k−1)th crate and key (level 2(k−1): old 2,
  3, 4 → 2, 4, 6), and an old pick (its `boss_pick` leg, old 10, 20) = today's (k/10)th pick (level 20, 40). A level pays only the
  parts not counted yet (`paidLevels`, `levelOwed`, `owedLevels`), so an account is never paid more crates, keys or picks than a fresh
  one at the same XP, and never misses one. Same single read per counted game; merged guests' old ops count too.
  Why not "every level up to the old level counts as paid": at 2,700 XP (old level 3, new 6) it would pay today's 4 again, the XP
  from 1,000 to 2,000 twice. One case pays part of a level: old level 10 (its pick at 9,000 XP) → today's 20 pays its crate and key,
  not a second pick. Decision: `docs/decisions/2026-11-20-levels-500.md`.
- Core `everyLevel(n)` ("every level", "every other level", "every 20th level") for the words.
- Area pages: `docs/areas/accounts.md`, `docs/areas/shop.md`.

### What players see
- Levels come twice as often: a +150 XP game moves the bar 30% (was 15%). Every level-up still pops "Level N!" in the XP box; the
  reward toast shows only on a level that pays (2, 4, 6, …), "Choose your boss crate" on 20, 40, ….
- Existing accounts' levels double (2,700 XP: level 3 → 6) on home's ring, the profile, the friends search and the XP box.
- The FAQ: "Each level takes 500 XP"; "every other level you reach (2, 4, 6, …) gives you 1 crate and 1 crate key, and every 20th
  level (20, 40, 60, …) also a boss crate you choose"; crates section "1 crate and 1 key every other level".
- Crates tab: "You need a crate key to open one: every other level gives you one." (was "each level"); the boss crate choice says
  "Every 20th level" and "Your next is at level 20/40/…".
- The XP box's fill takes a little longer for a typical game (about 1.8 s instead of 1.65 s: `xpFillMs` scales with the gain's share
  of a level; code unchanged).
