## The bundle split: the bosses' code off the first load (audit L6)

Targeted lane (the PR runs the whole e2e suite anyway: `lazy.tsx`, `game-screens.ts` and the new `lazy-game.ts` are shared foundations).
Decision: `docs/decisions/2026-11-16-bundle-split.md`.

### Sizes

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| Main chunk, minified | 2010 kB | 1259 kB |
| Main chunk, gzip | 707 kB | 446 kB |
| Script fetched before home | 710 kB | 448 kB |

**recluse.ts 36/12**, **recluse-ages.ts 35/14**, net.ts 32/10, sound.ts 32/11, **timber.ts 31/11**, **grex.ts 29/12**, **hollow.ts 29/11**,

**Before, by kind:** bosses' drawings, lines and effects 735 kB, their sounds 71 kB, their powers' screens and the boss figure 145 kB:
**951 kB of 2010 (47%) was boss code.** Also big: `packages/chess` 251 kB (the bosses' rules 189 kB of it), the app's components
(Items, Crates, QuickChat), `core`'s settings.

**After, what's left of the bosses in the main chunk:** the prerendered portraits 51 kB, their sounds 71 kB, the Hidden Boss's drawing
36 kB (his online phase change's timing reads it), the God King 26 kB (the shop's preview), small name files. `packages/chess`'s boss
rules (189 kB) are now the biggest piece: they come through `@chessroyale/chess`'s index; a narrower import for the app would take them off.

**Where the bosses are now** (all loaded with the match's screens): drawings 414/147 kB (one chunk Rollup made, shared with the town),
kits, effects and the Hidden Boss's ages 236/87 kB, powers' screens 136/49 kB, the match's screens 289/105 kB.

### What loads when

- **First load:** home, with every boss's portrait as prerendered pixels (`characters/portraits.gen.ts`, made by
  `npx tsx packages/app/scripts/portraits.ts`): home's boss card, the select's tiles, the menus, quests, profiles.
- **1.5 s after home shows** (where home made the tiles' portraits until now), or as play is near (the queue, the Boss raid screen, a
  match): the match's chunk, with every boss's drawings, effects and powers' screens. The select's stage figure comes from it; its spot
  keeps its size until it's there.
- **Not per boss.** Tried: a file per boss pulled the chess rules into the first boss's file (which home then loaded) and made cycles.
  Found: a chunk's name carries the hashes of the files it imports, so a one-word change on home renamed **71 of 86** script files.
  "Every deploy downloads everything again" is mostly this cascade; fixing it (an import map, or a shared chunk that rarely changes) is
  the next step. Loading each boss only for his match would also turn the closed tables of moments and effects into registries and lose
  their compile-time check. The service worker precaches every chunk after the first visit, so that split would save parse time, not
  bytes.

### Player-visible

None intended. In-battle faces still draw from the kits (Hollow's lights, the Hidden Boss's look). The Boss raid screen's stage figure can
appear a moment after the screen on a cold first visit that opens it within 1.5 s of home.

  for pixel; the portrait lookup names the same kit as `bossKitOf` for every roster boss and the Hidden Boss's child).
- Changed or removed: none. `e2e/areas.json`: the new files placed (`lazy-game.ts` as a foundation, the portraits and figure state under
  the bosses' shared art, `BossStage.tsx` under the select).
- Run locally, all green: `npm run typecheck`; the app's unit tests (186 files, 2610 tests); the boss kit
  (`packages/chess/test/boss-kit.test.ts`); `e2e/boss-smoke.spec.ts` (every boss) and `e2e/boss-select.spec.ts` on the phone (33 passed,
  2 computer-only skipped); `home-layout`, `home-card`, `public-profile`, `quests` and `boss-party` on phone and computer (84 passed,
  14 skipped by their own tags).
- Measured with a throwaway script (Playwright, iPhone 13, CDP's network at 9 Mb/s down, 1.5 up, 165 ms; home = the shell gone and PLAY
  drawn), the same local wrangler serving each build.
