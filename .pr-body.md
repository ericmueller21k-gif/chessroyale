### The shared helper
`settled(page, read, ms = 300, timeout = 20_000)` in `e2e/helpers.ts`: reads `read()` about every 50 ms until its value (as JSON) holds
for `ms`, and returns it. Used by town-desktop in place of a fixed wait. A tap in the street town stays `tapInTown` (it already waits
for a still view over drawn frames, in the page). Not swept through the suite.
