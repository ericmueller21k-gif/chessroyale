## The phone's move panel draws only the rows that fit (the page no longer grows with the match)

Eric, Nov 23: "if those pieces are showing 1987 we gotta fix this". Lane: targeted (drawing).

**The cause (no leak):** a census by selector after every turn (and through a 14-turn Grex battle: sparklers, the candle's waves)
showed only move-list rows growing. The phone's panel shows about 5 rows and never scrolls, but drew every move of the battle, about
14 elements a row, 10 of them a bare pawn avatar's two empty SVG layers. 120 moves = about 1,700 elements, drawn again each turn as the
panel mounts. Grex's fire tiles come and go with the fire (the page tops out near 300 elements and falls back as tiles burn out).

**What changed**
- `MovePanel.tsx`: the phone's panel draws the newest rows that fit its box plus two (measured with a ResizeObserver, kept across
  mounts; an even number left out so each row keeps its stripe). The sheet and a computer's column (they scroll) draw every row.
- `Items.tsx`: an avatar skips an item layer with nothing in it (a bare pawn: its piece alone).

**What players feel:** nothing changes on screen (same rows, same stripes: before/after stills, 120 plies in, on
`stills/perf-dom-growth`). Late in a long battle a phone stays lighter: 120 plies went from 1,943 elements to about 257, and the
idle frames (slowed phone) from p95 33-50 ms, 8-29% dropped (main, 3 runs) to 16.7 ms, 0-1%.

**Numbers** (`--grep @perf --project phone`, slowed 4x): Grex's barrage 1,987 (limit 391) → 296-320, passes. Long battle: counts pass;
its boss-turn frame budget is borderline on this machine on both main (p95 33-100 ms) and the branch (16.8-66.7 ms; 2 of 3 runs pass).
The Chess Town load checks fail here too (the other helper's lane, untouched). Every other `@perf` check passes.

**Changed checks (old → new)**
- `perf.spec.ts`, the long boss battle: "DOM elements ≤ early + 40" (from an empty list; the panel's own 5 visible rows are about 50)
  → "DOM elements but the move list's ≤ early + 40", plus new: 120 plies in, the panel's rows ≥ what fits and ≤ what fits + 4.
  `perf-probe.js` `counts()` adds `list` (elements inside `.mv-list`).
- `battle-tower.spec.ts`, a shared floor: boss rows alternate "from the first, Ginger" → "from the newest, Sawyer" (a phone draws the
  newest rows only).

Ran locally: typecheck; app unit tests (2,881); board-layout's two list tests, the shared floor (both), Folio's struck rows,
squads-watch's dressed pawn; all `@perf` on the phone. Docs: bosses.md, a decision note, LESSONS + its story.
