## Haptics: a tick when you tap a piece and when you move; a few boss moments (Eric, Nov 9)

Lane: **targeted** (the shared board component, the boss screens, Settings). Brief: `notes/briefs/brief-haptics.md`.

### What changed
- **One module**, `packages/app/src/haptics.ts`: `haptic(kind)`, `hapticOnce(key, kind)`, `cueHaptic(artId, anim, cue)`.
  The way is picked once: the Vibration API on a touch screen (Android), else an iPhone's hidden-switch tick (iOS 18+),
  else none. Never throws, never waits. Every pattern in `HAPTICS` (`packages/core/src/settings.ts`).
- **Settings → Sound, "Vibration"** (on by default, per device, `prefs.ts`). Greyed with "Not available on this device"
  where there's no way (a computer; an iPhone before the switch). Nothing hidden.
- **The board** (`board/OwnBoard.tsx`, every screen with a board): `select` (10 ms) on a tap that picks up one of your
  pieces, `move` (15 ms) on a move you make (tap-tap, a drop, a promotion picked, a premove placed, a try into Hollow's
  dark). No tick for a press held ≥ 350 ms (`HAPTICS.holdMs`), a drag's start, a drag let go on its own square or off the
  board, a refused move, the opponent's move, a replay or a premove playing by itself. `pickedUp` (logic.ts) decides the tap.
- **The two old buzzes** go through the module: the 1 v 1 match found (`duelFound`, still quiet with Sound off, as Oct 16
  set it) and a crate held (`crateHold`).
- **Boss moments**: `cueHaptic` beside `play` where the art plays its cue's sound (BossCharacter, SpriteAnim, the board's
  effect batch in BossEffect), so each fires once, only on the device that draws it, never for a quiet sprite; QUEEN
  SLAIN! in Boss.tsx, once per banner.

### Player-visible
- Phones: a light tick picking up a piece and moving; buzzes at the boss moments below. A new Settings switch.
- iPhone: the taps tick (iOS 18+); boss moments most likely don't (no tap to ride on). See the decision note.
- Computers: nothing new but the greyed switch.

### Checks changed (old → new)
- Added: `e2e/board-tools.spec.ts`, "a phone: haptics: …" (a stubbed `navigator.vibrate`: tap-select → `select`,
  tap-move → `move`, a held press → nothing, a held drag → only `move`, Vibration off → nothing).
- Added: `packages/app/test/haptics.test.ts` (the way picked; patterns from `HAPTICS`; Vibration off; never throwing; an
  iPhone's ticks capped; the boss cues and their animation; once per key; `pickedUp`).
- No check changed or removed. `ranked-1v1-browse.spec.ts`'s buzz checks pass unchanged (run locally, phone).

### Tested locally
`npm run typecheck`; vitest `haptics`, `board-logic`, `board-tools` (75 passed); Playwright phone: the new haptics test
and the two 1 v 1 buzz tests (passed). No frame-by-frame run: nothing the board draws or animates changed (the calls are
added after the existing move and selection logic; the one control-flow edit, the promotion picker's refused branch,
returns before the tick and does what it did).

### Calls made (docs/decisions/2026-11-09-haptics.md)
- `select` fires as the finger lifts (the only moment a tap is known from a hold); 350 ms is the hold line.
- A tap-tap move ticks as the second press goes down (where the board makes it). On an iPhone WebKit may not count a
  press-down as a gesture: if Eric's iPhone doesn't tick on tap-moves, move that tick to the release.
- The bounce is one three-pulse pattern at the crash, not a pulse per hop.
- "A boss's strike taking your piece" left out (each boss's strike is its own art: a line per boss if wanted).
- The town's boss intros reuse the same art, so a Big Boy intro's block buzzes there too.
- The switch sits under Sound; the Vibration API counts only on a touch screen (desktop Chrome has it without a motor).
- No FAQ line: the FAQ doesn't list Settings.
