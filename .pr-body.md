## Rooms: walking smooth (the back through WebGL), and `?report` counting inside

Eric, Nov 11 (iPhone 14 Plus, Safari): inside buildings "it's just like capped. It seems like it's just a slower speed inside", and
`?report` "is pausing when I run into the building". Decision: `docs/decisions/2026-11-11-room-smooth.md`.

**What changed**
- **`?report` in rooms:** the pill's "● Recording N s" was set by the town's frame loop alone, so it stood still inside a room (the
  frames were recorded all along). Now the recorder calls it each new second (`FrameRec.onSec`).
- **Walking in a room:** the room's back (ceiling picture, cave rock, the day outside, the floor and its still plans) is drawn by WebGL
  into one opaque picture (`shops/back-gl.ts`, about 300 lines) and copied onto the room's canvas in one `drawImage`. Before, the floor
  was 156 to 360 clipped triangles a frame on the 2D canvas. Pools, figures and darks still blend with it in the same canvas. With no
  WebGL or a lost context, the back is drawn the 2D way as before.
- **`?back=2d`** (anyone's, read once like `?report`): draws the back the 2D way, so Eric can compare both on his phone with the
  same build. The report's device line then says "(rooms 2D)".

**Player-visible:** the same picture. Drawn both ways at one view, tile edges sit about a pixel apart, mostly far off (the 2D way
stretched one triangle over two squares there): 1 to 6% of pixels differ by more than 48, mean difference 2 to 13.

**Numbers (headless Chromium, the eric.md way, walking frames only, medians of 2 runs).** This container has no GPU: WebGL is
software (SwiftShader), and copying its picture onto the canvas is a CPU readback here. So these numbers can't show the phone's gain,
and they get worse:

| | before (2D) | after (WebGL) |
|---|---|---|
| phone 428×746 (drawn 2), 1x: Smudgy / bakery / Boingo | 60 / 60 / 60 fps | 30 / 30 / 30 |
| phone, slowed 4x | 15 / 15 / 15 | 15 / 15 / 15 |
| computer 1536×864 (drawn 2), 1x | 30 / 30 / 20 | 12 / 12 / 12 |
| computer, slowed 4x | 7 / 7 / 6 | 5 / 5 / 5 |
| report "Outside our code", phone 4x | 60 / 55 / 67 ms | 22 / 17 / 20 ms |
| 2D floor triangles a walking frame | 156–360 | 0 |

Floor prototypes measured on the way (Smudgy's, phone slowed 4x): no floor at all, 15 → 60 fps (the floor is the whole cost); clip-free
pattern triangles, 15 → 20.

**What decides it: Eric's phone.** Open `/town?report` and `/town?report&back=2d`, walk in two rooms each time, and compare the Room
column. On a real GPU the floor is one draw call, and Safari's canvas copies one opaque picture, as it already does at 60 standing still.
If WebGL isn't faster there, making the 2D way the default again is a one-line change.
