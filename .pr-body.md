# Ride sounds: a hard start and stop, no fades

**Eric, Nov 16:** "forget the fade in and out, it should be a hard start and stop when you start moving and stop. Like boingo."

**What changed (targeted lane).** A loop ride (Dozer, Mower, Sled) starts at full level and speed on your first moving frame and stops
`holdMs` (120) after your last. Removed: `fadeInMs`, `fadeOutMs`, `RIDE_SOUND.engine` (`startRate`/`upMs`/`stopRate`), each ride's
`engine` flag and the mid-fade comeback (`ride-sound.ts`: the ramp tracking, `silence`/`fadeOut`/`letGo` are now one `stop`). The pogo is
untouched.

**Player-visible.** The engine no longer spins up or winds down; the sound is on while you move, off when you stop.

**Calls.** Each end keeps a 30 ms de-click (`DECLICK_S`, the Sound switch's existing value, now shared): measured from the files, a bare cut
clicks (the Dozer's loop begins at -0.19 against a 0.005 median sample step). `holdMs` kept at 120: a lower one (80) still bridges a
dropped phone frame, but a tap after arriving is human-paced either way and CI's slow frames would cut mid-walk more often. Note:
`docs/decisions/2026-11-16-ride-sound-hard.md`.

**Checks changed (old → new).** `packages/app/test/ride-sound.test.ts`:
- "starts … fades in over fadeInMs; an engine from startRate up over upMs" → "a hard start …: at full speed (no rate ramp); full level in DECLICK_S"
- "held through a 100 ms gap … never faded" → "… never stopped" (also: no gain change after the start)
- "stops holdMs after the last step: fades out over fadeOutMs, sinking to stopRate" → "a hard stop holdMs after …: level → 0 in DECLICK_S, source stopped then, no rate change, no timers left"
- "moving again mid-fade: comes back up from where it is" and "the gain comes back up from where the fade had it" → one test: "moving again once stopped: a new source at full level and speed"
- the pogo/dozer, sled/dozer and halted-frame-loop tests: "fades" → "stops" (shorter waits); the sled→dozer one also checks only one loop plays
- the pogo section's "the dozer is as it was": its speed `startRate` → the ride's `rate`

`e2e/town.spec.ts`, "rides (Oct 31; …)": the log `start … fade, end` with "a dip comes straight back" and "let go only after the 700 ms fade"
→ start/stop pairs (a slow CI frame may stop and restart mid-walk), never more than one source, stopped when you stand; `up` dropped from
the hook's state. Passed locally (phone). Unit tests (app, core) and typecheck pass.
