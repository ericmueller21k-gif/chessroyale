Three tests on main's full run of d68874aa (run 45816786113) passed only on their retry. Each one's cause, what changed, and the checks that changed.

### 1. `[phone] e2e/birdie-home.spec.ts:35`, Birdie's Station: `inRoom.length > 30` got exactly 30
- **Cause:** the room's frames were counted from the town report's default **30 s** run, which starts on the walk to his door. On a busy runner (3-4 fps) that run ended itself with about 30 frames in the room. The town street guard had the same problem (`frame-report.ts`, `reportMs`).
- **Fix:** the page asks for `/town?…&report=120`. Before pressing Done, the test waits (up to 60 s) for over 30 room frames in the run, checks that it is **still recording**, and then presses Done (the town-street guard's pattern).
- **Checks changed:** none removed or loosened. Added: "still recording with the room's frames in the run" before Done. `> 30` room frames and 0 uploads or paints are unchanged.

### 2. `[phone] e2e/hopper.spec.ts:301`, his fifth carrot (`?carrots=5`): the battle was still on after 14 turns
- **#972 checked first: not the cause.** In every local run the cast comes on his first turn and the pass guard never holds it (`passWaits` stays unset). The `ultWaitMax` cap only makes a held cast come *sooner*, so it can't make the race longer.
- **Cause (game code, test switch only):** at the e2e's level, a quarter of his moves are slips (`bossStumbleMax` 0.25, drawn with `Math.random`). A slip skips his race brain (`bossRace` is null unless the move kind is `"elo"`). That breaks the switch's promise that "he takes a carrot whenever a move of his pulls one", and a slip can also move the piece next to a carrot away. So whether his fifth carrot came within the race's 12 moves was down to chance: locally it took him 6 to 8 of them, and once on CI more than 12. The race then ended by moves, the spin came, and the battle went on. The unit tests had avoided this all along (`bossStumbleBelow: 0`).
- **Fix:** `MatchRunner.bossMoveKind`: with `bossCarrotTest` set and the race on, a slip's turn is played as `"elo"`, so his race brain (and its eager pull) runs. The slip is still drawn, so the match's random numbers come out as before. Real battles are unchanged.
- **Checks changed:** none. Added a unit test in `carrots.test.ts` ("a slip's turn too…"). It fails without the change.

### 3. `[desktop] e2e/squads.spec.ts:60`, the test final: no team clock carried `r2m0`
