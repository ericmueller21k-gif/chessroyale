## Boingo's pie: the splat before a freeze now says so (Eric's tower report, Nov 20)

### What Eric saw, and why
On Battle Tower floor 2 (Boingo + Smudgy) a pie froze his e4 pawn, when earlier pies hadn't frozen anything. That is Boingo's own rule (Eric, Nov 12: "2 splats on the same piece make it frozen for a turn"):

- **e4 in ice**: e4 had been hit once before; this pie was its second splat, so it froze for one turn (drawn with Gingerbread's ice, as designed), and its splat mark went away.
- **The cream smear on d4**: d4's first splat mark (one more pie on d4 would freeze it too).
- **The blots on d3 and f3**: the two halves of this same pie, landing on the two squares diagonally behind e4 (cream, little crust bits and a red cherry). They aren't Smudgy's paint; his paint is yellow and never stays on a square.

Earlier floors only showed the pie "normally" because those were first splats.

**No rule or art mix-up between the two bosses.** A seeded run of Boingo with Smudgy (both orders), Gingerbread and Moo (24 battles, about 40 freezes per pairing) froze a piece only on its second splat, and Smudgy never added ice. On screen, each boss's board layer draws only from his own part of the floor (`Boss.tsx`: one `PowerBoard` per boss, from `partsOf`). So nothing joins `BATTLE_TOWER.apart`.

**The real cause of the surprise:** the first splat's banner said only "Splat on your pawn", nothing about a second one freezing it. The freeze looked random.

### What changed
- Boingo's pie banner (`pieLine`, `packages/app/src/components/Boingo.tsx`): when the piece is one splat away from a freeze, the line is now **"Splat! One more freezes your pawn"** (it follows `pieSplatsToFreeze`, so it stays right if that number changes). The other two lines are unchanged: "Splat on your pawn" and "Splat! Your pawn is frozen".
- Docs: `docs/areas/bosses.md` (Boingo's row names the three lines); decision `docs/decisions/2026-11-20-tower-pie-freeze.md`.

### Player-visible changes
- The PIE! banner's second line on a splat that leaves the piece one short of a freeze. On a phone it takes three lines inside the banner. Stills (phone) on branch `stills/tower-pie-freeze`: `tower-pie-freeze/phone-first-splat-warns.png` and `phone-second-splat-frozen.png`.
