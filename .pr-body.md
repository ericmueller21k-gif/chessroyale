**Lane: FULL** (stored data, the server, the party protocol's tower fields): add the `full-e2e` label; an independent review and a delta re-review.

Eric, Nov 20: "there should be a solo mode, and then there have an invite friends, or another option, which is like basically like open party … everyone can keep that seed, but if you disband and then don't play with the same party, then you can continue on your own, but it's just solo from that point forward … the invite friends one should just give a pop-up for your friends list." Calls: `docs/decisions/2026-11-20-tower-party.md`.

### What changed
- **The clerk's card** (`town/TowerDesk.tsx`): three ways, always shown: **Solo** (Start/Continue, Start over, and "Alone: floor N" for your party copy), **Invite friends** (the party, then the friends sheet; invite kind `tower`), **Open party** (the leader's line in the town's chat with **Join**). One panel under them, the same size: your solo climb, or the party's 4 seats, its plan, READY/START, Leave party.
- **The party**: Boss Battle's `PartyCore` with kind `tower` (max 4, one seat an account, no bots, two or more to START), `POST /api/tower/party`, `/api/tower/party/CODE[/ws]`. Its plan is the Durable Object's read of everyone's copies; a change un-readies everyone; START checks it again.
- **One run, a copy each** (server `tower.ts`): a `tower_runs` row of kind `party` per member (`members` `{run, uids, alone?}`: no schema change). START begins the floor on every copy in one batch; the floor's match room writes each member's end (`takeTowerEnds` → `towerQueue` → `partyFloorWrite`), never a device. Party best, a crate on floors 25/50/75/99 once an account, quests "tower" (never boss quests).
- **Climbing on together**: the run's own members (2+, nobody new) from the lowest of their floors; anyone new → a new run from floor 1, asking first whoever's own party climb it ends (READY / the leader's `confirm`).
- **Alone after**: the solo routes take `kind: "party"`: the device's word; floors won alone raise the solo best.
- **Leaving mid-floor**: a leave on that member's copy (free before the party's 2nd move, else a loss); a room gone without a result: free (`settleStale`).
- **Merge/delete**: delete takes the id out of others' `members`; the merge swaps the guest's id for the account's (call 10: the brief said clear; a guest *is* that account). Download: how many climbed, never who. Privacy policy line updated.
- **The floor room**: the others' pawns walk in after you in seat colors; the run's strip and a party result (`TowerPartyResult`); the party's START is the way up from a floor won.
- `partyCloseCp` (Settings, default `BOSS_PARTY.closeCp`): the tower sets 0, ties always to the engine's best.
- A test switch: `?win=1` on `POST /api/tower/party` (TEST_SWITCHES only) opens its floors where Qxf7 mates.
