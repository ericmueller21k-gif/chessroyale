## Ranked 1 v 1: a 10 s vote that ends once both pick, tap a zone in every vote, typed chat that arrives

Eric, Nov 25 (ranked 1 v 1 against a resident, computer, Edge): "the augment choice was like 1 second long … needs to be like 10s, or
when both pick then it instantly ends, also, i said hello, it never appeared, twice", and "I tap on it and it should bring my pawn there".

**Lane: targeted** (rules, the room, shared chat and vote components). The settings change (`RANKED_1V1`) makes `ci-select` run the
whole e2e suite (it reaches `EndScreen.tsx`, a shared foundation).

### What changed
- **The vote:** `RANKED_1V1.voteSeconds` 5 → 10. Once both have picked it closes `voteBothPickedMs` (1 s) after the second pick, never
  later than its own end; a changed pick in that beat counts and doesn't push the close back (`pick` in `chess/duel/rules.ts`). The room
  sends the whole game when the close comes forward, so both countdowns follow (no protocol change). Residents and the app's bot pick
  1.5-5 s in (`RESIDENTS.duel.pickMs`, was 0.8-3.5 s), so a person sees the other pawn move.
- **Why Eric saw ~1 s:** I couldn't reproduce it. Played locally on a computer, the vote stayed on screen 9.9 s of 10 and opened
  0.1 s after its time. I ruled out the versus moment, the resident's pick, the clock offset and Maia. The most likely cause is a
  background tab while searching (Edge throttles hidden tabs; "found" plays a sound). Details in the decision note.
- **Tap a zone, every pawn vote (Eric's new standing rule, added to `docs/rules/eric.md` with its guards):** one shared piece,
  `components/VoteZoneHits.tsx`, used by `screens/Vote.tsx` (50 v 50, Squads, raids) and `DuelVote.tsx` (1 v 1, town games). In the 1 v 1
  a tap on a zone now moves your pawn there without picking it up first. Before, you had to tap the pawn, then the zone.
- **Typed chat in a 1 v 1, the cause:** the 1 v 1's chat bridge (`duel.ts`) rebuilt each message field by field and dropped the typed
  `text` and the `typed` opt-in (both added Nov 7). A typed line reached the server as an empty quick line, was refused as "unknown",
  and the app showed nothing. The bridge now forwards each message as it is, leaving off only `to`.
- **A second chat bug, found on the way:** turning chat on mid-match told the server before `chat_on` was saved, so typing stayed
  refused for the rest of the match (every mode: `MatchChat`). The match now waits for the save (`chatOnSaved`, which is now set before
  the switch shows).
- **Nothing hidden:** your own typed line always shows. When the other player won't see it, a note under the field says so: "Bo doesn't
  see typed messages, only the quick lines." or "Bo has chat off, so they won't see this." A mute is never revealed this way.
- Docs: `ranked.md`, `chat.md`, `crowd.md`, the FAQ's vote line, `docs/decisions/2026-11-25-duel-vote-and-chat.md`, the lesson in
  `docs/history/lessons.md` and `.claude/LESSONS.md`. `e2e/areas.json` now maps `VoteZoneHits.tsx` and `server/src/typed-chat.ts`.

### Player-visible
- The 1 v 1 vote: 10 s, or about 1 s after both have picked. A tap on a zone puts your pawn there. The first-time hint now says "Drag
  your pawn, or tap the speed you want".
- Typed chat in a 1 v 1 works, and you're told when the other player can't see it.
- No layout change: the same zones, the same buttons over them as before (they now show from the start, not only after picking the pawn up).
