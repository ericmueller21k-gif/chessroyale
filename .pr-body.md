## Every boss faces the right way, everywhere it stands (characters lane)

Eric, Nov 17: "Can we send a helper to have all the bosses facing the right way" (after Dash faced away from the board on a computer; #766
fixed that one place).

### What changed
- **One rule** (`kits.ts` `placeFacing`): the art's facing (a sprite's measured `drawnFacing`, else `ART_FACING` in `sprites.ts`, now
  every boss, by eye at 4x) against the place's (`PLACE_FACING`). Different: the drawing is mirrored about its feet (`.bc-mirror`; the
  select stage's `.bs-mirror`); front-facing art never. The 9 hand-set `artFacesLeft` flags are gone (the data replaces them).
- **The phone's bar turns too** (it never did): `barShift` measured from the mirrored drawing; the fit now also keeps every moment on the
  screen's left edge (Wrangler's lasso, Dash's entrance reach that way once turned). Wrangler's name column moves 52 px (Chandy's is 53).
- **Hand-tied effects follow the turn** (`figurePoint`, BossCharacter.tsx): Babur turns to his pump for his toss on a computer (a
  `FigureMove` now says which way to face, not "flip"), then back to the board; Puddles' seeds leave his hand; Dash's stolen time flies
  to his chest. Bubbles and words never mirror.
- **A computer's side placement** (`fitSides`): a mirrored figure's room (`SIDE_REACH`) is swapped and measured from his feet, not his
  turned box's left edge (since #766 a wide mirrored frame stood too far over the board: Wrangler 75 px once turned).
- **A turned side figure's swings toward the board** (`SIDE_SWING` in side-size.ts, `bossSwing` in `sideSizes`): mirrored, the side a
  figure held behind it faces the board, and its mid-beat frames reach past its rest (Timber's move, gnaw and build: 44 sprite px
  against 38), 41 px over a 1440 × 900 board (limit 38; `e2e/timber.spec.ts:165` red in CI). The swing is now kept within `over` too,
  at the same size; where the room is short the far side goes over the menu. Unturned figures keep their places (some swing past
  `over` too: Jefferson and Don Dough +7, Hollow +6, Chandy's rifle; the director's call).
- **Gardener's seeds check** (`e2e/gardener.spec.ts:243`, red in CI on desktop: "the first seed's flight starts at his hand", 132 px off):
  the seeds did leave his hand (the app's `figurePoint` is mirror-aware); the spec put his hand 23 px from his frame's left as drawn, not
  from its right once mirrored ((90 - 46) x 3 = 132 px apart). The spec now reads the mirrored hand; its limit (1.6 squares) stands.
- The town's walkers and the rooms read the same data: the left-facing bosses no longer walk backwards one way in the town.
