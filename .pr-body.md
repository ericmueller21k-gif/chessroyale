## Fix main: no door cards from a door's picture during a challenge or Don Dough's beat

Main's full e2e run on b31cbfe (#836's merge) failed on both tries: `[phone] e2e/zebra-home.spec.ts:273` (Don Dough's Challenge), at `walkTo([4, 8])`: "4,8 on screen … Received: null".

### Cause
- In the back room, once you press Don's Challenge, the spec walks you to 4,8 (beside the back door) to check that the crew turn to aim at you. Square 4,8 sits behind the back door. The door stands in the back room's near (south) wall, and its tall picture is drawn over the floor behind it, so the tap point (the square's middle) is on the door's picture (stills checked).
- Under #836 a tap on a door's picture opens its card (`aimDoor`). The game already had a rule: no cards in a challenge, or from Don's Challenge on (his beat and the walk-out). But that rule lived only in `stepCards`, the cards that show as you arrive, and #836's new way to open a card skipped it. So mid-beat the tap opened the back door's card and stopped your walk. The card then stayed open through the whole walk-out, the scene carried you into the front room, and 4,8 was never reached.

### Fix (`packages/app/src/town/shops/ShopRoom.tsx`)
- There is now one rule, `noCards()` (a challenge, or Don's Challenge on), and every way a card opens checks it: `stepCards` and `doorTap`. While it holds, a tap on a door's picture walks you to the floor under it, as before #836.
- #836's behavior is unchanged everywhere else: a door's picture tapped from anywhere opens its card, and its button runs you to the door.

### Player-visible changes
- During Don Dough's beat and walk-out, a tap on the back door's picture no longer opens the door card (which also stayed open over the walk-out); you walk to where you tapped. Nothing else changes.

### Checks changed (old → new)
- None. The spec's tap was right for the rule (taps walk during the beat), so `e2e/zebra-home.spec.ts` is unchanged. No guard tests were touched.

### Docs
- `docs/areas/town-square.md` ("A door tapped from anywhere"): the `noCards` rule, plus the zebra spec as a test.
- `docs/decisions/2026-11-20-no-door-cards-in-a-beat.md`.
