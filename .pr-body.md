# The flaky batch (Nov 9): three e2e tests, three causes

Eric, Nov 9: fix the flaky tests. Each had a real cause, and two were player-facing app bugs. Details:
`docs/decisions/2026-11-09-flaky-batch.md`.

## 1. `squads-three-boards.spec.ts:152`: a Relay match to a result (desktop)
- **Cause (app):** the tiny-board strip (`shownMatches`) hid every finished match as soon as any match from a later
  round existed. The next round's match is created (in its 20 s break) the moment both its squads are known, so your
  match and its winner's check marks disappeared within a second of the result (measured: 0.8 s). That gave the
  `is-won` count of 0, then `.tiny-board` not found.
- **Cause (test lobby):** `bots=strong` bots still made a random move 2% of the time, so the test's mate in one was
  sometimes not played (reproduced locally: a bot played Qxh7 instead of mate).
- **Fix:** a round counts as the latest only once one of its matches has begun. Until then the round just played stays,
  results included. `spectateDefault` still picks from every match not over. `bots=strong` now also means no random
  move (`NO_RANDOM_MOVES`, test lobbies only).
- **Proof:** with the new check (marks still there once the match is over and the next round waits), the test fails
  without the strip fix (0 of 3 boards) and passes 6/6 with it (`--repeat-each 3`, phone and desktop).

## 2. `town-leave.spec.ts:144` and `:193`: the circus's card (phone)
- **Cause (test):** a tap meant for Babur's Circus met Babur strolling in front of it (or landed while the camera was
  still gliding), so his card opened. That card then covers the circus's tap point (`__townAt` sees only the canvas),
  so every later tap landed on his card. The CI trace from #561 shows his card open for the whole 20 s. A local probe
  showed the taps hitting `DIV.town-card`, and once its "Challenge alone" button.
- **Fix:** `openCard` closes any open town card with Escape before tapping, as `town.spec.ts`'s `openCard` already does.
- **Proof:** the whole spec, phone `--repeat-each 3`: 18 passed. The race depends on where Babur strolls, so a forced
  repro (his card opened first) didn't fail every time locally without the fix (3/3 passed); the probe log above is the
  direct evidence.
- **Note for the director:** "inside a room…" guards an Eric rule (`docs/rules/eric.md`, Leave Chess Town). Only its
  helper changed; no assertion changed.

## 3. `squads-watch.spec.ts:167`: who's watching (phone)
- **Cause (app):** `notWatchers` (the board's own players, never shown as watchers) used `myBoards().you`. For a squad's
  waiting seat on 3 boards, that is the board of its *next* move. When the seating made Wes the waiting seat with his
  next move on Tess's board, his pawn was hidden. The CI failure snapshot's turn order shows "Wes, in 3" on that board.
- **Fix:** only players whose move this turn is on that board are left out.
- **Proof:** a new unit case checks every seat of a 3-board Relay match. It fails without the fix and passes with it.
  The spec, phone `--repeat-each 3`: 12 passed.

## Player-visible changes
- Squads: after your match ends, the strip keeps the round just played, with your check marks, until the next round
  begins (it used to vanish within a second).
- Squads Relay: a teammate waiting this turn now shows as watching the board they play next.

  matches alone both show.

  changed.
