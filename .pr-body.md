## The Hidden Boss's fight: the small follow-ups

Five follow-ups from #707's review (m2, m4, m5) and the visual check of his fight. Boss lane. Decision note:
`docs/decisions/2026-11-14-recluse-polish.md`; bosses page updated ("The small follow-ups").

### 1. A device late for the transformation's intro (#707 m2)
- **Cause:** `switchPlan` with a drop time treated any start after the intro's start as "too late" and played the loop alone, from
  the drop. A phone that reloaded or decoded late was silent for up to about 19 s.
- **Fix:** between the intro's start and the drop, the intro starts now, from where it would be (`now - its start`:
  `MusicPlan.intro.offset`, passed to `AudioBufferSourceNode.start(when, offset)`), and the loop still lands on the drop. What was playing
  fades from now. Past the drop: the loop alone, as before. A handover to a new audio context carries the offset (`carriedStarts`).
- **Player-visible:** a late device hears the transformation's build from where it is, not silence until the split.

### 2. The suit on the canvas (#707 m4)
- **Cause:** the `?phase=2` test checked the `data-suit` attribute, not the picture.
- **Fix (test only):** it counts the suit's colors on his `.boss-char-sprite` (over 300 pixels) and none of his shirt's white
  (`RECLUSE_PALETTE`).

### 3. The cutscene's loop carried on, fitted (#707 m5)
- **Cause:** `sceneStand` came from the full cutscene timeline. With a shorter hold (e2e's 12 s, `?cutscene=`), the man's and the
  child's `together` loop jumped at the handoff.
- **Fix:** `sceneStandAt` (recluse-cutscene.ts) takes the scene fitted to the hold that led into this phase change (its end within 2 s
  of the phase change's start); otherwise the full scene ending as the phase change starts (a late joiner, a test link).
- **Player-visible:** none at the real hold (the same numbers). In tests, the loop runs on without a break.

### 4. The Moves list's boss rows in phase 2
- **Cause:** the rows used `bossPortraitUrl`, his plain portrait in a shirt, while the bar shows his face now (his suit).
- **Fix:** `useLiveFace` (BossCharacter.tsx): his face as the bar draws it now, as one image per look, cached, with one loop for the
  whole list (never a loop or a canvas per row). Bosses without a `barFace` keep the plain portrait. BossFace's live drawing now shares
  `liveLook`/`drawLook` with it.
- **Player-visible:** his rows show his helmet in phase 2, matching the bar.
