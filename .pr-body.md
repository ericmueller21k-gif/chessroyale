## The flakes since the bundle split: the match's chunk loads when play is next

**Cause (one):** #759 moved the bosses' code into the match's chunk. Evaluating it costs 0.4 s on a fast machine (81 ms before) and
seconds on a busy runner. Home fetched it 1.5 s after it shows, and not at all while the Bot level box or queue was open. Every test taps
on within a second, so the work landed after its tap, inside the wait it times: solo:80/151 ("Today's openings"), chat-mod:31 (the lobby
heading), boss-select:304/375 (the stage figure, patched in #764). A fast player saw a blank lobby screen meanwhile.

**Fix:**
- Home loads the chunk at once when play is next (the Bot level box, the queue, Play with friends); still 1.5 s after home otherwise.
- Making a lobby keeps home (loading) until the chunk is in: no blank lobby screen.
- `<html data-match-chunk>` once it's in. The shared helpers wait for it once (`matchScreens`) before the tap into a match:
  `startBotLevel`, `createLobbyFromHome`, `openSoloBoss`, `openBossRaid`.

**Measured** (emulated iPhone 13, cold cache):
- tap → lobby at 6× CPU: 1.3-1.6 s → 0.25-0.34 s (before the split: 0.4-0.5 s).
- Bot level OK → openings at 6× CPU: 4.4 s → 3.6 s (before the split: 3.7 s).
- The specs' 5 s windows at 12× CPU with two workers, on main → with this:
  - lobby: 2.6-3.2 s → 0.4-0.8 s.
  - solo: 4.2-6.5 s (one run past 5 s) → 4.1-4.3 s.
- The failed CI trace (solo:80's retry) shows the same: OK → match 1.6 s, plus the fill's 3 s.

**king-attack:192 (Wrangler) is a different cause:** the watcher starts before the move, and his attack's code and frames are in before
the battle starts. Locally the attack's first frame comes 22-43 ms after it starts (1× and 4× CPU). On CI no frame was painted for about
1.7 s from the attack's start. The passing retry's longest frame gap (415 ms) is at that same moment. That points to the runner's
painting, not a load order. Left as is; details are in the decision.

**Player-visible:** no blank screen between "Get friend code" and the lobby; a match after the Bot level box starts sooner.

**Checks changed:** no assertion is changed. The shared helpers above now wait before their tap into a match. Guard specs of
`docs/rules/eric.md` that use them: boss-powers, boss-side, crowd, hollow, home-ranked, lobby-close, match-menu, opening-animations,
problems, shop, smudgy.

**Lane:** targeted. Ran locally (phone): chat-mod, solo, boss-select, king-attack (Hollow, Wrangler), lobby-close all pass; typecheck; the
app's unit tests.

Decision: `docs/decisions/2026-11-17-match-chunk-on-intent.md`. Lesson: `.claude/LESSONS.md`, "Off the first load is onto the first tap".
