## 50 v 50 rework C2: the runner, server and protocol (every 50 v 50 plays the rework)

Eric, Nov 16: "Yeah, the 50v50 rework. We're doing away with the hidden boss on that for now." Step C2 of `notes/designs/crowd-rework.md`,
on C1 (#760). `CROWD_SETTINGS` takes `CROWD_REWORK`. Decision: `docs/decisions/2026-11-16-crowd-rework-runner.md`; area page `docs/areas/crowd.md`.
**Lane:** targeted (runner, server, optional protocol fields; xhigh).

### What changed
- **One game to its end:** `MatchRunner.endGameIfOver()` (mate, stalemate, repetition, fifty moves, insufficient material, or after
  Black's move 60, `crowdLimitReached`) then `finishGame`; at the limit `gameWinner()` is the judge's verdict (`crowdLimitVerdict`, 75%,
  the board's agreed numbers). Lobby and solo check it after each reveal and before each deal, **ahead of the cut**: a game over on a
  cut's move cuts nobody. No team final, boss final or duel (code kept, unreached). The 1 v 1 is a team turn of one; no stage ends after
  the last cut. Strong is drawn in the server's `resolveBoard` from the accepted judgment, never on a device.
- **Who left** (`PlayerState.leftAt`, from `retire` and `handOver` once a move is played): never the best player; their "out at move".
- **Protocol (optional only):** `results.podium { winner?, best }`, each results row `outAtMove`, `perMove`; `NetVote.id`.
- **Server:** the pick vote's patch, the end, places in `finishGame`'s order (no schema change). `?format=` only skips the votes; new test switch `?limit=N`.
- **App, only to not break:** votes via `heldVotes` (`net.ts`, `Vote.tsx`); the reveal's chance by the pick mode; "1 v 1" in the top
  bar, cut screen and results row. FAQ pinned to the old 50 v 50 (`CROWD_PRE_REWORK`) until C3 rewrites it. Podium/chart screens: C3.
- **Director's call:** no shared-blunder relief with 4 or fewer real picks (`CROWD_SCORING.sharedMinPicks` 5; raids unset).
