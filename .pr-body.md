## What changed

A boss battle that ends on the boss's move by a draw (stalemate, repetition, fifty moves, too little material) no longer replays that turn's moments before the result, and the last show is no longer lengthened by them (review of #787, finding 4).

**The cause:** `runner.preparePowers` returned early at game over, before `prepareTurn` could replace the turn's events. All of them stayed in the view: the ones that play while he thinks (Moo's Full Moon, Folio's fold/Revision, Don Dough's dough, Chandy's shot, and Ginger's Super Freeze once #787 lands) and also the turn-start ones (the repro showed Moo's rage `warn` lingering too). So `bossTurnShowMs` added their length again and `momentsOf` played them again.

**The fix (one place, every boss, no list of kinds to keep in step):** once his move ends the battle, `preparePowers` clears the turn's events, as a new turn would. His fifth carrot's win still sets up its own turn (unchanged). A mate was already safe (`mateMoments`) and is unchanged in effect.

- `packages/chess/src/runner.ts`: `preparePowers`.
- `packages/chess/src/boss-timing.ts`: `bossTurnShowMs` doc comment.
- `docs/areas/bosses.md`, `docs/decisions/2026-11-18-draw-on-his-move-clears-events.md`.

## Player-visible

At a draw on the boss's move: the result comes after his move alone. No second SUPER FREEZE! / Full Moon / Revision, and no extra seconds of waiting.
