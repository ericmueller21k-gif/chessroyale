## What changed

- **The daily reward:** the XP day's first counted game with 15 of your own moves (a solo game also 3 minutes of the server's time)
  pays +200 XP and 1 Winter Crate, once a day (ledger op `daily:<XP day>:<ledger id>`, in the game's XP batch).
- **Level-up rewards:** each level 2 Winter Crates + 1 crate key; every 10th level also a boss crate you pick among the live bosses.
  One op per level (`level:<n>:<ledger id>`); every level owed is paid at the next counted game (levels from phase 1, a test switch's
  jump, a merge, a pause), at most 20 a game; never twice.
- **Crates need keys, for everyone:** `CRATES_FREE` is gone. A series crate opens only with one held and one key (one op, both checked
  in the transaction). No admin free opening; `?fischer` / `?shiny` (and the "Testing:" boxes) only where `TEST_SWITCHES` is "1".
  Squads' placements give Winter Crates; crates held from before become Winter Crates as the locker is read.
- **Admins grant up to 100 keys at once** (`BOSS_TOKENS.keysGrantMax`; a "+100 crate keys" button).
- **`PAUSE_REWARDS`** kill switch: no XP and no rewards while on ("Rewards are paused for a moment."); the day's reward and owed levels
  come at the next game after it.

- **FAQ:** crates and keys, the daily reward, level-up rewards, the pause; every "free while testing / unlimited while we test" line
