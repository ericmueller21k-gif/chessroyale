## Smudgy's hidden rook on a shared tower floor: masked whoever is to move

Eric (Nov 21, a floor of Smudgy and Jefferson): "it shows the rook when it's the giraffe's turn or your turn, not his turn. So you just
need to make sure he's on whatever team he's on that it keeps that same thing."

**Cause:** on a floor of several bosses the view's top level is the boss in focus. The screens' mask (`camo.ts` `trackCamo`) read
Smudgy's Camouflage only when that top level was Smudgy, so on the other boss's turn, and on your turn before it, every screen drew
the true board. The home page's live window (`lobby.ts` `hiddenRook`) read `state.boss`, the boss in focus, the same way. The rules
(judge, move names, allowed moves, bots) already used the whole side.

### What changed
- `packages/app/src/camo.ts`: `camoOf` reads Smudgy's own part of the side (`side.bosses`) whoever is in focus (alone: his own, as
  before); `trackCamo` and `hiddenTrail` use it. This one module feeds the board, the dots, the move list, the hints, the dock's words
  and the boss character, solo and online alike.
- `packages/server/src/lobby.ts` `hiddenRook`: the side's bosses (`sideBosses()`), not the boss in focus.
- **Hollow's dark, same class:** `Crowd.tsx`'s reveal (after your move) drew only the focused boss's board layer, so on a shared
  floor his dark lifted off its squares while another boss was next. It now draws every part, as Play and Boss already did.
- Docs: `docs/decisions/2026-11-21-smudgy-shared-mask.md`; `docs/areas/battle-tower.md` (shared floor: hidden information);
  `docs/areas/bosses.md` (Smudgy's row).
