## The Hidden Boss's words above his head as he grows (Eric, Nov 14 playtest)

Boss lane. Eric's three screenshots: grown to 20 his line covered his face; the cutscene's man had his box over his face; in the phase
change his line floated by his empty place, far from him on the board.

**Causes**
- Beside the board a bubble's height was one CSS number per character id. His child form is one drawing for every age, so it got the
  generic one (a ~60 px boss, about the 9-year-old's head): measured 23 px into the kid's head at 1440 px, 159 px below the 20-year-old's.
- The cutscene's head spots were typed in; the man's was 38 rows low (the scene's headroom, `LIFT`).
- In the phase change his words stayed in his place's box while the board drew him in the middle.

**Fix**
- `BossKit.heads` (the child kit: `GROWING_HEADS`, each animation's tallest frame over his feet): BossCharacter sets `--bc-head` as his
  animation changes; `.bc-heads` stands the bubble on it. As he grows, the bubble is placed for the age he's growing into from the
  step's first frame (it moves with him rather than holding the line: simpler, never over his face).
- `CUTSCENE_SPOTS` measured from the frames (man 102 over the ground, was 64; kid 87, was 82).
- Phase change: a bubble over his figure on the board (`.rc-say`) while he's there; his place's box (and a phone's bar bubble) hidden
  meanwhile. On a phone his chant lines show whole over his head, sentences typed in turn, instead of paged in the bar.

**Player-visible:** on a computer his words sit just above his head at 9, 13, 20 and the man; the cutscene's boxes sit over each
speaker's head (phone and computer); the phase change's lines come from above him on the board (phone and computer). A phone's battle
is unchanged (his words in his bar). Stills: branch `stills/recluse-bubble-grows`.

### Add-on (director, Nov 14): a conversation as he grows up

Eric: "there was no conversation" when the beaten child grows into the man.
- `RECLUSE_GROWUP_EXCHANGES` (recluse-lines.ts): Eric's five pairs. One a fight, the same for everyone in it: `growUpExchange` picks by
  `startMove + levelBase` (the level's offset is drawn from the battle's seed; `startMove` alone is 1 in every Boss alone fight, so it
  would never mix). The kid's line replaces his `beaten` line (the child kit's `lineOf` for `defeat`); the man's is said, critical, as
  he's grown (`growUpAt` + the growing's 2.35 s = about 3.5 s in), each over its speaker at his size.
- **The hold grew:** the fall was 4.2 s, so the man's match (which clears his voice) began 0.7 s after his first letter.
  `RECLUSE_CHILD.fallMs` 4200 -> 7500 (one setting: solo and the server's fall alike). Lines need: the kid's typed and firm by 2.7 s at
  most (before the man answers at 3.5 s); the man's longest ("Nobody's supposed to win…", 59 characters) typed by 5.2 s, then 2.3 s to
  read before 7.5 s. The man's match starts 3.3 s later than before.
- Checks: new unit `recluse-child.test.ts` "beaten: one exchange a fight…" (deterministic per fight, all five come up across fights, the
  kit's `defeat` line is the kid's half, every pair fits the fall). Ran: the server's `recluse-online`/`crowd-hidden-boss` tests (read
  the setting), e2e `recluse.spec.ts` "his child form first", `hidden-boss-room.spec.ts` (the room's fight online, child -> man ->
  four boards), both projects: pass. Still: `growup-exchange.png` on the stills branch.
