# Outfit slot (secret) and the Banana Suit, the Mythic Crate's secret item

**Lane: targeted** (no migration: equips are stored per slot, so `outfit` is just a new row). Effort high.

## What changed for players
- A new, **secret** item slot, **Outfit**. With an outfit on, your pawn shows only the outfit, plus goggles or shades (eyewear) over its face hole.
  Everything else stays equipped underneath and comes back when you take the outfit off.
- **Banana Suit** (Mythic, outfit): "Fully committed to the bit. Opponents keep slipping up." Your pawn's head shows in the face hole. It does a slow
  two-frame bounce standing, a side-on sprint running left or right, and front and back runs. The peel takes the item's color.
- It's the **Mythic Crate's secret item** (`MYTHIC_CRATE_ITEMS`). The crate stays a look-only teaser: no purchase, roll or odds. It's in no Beta, Winter or
  boss crate, so their items and odds are unchanged from main. Admins own a copy (as of every item); tests give it straight.
- The **locker**: no Outfit tab for anyone without an outfit (main's tab layout unchanged for them). Once you own one, an **Outfit** tab appears, and the tabs become
  an even 4 + 3 grid on a phone and one row on a computer. While an outfit is on, every other item except eyewear shows greyed with "Hidden by your outfit", and you can still wear it.
- It's drawn everywhere a dressed pawn is: the town, home's pawn and ring, the locker, profiles, crate tiles, and match screens (it shows there even though rides don't). Profile rows and
  Equipped Cosmetics show only what's visible. The FAQ is unchanged (the slot is secret).

## How it works
- In core: `ItemSlot` gets `"outfit"`; `eyewear: true` on Ski Goggles and Dark Shades; `shownLook`, `mountOf`, `hiddenByOutfit`, `ownsOutfit` and `MYTHIC_CRATE_ITEMS`;
  and `OUTFIT.eyewear` in settings (set it to false to hide eyewear too).
- The suit is drawn with the rides' kit (`components/rides/banana-suit.tsx`, its cells measured into `ride-cells.ts`), so the town and `Avatar` use the code they already have.
- `scripts/measure-rides.ts` and `preview-items.mjs` now accept `PW_CHROMIUM`, as the other scripts already do.
