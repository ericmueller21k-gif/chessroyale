# Phone: the Friends button opens a centered friends overlay

Eric, Nov 9: the friends list "in the same way that the quests are … a centered kind of rectangle overlay", grey offline friends
or "last online", tap a name to invite or view profile. Brief: `notes/briefs/brief-friends-overlay.md`. Lane: targeted (hub/social front end).

## What changed
- `components/FriendsOverlay.tsx` (new): the Quests overlay's shell (`qo-back`/`qo-card`/crest/✕; dim, no blur; tap the dim or
  Escape closes). Fixed height, the list scrolls inside: invites to you (Join, ✕), friend requests (Accept, Decline), friends
  online first (the server's order), offline ones greyed with "Last online 2h ago". Tap a friend: a menu over the box's foot
  with **Invite to play** (the existing `InviteButton`/`invitePlayer`; greyed with its reason when they're offline) and
  **View profile** (`openProfile`). Above the list, **Private lobby ›** ("Make one or join with a code") opens the old sheet, signed out too; footer: "Requests sent and blocked ›" (`/friends`).
  Empty states: signed out (Sign in), no friends, paused (`pausedLine("friends")`).
- `Home.tsx`: only the top bar's Friends `onClick` (now the overlay) and the overlay's mount; the Play with friends sheet is kept
  unchanged, reached from the overlay's "Private lobby ›" (Eric, Nov 9: the private lobby stays; Party is a different thing).
- Last online, no migration: the friends query already read `users.last_seen`; the list now carries it as an optional
  `FriendEntry.lastSeen` (null for a friend who hides it). `friendStatus` says "Last online …" (the profile's `ago` words) on
  every friends list; "Offline" when there's no time. The app marks a friend's last-seen as "now" when the minute's refresh sees
  them go offline.
- CSS: its own commented block (`fo-`), after the Quests overlay's.
- `e2e/areas.json`: `FriendsOverlay.tsx` in the friends area.
