## The rule
A tap counts as a home's when it lands on:
1. its picture;
2. a square of its plot (the half-hidden row under its front among them);
3. its **foot row**: the one row right in front of its front edge, across the plot's width (the door square and the one beside it among them; `world.ts homeNear`);
4. a **see-through pixel of its picture's box** over ground within one square of its plot that isn't another home's plot (`render.ts pick`);
5. **your own pawn** over any of those (`Town.tsx pickTap`; a tap on you anywhere else still does nothing).

What it never takes:
- the corner squares beside the foot row, or anything two rows out (open ground there is still a square);
- a person, a boss, a table or a statue: those are still picked first.

Of two homes, the nearer door wins. The walk and the card are today's: you walk to the door square (the one beside it if taken, `destFor`, unchanged) and the same card opens, with "Go in" for a shop. Both towns, phone and computer. The Colosseum keeps its own rule.
