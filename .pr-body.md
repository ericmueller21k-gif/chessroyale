## The Hidden Boss's phase 2: the scoreboard shows who's still playing

Found by the same-frame job (#703) from Eric's playtest (Nov 14): from phase 1's end the scoreboard read "out" for all four,
their points blank, and a phone's Players tab was empty, while all four were playing their boards.

**Lane:** targeted (match logic shown on screen; nothing stored or rewarded changes). Decision:
`docs/decisions/2026-11-14-recluse-phase2-standings.md`.

### Cause

As his battle ends (`MatchRunner.finishBossBattle`: the server's lobby for his room's fight and the Crowd final, solo's runner for
Boss alone and a solo Crowd final), core `finishBoss` places everyone still in (`alive: false`, a placement), and the runner's
leaderboard reads `out: !alive`. His fall, cutscene, phase change and the four boards all come after that, so every screen showed
the four as knocked out. The same "out" also kept his phase 2 chatter silent (`talkWhen`: never for a player who's out) and hid
the party's pawns during his fall and cutscene.

### Fix (what's shown only)

- `phase2Standings` (shared, `packages/chess/src/bosses/recluse-phase2.ts`): from his fall (phase 2 to follow) until the results,
  each board's player is in (`out: false`) with their board's state (`board`: playing, won, lost, drawn), first and in the order
  they stood, with phase 1's points (phase 2 scores no moves); everyone else as they were. Someone who left a board (a bot plays it
  on under its own id) is out; a bot with no row gets none.
- `NetMatch.standings()` and `SoloMatch.standings()` apply it while his fall (`toPhase2`), the phase change and phase 2 are up; who's
  on the boards: the phase 2 view, or his fall's word before it (`phase2Boards` online; solo, you and the mates phase2Mates keeps).
- `RaceTower` shows a board's result after its player's name once it ends: WON (green), LOST (red), DRAWN (`.t-board`; the name
  gives way, never it); on a phone panel's small rows a mark, ✓ / ✕ / ½, with the word as its title (the sheet a tap opens has the
  words). Nothing while the board is on: "PLAYING" on every row cut a phone panel's names to one letter (about 57 px of name
  column there) and a computer's to a few, so the row simply reads as in, with its points, as in phase 1.
- The exit note online once every board is decided keeps "You're already out, so your result stands." (it read the row's `out`).

A Crowd final against him goes the same path (the server's `startPhase2`; solo's phase change), so it's fixed there too: the top 4
show in, the others out.

### Player-visible

- A computer's crowd table lists the four on his boards from his fall until the results, in, with their points, then "Won" /
  "Lost" / "Drawn" as each board ends. A phone's Players tab shows the rows its box fits (three on an iPhone 13, as in phase 1),
  none out, with ✓ / ✕ / ½ as boards end; a tap opens all four. A phone's title line keeps your place ("P1/4").
- The party's pawns stay in the dock through his fall and cutscene too (they vanished there until the phase change).
- **His phase 2 chatter now plays** for those on his boards (Boss alone, his room, a Crowd final's top 4), as `talkWhen` and
  bosses.md always meant; it never did, because they were "out". A Crowd final's watchers still don't hear it. Flagging it: it's
  new to Eric's ears.

### Stored or rewarded

**Nothing.** No server code changed. The placements (results, XP, rank, profiles, feats) are phase 1's end's, as before; the
server sends the same standings it did, the results' included. Fields touched: none stored; the app's `Standing` gains an
optional `board` (display only, never sent).
