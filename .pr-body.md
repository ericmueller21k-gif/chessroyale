## Birdie: his train rides even when you saved the piece, and misses

Eric (Nov 24): "we still need to show the birdie driving his train across the board animation, even when there's no pieces to hit. He
just simply drives across and then everything misses."

Boss lane. Touched areas: Birdie's rules and screens, plus his dock words. The e2e specs CI selects for this diff include
`e2e/birdie.spec.ts` and `e2e/boss-smoke.spec.ts`.

### What changed
- **The miss ride** (`packages/chess/src/bosses/birdie.ts`). Whenever his warning comes due and your move saved the piece, his train
  still rides the warned line. The event is `trainSmash` with `missed: true`, the state is `ride.missed`, and the board is unchanged
  with no base. It's the same event kind as a smash, so every place that times a ride already covers it: `birdieTurnMs`, `POWER_FX`,
  the lobby's hold of his move (`thinkMinAt`) and solo's beats (`birdieTurnBeats`). His fall on the same turn still comes after the
  train. Nothing rides once the game is over on the board, at the move cap, or with the battle decided, as before.
- **What counts as saved.** Your move started on the warned square, or the square no longer holds your piece of the warned kind.
  Before, any piece of yours on that square was smashed. With one boss, no single move can put a different piece of yours there; on a
  floor with several bosses, another boss's power could in principle. Now that piece is never smashed, and the train misses it.
- **On screen** (`components/Birdie.tsx`). The same ride as a smash: he ducks in and pulls away, the train crosses the line, and every
  piece on the line (his and yours) steps aside and back. There's no warning sign during the ride, no smash, and no smash sound. The
  ride's own sounds play as on a smash ride.
- **The dock's ride lines.** On a miss the dock says "Birdie: Nothing hit!". I also shortened the smash and sparing lines to
  "Knight smashed!" and "Bishop spared.". On the test phone (iPhone 13) the dock line is 196 px wide, and the old lines ("His train hit
  your knight." and "The train spared your bishop.") were cut off with an ellipsis: about 218 and 253 px with his face and name,
  from their widths in the app's font scaled by two lines measured in that dock. The new lines leave at least 21 px to spare.
- FAQ (Birdie's Train and Fall), the sim's ride record (`missed`), `docs/areas/bosses.md`, and
  `docs/decisions/2026-11-24-birdie-train-miss.md`.

### Player-visible
- Moving the warned piece no longer just ends the warning: at the start of his turn his train rides across the board anyway, every
  piece on the line steps aside, and nothing is hit. His move waits for the ride, about 4.8 s, the same as after a smash.
- If he falls on a turn where you saved the warned piece, the miss ride plays first, so his fall starts about 4.8 s later.
- The dock's ride lines: "Nothing hit!" (new), "Knight smashed!" (was "His train hit your knight."), "Bishop spared." (was "The train
  spared your bishop.").
- Judge and fair play are unchanged. Saving the piece scores as before, and the warned turn is still a `powerTurn`.

### Noticed, not changed
- In one of three recorded runs, one screenshot (of about 45 taken every ~150 ms) showed the move list under the board as an empty
  dark panel (icons, no rows) as the screen went from scoring to his thinking. The panel was full again in the next shot. The ride
  doesn't touch the move list, so it isn't from this change, but I didn't check it against main.
- At a 320 px phone the dock line is narrower than at the test phone's 390 px. I didn't measure it there, and other bosses' dock
  lines would be cut there as well.
