## Dash's clock tap only when you're slow

Eric, Nov 17: "Bugged already, it fired off like 5 seconds into the match even tho I played all my moves instantly." It came on a seeded turn 3-9, 900 ms into that move, however fast you played.

**Now** (the director's rule, `DASH_TAP` in settings.ts): once a battle, from move 3, on the first move of yours that runs **10 s** of a bank (Boss alone, a party) or **30%** of a move clock (raids, group battles: 6 s of 20). Move before then and nothing happens; it stays armed. Never on a move with another moment of his. It must end before his stall skit's slam (20 s / 50%) could come, or it waits for a later move. A 20 s clock just fits; a clock his slaps cut to 14 s doesn't. Screen only, as before: your clock keeps running.

**Design (simplest):** the screen's own clock decides (`tapAt` + `DashTap` in `DashStall.tsx`), the same solo and online. There is no rules event any more: `clockTap`, `tapTurn`/`tapNow`, `DashState.tap` and its moment in `Dash.tsx` are gone, and `quietMove` no longer has to skip it. Each screen gets it once a battle, a reload included: the battle's tag (`DashView.key`, 12 bits from the seed like Hollow's `lights`; an optional boss field) goes into localStorage as the tap starts. The King's strike pause is left out of the move's time. `?power=clocktap` is the same from move 1.

**Player-visible:** Dash lies down by your clock only after 10 s of a slow move (or 30% of a raid's clock), once a battle.

**Stills:** branch `stills/dash-tap-slow` (phone 428x746 first, then computer 1280x800), both at 10 s of move 2.

**Lane:** boss (targeted + boss kit). `Play.tsx` is a shared foundation, so the e2e run covers the whole suite.

**Checks changed (old → new):**
- `dash.test.ts` "his clock tap: once a battle, from his seeded turn …" → "his clock tap is the screen's own: no rules event … the battle's view carries its tag". The stall-plan check drops `clockTap` from its event kinds.
- `dash-wired.test.ts` "the clock tap's beats: after your clock starts …" → "… his lie-down, three seconds of taps, his zip off" (no 900 ms delay).
- `dash-wired.test.ts` new: "only when you're slow" (10 s / 30%, from move 3, never with another moment), "never with the stall skit's slam" (every bank and move clock from 3 to 120 s), "once a battle on this screen, a reload included".
- `dash-wired.test.ts` "the clock tap is screen only …" → "no moment of his for it, so his move keeps its own line" (the `clockTap` quietMove and powerLine checks removed with the event).
- `e2e/dash.spec.ts` "the clock tap (&power=clocktap): he lies down …" → "… only when you're slow": no tap on a quick move 1; at 10 s (±1 s) of move 2, with the same placement checks; gone before the slam on that move; none on a slow move 3.
