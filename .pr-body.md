Title: The engine pool: flagged players only ever meet each other (or bots)

**What changed**
- Fair play has a new case status, `pool`. A player goes into it when detection reaches the old ban level (`FAIRPLAY.enforcement` is now `"pool"`), or when a new account has 2 of its first 10 fair-play matches at the one-match ban bar (`FAIRPLAY.pool`). There's no review and no email first. Detection still never bans, and the reviewer's token can't touch a pooled case.
- Every queue sends a pooled account to the pool's own queue (`queueName(..., engine)` → `crowd-default.engine`). The server reads this from the account on every ask (`mayPlay`), never from the browser. That covers 50 v 50 and online Boss Battle (Default and Bots off), Squads alone, a Squads party with any pooled member, and 1 v 1 with its Cancel. Bots fill after the usual wait.
- Pooled players' results aren't held, so their own profile looks normal. They're left out of everyone else's "Top N%" and out of 1 v 1's top places.
- `/admin/fairplay` has a new "Engine pool" list. It shows when and why each player went in, their ratings and games, the evidence (matches at the bar, how often the deep re-check agreed, best strength), and how they do in the pool: crowd wins, 1 v 1 W-L-D (with how many against pooled players), strength. A "getting crushed: likely a false positive" note appears where it fits. Each row has a Release button (logged as a clearing, no email). The page links a JSON download (`/admin/fairplay/pool.json`, no emails). The case page also has a Release card, and the tabs have a `pool` filter.
- Data is kept: pooled players' judged moves and 1 v 1 games aren't purged on the usual timer. Deleting the account keeps the case, and a new account with the same identity goes back into the pool.
- Docs: decision note `docs/decisions/2026-11-10-engine-pool.md`, area pages for fair play, matchmaking and ranked (one line), and `docs/fairplay-reviewer.md`. I left the FAQ alone: it doesn't mislead, and the pool isn't announced.
