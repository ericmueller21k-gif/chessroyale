## The boss music plays clean: no low-pass, no break when the sound is rebuilt

Eric, Nov 14: "it sounds much lower quality... we can't compromise on these songs", and in the Hidden Boss's second form: "it froze,
stopped for a second, and then it was almost like it restarted at a different part" (he switches windows a lot).

**Causes**
- Muffled: every sound, the music too, went through the game's 6 kHz low-pass ("gentle on phone speakers").
- The break: back in the window after 2 s or more away, the next click rebuilt the audio context. The old one was closed at
  once, cutting the music, and the new one started after the new context had opened its device. The loop seams are exact, so the
  files weren't the cause.

**Fix**
- `sound.ts`: the music has its own gain (`musicOut`, at the master's 0.9) straight to the speakers. The game's sounds keep the
  low-pass. A rebuild listener can ask to keep the old context open (at most 2 s); with nothing asking, it closes at once, as before.
- `music.ts`: on a rebuild, it waits until the new context's clock runs (at most `MUSIC.handoffWaitMs`, 1 s), then starts the
  music there at the place the old one is heard at (`carriedStarts`, intro included). After that, the old one fades out over
  `MUSIC.handoffMs` (0.3 s). If the old one was still sounding, nothing is heard; if it was dead, the new one comes in. A splash's
  music is handed over too (before, it was dropped).
- **The "away" trigger is kept.** It catches Nov 3's Chrome case (the context "running", its clock moving, nothing coming out).
  The clock checks can't see that, `devicechange` fires only when the device list changes, and the context's `error` event is
  only in newer Chrome. Its cost was the audible cut, and that's gone. Details: `docs/decisions/2026-11-14-music-clean.md`.

**The phase-3 theme "restarting" on the four boards** (director's follow-up, Eric: "it's restarted twice and it's only been like
a minute"). Two possible causes were checked:
- The theme flipping (`recluseMusic` in App.tsx's `musicBattle`, and `switchPlan`): ruled out. Through the four boards, solo
  (solo.ts) and online (recluse-online.ts) only set `simul` phases: the countdown twice, then the clocks. `recluseMusic` names
  `recluse-phase3` for each of them, whether phase 1's `result` is set or not, until every board is decided. The battle's key (the
  match object) stays the same, and the same theme with no drop never restarts (`maybeStart` returns early). Proved by a unit test
  over the four-board states, fed through the player (one start), and by `music:` lines in e2e. "beaten in phase 1" logs exactly
  phase2, beaten, phase3 starts, then `music:end` at the result. "phase 2's slow moves" logs no `music:start/out/end` through a
  minute and more of play, the slow moves and his ultimate.
- The rebuild: the only path that restarted it. Each click back into the window after 2 s or more away cut it, and the new
  context brought it back late (before #691, from the loop's start). Fixed above. No other change needed.

**Player-visible:** the boss music sounds brighter and clearer (full highs). Clicking back into the game after another window no
longer cuts or restarts the music.

**Lane:** targeted (audio timing). Not changed: the files' bitrate (the songs' maker re-renders them).
