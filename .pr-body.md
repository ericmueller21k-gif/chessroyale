## The live window's e2e watches its own match

**What failed:** `live-window.spec.ts:114` [desktop] passed only on its retry on main (run 44396356591, shard 13). For 90 s the line read "A match is starting" and "The match is on" by turns. One match's ply never goes back to 0, so the window was changing matches.

**Cause (a test problem, not an app bug):** the window features the matchmade match with the most people, then the newest, across the whole server. On CI all the specs share one server, and `lobby-chat.spec.ts:144` (a queue match with two people) ran beside it and took the window. Reproduced locally by running the two side by side: the old test failed 2 of 3 with CI's flipping call log, and the new one passed 3 of 3. The director's lead (votes dropped as the board's history moves on) was checked and ruled out: sampling `liveSummary` through a whole bot-filled Crowd match shows the votes kept from each reveal through the next round. A raid shows none after the boss's move, as `LiveMatchBoard` documents.

**Fix:**
- A test switch, `/api/live?watch=CODE` (TEST_SWITCHES only), puts that lobby's match alone in the window (`live.ts` `counts(…, only)`, the hub, `api.ts`; about 10 lines).
- The spec opens Rita's page with `?debug`, reads her lobby code, and its `/api/live` route adds `watch`. It then waits on what it means: her match's first reveal (up to 120 s, the match's own pace), then the votes in the window within 15 s.

**Player-visible changes:** none.

**Changed checks (old → new):**
- `live-window.spec.ts:114`: the votes in the window within 90 s of it showing → her match's first reveal within 120 s, then the votes within 15 s, with the window watching her match only.
- `capacity.test.ts` (the hub's board): adds a `watch` check (that lobby's match even with fewer people; none without a board).

Lane: targeted (server test switch + one spec). Typecheck, `capacity`/`live` units and the whole `live-window.spec.ts` pass locally. Docs: `docs/areas/hub.md`, `docs/decisions/2026-11-18-live-window-watch.md`.
