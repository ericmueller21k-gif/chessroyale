## Battle Tower: Hollow's wrong tries kept on a reload; floors out of the boss quests

Targeted lane (the restore and the solo result's quest mode). Brief: `notes/briefs/tower-hollow-quests.md`.

**What changed**
- A wrong try into Hollow's dark on a tower floor keeps the floor at once (`solo.ts` `darkTry` → `keepFloor`): a reload mid-turn no longer gives the bulb back. The try that brings the dark's move isn't kept on its own (a rejoin can't play a dark's move still due); the next step keeps it seconds later. The blackout was already kept.
- Eric, Nov 20: a tower floor counts for XP and the profile, never the boss quests. The app sends `tower: true` with a floor's result (`App.tsx`, `account.ts`); `/api/results` reads core's new `soloQuestMode(mode, tower)`: a floor is a plain solo game ("finish games" only).

**Player-visible**
- Reloading mid-turn after a wrong try into the dark keeps that bulb out.
- Tower floors no longer move "beat a boss" / "play a boss battle" quests; XP and the profile are unchanged.

**Checks (old → new)**
- `packages/app/test/tower-keep.test.ts`: + "Hollow's wrong try into the dark is kept as it happens" (fails before the fix: kept bulbs 0, not 1).
- `packages/core/test/quests.test.ts`: + "a Battle Tower floor moves no boss quest".
- No check changed or removed.

**Run locally:** typecheck; `tower-keep`, core and server `quests`, app `tower` tests.
