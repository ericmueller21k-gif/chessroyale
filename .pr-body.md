**What players get**
- Each daily quest finished rolls once on the server, in its completion op: 4% a **Coin Boost Crate**, 4% a **Crate Bonus** (`COIN_BOOST`, `LEVEL_CRATE_BONUS` in settings). The weekly doesn't roll.
- **Coin Boost Crate:** opened free in the Crates tab, whenever you choose: 2.5x coins (play coins and bonus) over 120 minutes of games that pay play coins. Base coins alone count toward the day's 300 and the bonus steps; past the 300 the boost waits for the next day. A second crate adds 120 minutes; the multiplier never stacks. XP is never boosted.
- **Crate Bonus:** your next 5 paying level-ups give their crates twice (keys stay 1); another adds 5.
