## Round 2 (Nov 25): the phone's standing box, one check, the computer's foot line

**What changed (phone):** the box now stands on the phone too (Eric: "do that also on mobile and desktop"). The middle box is the power-up row, then the vote rows (gray skeleton until the reveal fills them in place), then the status line (the waiting line between reveals, the result line at the reveal), 6 px apart. It's the same height in every phase. The power-up row stays on top through the reveal, greyed (`LockedPowerRow`, shared with the other team's turn). The reveal's box lost its old 10 px side inset, so its rows and power-up row line up with the standing box.
- **Height:** 151 px with six rows (was 104). The scoreboard sits 47 px lower, all game; it never moves during play. The board's size is unchanged (390×844: 362 px; 375×667: 347 px).
- **One-line status:** 24 px (was 42), 13 px text, 12 px under 390 px wide, 11 px under 360. Measured inside the board's width at 390, 375, 360 and 320 px, with nothing cut ("White is voting. Your team moves next. 50/50").
- **Fewer rows on shorter phones (a call):** six rows from 760 px tall, five from 700, otherwise four (was five under 640 px and four under 600). At 375×667, six or five rows pushed the scoreboard under its three-row floor (MiniTower), and its last row ran off the screen (measured: 671 px on a 667 px screen). With these thresholds no scoreboard row is clipped at 320×568, 360×640, 375×667, 390×700, 414×736, 430×740, 360×740, 430×760, 390×844 or 412×915, and nothing scrolls. `useBarSlots` reads `--slots`, so the reveal shows the same count.
- **Every row filled:** the box now shows as many of the most-voted moves as it has rows (it was five plus yours), so a row never stays gray while moves are left out (50 v 50 only).

**Computer:** a watched turn's empty result line is now the box's own foot line: a row's width and look (the rows' surface, rounded) with a short gray bar, in the same fixed spot. The phone's is the same.

**The laptop still's d3 (9 votes) played over Nf3 (21): not staging, not a bug.** It's the real pick rule. Since the Nov 16 rework the default is **Strong** (`CROWD_PICK`, settings.ts; `drawMove` in scoring.ts): the judge's best of the team's three most-voted moves is played, or one of those within 3% expected score, drawn. So the played move is often not the most voted. "50 votes" against rows summing to 45 was the box showing only the top five, with the sixth row left gray (fixed above: it now fills). With more than six different moves the rows still won't add up to the total; the line above the board counts every vote. Nothing in the staging needed changing.
