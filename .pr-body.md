## The town loads fast again, and the loading card says what it's loading (Eric, Nov 14)

Eric (computer, right after #708 went live; the bar stuck near 20%): "christ it's taking forever. Also what happened to it showing
what it's downloading? Like loading the sprites, music etc."

### Cause
- Since Nov 12 the town fetched **every boss theme from its loading bar's start**, beside its own painting, **all at once** (22 fetches),
  then waited up to **3 s more** on "Loading music…" for whatever hadn't landed. #708 took that music from 14.8 MB to **44.4 MB**
  (about a minute at 6 Mbps), all of it competing with the town's own files (its code and the sidekicks' sheets) and filling the 3 s wait.
- The service worker kept the music in the version's cache, which **every deploy drops**. After any deploy, the next open asked for all
  22 pieces again (from the HTTP cache when it still had them, otherwise from the network: after #708 the files themselves were new, so all 44 MB).

### Fix
- **The town never waits on music.** `preloadMusic` starts in the background `MUSIC.townPreloadAfterMs` (2 s) after the town's first frame and
  fetches **one piece at a time** at low priority (`fetchPieces`, which a fight's `ahead` also uses). The "Loading music…" step and
  `MUSIC.townShare`/`townWaitMs` are gone. A theme that hasn't arrived yet is fetched when its battle or room needs it, as before (the battle's loading
  screen and the splash's hold are unchanged).
- **Service worker:** the music has its own cache, `brc-music`, shared by every version. Each piece is stored with its contents' hash
  (`MUSIC_HASHES`, written by vite.config.ts) and served from the cache only while that hash matches the build's. A deploy fetches only the songs it
  changed, and activation drops pieces that are gone or changed. Music no longer feeds the version's hash. Page loads and the town's own files
  never wait on music: the worker answers them as before.
- **The loading card names its step** under the bar, using the boss card's layout (title, bar, note): title "Loading Chess Town…", then
  "Loading the town's pictures…" (painting, plus waiting for the sidekicks' sheets) and "Building the town…" (uploading to the GPU). No
  older version named steps on the town's card (checked in git history: its title was "Building the town…", then Nov 12's "Loading music…"),
  so the brief's wording is used.

What costs what:
- **The music during loading** costs about 3 to 6 s on a slow first visit: 10.1 → 7.1 s on the computer, 26.0 → 19.8 s on the phone. That's the 3 s "Loading music…"
  wait plus the link the 44 MB took from the town's code and sheets. It also cost 2.7 s on the open after a music deploy (7.3 → 4.6). With the fix, no music
  byte is fetched before the first frame in any row (before: up to 44 MB on a fast link, 0.6 to 11 MB on a slow one).
- **The town's code** (its chunk, before the card shows): 0.4 s on a fast computer, 2.5 s on a slow first visit, 1.5 to 4 s on the phone.
  Unchanged. The page is still plain until it arrives (see "Not done").
- **Painting** (CPU): about 4 s on the computer and 16 to 20 s on the slowed phone, here with software WebGL. Unchanged and not part of this brief.
- My local server speaks HTTP/1.1. The live site's HTTP/2 can put the 22 music streams ahead of the town's images when a worker re-issues the fetch
  without its low priority, which would explain a stall bigger than these numbers show. Either way, the fix removes the music from the load.

### Player-visible
- The town opens without waiting on the bosses' music; the music downloads quietly afterward.
- The loading card reads "Loading Chess Town…", with the current step under the bar ("Loading the town's pictures…", then "Building the
  town…"). "Loading music…" is gone. Stills: `.pr-stills/town-loading-computer.png`, `.pr-stills/town-loading-phone.png` (drop them,
  and this file, before merging).
- After a deploy, the music is fetched again only if that deploy changed it.

### Not done (a call)
- The few seconds while the town's code chunk downloads on a slow first visit still show the plain page with no card. A card there would need to match
  the town's box on every layout (the phone's bar, the computer's frame) before the town's styles load. Left for a follow-up if wanted.
