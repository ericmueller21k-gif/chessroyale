## Residents PR 2: the program and ranked 1 v 1

The headless program that plays the resident accounts (`users.resident`, PR 1's route) as people with browsers, through the same
routes and sockets as the app. Ranked 1 v 1 only (50 v 50 is the design's PR 5). Design: `notes/residents-design.md` on
`claude/director-notes`, §1 and §3 "PR 2". **Needs PR 1 (`claude/residents-server`) merged first**: it calls `/api/admin/residents`,
`/api/admin/residents/beat` and honors `PAUSE_RESIDENTS`.

Lane: targeted (a script, its unit tests, a local run against `wrangler dev`). Nothing in the game changes: no server, app or
protocol code; one new settings block (`RESIDENTS`, used only by the program) and `packages/sim`'s tsconfig taking in the program
so `npm run typecheck` covers it.

### What's in it
- `scripts/residents/run.ts`: `--base` (default https://hunchess.com; Access headers from `CF_ACCESS_CLIENT_ID` /
  `CF_ACCESS_CLIENT_SECRET`, never printed; a local server takes `E2E_SQL_KEY`; any other host refused), `--accounts 200`,
  `--target-online 30`, `--hours` (0 = forever), `--modes 1v1`, `--flat` (no schedule, for local tests). Refuses to start while the
  server says paused or without Maia; stops every resident cleanly on SIGTERM/Ctrl-C (out of the queue, games resigned, last
  heartbeat) and when the server pauses them (any "Residents are paused." answer, or a refused game socket confirmed by the route).
- `names.json`: 200 reviewed handles (no real people or hints of one, so no openings named after people; never "bot"/"resident").
- `profiles.ts`: per resident, from its name: Maia rating (BOT_LOBBY's bell: 600-2200, steps of 100, around 1150), home time zone
  (55% Americas, 25% Europe, 15% Asia/Oceania, 5% elsewhere), keenness 0.3-0.9, behavior (normal 70, slow 8, leaver 5, AFK 4,
  chatty 8, rage-quit 5), favorite time control, pawn color and a free icon (set when the account is made; seeded items put on once).
- `schedule.ts`: each UTC day planned minute by minute, seeded by resident, day and minute: sessions of 10-60 min, 20 min rest,
  at most 6 h a local day, keen residents first and the rest topping up, so about `--target-online` are on, in waves (0.5-1.8 times
  the target). A restart plans the same day and rejoins the sessions still running.
- `play.ts`, `duel-client.ts`: the 1 v 1 game as the app's NetDuel plays it: hello, the vote by temperament, moves DRAWN from
  Maia's policy at the resident's rating, clock-aware think times (quicker in the opening, longer in check or with a close choice,
  slower for slow residents), rare draw offers and answers, resigning lost games, quick-chat presets, behaviors acted out (no-shows,
  stalls, walking away, a dropped socket that comes back with its ply).
- `resident.ts`: sign-in with its own cookie jar (session + fair play's device cookie), home (the live line every 5 s, the 30 s
  heartbeat in games), the queue with its ticket (busy, expired, lockouts, a re-sign-in on 401, a 3-minute give-up), and a queue gate
  so one resident is often alone in line and a person who queues meets one within seconds.
- `engine.ts`: one Maia process for everyone (restarted if it dies; moves meanwhile are random legal ones, counted).
- Heartbeat each minute to `POST /api/admin/residents/beat`; one log line per event and a summary every 5 minutes, no cookies or tokens.

### Proof
- Unit: `packages/server/test/residents-program.test.ts`, 20 tests (names vs the server's `cleanName` and `ICONS`, profiles,
  the schedule's waves, caps, rests and midnight join, think times within the clock, resigning, behaviors' shares).
- `npm run typecheck`: clean. `npx vitest run packages/server/test/residents-program.test.ts`: 20 passed.
- Local run against `npx wrangler dev` with PR 1 merged in: 
  - 6 residents, `--flat`, 4 min: all 6 made and signed in (looks set), queued, paired and played; at `--hours` the games in
    progress were resigned, a last heartbeat, exit 0.
  - **20 residents, the real schedule, 13 min:** 24 games started, 12 played to their end (a checkmate, resignations of lost games,
    two rage-quitters logging off after a loss), queue waits median 2-5 s, every move Maia's, no errors. The route's report: the
    games recorded, the heartbeat kept.
  - **The switch:** with 10 games in progress, wrangler restarted with `PAUSE_RESIDENTS=1` (as a dashboard change deploys a new
    version): every game socket's reconnect was refused 503 by PR 1's gate, the route confirmed `paused`, every resident stopped
    within 3 s and the program exited 0. Started again while paused: refused ("not starting").
  - SIGTERM mid-game: games resigned (aborted before both first moves), last heartbeat, clean exit.
  - Not run against hunchess.com: the director does the first production run. (One slip while checking the option errors: a run
    with no `--base` and Access variables present in the environment made a single read-only `GET /api/admin/residents` to
    hunchess.com, answered "Not found", and exited before doing anything else.)
