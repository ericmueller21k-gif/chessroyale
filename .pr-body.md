  all ~180 buildings' pixels in the Renderer's constructor (about 2 s on a slowed phone); every home was painted as the town's code loaded.

  15,762 frames of 2,510 characters, before and after), less work. Every sprite uses it, bosses too.
- **Each statue's world-scale picture is made when first drawn**, not all in the constructor.
- Not done (decision note): redrawing only on change (3% of idle frames match the last by day, 20% zoomed in, 0% at night; not worth
  tracking every animated input) and the boss sprite's 60 Hz check (not in the town's numbers).
