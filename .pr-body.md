## Chandy's queen lock: the piece goes when the round hits it, and one round that travels (Eric, Nov 20)

### What changed
- **The hit piece stays until the round reaches it.** Cause: the server takes the piece off the board as his turn begins (`chandyBattle.afterCrowdMove`), and `Boss.tsx` drew that position at once while the round was still to come (up to about 1 s later). New `chandyFen` (Chandy.tsx) puts it back on its square until `shotHitAt` (the round's head at the middle of its square), the same way Timber's and Don Dough's moments hold their pieces. It's wired into `fenShown` in `Boss.tsx`. The rules and server are unchanged.
- **One round, not dotted lines.** Cause: the round's `fly` animation looped, and a loop never hands over to `then`, so `trail` never played and every square kept its own flickering bolt, which read as a dashed line. Now `PIERCING_ROUND` has `pass`: one round (a white-hot head and a tapering streak) moving down each square a quarter square at a time, so the slices line up into one round traveling smoothly. It sheds 2 × 2 px sparks that cool from white to dark and fade. `hit` stops the round at the piece's middle, where the existing `SHATTER` takes over. The art is built in code from his existing palette; there are no new image files.
- **Setting:** `BOSS_POWERS.chandy.shotStepMs: 65` (the round's time per square; was a hard-coded 60 in Chandy.tsx). The longest path (8 squares) plus the shatter still fits the shot's 1400 ms moment, which is unchanged.
- **Docs:** `docs/decisions/2026-11-20-chandy-queen-shot.md` (with the future note: shatter the piece with the king-shatter effect, Dodge's `DODGE_KING`) and Chandy's row in `docs/areas/bosses.md`. The preview page (`chandy-preview.tsx`) plays the new shot.

### What players see
- The queen lock's round visibly flies from his side down the file to your piece, as one bright round with sparks peeling off behind it.
- Your piece stays on its square until the round reaches it, then shatters. Before this fix it vanished as his turn began.
- The round travels slightly slower (65 ms a square instead of 60).
