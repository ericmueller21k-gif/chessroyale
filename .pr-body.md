**How**
- As a figure is dressed (`you-look.ts` `dressedPawn`, `play`), the item's own CSS animations are stopped at N moments and painted once.
  Each frame is kept only as a patch: the pixels it changes over the still (tube 45×129 px, cane 52×130 px, about 13% of a figure).
  `render.ts` `loopAt` picks the patch by one shared clock, and `drawSprite` draws it after the still. Nothing is painted or made per frame.
- `TOWN_ITEM_FRAMES` (settings.ts): one line per item (classes, loop, stretch, N). Tube 8 frames, cane 12. `othersLooks` 6.
- Identical looks (pawn, tint, items, colors) share one set of pictures (`looks` in Town.tsx). Yours and the bots' are kept on the loading screen.
- **Atlas:** 6 playing looks ≈ 0.45 MP, a tenth of a 2048 px page (≈1.6 MB). 50 different looks uncapped would be under a page.
