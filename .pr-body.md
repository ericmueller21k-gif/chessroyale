Main's full run on f25c38a (run 44826720015) went red on two tests that passed only on their retry (phone). Both are fixed at their cause; the product was right in both.

## 1. `recluse.spec.ts:951`, the child form: "Expected play, received results"

**Cause.** The failure was at line 981, the move *before* Win this form, not the adult form's start. The CI error-context snapshot shows the result screen: **Defeat, "The Hidden Boss wins"**, the review at "Move 1 of 5 · a3". The test's own `quietMove` pushed the a-pawn (a3, a4, a5, a6…) and never defended, against a real engine: `…e5, …Bc5, …Qf6/Qh4, …Qxf2#` is open by move 4 (checked with chess.js: the old picker walks into it on every one of three such lines). When the child happened to find it, the match ended in a mate, and `yourMove` waited its full 120 s for a "play" that never came (2.4 min failing vs 52 s passing). The new end screen (#855) played no part.

**Fix (the cause, not a checker).** Every spec that "just needs a move" had its own picker, and each had missed a case before (a frozen piece, Oct 15; no quiet move left, boss-bar, today). Now there is one: `pickMove(fen, { allowed, prefer })` in `e2e/helpers.ts`. It plays a move the game takes, quiet when it can, `prefer`'s first, and **never one that lets the other side mate at once** while another move doesn't (3 ms a pick). recluse, boss-bar and boss-dock use it, each keeping its old preference (non-d/e pawn, knight, pawn). banner-time scripts its moves (`want`), so it keeps its own.

## 2. `boss-select.spec.ts:874`, the speech tail: "Cannot read properties of null (reading 'getBoundingClientRect')"

**Cause.** The stage's boss figure comes with the match's chunk (`lazy-game.ts`), loaded as the Boss Battle screen opens. The new test tapped Ginger and measured `.bs-sprite` straight away; on a busy runner the tap beat the chunk. The spec's other stage checks already wait for the figure's paint (`figureDrawn`). The tail itself is placed correctly once the figure lands (the stage's MutationObserver).

**Fix.** The test waits for the figure's paint after each tap, as the others do. **Proof:** with the chunk delayed 2.5 s, the old body throws the same TypeError and the new one passes.
