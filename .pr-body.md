## Dash's "Hang on", round 2 (Eric, Nov 26)

Boss lane. Eric played the new gag and saw no clock presses and no head-up pose. Brief: `notes/briefs/dash-hang-on-2.md`.

### The cause (watched frame by frame with real taps, phone and computer; test switch and the real pop-off, then "Hang on" path)
The animations were chosen and did play: nothing overrode or hid them. They just didn't read on screen.
- **The paw barely moved.** Round 1's smack was his move's slap: the paw went 10 of his 104 pixels, from his chin to the button, and the slow smacks held it at his chin for most of each beat.
- **The head barely moved.** "Head up" was his head shifted 2 pixels.
- **He was tiny.** In a phone's bar he is 58 px wide, so all of that came to 2 or 3 screen pixels.
- **Cheetah speed wasn't the fastest.** At 80 ms a smack it was slower than the pop-off's 70 ms.

### What changed
1. **Presses that read.** Every smack now winds up over his head and slams onto the button, with sparks, a shake and the flag jumping (the stall slam's idiom, reusing his rig, nothing newly drawn).
2. **Cheetah speed is his fastest.** `DASH_HANG_ON.hitMs` goes 80 → **44** ms a smack, quicker than every other smack of his. A second paw's blur sells the frenzy.
3. **Head thrown back.** His head is drawn alone and turned 50° about his neck (`headBack`), so the snout and open mouth point up and his eyes are shut. A quarter turn was tried first; it was unreadable.
4. **He jumps onto the board's middle** (the director's call). As "Hang on" begins he jumps from his spot to the board's center, half the board's height and always whole on screen. He does the whole routine there, then zips back as your piece slides home, before his move.
   - Built like his clock tap: a portal on the top layer. His spot keeps its place (`dash-away`) and his words stand over him.
   - The jump and the zip are the element's own animations, started from the gag's clock.
   - The routine is its own figure (`DASH_HANG`), so his bar fit and computer fit are unchanged.
5. **Longer.** Cheetah speed 3 → **6 s**; the whole gag **12.6 → 15.6 s**. A new `jumpMs` 600 is taken from the slow part. Solo and the server both follow the settings through `popOffMs`.

### What players see
On "Hang on, one second." he leaps from his spot onto the middle of the board. Slow, big smacks speed up. Then "Time for cheetah speed!": a blur of smacks with his head thrown back and his mouth to the sky, for 6 s. Then the wind-up and the sorry, and he zips back before his move. The FAQ line now says he jumps onto the middle of the board.
