## Phone top bar in color (light lane, Eric Nov 9)

Brief: `notes/briefs/brief-topbar-color.md` on `claude/director-notes`. Decision: `docs/decisions/2026-11-09-topbar-color.md`.

### What changed
- Quests, shop and Friends icons are about 30% bigger (29 px) in the same 44 px squares (40 px below 360 px, as before).
- Quests: a gold clipboard with a dark check (`QuestsBarIcon`, new; `QuestsIcon` for the panels is unchanged).
- Shop: gold bag. Friends: blue people (`--fd-blue`, with a dark-mode value). Settings: unchanged plain outline.
- Profile button: the shop's square (same size and corner radius), no gold ring, showing only your pawn's face,
  still (no ride, no animation). The pawn is about 184 px wide inside the square, so the head sits near the top with
  its shoulders cut by the bottom edge. The square's rounded rectangle does the clipping (`clip-path` on the button,
  not on the pawn). Signed out: the default pawn's face.
- A computer's top right pawn is unchanged (the `face` option is passed only on a phone).

### Player-visible changes
- The top bar is colored and the icons are a little bigger; the profile button is a square like the others.

### Checks
- `npm run typecheck`: passes.
- `e2e/home-layout.spec.ts`, phone project: 9 passed, 0 failed (includes the top bar at 320/360/375/390/430 px, the
  Quests entry at 320 px, and the no-scroll check in every mode).
- Changed: the top-bar test gained one check, that the profile button matches the shop's box (width, height, radius),
  has no ring and no running animation. Old → new: no check removed or loosened.

### Stills
Branch `stills/topbar-color` (orphan): light and dark at 390×844 and light at 320 px, for a signed-in dressed look
(helmet `present` and gun `candy-cane`) and the signed-out default pawn; full screen and the bar alone.

Finding on the dressed look: the helmet is wide enough to fill the square, so the background margin around the head
does not show there; it shows with the default pawn. Not tuned further; Eric's call whether the helmet should be
cropped more or the face kept as it is.

`overflow: hidden` on the button would have been the literal ask, but the Quests spec's "cut" guard flags any
overflow-hidden element whose content overflows it, and changing that guard needs Eric's OK. The clip-path on the
button gives the same rounded clipping and passes the guard.
