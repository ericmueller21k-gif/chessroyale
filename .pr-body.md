Power-up hints: the multiplayer e2e expects every legal move when there are fewer than 3

**Cause.** The flake (`multiplayer.spec.ts:58`, 2 hints instead of 3; main Nov 11, 12, 16) wasn't the engine. Classic deals Black's boards 9 plies in, and the opening library has one such start in check: the Caro-Kann's 5.Nxf6+, with only exf6 and gxf6 legal. The power-up correctly showed both. That happens in about 1 run in 170.
Ruled out: short or broken top-8 searches (all 2,421 opening starts give a full list at the app's settings, also after other kinds of search on the same engine), a cached result being changed, and boss move limits.

**Fix.** The spec expects `min(3, legal moves)` hints for the position on screen.

**Player-visible:** none. The game already shows every legal move when there are fewer than 3. Open question for the director: should the library deal starts in check with 1 or 2 replies at all? (There are five, at 9, 12, 13, 14 and 17 plies.)

**Checks changed:**
- `e2e/multiplayer.spec.ts:58`: `.hints li` count `3` → `min(3, legalMoves(fen))`.
- New: `packages/chess/test/uci.test.ts`: the library deals the Caro-Kann start at 9 plies, and the top-moves search lists exactly its 2 legal moves.
