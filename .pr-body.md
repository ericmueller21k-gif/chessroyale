# The Stranger's swords: his finisher, slashes, block and flourish

Eric's words (Nov 27): "every few moves he draws his swords"; taking a piece that isn't a pawn, "he jumps down and does either like a flame strike ... or a literal strike with a sword ... the piece disappears, and then ... we move our piece"; his piece taken, "he takes them out and blocks"; his strikes use the God King's shouts.

## What players see
- **His finisher:** when his move takes your knight, bishop, rook or queen, he hops onto the board beside it and plays one of four finishers (flame strike, ice then shatter, a clean cut that splits the piece in two, or frostfire: ice then fire). The piece disappears, he hops back, and then his piece slides onto the empty square. It takes about 4.6 s. The finisher is picked from the battle's seed, so every device shows the same one. The dock says "Your bishop falls." The move itself is an ordinary capture.
- **Your pawn taken:** one of three slashes where he stands (the double X, a sweep, a rising cut), picked by the move.
- **His piece taken (not a pawn):** he blocks with crossed swords, takes the blow (a clang and sparks) and sheathes. When his reply is a finisher, it waits for the block (the hold grows by 1.8 s).
- **Every third quiet move:** he draws his swords, twirls them and sheathes.
- **Sounds:** each strike's wind-up plays one of the God King's four recorded shouts (`gkHyuah`, Eric's choice). Two new sounds of his own: a clang (the block) and a shatter (the ice finisher).
- On a computer he hops from his place by the board's bottom-left; on a phone, from the bar. His ultimate (`strangerult`) is untouched.

## How
- Rules: `strangerBattle.finisher` marks a `finisher` event (`square`, `shape`, `pick`). `bossMoveMoment` holds his move for `STRANGER_FX.finisher`, plus `finisherLeadMs` after a block. This is the same path as Chandy's lock: the server holds the move and solo waits. New event kind in core (a boss's own, optional).
- Screen: `components/Stranger.tsx` (`finisherMoment`, `finishTimeline`, `strangerTurnMoments`, `strangerBoard`). Board art: his square's new `shatter`, `blaze` and `cutLine`. Grex's `pieceBurn` is reused with its sound off.
- Kit: a new `animOf` hook (`strangerAnim`) picks the animation per beat. It runs on the shared state alone, so every device plays the same thing.
- Test link: `?boss=stranger&wip=1&power=finisher` (or `finisher-flame|ice|cut|frostfire`). It starts where your bishop can take his knight, and he always takes a piece back.
