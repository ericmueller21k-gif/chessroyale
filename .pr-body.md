# Chess Town: "Leave Chess Town?" from inside buildings too, and the town-leave:148 flake

Eric, Nov 13: "even if you're in a building, I don't want somebody to click out … There should always be 'are you sure you want to exit' if you're in the town."

Targeted lane (App.tsx history and invite wiring, friends.ts, an Eric guard spec with his OK of Nov 13).

## What changed
- **The back gesture / browser back always asks** (street and buildings). The town opens with `replaceState`, so when nothing of the app came before it (opened from home, or `/town` typed or linked), back left the site with no question. The town now pushes one history step of its own as it shows (`TOWN_STEP` in `App.tsx`; none if the current step is already the town's, e.g. after a reload or a bot game). Back takes that step, and the popstate asks as before. Stay puts the step back, so the next back asks again.
  - When the town is left some other way (side menu, a game, the AFK drop), its step stays in the history. A later back onto it only sets the address back to `/`: one back that changes nothing visible. I didn't pop it as the town closes because that races the screens' own address changes (decision note).
- **An invite toast's Join** (computer; the toast shows over the town and its rooms) asks "Leave Chess Town?" / "You'll head back to their game." Stay keeps the invite and its toast. Before this, Join went straight to the lobby, and the invite was dismissed even before any question could be asked (`joinInvite` now dismisses only once you go).
- **A profile's Challenge over the town** (a profile opened from the town chat) made a lobby and left the town with no question. It now asks first ("You'll head back to your game."). Stay makes no game and sends no invite (`InviteHost.create` returns `"stayed"`, and `invitePlayer` resolves null).
- Unchanged (the director's call): Back inside a building still goes out to the street; boss challenges, the AFK drop, the guest time-up and the Hidden Boss's summoning don't ask.

## Player-visible
- From anywhere in Chess Town, a building included: back, the side menu, an invite's Join and a profile's Challenge all ask "Leave Chess Town?" (Stay is the default).
- On a phone, a back swipe in the town no longer drops you out of the site.

## The town-leave:148 flake: the test was wrong, not the game
CI's failure (main run 43489794469, shard 6) is the second half of "inside a room…": after Go in, `expect(room).toHaveCount(1)` saw 0 for 20 s ("24 × resolved to 0"). In that step the test's observer taps Back the moment the room is drawn, so the room is up for only about 0.6 s (measured: 586 to 685 ms). `toHaveCount` polls at about 0, 0.1, 0.35, 0.85 and 1.85 s, so on a slow runner the room opened and closed between two polls, and the check then waited 20 s for a room that was already gone. The game did the right thing: it went in, waited for the room, then went out. Fix: the page records the room drawn (`__roomDrawn`, set by the same observer), and the test waits for that flag, then for the room to be gone.
- Before: `--repeat-each 10`, phone: 10/10 passed locally (no local repro; the race needs a slow runner). After: 10/10.
- Also found, not fixed (not this flake's cause): `goIn` drops a "Go in" tapped during the 600 ms zoom out of a room you've just left, because `doorBusy` is still set. You'd have to tap the home and then Go in within 0.6 s of coming out. My scratch repro's taps took about 2 s, so it never hit. It could queue like `outNext` if wanted.
