Main red: tiger.spec:552 (Babur's pump on a computer) passed only on its retry: at 1440 × 900 the pump came out `tall`, not `side`.

**Cause.** Since #774 Babur's toss turns him to his pump (`.bc-flip`), and his drawn box then stands 4 art px over (his feet aren't his
frame's middle). His side place (`fitSides`) and the pump's (`pumpPlace`) both read his sprite's box *as drawn that moment*. A screen that
mounted mid-toss (a turn change landing inside it) ran `fitSides` from that box and stood him 13 px further left. At 1440 the pump,
pressed against the side menu, has 3 px to spare (57.7 px over him, limit 60.7), so from the next turn it went in the taller bar and
stayed there.
Reproduced on main every time: turn him, re-measure, play a turn → `--side-d` 66 → 79 px, pump `tall` (2/2).

**Fix.** `figureRestBox` (BossCharacter.tsx): a figure's canvas box as it stands at rest in its place (mirrored by its place or not),
whatever a run turns it to this moment. `fitSides` and `pumpPlace` measure from it. In steady state it's the same box as before, so
no layout changes anywhere.

**Proof.** `npx playwright test e2e/tiger.spec.ts:552 --project desktop --repeat-each 10 --trace off --retries 0`: before 10/10
(the race didn't hit locally), after 10/10. With the new check: main fails it (1280 × 800: `--side-d` 66 → 78 px), the fix passes.
The same probe after the fix: 66 → 66 px, `side` (2/2). Typecheck clean.
