## XP phase 3: quests and the daily overlay

Lane: **full** (stored data, rewards through the ledger): add the `full-e2e` label; needs an independent review.

### What changed
- **Server (`server/quests.ts`, core's `quests.ts`, `QUESTS` in settings.ts):** 2 daily quests a day and 1 weekly a week, dealt lazily
  on the day's first read (GET /api/quests or a game's XP grant) from a seed of the account and the day, once (a nonce claim in
  `quest_days`). Unfinished dailies carry over, topped up to 3 at most; the weekly resets Mondays 08:00 UTC. One free reroll a day
  (POST /api/quests/reroll). Progress and completion go in the **same batch as the game's XP** (`grantXp`): one progress row per quest
  per game, then op `quest:<kind>:<quest id>:<ledger id>` (reason `quest`) guarded by the progress count, so each quest pays once
  (replays, two tabs, two games at once). Only counted games move quests; solo games only "finish games" quests with 3 of the server's
  minutes; a win counts only if its win XP was paid. `PAUSE_REWARDS` stops progress and pay. Quest XP is a part of the box
  ("Quest +300") and can level you up (phase 2 pays the level).
- **Pool:** Boss Hunter (win a raid, +crate), Raid Party (2 raids), Squad Up (2 Squads), Fifty-Fifty (a 50 v 50), Warm Up (3 games),
  Winner (2 online wins, +crate); weekly Regular (10 games: 1,500 XP + crate + key). Not "win a ranked 1 v 1" (needs another person)
  and not "fight boss X" (a raid's boss is random; Boss alone is the device's word): decision note.
- **App:** the approved `QuestsOverlay` on real data (`quests.ts`); opens by itself on the XP day's first home visit (signed in, this
  device, not over the walkthrough), and from the Quests button any time. The button shows for everyone, greyed "Sign in for quests"
  for a guest where sign-in is set up. The reward toast gets quest lines ("Quest done: Boss Hunter +300 XP"). The mock
  (`quests-mock.ts`) is gone; the test switch `?quests=<kind>[:<progress>],…` sets real quests (test servers only).
- **320 px top bar:** the mockup's 36 px squeeze is gone; below 344 px the logo steps down to the app icon so every button keeps 40 px.
- FAQ (quests in "XP and levels"), privacy policy ("XP, levels and quests"), area pages (accounts, hub, shop, ops), decisions
  `2026-11-09-quests-rules.md`, `-storage.md`, `-screens.md`.
