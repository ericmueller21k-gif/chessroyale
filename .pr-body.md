## The town's loading screen: every picture painted and uploaded up front, then it stays

Eric (Nov 11): "I have no issue with doing a loading screen for the town... even if it takes 3 or 5 seconds... loads it all in at once and then it stays there if you go in and out of buildings."

### What changed
- **Loading screen.** The street town opens on "Building the town…" (the boss loading card's look; its bar shows real progress: half painting, half uploading). It paints every picture the town can show: every building's sides, back and front frames and each a step darker, every roof for all four headings, and every home's, boss's, prop's, statue's and sidekick's frames. Then it uploads them for good, tallest first, onto an atlas made once at the size they need. After that the town starts.
- **The atlas stays.** 10 kept pages (2,952 pictures, 97% full) plus 2 spare pages for tags, boards, the bubble, signs and text. Only the spare pages ever reset. Before, the 6-page atlas never got room back from pictures it let go or that changed size, so it kept filling and resetting. That's Eric's 12 resets.
- **Uploads no longer wait for the GPU.** The town's 2D pictures are now made in memory (`art2d`, `willReadFrequently`). Uploading a canvas drawn on the GPU made the browser finish the GPU's frame first: 30–120 ms an upload headless (Eric's "Outside our code" and gaps). Now it's about 0.3 ms.
- A sidekick sheet that fails to load no longer holds up the load.
- **Rooms stay behind the door's fade** (measured: going in takes about 2.0 s from the tap, and its one long frame, about 170 ms, falls under the black). Each room has its own canvas and painter, so loading them all up front would download and paint every room's art and keep it for the whole visit.

- **Load time:** 4.5 s headless at 1x (painting 4.3 s, uploading 0.2 s). Slowed 4x: about 20 s from the tap; the loading card shows 1.9 s after the tap. Almost all of it is the art being painted pixel by pixel. An iPhone 14 Plus should be close to the 1x figure, but only Eric's phone will tell.
- **Memory:** 12 pages × 16 MB = **192 MB** of GPU memory (was up to 96 MB). The same on a phone and a computer: the pictures are pixel art, so their size doesn't depend on the device's ratio. Fine for 6 GB phones; a 3 GB phone is the one to watch. If one struggles, about 15% of the pictures are duplicates that could share.
- **Tags and boards:** in a busy 30 s stretch, about 150 uploads (the bubble 60, signs 30 and text 23 the first time each shows, tags 25, boards 7, clouds 3). That's 0.1–0.3 an upload a frame, about 0.3 ms each.
- **Building in and out:** 67 frames at a 60 fps median. The worst frame is about 170 ms going in, under the black. Nothing of the town is uploaded again on the way out.
- Caveat: with `--use-angle=swiftshader` (software rendering), a 12-page texture costs every frame (20–30 fps against 60). With the browser's default headless flags, which e2e uses, it doesn't. A real GPU doesn't touch memory it doesn't sample.
