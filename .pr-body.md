## Boss music: mono 64 kbps, fetched during the town's loading bar

Eric (Nov 11-12): "reduce the size [of the songs]... why not just add all of the songs to the town when you load into the town?... loading music in the loading bar"; "Mono 64 sounds... it's totally fine".

### What changed
- **Every theme mono at 64 kbps, rendered from the source, not re-encoded.** `handoff.py` on `claude/trailer-v2` now folds the mix to mono right after rendering (so the level, the ceiling and the manifest are measured on what ships) and writes the MP3 at a constant 64 kbps. That's the default now (`MONO = False` brings back stereo). Its own commit there: `c3564a7a`. grex, bigboy, hollow, folio and zebra were rebuilt with it.
  - Lengths and `pad` are unchanged. Level within 0.1 dB of before (lufs -16.8 → -16.7 for grex and zebra, -16.1 → -16.0 for bigboy; peaks unchanged except bigboy's -1.3 → -1.6 dBFS).
  - Loop seams checked by decoding each loop: the decoded file is exactly as long as the source WAV, with 0 samples offset from it. The pad after the loop's end lines up with its start at 0 lag. At the loop point the jump between samples is far smaller than the song's own 99.9th-percentile step. I plotted 10 ms on either side of the loop point and saw no click or gap.
- **The town's loading bar fetches every theme** (`music.ts` `preloadMusic`). It only fetches (no decode), so the service worker keeps the files. Fetching starts with the bar, alongside the painting, at low priority, each file once per visit.
  - With Music on, the town fills the bar to 85% and **"Loading music…"** fills the last 15% (`MUSIC.townShare`). That step shows only for files that haven't arrived by the time the town is built.
  - The town waits on that step at most **3 s** (`MUSIC.townWaitMs`). Why: counting the painting, that's about 7 s of fetching, enough for 5 MB at about 6 Mbps. On a slower network the town opens anyway and the rest arrives in the background. A theme that isn't in yet is fetched when it's needed, as before.
  - Music off or Sound off: nothing is fetched and there's no music step. Headless timing: painting 4.4 s; with the music held back, the town opened 3.0 s after painting finished.
- **Decoding is unchanged:** a theme is decoded when its battle, room or splash needs it, and `forgetTheme` lets it go afterward, so at most one decoded theme sits in memory. The themes stay out of the precache (vite.config.ts unchanged).

### Player-visible changes
- The town's loading card can end on "Loading music…" (still: `docs/music-mono-preload/loading-music-phone.png`, 428x746, with the music held back so the step shows). Its bar never goes backward.
- Boss music is mono (Eric picked this by ear). A battle's or room's theme no longer waits on the network after a visit to the town.
