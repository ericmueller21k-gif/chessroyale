**What players get**
- Each daily quest finished rolls once on the server, in its completion op: 4% a **Coin Boost Crate**, 4% a **Crate Bonus** (`COIN_BOOST`, `LEVEL_CRATE_BONUS` in settings). The weekly doesn't roll.
- **Coin Boost Crate:** opened free in the Crates tab, whenever you choose: 2.5x coins (play coins and bonus) over 120 minutes of games that pay play coins. Base coins alone count toward the day's 300 and the bonus steps; past the 300 the boost waits for the next day. A second crate adds 120 minutes; the multiplier never stacks. XP is never boosted. Each game uses the minutes that earned its play coins, at least 5 (review fixes).
- **Crate Bonus:** your next 5 paying level-ups give their crates twice (keys stay 1); another adds 5.
- New text: the reward card's "Coin Boost Crate!" / "Crate Bonus!" title and line, "+2 Winter Crates (Crate bonus)"; the Crates tab's "Coin boost: x2.5 coins · 95 min of play left" and "Crate bonus: 4 level-ups left" (greyed "off" / "none" while off, always shown), the crate's row ("You have 1 · No key needed") and dialog (Open, greyed with none; Not now; "Coin boost on!"); the coin strip's "x2.5 BOOST / 108 MIN LEFT" badge and its "Boost +33" part; the FAQ's "Drops" and "Coin boost" bullets; admins' "+1 Coin Boost Crate" and "+1 Crate Bonus" grants.

**How**
- Stored: three new wallet columns (`boost_crates`, `boost_minutes`, `crate_bonus`, default 0), moved only by the ledger (new asset kinds `boost_crate`, `boost_min`, `crate_bonus`); reconciled, merged, erased and downloaded with the wallet.
- The drop's leg rides the quest's own op (`quest:<kind>:<quest id>:<ledger id>`), rolled from that key (it holds the hidden ledger id: same on every retry, checkable from the ledger, unguessable from a quest's id).
- A game's boost is its own op (`boost:<game>:<ledger id>`, reason `coin-boost`) after its coins settle, from what they paid; guarded by the minutes left and the coin ops' amounts; refused, worked out again (3 tries). No extra read for an unboosted game.
