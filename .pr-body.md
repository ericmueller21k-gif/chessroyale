## Rides: a moving sound, on Sawyer's Dozer and Boingo's Pogo first

Eric (Nov 14): "when you move around ... it sounds like ... a little bulldozer moving ... one continuous sound"; "test this on two of them
here, and then we'll kind of go more"; "when you stop moving ... it kind of fades over like a second or whatever ... I guess we'll have
to test it." Targeted lane (sound timing beside the town's frame loop). Decision: `docs/decisions/2026-11-14-ride-sounds.md`.

### What changed
- `packages/app/src/ride-sound.ts` (new): your own ride's loop. The town's and a shop room's frame loops call `rideMoving(id)` only on the
  frames you move (a timestamp and two compares once it plays); a timer off the frame sees the steps stop `holdMs` after the last, then
  fades it out and lets the source go once silent. Moving again mid-fade ramps level (and the dozer's speed) back from where they are, on
  the same source. One source at most. Level and speed "where they are" are worked out from its own ramps, not read back from the browser.
- `packages/app/public/sounds/rides/` (new): `sawyer-dozer.wav`, `boingo-pogo.wav` as made (WAV kept so the loop is exact; 58 KB each),
  and a CREDITS.md. Fetched and decoded via `loadAudioFile` once, after the town's first frame (your ride's), never in its loading (#709);
  a ride put on later loads on its first step.
- `sound.ts`: `gameBus()` (the master: the Sound switch's sounds, through the 6 kHz softening). The loop checks the switch and the page
  itself; switching sound off or hiding the page takes it out in 30 ms.
- `Town.tsx`: one line in the frame loop (`if (moving) rideMoving(r.youRider?.id)`), the warm on the first frame, `sound` in `__townRide()`.
  `ShopRoom.tsx`: the same one line (your ride inside, `riding()`).
- `e2e/areas.json`: a "ride sounds" area (ride-sound.ts, the WAVs) → town.spec, shop.spec, so a change to them doesn't run everything.

**Which ride is which:** a ride has a sound when its item id (crates.ts: `sawyer-dozer`, `boingo-pogo`) is a key of `RIDE_SOUND.rides`;
its file is `/sounds/rides/<id>.wav`. The loop points (0.1 to 1.1 s) are the README's shape for every ride's loop, fixed in ride-sound.ts.

### Player-visible
Riding Sawyer's Dozer or Boingo's Pogo, your pawn's moves have a continuous sound (the dozer coming up to speed); standing, it fades
(dozer 0.7 s, pogo 0.35 s). Other rides, on foot, other people's rides: silent, as before. The Sound switch off: silent.

### Settings to tune (`RIDE_SOUND` in packages/core/src/settings.ts)
`level` 0.3 · `holdMs` 120 · `engine.startRate` 0.8, `engine.upMs` 300, `engine.stopRate` 0.88 ·
`rides["sawyer-dozer"]` engine, fadeIn 60, fadeOut 700 · `rides["boingo-pogo"]` fadeIn 60, fadeOut 350. A new ride's loop: its file in
`public/sounds/rides/<id>.wav` (same shape) and an entry here.
