## Sawyer's Saw Swap (Eric, Nov 20)

Boss lane. A third power of Sawyer's, once a battle: when your move puts his king in check while it stands on his half, he saws the
board across the middle and his half down its center, swaps its two 4 x 4 blocks out of check, then plays his move. Off in the
Battle Tower by default (`BOSS_POWERS.sawSwapInTower: false`). Decision: `docs/decisions/2026-11-20-sawyer-saw-swap.md`; Sawyer's row
in `docs/areas/bosses.md`.

### What changed
- **Rules** (`packages/chess/src/bosses/sawyer.ts`): `sawSwapFen` (the swap and its legality: out of check, the crowd's king safe,
  a move for him, a position Stockfish plays), `sawyerBattle.sawSwap` from his `afterCrowdMove` (a base at that ply, his castling,
  en passant and the fifty-move count reset, the last move cleared, his split halves moved with their blocks; never on a mate, a
  draw, the battle's end or the move cap; once a battle; tower switch). `?power=sawswap` (`SAW_SWAP_TEST_MOVES`, `?side=b` too).
- **Core**: `sawswap` power id, `sawSwap` event kind and state field, `BOSS_POWERS.sawSwapInTower`.
- **Timing**: `SAW_SWAP` beats (4 s + banner), `sawyerTurnMs` holds his move for it, in solo (`solo.ts`) and on the server
  (`lobby.ts`, one line: `thinkMinAt`). QUEEN SLAIN! plays first if the check took his queen.
- **Screen** (`components/Sawyer.tsx`, `screens/Boss.tsx`): SAW SWAP! banner, his `sawDown` at the corner, the board saw's gap reused
  (turned for the cut across the middle, halved for the cut down his half; two faster runs of its own frames), the blocks sliding
  past each other in the board's own colors and pieces, the grooves closing. His existing sounds only. Lines: "Let me saw my way
  out of this.", "Pardon the renovation!", "Measure twice, swap once.", "Swap shop's open!". Dock on your next turn: "Swapped out of
  check." The move list holds his move back while the swap plays (as Dash's pop-off). FAQ: a Saw Swap entry under Sawyer.

### Player-visible changes
- Against Sawyer (not in the Battle Tower): once a battle, a check of his king on his half can bring the Saw Swap (about 6 s
  while he thinks), then his move. The FAQ explains it.

### Checks changed (old → new)
- `packages/chess/test/boss-kit-lib.ts`: `KIT_EXTRA_TESTS` adds `sawyer: ["sawswap"]`; new `KIT_FIRST_MOVE` (a switch whose power
  waits on one crowd move plays it first: the swap's check); `runBattle` uses it. Every other boss's battles unchanged.
- `packages/chess/test/boss-kit.test.ts`: a `KIT_FIRST_MOVE` battle must show its power's event (new assertion).
- `packages/server/test/boss-kit-parity.test.ts`: `online`/`solo` take an optional crowd pick (default unchanged); new test
  "sawyer, ?power=sawswap … the same solo and online".
- `packages/chess/test/sawyer.test.ts`: new describe "Sawyer's Saw Swap" (10 tests).
- `e2e/sawyer.spec.ts`: new test "Sawyer's Saw Swap (?power=sawswap …)" (phone).

- No check removed or loosened; none of these guards a rule in `docs/rules/eric.md`.
