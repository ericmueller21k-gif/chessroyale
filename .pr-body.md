Auto chess: the bot saves when you save

## What changed
- From round 2 the bot reads your gold at the round's start and what you carried over from the last round (never this round's buys) and
  decides its bank by `botBank` (`packages/app/src/autochess/bot.ts`), replacing the blind 30% coin flip:
  - you saving (10+ carried over, or 30+ to start): it banks to your interest steps (the tens you kept beyond this round's income), 85% of
    the time, still spending 8, and buys only what it needs: no reroll, XP or hold that round;
  - you spending down: it banks nothing and spends to keep up;
  - round 7 on, or either side at 8 HP or less: it never banks.
  - Round 1 (nothing to read) keeps the old 30% coin flip, so round-1 seeds the e2e tests pick are unchanged.
- Thresholds in `AUTOCHESS.bot.follow` (`banked: 10, gold: 30, chance: 0.85, lateRound: 7, lowHp: 8`).
- The bank draw is the same single `rng()` at the same point as before, and the lock-in time still comes from its own draw.
- Docs: `docs/areas/autochess.md` (the bot's line), `docs/decisions/2026-11-10-ac-bot-saves.md`.
