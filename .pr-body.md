## Quests overlay: a mockup in the real app (Nov 9)

Eric's reference (notes/briefs/assets/quests-overlay-eric-ref.png), rebuilt in our look. It's a real component, so it can be wired up later. It shows over home with fake data behind a test switch. No server and no storage yet.

**What's in it**
- `components/QuestsOverlay.tsx`: takes the quests, the two countdowns and today's reroll as props, with `onReroll` and `onClose` callbacks. Fake data is in `quests-mock.ts`.
- Test switches (gated like the app's others): `?quests=mock` opens it as home loads, `?quests=done` is the same with Boss Hunter done, and `?quests=entry` shows only the entry.
- **The card:** a gold frame over home (dimmed, blurred 3 px), the board's pawn in a black-and-gold crest, the "Daily Quests" banner and "New quests in 12h".
  - Two daily rows, then the weekly under its own "Weekly · Resets in 4d" label, tinted gold.
  - Each row has a picture: Wrangler's portrait for Boss Hunter, three pawns for Squad Up, a knight on board squares for Regular. Then its name, goal, bar and count, and its reward ("+300 XP", the shop's Winter crate and key art).
  - Each daily has a small reroll button on its top-left corner. It's always there, and greyed once today's reroll is used or the quest is done.
  - "Got it", ×, Escape or a tap outside close it.
- **Done row:** the bar is full and green, with a check on the picture and "Done!".
- **The Quests entry** (always there; nothing on home moves): it sits on your card.
  - Computer: a "Quests" pill at the top right.
  - Phone: the bottom right (a tall card's name sits at its top).
  - Short card (small phones): only its icon shows, and the card keeps that corner free.
- Light and dark, phone and computer; plain CSS, no new art.
