# Discord alerts: pages and a morning digest from the 15-minute cron

**What changed**
- New `packages/server/src/alerts.ts`, run by the existing 15-minute cron (beside its other jobs, so a dead D1 still pages). It posts to the Worker secret `ALERT_WEBHOOK` (a Discord webhook). Unset, not a Discord URL, or `PAUSE_ALERTS=1`: it reads and posts nothing.
- **PAGE** (pings `ALERT_PING`'s Discord user id, else @everyone), reusing the stored error counts (`ops_errors`):
  - the Worker's errors on the match, lobby and queue routes (`ALERTS.playRoutes`), or the Durable Objects' errors (`Class.method`), with at least 20 in a 15-minute window and at least 3× the same hour's 7-day median, two checks in a row;
  - D1 failing two reads 30 s apart.
  - Pages are de-duplicated: "Still happening" at most once an hour while it lasts, and "Recovered" (no ping) after two checks under the line.
- **Morning digest** (no ping): once a day, on the first check from 8 am Central. It covers pages, errors against the day before, new kinds (not seen the week before), Durable Objects, players' reports, the live line, D1, and the engine and fair-play budgets. Text players can write appears only inside a code block, backticks and invisible characters are removed, and `allowed_mentions` stops any ping.
- State is one JSON row in D1's `meta` ("alerts"). There is no new table or column.
- `/admin/ops` Health has a new line: "Alerts: alerts off: no ALERT_WEBHOOK" or "on", plus what's paging now. The JSON view adds `alerts`, and `npm run ops:report` prints it (paging also goes under "Needs attention").
- Thresholds are in `ALERTS` in settings.ts. Docs: ops.md ("Alerts to Discord"), DEPLOY.md (Eric's setup steps, Monitoring item 5), the live-ops idea's status line, and decision note 2026-11-10-discord-alerts.md.
