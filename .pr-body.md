## Quests overlay, round 2: the entry in the top bar, a dim instead of a blur, the app icon in the crest (Nov 9)

Eric on round 1: "absolutely exactly what I'm envisioning." Three changes, nothing else (sizes, rows and rewards are as they were).

**What changed**
1. **The Quests entry on a phone is in home's top bar**, one more square button beside your pawn, before the shop, Friends and Settings (Eric: "We have room for one more spot on mobile … That's where I want it to go"). Same 44 px as the others, the quests icon, accessible name "Quests". Off the home card on phones. A computer keeps the entry on your card's top right (its top bar is hidden at 1024 px and up).
2. **Dim, not blur**: the overlay is a plain dark layer (rgba 0, 0, 0, 0.55) in light and dark mode. No backdrop filter.
3. **The crest shows the HunChess app icon** (`public/icons/icon.svg`, the browser tab's) in the same round crest, instead of the pawn.

**Fit at 320 px.** The entry fits, with "HunChess" whole. With 40 px buttons the row was 25 px short: the pawn overlapped the entry. Below 344 px the round items are 36 px with 4 px gaps, and the logo 14 px. The row then has about 15 px to spare. That spare room is for other fonts: iPhones draw text a little wider than headless Chrome. At 344 px and up nothing changes.

**Player-visible changes**
- Phone: a Quests button in the top bar; the card no longer has its tag.
- Both: the overlay is a plain dim; the crest is the app icon.

**Changed checks** (`e2e/home-layout.spec.ts`, "quests mockup …", phone and computer)
- Old: the entry is on your card; at 320 px the entry must not cover your tag, and the card fits.
- New: the entry is in the top bar on a phone and on your card on a computer (one of the two, never both); at 320 px the top bar's items (logo, pawn, the four round buttons) don't overlap, none leaves the bar, all sit in one row, and the entry is the same square as the shop; the card still fits with nothing cut or sideways.
- No existing check was changed or removed, and no rule in `docs/rules/eric.md` is touched.
