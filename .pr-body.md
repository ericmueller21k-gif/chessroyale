## Dash's clock tap only when you're slow, never on the stall skit's move

Eric, Nov 17: "it fired off like 5 seconds into the match even tho I played all my moves instantly." It came on a seeded turn 3-9, 900 ms into that move, however fast you played. He also asked: "have him stay in the crawl position", "let the crawl finish", "his chat box didn't follow him", and "the bigger gag is reserved for another turn".

**What players see:**
- **When:** once a battle, from move 3. He lies down by your clock when your move reaches 10 s of a bank (Boss alone, a party) or 30% of a move clock (raids, group battles: 6 s of 20). Move before then and nothing happens; it stays armed. Never on a move with another moment of his.
- **No skit on his move:** the stall skit's slam and fake move never come on the tap's move, however long you take. They wait for the next slow move.
- **He stays until you move:** he keeps lying there, then zips back once your move is in. If you still haven't moved 20 s after he's down, he says "Bro, I'm not moving till you do."
- **The crawl always finishes:** if your move goes in before he's down, he still lies down, says "Ayy homie, relax, it's just a joke.", then zips back.
- **His words follow him:** his speech bubble sits over him, a little over the board's bottom edge, never covering him or the clock's digits. His usual bubble is hidden meanwhile. Your clock keeps running throughout.
- The lines are placeholders for Eric.

**Design (Eric: "it's a variable state"):** one value in Dash's state, `dash.tap` (the move it came on). The tap is the first beat of `stallPlan` (`tapAt`). Solo and the server time it from their own clocks, alongside the slam and the fake move (`nextBeat`: the first beat to come takes the move). The server sends it as the existing `stall` message with beat `tap`, with no pause. A reconnect gets it in the round, so he shows lying. The old turn-start event (`clockTap`, `tapTurn`) is gone. `DashTap` is now drawn beside the match screens (`App.tsx`), so a move going in doesn't cut him off.

**Review fixes** (the boss lane's review, `review/dash-tap-slow`):
- **Only players still thinking see him.** If you've already moved (a raid player in `scoring`, or a party member with a pick), you never get the crawl or the "just a joke" line. Your pick gets him up, the same as your move going in (`tapSeen` in `DashStall.tsx`). A reconnect before your pick still shows him lying.
- **The ticking stops after 3 s.** He keeps tapping, but his taps make no sound after `DASH_TAP.tapMs` (3 s) from when he's down. Before, he clicked about 2.5 times a second for as long as you thought.
- **Never in a move's last 2 s:** `stallPlan` now needs `tapMs <= allowedMs - DASH_STALL.marginMs`, so solo and the server agree, and solo can't start a crawl right before you flag.
- **Accepted, no code (Eric: "let the crawl finish"):** in the joke case, his reply's slap animation plays while his figure is away. This is noted in the decision file.

**Lane:** boss lane, plus the server's beat timing (the stall's own code, kept small) and the stall message's beat value. `Play.tsx` and `App.tsx` are shared, so CI's e2e run covers the whole suite. The director decides on anything more.

**Checks changed (old → new):**
- `dash.test.ts` "his clock tap: once a battle, from his seeded turn …" → three tests: "only when you're slow" (10 s / 30%, from move 3, `clocktap`, never with another moment), "never share a move" (`nextBeat`), and "through a battle" (recorded once; no plan on its move; the skit on the next). The stall-plan checks drop `clockTap`, and "through a battle"'s later moves now check that the slam and fake are null, since the tap may still come.
- `dash-wired.test.ts` "the clock tap's beats …" → "the crawl finishes; lying until your move; the joke; bored at 20 s; no clock stopped". "Screen only …" → "no moment of his: his move keeps its own line" (the `clockTap` checks went with the event).
- `dash-lobby.test.ts` "each once a battle, apart" → its later move has the tap marked spent earlier, so that move stays the fake move's. New: "his clock tap": 30% of the clock, no deadline change, nothing else on its move up to its deadline, the beat in the re-sent round, and the slam on the next move. The plan's equality check gains `tapAt: null`.
- `e2e/dash.spec.ts` "the clock tap (&power=clocktap) …" → two tests:
  - "only when you're slow": nothing on a quick move 1; at 10 s (±1 s) of move 2; the same placement checks plus his words over him and clear of him and the digits; no slam or fake past 30 s; the bored line; up when you move; on move 3, the slam and no tap.
  - "the crawl always finishes": an instant move after he starts, then the joke line, then he's up.

- Review fixes, new checks: `dash.test.ts` "only when you're slow" adds the margin (a 10.3 s or 11.999 s bank gets no tap; 12 s does). `dash-wired.test.ts` adds two tests: "ticks aloud only for DASH_TAP.tapMs" (`quietAt`) and "only the players still thinking see it" (`tapSeen`: scoring and a pick get nothing; a pick, scoring or the next move gets him up). `e2e/dash.spec.ts` "only when you're slow" adds two checks: not quiet as he lies down, and `data-quiet` by the bored line.
