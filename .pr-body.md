## Moo & Moon's Full Moon: the moon rises from behind the hill

Eric, Nov 20: "the moon should start half hidden behind the horizon/grassy hill and then move upward into the sky for a few seconds (same timing as is now), moon should never be in front of the background hill terrain ... the other zoom in part stays the same, so does the light but just make sure it's emitting from the new moon location."

### What changed
- The moon (the field's own, lifted: `characters/moo-moon.png`) now **rises** from its middle on the hill line (about half hidden) to the field's own moon's place, easing out, over the same 5.5 s as the descent took (`MOO_CINEMATIC`: numbers below). Every beat and the 13.38 s total are unchanged.
- **Always behind the hills:** until the cut the field is drawn as layers lifted from its own pixels by a new script, `scripts/lift-moo-field.py` → `characters/moo-field-layers.png` (the sky without its moon or the moon's glow, then the front: hills, barn, silo, fence, grass, flowers), with the moon between them (`.mo-front`, z-index 2 over `.mo-bigmoon`). The hill line per column goes to `characters/moo-horizon.ts` for the tests. No new art; nothing drawn by hand.
- **The light** (`recluse-light.ts`, unchanged) comes from the moon's middle as it rises (`moonMiddle`), so at the white-out from where it ends.
- The close-up and everything after: unchanged. The field after the close-up is the original picture, whose moon is exactly where this one ends (no second moon, no jump).
- Settings: `moonFrom { scale: 1.65, y: -37 }` → `{ scale: 1, y: 57 }`; `moonTo { scale: 1.95, y: 42 }` → `{ scale: 1, y: 0 }`; `moonPow: 1.4` → `moonEase: 1.6` (progress `1 − (1 − p)^1.6`).
- `e2e/areas.json`: `moo-field-layers.png` placed in Moo's area.

### Player-visible
The Full Moon's field: the moon starts half hidden behind the grassy hill and rises into the sky behind the hills, barn and fence, then glows and bursts into light from where it rose to. Same size as the field's own moon (it was 1.65 to 1.95 times that); the decision note explains the call.
