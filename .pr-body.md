## 1. The move mix: the cause, in plain words (measured first; the dice were fine)
- The meter picked at random among the unlocked moves, so a battle's six or seven moves often went three or four to one move and

- The queen lock wasn't rare by its own rule (it could come on 41% of turns). It was one random pick in four or five: 1 battle of 10.
