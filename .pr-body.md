## Town: no uploads while walking at night (the lights' glows made on the loading screen)

Eric's `?report` (Nov 26 evening, Edge on Windows, 1360x948): 1,211 uploads in 16.7 s of walking, worst frames 50 to 117 ms, all walking with 3 to 12 uploads.

**Cause.** At dusk and night each lit window's, front's, side wall's and lamp's glow was baked the first time it shone, and each of its pieces (2,551 in the town) was a canvas of its own, uploaded as it first showed. #937's guard walked by day (`?sky=day`), when no light shines, so it never saw them. Reproduced headless at 1360x948 and `?sky=night`: 1,145 uploads in 30 s of walking, every one a glow piece (by day: 3, the bosses' speech bubbles). It wasn't #949: main uploads only bubbles by day on both visits. A smaller one: the bots' dressed pawns (their crate items) were drawn just after the loading screen, each uploaded when first seen.

**Fix.**
- The loading screen bakes and keeps every glow whatever the sky (render.ts `plan`, "glow:" jobs; the clock can bring dusk while you play).
- One sheet per glow, its pieces 2 px apart like the atlas's padding (paint-jobs.ts `glowPixels`): 338 canvases instead of 2,551, with identical pixels (all 2,551 pieces checked, 5.76 M values).
- On a first visit the paint workers bake the glows. A later visit takes each sheet and its pieces from the kept pages (keep-pictures.ts `Manifest.glows`) and bakes nothing.
- The spill round each lit pixel is now stamped outward from the lit pixels instead of searched round every pixel. Same numbers (1.05 M compared), and 2.7 s → 0.4 s on the main thread at 1x.
- The loading screen waits for the pawns' crate items (up to `loadSheetWaitMs`; measured 0.1 ms) and keeps them. The sky keeps its sun and clouds for every look, not just the current one.

**The report** now names what it uploads: "Uploads by source: glow 1145 · bubble 3" (frame-report.ts `upName`). A reason follows the name when there is one ("redrawn", "after a lost context", "its page started again"), so the next report can place its own uploads. See `night-report.png` on branch `stills/town-uploads-2` (night, 1360x948 at ratio 2, after the fix: "bubble 2").

**"Outside our code"** is the frame's time minus our measured work: the browser's own work, garbage collection, the GPU process running our draws and uploads, and the wait for the next refresh. Uploads stall in the GPU process, which other GPU apps such as League's client compete for. Things of ours that could add to that time: the canvas size, 4x MSAA, the alpha canvas, the stencil, 224 MB of atlas pages. Nothing measured here points at any of them (WebGL is software headless), so none is changed. Eric's next report, with uploads at 0, will show whether outside time stays high. If it does, `alpha: false` and multisampling off at ratio 2 are the first things to try.
