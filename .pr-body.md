### What changed
- **The rule** (`packages/chess/src/bosses/boingo.ts`, shared: solo, raids, online, the Crowd's boss final). It replaces the
  old pie on an empty center square. Every 2 crowd turns from turn 2 (`pieEvery`), he throws a pie in the face of one of the
  crowd's pieces (never the king) whose two squares diagonally behind it (toward the crowd's back rank) are on the board and
  empty. The pie splits onto those two squares: nobody, either side, may move onto them for 1 crowd turn (`pieTurns` 3 → 1).
  - **Target** (`choosePieThrow`): ranked by how many of the crowd's moves the pies take away (the piece's own retreats count
    double), then value, then square; picked at random from the seed among the top three. No target: no throw, and he tries
    again next turn.
  - **Guards**: a piece isn't a target if its pies (or its freeze) would leave the crowd no legal move (in check too), or leave
    the boss none in the position as it stands.
  - **Splats** (`splats` in the state): the piece hit gets a mark that follows it through every move (castling's rook, a
    promotion), and goes when the piece is taken. A second splat (`pieSplatsToFreeze` 2) freezes it for 1 crowd turn
    (`pieFreezeTurns`; the held pieces' rule and Ginger's ice), and the mark goes.
- **Protocol**: one optional field, `NetBossPowers.splats` (missing from older servers). Its core is unchanged. The pie event
  now carries `squares` (the two pied squares) next to `square` (the piece hit).
- **On screen** (`components/Boingo.tsx`): the banner as before ("PIE!"), its line now "Splat on your pawn" or "Splat! Your pawn
  is frozen". The beats: his throw (1.45 s), the pie in the piece's face (1.8 s, the king attack's `pieHit` art with the splat
  sound), two pieces of it lobbed onto the squares behind (`Flight`'s new `fromSquare`/`bit`), and the old pie art splatting
  there (2.16 s, one sound for the pair). A freeze's ice forms at 1.9 s. The moment is 2.7 s (was 2.3; the banner is unchanged).
  The mark is a small cream smear with a drip on the piece's face, drawn as part of the piece (`boingoDecor`, the board's
  `decor`), so it slides with it. It shows on the boss, play and crowd screens, and not in the history view.
- **FAQ**: Boingo's pie text rewritten to the new rule; its numbers come from the settings.
- **Tools**: `frames-boss-powers.mjs` and `frames-boss-move.mjs` were stuck behind the first-time walkthrough and the old Boss
  alone button. Both are fixed, and the powers script now takes `POWER=pie`, `PHONE=428x746` and `DPR=2`.
