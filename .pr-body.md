## Character lore and the Battle Tower's banter (data only, not wired)

Eric (Nov 21): a file on every character that talks, with its lore, and banter between the tower's bosses, plus lines for the Battle
Tower's clerk ("have fun" going up, "how'd it go?" coming down). This PR writes the two data files and their test. Nothing reads them yet:
the wiring (the floor's banter, the desk's lines) is a later job.

### What's in it

- **`docs/lore/characters.md`** (318 lines): one section per character that talks. Each has what it is and how it looks, its
  personality and voice, its powers (one plain sentence each), relationships the game hints at, and 3 to 6 existing lines as voice
  samples. Lore is marked **Eric's words** (with the date) or **sketch** (nothing written yet; waits for Eric, as the boss-select decision asks).
- **`packages/app/src/characters/tower-banter.ts`** (289 lines): `TOWER_BANTER` and `CLERK_LINES`.
- **`packages/app/test/tower-banter.test.ts`** (48 lines): the coverage, length and Hidden Boss checks.
- **`e2e/areas.json`**: the new data file in `none` (no browser test needs it).

### Calls made

- **The admins-first bosses have no banter yet**, but they have lore. The test uses `towerPool()`, so when one goes live it fails until its lines are added.
- **Moo & Moon's lines** are the Moon's talk with Moo's moos in parentheses, since the cow doesn't talk (Eric's note).
- **Lore marked as sketch** wherever Eric hasn't written the backstory. Only Ginger, Moo, Smudgy, Babur and Hollow have Eric's own words on their lore.
- **The Hidden Boss** has one line and no name, as the brief asks. The God King's section doesn't mention him.
- **Pronouns** follow the game's own text or Eric's notes; elsewhere the sections use the name.
