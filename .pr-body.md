Six Legendary/Mythic items get the polish from Eric's approved mockup ("yes go ahead", Nov 25).

**Lane:** targeted (item art + a core name roll; touched areas' specs: `public-profile`, `profile`, `crate-banner`, `welcome-crate`, `shop` and what `e2e/areas.json` traces from `crates.ts` / `Items.tsx`).

## What changed (player-visible)
- **Fire & Ice Crown:** three real flames rise out of the ice between four shards, flickering (every color; ice and gem stay ice-blue).
- **Candy Cane:** a gold strand spirals up round the hook to the tip, with motes and a tip sparkle, then rests (6.5 s cycle).
- **Gift-Wrap Tube:** a confetti puff (five bits, its two colors, white and gold) up and left every 5.5 s.
- **Parrot:** a double wing flap every 6 s.
- **Tommy Gun:** the drum's winding key turns, then a muzzle puff a second later (7 s cycle).
- **Smiley Face:** six expressions (Smile, Wink, Tongue, Cool, Surprised, Laughing), rolled from the item's seed, even odds, never stored. Its name says which: "Smiley Face · Wink" on the reveal card, locker tile and its label, the delete dialog and the profile. Each expression stacks on its own.
- Animations play only where the item is the focus: your pawn on screen, the locker's preview, a crate's reveal, a profile's Wearing rows and tiles. Grids, lists and the power banner stay still. With reduced motion, still drawings.
- Banana Suit and Pirate Hat untouched.

Stills and one GIF per item: branch `stills/item-polish` (phone, dark; plus reduced motion and the locker tile at 320 and 390 px: the long smiley names wrap after the "·", inside the tile).

## Performance
CSS on SVG transforms and opacity only, no frame loop. New `@perf` check: a profile wearing the crown, a smiley and the cane (5 items playing), phone CPU 4x slower, 7 s: p95 16.7 ms, 0 of 421 frames over 20 ms.

- `e2e/public-profile.spec.ts`, "four worn items all fit, nothing cut": the smiley tile's name "Smiley Face" → "Smiley Face · Laughing" (its seed 5). Same fit checks, passing on phone and desktop.
- Added: unit `crates.test.ts`, "a Smiley Face's expression comes from its seed…"; `@perf` `perf.spec.ts`, "worn items playing on a slow phone" (local only, CI leaves `@perf` out).
