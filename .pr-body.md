## What changed

**1. Your pawn and the God King stacked (Eric, Nov 11: "put the king and pawn vertical… the king on top").**
- In a phone's strip under the move list (Boss alone), BossDock now measures the strip's height.
- Where the two fit one above the other at least at today's size, they share one column at the strip's right: the God King on top, your pawn under him (`dk-stack`).
- Each is `--dk-k` times its side-by-side size: as big as the height allows, at most 2× (the old two columns' width). The cap also keeps his canvas frame inside the window, so the page never scrolls sideways.
- Where the strip is too short, they stay side by side as before.
- Measured sizes:
  - stacked: 320×640 (1.1×), 428×746 (1.08×), 428×926 (1.75×), 390×844 and a 768 tablet (2×);
  - side by side: 390×664, 360×640, 320×568.
- A computer is unchanged, and so is a party's battle (its teammates stand under the two).
- His menu, his words, his ⚡ and 🐟 and the carrot count all move with the column.
- Watched frame by frame with real taps: Jefferson at 428 (pawn hop, boss move) and Smudgy with a forced Last Stand at 428 (leap, away, attack, fall back with the ⚡ pill).

**2. Jefferson's fireworks in his boss card.**
- The candle's "shots still up" were a column at the board's right edge, cut off on Eric's phone.
