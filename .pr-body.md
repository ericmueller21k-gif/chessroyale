## Chandy: the queen lock first only when it can come; a refused queen lock tries again (the review of #797's follow-ups)

Boss lane (boss brain). Decision: `docs/decisions/2026-11-18-chandy-queen-lock-first.md`; sim: `reports/chandy-sim.md`, run G.

- **The queen lock is his first unlock only when it could come then** (`queenLockAim` on the board after his capture); else the usual random unlock (the review's low 1).
- **Measured, the dead slot had another cause:** in all 10 of 20 battles where his first unlock never fired before upgrade 2, the pass guard refused the queen lock (mid-trade) and, with nothing else unlocked, the meter emptied. The check above alone changed nothing (10 of 20). Now a refused queen lock with no other move unlocked **stays due and is checked again on his next turn** (the pass guard's own Oct 18 rule for every power that spends his turn). Result: **10 → 5 of 20**, 4 of those 5 with upgrade 2 the very next turn. Trade: the aim is his whole turn, so upgrade 2 comes about 1.5 of your moves later; upgrades 3 and 5 unchanged.
- **Test:** a queen shot and his second taught capture's lock in one turn, online: the shot's event before the `first` lockOn, the hold `bossMinAt` ≥ the shot's end + `CHANDY_FX.lockOn`, then the move.
- **Comments:** the stale "first capture"/"first lock" ones the review listed (chandy.ts, boss.ts, lobby.ts, runner.ts, boss-timing.ts, Chandy.tsx).

**Player-visible:** the queen lock comes sooner after his first upgrade; with your queen unshieldable his first unlock is random. FAQ updated.
