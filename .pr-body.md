# Battle Tower polish from Eric's Nov 22 playtest

Targeted lane (the tower's bar and end screen, the town chat's server side). Decision: `docs/decisions/2026-11-22-tower-polish.md`.

1. **Moo's Full Moon glow lost in the accordion card.** Cause: the card's figure (`TowerBar.tsx` `Figure`, also a computer's `TowerSide`) played a fixed idle and never read the boss's live look, which the lone bar's `BossCharacter` reads every frame. Fix: it now draws the idle of `liveKit` (powered or staged), read as each frame is drawn (`SpriteAnim`'s new optional `pick`), plus `lookOf`. Its box is still his plain idle's. Other persistent looks checked: Big Boy's recolor and Chandy's stages are now carried (neither is met on a floor), the Hidden Boss isn't in the tower, and Hollow's bulbs already worked.
2. **"Back to the Battle Tower".** The floor's end button (solo and party), the free-leave note, and a party floor's leave note (it said "You'll go home") now name the Battle Tower. Nothing else changes.
3. **No "entered the town" line for tower floors.** Cause: leaving the town for a floor's match drops your pawn, and a floor takes longer than `TOWN_CHAT.enterRejoinMs` (2 min), so coming back counted as a new arrival. Fix: when the town opens back from a floor, its beats send `floor: true` until one is answered, and `TownHere.beat` never counts that pawn as fresh. Going up to a floor never posted a line. A real arrival still posts one.

**Player-visible:** Moo stays gold and glowing in his tower card (folded and open) and beside the board on a computer. The button says "Back to the Battle Tower". No chat line when coming back from a floor.

**Specs run locally (phone and desktop):** `battle-tower.spec.ts` "an admin's 🏆 Win this match" (now signed in; checks the first beat back is `floor: true` and later ones aren't) and the new "Moo & Moon's Full Moon on a shared floor" (gold on his card canvas, folded and tapped open; a computer's side figure). The Moo check fails on main's card (0 gold pixels). Unit tests: app and server all pass (`town-here.test.ts`: a new case for a floor's return).

**Changed checks, old → new:** `battle-tower.spec.ts` and `tower-party.spec.ts` look for the button "Back to the tower" → "Back to the Battle Tower". No guard tests in `docs/rules/eric.md` changed.
