## Battle Tower on a computer: the floor's title over the right column, and its room in a taller boss bar

Eric, Nov 21: "We really don't want it to say Battle Tower Floor 4 right there ... we need all that space for the boss for the bar at the top ... put it to the right above the moves black container". Then, after the first stills: "Why would we make the board bigger? Shouldn't we be making the boss thing bigger? ... I would be going with the one of these two that you'd use the bigger boss bar."

`main` merged in (with #871, the accordion bar). Targeted lane: layout on the boss screen that touches the board's size math on computers.

**What changed**
- `Hud.tsx`: on a computer, the tower's `.tw-strip-line` between the bar and the board is gone. `TowerStrip` is now the first row of `BossTools` (`.tw-col-title`).
- `tower.css`: on a tower floor, the column's tools are two fixed rows: the title (20 px, centered over the Moves box, its top level with the bar's top), then the tools in their usual 28 px row.
- `tower.css`, computers 900×600 and up with a mouse: the tower's boss bar is **89 px** (it was 61). That covers the split bar (each card 83 px inside its outline) and a lone floor's bar (`min-height`, padding matched to the card), so both kinds of floor look the same. What's inside grows:
  - each boss's face sits in one 84×74 box, fit whole (`object-fit: contain`), so the name column starts at the same place for every boss;
  - the name column (name, LVL, meters) is scaled up 1.2×;
  - the status is 13 px;
  - Hollow's bulbs and Jefferson's candles are 1.5× (drawn at 2×, so 3× here, whole pixels), never wider than the room they have.
  - Timber's workshop, Tick's tally, Don Dough's timers (those bosses aren't in the tower's pool yet) and Babur's pump keep their sizes.
- `board-size.css`: on a tower floor `--bd-room` is 151 px, as on main, so the board keeps main's size. The rule now keys on `.tw-col-title`.

**What players see (computer only; phone, short computer windows and non-tower boss battles unchanged)**
- "Battle Tower · Floor N" sits at the top right, over the tools and the Moves box. Only the current floor shows, never a total.
- The boss bar is 28 px taller, with a bigger face, name, LVL, meters and bulbs or candles. The board is right under it (8 px gap).
- The board keeps main's size at every window: 600, 616, 744, 744 and 872 px at 1280×800, 1366×768, 1440×900, 1600×900 and 1880×1025. On a floor of several it also keeps main's place (103 px down). A lone floor's board now sits in that same place, about 10 px lower than on main.
- The tools sit 3 px lower on a floor of several bosses and 8 px lower on a lone floor. The Moves box keeps its size and place.
