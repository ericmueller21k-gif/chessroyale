## Flake at its cause: xp.spec.ts:224, the coin strip's "never down"

**Cause (in the test, not the game).** The spec reads the strip's count from its digit columns' transforms. The tens column is a gas pump
(`CoinStrip`'s `setCount`): it shows its digit **plus** how far the units are past 9, so at a count of 9.99 it stands 0.99 of the way to 1.
The decode floored it with 0.01 of slack (`Math.floor(v + 0.01)`, meant for a zoomed transform reading a few hundredths short), which turned
0.99 into 1 while the units still read 9.99: a count of **19.99**. The next frame's real 10.1 then looked like the count going down. A frame
lands in that 0.01 window before 10 or 20 only now and then (the count climbs about 0.6 a frame), so it failed about one run in ten across
the three window sizes; on #974 (run 45797983484, shard 9) it hit at laptop 1280×600 and passed on the retry. The strip itself never moved
backward.

**Fix.** A higher digit is decoded as what's left after the roll the digits under it cause: `Math.round(v - max(0, lower - (unit - 1)))`.
Exact at every count, and it still absorbs a zoomed transform a few hundredths short (checked offline over 0 to 22 in 0.001 steps, exact
and ±0.004 short: old decode 2 drops, new 0).

**Proof.** `--repeat-each 20 --project phone -g "coin strip under the XP box" --retries 0` in `mcr.microsoft.com/playwright:v1.56.1-noble`,
under the shared e2e lock: **20 passed, 0 failed** (21.7 min, 3 window sizes each; the test now sits at `xp.spec.ts:228`).

**Player-visible changes:** none (test only).

**Checks changed (old → new):** `e2e/xp.spec.ts` `sampleCoins`, the count decode: tens and up `floor(v + 0.01)` → `round(v - max(0, lower - (unit - 1)))`.
The checks themselves ("never down", "0 until it starts", "rolls through the numbers", the count ending at 22) are unchanged in meaning. Not
a guard of `docs/rules/eric.md`.

Lane: light (one test's measurement).
