## Moo & Moon's Full Moon cinematic

Eric (Nov 17): "he jumps down onto the center of the board, then the board and everything above it like the boss bar, is replaced
with a singular cinematic drawing of a field of grass with a starry night and a giant moon… a super close up of moo and moon…
their eyes start turning white, then it goes back to the first scene and this is where he does the transformation, then the
background fades and then once it fades he hops back to his spot." He approved both scenes ("moo and moon closeup is perfect now").

**Lane:** boss lane (boss content: art, timing, wiring). Specs it runs: moo, king-attack, boss-speech, boss-smoke, recluse (one export).

### What changed
- `components/MooCinematic.tsx` (new): the Full Moon's moment while he thinks. He hops from his spot onto the board (his `move`).
  The field fades in over the board and everything above it. A quick cut goes to the close-up, where their eyes turn white, and
  a cut goes back to the field. His transformation plays there (`fullMoon`, gold at `swap`), then a beat. The field fades out and
  he hops back (`p_move`). Everything is timed from the moment's start, so online every player sees the same thing.
- What it covers: on a phone, the whole width from the screen's top to the board's bottom. On a computer, the board's column from
  the top (the boss bar); the side panels stay, and his figure beside the board hides while he's out on the field.
- Art: `characters/moo-cinematic.png` (85 KB, one sheet: the field's 4 twinkle frames and the close-up's 5 eye frames), fetched
  ahead with his prewarm. The field was drawn by the API runner; the close-up is his rig's own sprite zoomed in (Moo 4x, the Moon
  5x). Both are on claude/boss-art: `scripts/boss-art/out/arena-moo-field`, `scripts/boss-art/moo-closeup/`.
- Timing: `MOO_CINEMATIC` in settings.ts has each beat (hop 720, fade in 500, field 700, close-up 2200, transformation 1340,
  beat 400, fade out 500, hop back 720): 7080 ms. `MOO_BEATS.fullMoon` adds the beats up (`transformAt`, `swapAt`, `total`), so
  `fullMoonMs` lengthens his think in solo and on the server alike. There is no new pause, and no clock runs for the crowd.
- The Moon's line for it now comes as he lands back (`lineAt`); on a phone the field covers his bar until then.
- `RecluseCutscene.tsx`: `boardOnScreen` is exported (reused). `scripts/frames-moo.mjs`: it now gets past the first-time
  walkthrough and uses Boss Battle, then his tile, then START (the old "Boss raid" button is gone); its `full` stills cover the
  cinematic's beats.
- Docs: `docs/areas/bosses.md` (Moo's row), `docs/decisions/2026-11-17-moo-cinematic.md`.

### Player-visible
- His Full Moon now plays a cinematic of about 7 s at the start of his turn in place of the 1.3 s transformation at the board's
  corner. His move and the flurry follow as before.

  add up to `fullMoonMs` (6.5 to 8 s) and come in order, the hops fit his `move`/`p_move`, the eyes whiten inside the close-up,
  and the opacities, frames and animations follow each beat.
