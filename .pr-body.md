Fixes main's red on 729e639 (run 42552923157, shard 11): `e2e/boss-result.spec.ts:44`, phone, both tries, `.results h1` never came.

**Cause.** The defeat test pressed START with no boss picked, so it met a random playable boss. `?mate=1` (`bossMateTest`) can't mate at once against 2 of the 14:
- **Wrangler**: his first move is always the long pull (`pigTurn` plays it before the switch's mate is looked at).
- **Sawyer**: his first move must be a pawn's (his split), and the switch picks its mate among his allowed moves.

Both played 2…d5, the battle went on, and the result never showed. With 2 bosses in 14, a try hits one about 1 time in 7, so the test came and went. It has nothing to do with #567's CSS, and load isn't the cause.

**Fix.**
- `boss-result`'s defeat test names Hollow: `/?debug&pace=quick&mate=1&boss=hollow`, as `king-attack` and `shop` already do with `?mate=1`.
- `bossMateTest`'s comment in settings.ts and the bosses page now say which bosses the switch can't mate at once.
- The game isn't changed. Bending Sawyer's or Wrangler's first-move rule for a test switch would touch `applyBossMove`'s last check and Sawyer's split.
- The victory test keeps its random pick: `?win=1` works for all 14 bosses.

**Proof (local, phone).**
- The defeat path once per playable boss (`?boss=<id>`): 12 passed; `sawyer` and `pig` failed, each showing "Your move." after 2…d5.
- `?win=1` once per boss: 14 of 14 passed.
- After the fix, `boss-result.spec.ts` on both projects with `--repeat-each 3 --retries 0`: 12 of 12 passed.
- `npm run typecheck`: passes.

**Changed checks (old → new).**
- `boss-result.spec.ts` "victory and defeat screens": a random boss → Hollow, named. The checks on the result screen are unchanged.
