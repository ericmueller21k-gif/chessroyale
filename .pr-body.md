## Cause and fix, per test
1. **`e2e/dash.spec.ts:778` [desktop], the clock tap's joke.** Dash's lying-down frames were made lazily, the first time he lay
   down, in the tap beat's render: one long task of 250-380 ms on a fast computer (measured with a long-task observer) and about
   1 s on a slow one. So the test's move, sent "at once", went in after he was down (540 ms), and no joke was due. Fix: `DashTap`
   makes and draws his frames ahead as his battle shows (`prewarmLie`, using the existing `prewarmAnims`/`prewarmSprite`, 4 ms
   slices). The beat's render is now 8 ms. Also, per the brief, `moved` is now when your move went in (the scoring phase's
   `movedAt`, solo and online; a party's `myPickAt`), not when the screen drew it. No protocol change: client phase fields only.
2. **`e2e/shop.spec.ts:493` [desktop], Hollow Peak.** A test aim, not the room. `walkTo` tapped a square read while the camera
   still glided, so the tap for (7, 6) landed on the dog's picture. The walk to his counter passed through (7, 6) (`data-you`
   shows the square being stepped onto), so `walkTo` passed, and you stopped at his card. Fix: each walk aims once the view rests
   on you and counts only once you've stopped there (`viewOnYou`, as the other rooms' walks already do).
3. **`e2e/xp.spec.ts:81` [phone], the level-up bar.** Real. The end screen's `run()` took progress from the frame's time, which on
   a busy main thread can be earlier than the run's start. `smooth()` of a negative value overshoots (smooth(-1) is 5), so the bar
   shot full for a frame and fell back to 0.98, and the "+0 to +170" count could step back. Fix: progress never goes below 0.
