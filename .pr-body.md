## What changed

Round 2 of the auto chess player size (Eric, Nov 9, from his phone: "the pawns bigger, the block takes the full height, the top the same as the bottom").

- **The rows grow into the spare height** under the action box: each player row takes half of it, at most 1.7x its old height. The board and the action box keep their size; the box moves down by what the rows grow.
- **The pawn's drawn figure** is the row's usable height (the row less its gap and the hop's room). The avatar is scaled to it with its feet on the row's foot; the layout box keeps only the figure's width.
- **The block fills its row:** name at the top, level, then HP and XP at the foot, text and bars sized from the usable height, the name capped by the block's width (12 letters fit).
- **The top block matches the bottom one** (same block, same sizes).
- **The meeting's middle pawns** grow with the row too (at least as tall as a corner pawn; still capped at 36% of the board).

Bench tiles, the board and the action box keep their places. At 320 x 568 the rows do not grow.

**Not met, by design:** on a 390 x 844 phone about 64 px stays under the action box, not the brief's free margin + 8 px. Half the spare would take the rows to about 2.1x, which pushes the figure and the block's width past their column. The brief's 1.7x cap stands; raising it is one number (`0.7` in `--grow`, `packages/app/src/styles.css`) and is the director's call.

## Changed checks (old → new)

- `e2e/autochess.spec.ts`, "the players (Nov 9)" → "the players (Nov 9, round 2)" (phone at 390 x 844, and the computer):
  - the drawn pawn ≥ 85% of the row's usable height (was: the pawn's box ≥ 80% of a bench tile);
  - the figure ≥ 1.5x a bench tile on a phone (new);
  - each block fills its row (≥ the row less 5 px; was ≥ 90% of it);
  - both blocks and both pawns the same size (kept);
  - the name, the numbers and the block's height fit (new);
  - the rows at most 1.7x their old height and never smaller; either at that cap or the room under the box ≤ the free margin + 8 px (new).
- v5 screen test: a thin bar ≤ 10 px → ≤ 13 px.
- "the meeting before round 1": the middle pawns ≥ 1.6x a corner pawn's box → ≥ 1.0x (the corners grew with their rows).
