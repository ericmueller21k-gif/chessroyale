# Bosses' ultimates come when the boss is in danger

Eric, Nov 26 (beat Birdie in a test game, his ult never fired; Birdie down only a bishop and two pawns, positionally lost): "his ult is
supposed to fire. It's not about like how many moves have been played. It should have detected like he was basically in danger, or like
it was already heavily in my favor, and it should start triggering very quickly thereafter, like a turn or two at most."

**Lane:** boss (rules + brain calibration); the director decides. Decision: `docs/decisions/2026-11-26-ult-danger.md`. Audit:
`reports/ult-danger.md`.

## The cause

Birdie's Fall came only at `fallLost` (9) points of material lost, and he can be lost on the board with far less down. The same in kind
across the roster: the rage meter gains only `rageAheadMax` (4) more a move while the crowd is ahead, so a winning crowd waits about 12
crowd moves from an emptied meter, and Chandy's Dodge waits for 5 rifle upgrades a good crowd never gives him. Measured before the
change (a Stockfish crowd, 3 seeds a boss): from the crowd's first move at 0.70, the ultimate took 2 to 26 of the boss's turns, or never
came.

## What changed

- **The danger line** `BOSS_POWERS.dangerAt` = 0.70: a crowd move the judge puts at 0.70 or more for the crowd (the boss's `judged`, the
  number "your chances" shows).
- **The rage meter** (every boss but Birdie, Chandy, Wrangler and the Hidden Boss): full as the next crowd turn begins, whatever it held
  (`dangerCharge` in `prepareTurn`; Folio's `creaseDue` predicts it the same way). The usual warning, then the ultimate: the boss's
  second turn in danger.
- **One a danger** (`dangerSpent`, set by `unleashed` when an ultimate comes with the crowd at the line, cleared by a crowd move below it):
  after that the meter fills at its usual pace. The first version refilled at once after every ultimate, and the short ones chained (Big
  Boy's Bounce 32 times in a battle).
- **Birdie's Fall:** at the start of his turn once two crowd moves in a row are at the line (`birdie.fallDangerMoves` 2; the material rule
  stays). He has no warning, so the second move still brings it within two of his turns.
- **Chandy's Dodge:** warned as if his meter were full, his rifle whole or not.
- **Unchanged:** each ultimate's own conditions (the pass guard, the board saw's half, Smudgy's pair, the gild pick, the hoops' fit,
  Folio's guard), Wrangler, and the Hidden Boss (report only). Below the line nothing changes.
- The FAQ's rage meter section, Birdie's Fall and Chandy's Dodge say so; `docs/areas/bosses.md` updated.

- **After:** every boss whose ultimate needs only a full meter lands in his second turn in danger, in every window. Waits that are left
  come from each power's own rules, which this PR keeps: **Smudgy** 10 to 14 (his Repaint needs a pair to paint, and there is none while
  the crowd keeps all eight pawns, Eric, Nov 7), the **hoops** 4 once (they wait when his move gives check or none fit), the **watering**
  5 once (the pass guard, Eric, Oct 18).
- **Wrangler:** his Showdown came in every battle before any danger (his meter is full from his fifth move). **The Hidden Boss:** his
  bullet clock comes on its own turn (7).
- **Birdie:** against a Stockfish crowd his train kept the crowd under the line in all 13 of his battles, and his Fall came by material
  (turns 13 to 28). His danger path is covered by the unit tests.
- **Ultimates a battle** are about as before (2 to 5). The bracketed column is the first, chaining version.

**Even games: the same before and after.** I ran nine level battles (Ginger, Folio, Birdie; a strong but fallible crowd; the boss's move
seeded so a seed replays the same battle). They played identically with and without the rule, every ultimate on the same turn. The one
move at the line (Birdie, 0.72) fell back the next move, so two in a row brought nothing. A unit test checks the same for every boss.

**Why two in a row for Birdie only:** in the before battles, 57 of 120 crowd moves that reached 0.70 fell back under it the next move,
mostly because a boss power hit back. His Fall is once a battle and armors him for the rest of it. The warned ultimates stay at one move:
two would make them wait three of his turns.
