- match.ts: cuts counted from `keep` (today's tie rules; a short side never cut below its keep; nobody cut after the last), the

  `bestPlayer`, `crowdPodium`, `crowdLimitReached`, `crowdLimitVerdict`. settings.ts: `roundsInStage` → `crowdStageRounds`, by the
  game's move number (a Random opening still cuts after move 14).

### Calls made (decision file)
The schedule by the game's move number; cuts by keep; the carry to 2 decimals; the draw in scoring.ts (not match.ts); places'

### For the director
- **Shared-blunder relief at small sizes:** at 4 or fewer a side every lone pick counts as "shared" (`sharedShare` 25%), so at 2 a
  side a 6-point loss counts as 3, halving the 2 v 2's gaps (0.75× in the 4 v 4). The sim scored without it. Pinned in
  `crowd-rework.test.ts`; not changed here.
- **For C2/C3:** the app reads `PREGAME_VOTES` by index (`net.ts:830`, `Vote.tsx:122,293`): switch to `pregameVotes(settings)`
  (the FAQ's lookups by id are fine). The runner: stop on `crowdLimitReached` (the position's fullmove), then `crowdLimitVerdict`, then
  `finishGame`; a game over on a cut's move cuts nobody; pass who left to `bestPlayer`/`crowdPodium`; `outAtMove` =
  `CROWD_CUTS[outInStage].after` for a cut player.
