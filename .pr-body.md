## Added (Eric, Nov 12): every boss as tall as his card (phone)
- Eric: "every boss as tall as their container... make it the height, minus a few pixels, of the container, and then if it reaches over to the left... just shift everything over to the right... we can just shove over his name and stuff."
- Each boss's size in the phone's boss card is now measured from its art. It is the largest size at which every frame he rests in reaches no higher than `BAR_TOP_GAP` (3 px, kits.ts) under the card's top, in his own proportions.
- His drawing's shift and his name column's move right are likewise the least that keep him inside the card's left edge and clear of his name. All three are computed by `packages/app/test/bar-fit.ts`; `npx tsx scripts/bar-fit.ts` prints them after a change to a drawing or to the gap.
- Most bosses' idle is now 86 to 94 px tall, up from 62 to 84. Don Dough goes from 0.9× to 1.21×, Sawyer 1.34×, Ginger 1.45×.
- Moo & Moon (0.96×) and Puddles (1.15×) stay a little shorter. Their thinking pose, which plays every turn, is their tallest frame.
- The name column is now only as wide as its name and bars (at most 130 px), and "N moves left" never wraps. A column moved right takes its room from the empty middle (the extras' slot).
- The extras' slot is a container its prop can size to. Don Dough's two timers do, and are 61 px each at 360 instead of overlapping.
- Calls (decision note `docs/decisions/2026-11-12-boss-card-height.md`):
