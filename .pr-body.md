Fixes the flake at `e2e/crowd-scoring.spec.ts:96` ("360px, scoreboard: a name's room with and without the fire"; PR #970's run, shard 3, passed on the retry).

**Cause.** The phone's scoreboard under the board picks how many rows fit through a ResizeObserver (`MiniTower.tsx`), which runs a frame or so after the viewport changes. `checkTower` read the rows straight after `setViewportSize`, so it sometimes got the previous width's rows. In CI, at 360 px the read with the fire had 3 rows and the read without it had 2 (the widths matched, 109 px each; the lists had different lengths). A probe showed the same thing locally: right after a resize back to 320 px there were 3 rows, then 4 once things settled. The app is fine: a player sees the right rows one frame later. The test was measuring too early.

**Fix (test only).** `checkTower` reads the rows (with the fire and without it) through the shared `settled` helper (`e2e/helpers.ts`), which waits until they hold still for 300 ms. Nothing in the app changes, and no check is loosened or retried.

**Proof.** `--repeat-each 20 --project phone -g "scoreboard's fire"`, run in CI's own image (`mcr.microsoft.com/playwright:v1.56.1-noble`) under the e2e lock: 20 passed. The `desktop` project: 1 passed. `npm run typecheck`: clean. (This container's newer Chromium and fonts fail the 320 px "clear" check with or without this change, so the proof ran in CI's image.)

**Player-visible changes.** None.

**Checks changed (old → new).** `checkTower`'s two row reads: read straight after the resize or toggle → read once the rows hold still (`settled`). Every assertion is the same. It doesn't guard any of Eric's rules in `docs/rules/eric.md`.

Lane: light (test timing only). Decision: `docs/decisions/2026-11-26-scoreboard-fire-flake.md`.
