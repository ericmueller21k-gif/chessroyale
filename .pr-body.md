## Chandy's checkmate way into Dodge: the art (phase 1)

Eric, Nov 26: on your mate he jumps onto the center, collapses, reaches toward your king, zaps it, then the Dodge; after a
survived Dodge, a clear win beat. This PR is the art only (not wired to the rules job's timeline yet).

**What's drawn** (`packages/app/src/characters/chandy-mate.ts`, `CHANDY_MATE`: his rig, palette and size, a 174 x 150 frame,
every animation at every rifle stage, 0 to 5 attachments):
- `jump`: crouch, spring (off the ground, arms up), tuck in the air: 3 frames (180/110/400 ms), the last held through the leap.
- `land`: the landing's squash, knees buckling, slumped on his knees: 3 frames; `slumped` loops (2 frames, shoulders heaving).
- `reach<dir>` (2 frames) and `reaching<dir>` (loop), at `U30`, `0`, `D30`, `D60` (mirror for a king on his left; `nearestReach`).
- `zap<dir>`: the hand charging (3 frames), the flash and a bolt off his claws (2), back to the reach: 6 frames, about 0.9 s.
- `out` (the win beat): his lens sputters and dies, he slumps, falls flat on his back: 5 frames; `ko` loops with dizzy stars.

His rig (`chandy.ts`) gains optional pose fields: `legF`/`legN` (knee and ankle), `handF`, `lift`, `shadow`, and
`groundTailPart`; every existing pose draws exactly as before.
