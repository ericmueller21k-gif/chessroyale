## Dash: Time Scramble's seconds by use, and "Hang on" replaces his second pop-off

Eric, Nov 26. Boss lane (boss rules, clocks and server timing). Decision: `docs/decisions/2026-11-26-dash-hang-on.md`.

### What changed
- **Time Scramble:** 3 moves (was 5) of **5 s the first time, 3 s the second** (`scrambleSeconds: [5, 3]`, read by use with
  `scrambleSecondsOf`). The late-pick grace is unchanged. The banner, Tick's tally, the countdown and the FAQ say the right seconds
  each time. His `ultimate` line no longer names "three seconds".
- **"Hang on"** in place of his second pop-off (the first pop-off is unchanged). It's presentation only: your move is played and
  confirmed as ever, and nobody's clock runs while he thinks. While he thinks after your move, the screen shows:
  - your piece slid back to its square (0.45 s in), with your clock shown ticking down;
  - "Hang on, one second." and slow smacks of his clock, speeding up;
  - "Time for cheetah speed!" (6.2 s in): super-fast smacks with his head thrown back and mouth open;
  - "Almost done, bro." (8.0 s);
  - the wind-up on the clock's front (9.2 s);
  - "Sorry about that, bro. Here's your time back." (10.1 s), with the clock counting back up to exactly its real value;
  - your piece sliding to its played square (12.1 s), then his move (12.6 s plus a 0.3 s margin).

  The server and solo hold his move for it, the same way they hold the pop-off (`popOffMs`).
- The pop-off and "Hang on" now play only in his thinking after your move. Before, his thinking for a failed Scramble Rush's extra
  move on the same turn also counted.
- During the gag the board shows any pre-moves already waiting but takes no new one.
- Test switches: `?power=hangon` (Hang on on his second turn); `?power=popoff` is now the pop-off on his second turn and Hang on on
  his fourth.

### Art
Nothing new drawn. The smacks are his slap frames. The head-up, mouth-open pose is his jkLaugh's head. The wind-up is his paw circling
the clock with his whirr, then a press of the clock; the sorry is an open paw. Watched frame by frame with real taps on a phone and a computer. Stills and a
phone GIF are on branch `stills/dash-hang-on` (`dash-hang-on/`).
