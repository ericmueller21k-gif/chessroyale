# 50 v 50 rework, C3: the screens

Step C3 of `notes/designs/crowd-rework.md` (rules 6 and 8, mockups `crowd-cut`, `crowd-podium`). Targeted lane (screens with
logic; one small server change: who gets the watching rounds). Built on C2 (#762).

## What changed
- **Top bar:** "Move 31 · cut in 2", "4 v 4 next" on a cut's own move (was "cut now"), "1 v 1 from move 43" through the 2 v 2,
  then "1 v 1" (`cutCountdown`, game.ts); a narrow phone's line has about 60 px: "to 34v34", "1v1 at 43" there.
- **One banner** as the 1 v 1 begins: "1 V 1!" with the two names, the boss battles' `FightBanner`, once a match.
- **Cut screen, out** (`CutOut`, Crowd.tsx): "You're out: 9th of 100", "Cut at move 33 · 0.18 behind the last one through",
  "Why was I cut?", a fixed still-in box (each side's count and names, the stages to come), and at its foot **Spectate** and
  **Leave the match** side by side, always both, sticky at the bottom, the same place every cut.
- **Spectating** is the watch screen (live board, picks, scoreboard; "you're watching", no premoves), solo and online. Not
  tapping either spectates once the break ends. Server: a cut player in the rework now gets the watching rounds, tallies and
  reveals (`LobbyCore.spectatorsWatch`); solo watches instead of fast-forwarding.
- **Podium results** (`components/Podium.tsx`): mode line, how it ended, 2nd / 1st / 3rd with each player's dressed pawn, name,
  points a move, Winner / Best player tags; a fixed best-player line; your row (RP's spot greyed until C5); the chart of all 100 in
  a fixed box, your row pinned. Solo reads `runner.crowdResults()`, online the results message.
- **FAQ and landing page:** the 50 v 50 in plain words from the rework's settings (the pick vote, the cuts, half kept, the 1 v 1,
  move 60's verdict, places, the podium, Spectate); `CROWD_PRE_REWORK` no longer read. The Hidden Boss is met in his room only.
- The breakdown's cut line is now your team's (it took the lowest of both sides).
- Vote cards: unchanged (C1/C4 already draw them from the data).

## Player-visible
All of the above on a 50 v 50; nothing changes in other modes.
