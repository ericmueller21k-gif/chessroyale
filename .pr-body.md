## An open tab survives a deploy

**Cause (Eric, Nov 15: "challenged Wrangler the Pig ... it reloaded the town and put me back in the center"):** a new version's service
worker, on `activate`, deleted every other version's cache and took over the open tabs. The deploy had also taken the old files off the
server, so a tab from before it had its own code nowhere: his challenge's `shops/intro.ts` and `shops/ShopRoom.tsx` chunks failed,
`stale.ts` prevented Vite's `preloadError` (so the imports resolved `undefined`: "reading 'loadIntro'" / "'loadRoom'" of undefined in the
ops errors) and reloaded the page: the town again, at its center.

**Fix:**
1. **The worker keeps each version's files while a tab runs it** (`sw-template.js`, `prune`). It asks each tab for its entry script
   (`install.ts` answers `brc-entry?` on the message's port) and keeps every version whose cache holds one; a tab that doesn't answer in
   1.5 s (frozen in the background, or older than this) keeps the newest `APP_CACHE.keepVersions` (3, settings.ts). The rest go as the
   new worker takes over and on every page load (`brc-loaded`). Files under `/assets/` come from any kept version (hashed names);
   any other file from this version only (so a new page never gets an older copy of `/engine/…`, a sound or `/`). A `text/html`
   answer for a file (the SPA fallback for a missing one) is never cached as that file.
2. **The fallback reload keeps your place** (`stale.ts` `setPlace` / `takePlace`, session storage, read once at the same address): the
   town comes back at your square and facing (a challenge: where you stood as you called it) and opens the room or challenge your tap
   was opening. A room-to-room door or Don Dough's inside challenge comes back as the home's front room. The once-a-minute rule stays.
3. **No crash on a failed chunk:** every `import(...).then((m) => m.x)` goes through `chunk()` (stale.ts): an import Vite gives as
   `undefined` while the page reloads never settles (Town.tsx's 7, the rooms' and intros' art maps, rides, the claw machine, tokens,
   the openings, the Colosseum).
4. **The Safari `[d,f,p,m]` error (7453120ed891):** `RideLayers` (Items.tsx) destructured the seat of idle frame `f` of a ride with
   fewer frames: `useIdleFrame` kept its frame for one render after a riding avatar changed ride. The frame now carries the ride it
   counts, so a new ride starts at frame 0.

Not covered (in the decision): a picture under `/assets/` that the deploy changed and the tab never showed (pictures aren't precached).

**Player-visible:** a tab left open across a deploy opens rooms and challenges as before, no reload. Where a reload still happens (no
service worker yet), you come back where you were with the challenge or room opening, not at the town's center.

**Lane:** targeted (the brief). The PR runs the whole e2e suite anyway: `sw-template.js`, `vite.config.ts`, settings.ts and the server's
`index.ts` / `e2e-sql.ts` are shared foundations. Server change: a test-only route, `GET /api/e2e/sw.js` (e2e-sql.ts: only where
`E2E_SQL` is set, its key in the query), serving the next deploy's worker; the browser fetches a worker's script past Playwright's
routes (tried `context.route`, the SW network-events flag and a browser-level CDP `Fetch`).
