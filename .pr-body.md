## Admins' 🏆 "Win this match" in every boss battle (Nov 20)

Eric: "Put a button that lets me just end the current match as like a victory, so I can test going up each floor and stuff like that."

**Lane:** targeted (admin-only; the server's admin gate holds). `packages/server/src/lobby.ts` is a shared foundation in `e2e/areas.json`, so this PR's e2e run runs the whole suite.

### What changed
- The Hidden Boss's 🏆 (Nov 13) now shows for an **admin in every boss battle**: Boss alone, solo raids, online raids and parties, and **Battle Tower floors (solo and party)**, labeled "Win this match (testing)". Pressed, your next move (the team's) wins as a mate would (the runner's `bossForceWin`), so the result, the tower floor's write and the elevator up all follow. It's greyed once pressed and whenever nothing is left to win. The Hidden Boss keeps "Win this form" and its per-form meaning.
- App: `winFormAllowed(bossOn)` (`boss-brain.ts`), `SoloMatch.winFormShown` (any solo boss battle), `NetMatch.winFormShown` (any online boss battle), `winFormLabel` (`BossDock.tsx`).
- Server: `LobbyCore.winFormTrigger` still needs the Durable Object's `admin` (ADMIN_EMAILS) and a person in the match. It no longer needs the Hidden Boss's room or his id. A player's `winForm` message still does nothing.
- Tower solo floors: `TowerEnv.admin` (from the session's account and ADMIN_EMAILS). An admin's quick win report is taken at once; everyone else is still held to `BATTLE_TOWER.soloWinMinSeconds` (10 s).
- Calls (`docs/decisions/2026-11-20-admin-win-match.md`): online, a battle the 🏆 touched stays a test game (no XP), as the ⚡ already makes it, and a party floor's ends are still written. Solo is unchanged: the 🏆 doesn't mark the game.
- Docs: `docs/areas/bosses.md` ("Win this match"), `docs/areas/battle-tower.md` (a new section, its tests).

### Player-visible (admins only)
- A 🏆 after the 🐟 and ⚡ in every boss battle: on a phone beside the God King (a party's in the title line), on a computer in the title line. Players see nothing new.
- Stills: branch `stills/admin-win-match` (`admin-win-match/win-match-phone.png`, `win-match-desktop.png`).
