# End screens, round 2: every mode tighter; ranked 1 v 1's end page is its game screen; `npm run stills:end-screens`

Targeted lane (shared component: `EndScreen.tsx` runs the whole e2e suite). Decision: `docs/decisions/2026-11-25-end-screens-2.md`. Stills: `stills/end-screens-2`. `end-screens.png` is the sheet of every case at every size; the other files are its separate pictures, each named by case and screen size.

## The cause (and its fix)
End screens were built and checked one mode and one size at a time, so the gaps and duplicates only showed up on Eric's devices. `npm run stills:end-screens` (in `scripts/screen-stills/`) plays every case for real, at several sizes, and puts them all in one labeled sheet:
- **Cases:** Boss alone won (with XP and quests), lost and resigned; the 50 v 50 resigned; Squads resigned; a 1 v 1 placement game lost and won, a 1 v 1 won with XP, and a draw.
- **Sizes:** phone 390×844 (both the screen and the whole page), laptop 1536×730 and desktop 1920×960. The 1 v 1 also runs at 375×667 and at Eric's 1735×1035 window.

Look at the whole sheet before shipping any change to an end screen.

## Every end screen (`EndRewards`)
- **Quests:** when none moved, one line: "QUESTS  No quests this game." Rows are kept only while XP is coming or once it has counted, so the buttons don't move during the sequence.
- **Numbers:** "XP EARNED" and its number are a size bigger.
- **Placement card:** no RP slot over a placement or top-tier card. The card says "Placement games · 1 of 5 placement games".
- **Boss battle:** the boss is drawn at 2.5× (his attack too) and your pawn at 2×, with 24 px less empty room above them.

## Ranked 1 v 1's end page
- **On a computer (every size):** the game's own screen with its right column swapped. The board stays where it was, at the same size, and steps through the moves with ◀ ▶ in your strip. The right column is the play panel's width and lines up with the board's strips top and bottom. It holds:
  - the result;
  - the rewards and quests, then your coins;
  - Play again and Home, stacked at the column's full width;
  - the chat filling the rest, with post-game quick lines: GG, Well played, Thanks!, Rematch?.
- **On a phone:** one tight column with the buttons stacked. The chat has a message area, and the move by move sits under it with room for Safari's bottom bar.
- **Chat height:** a short window gets a shorter chat (about 60 px of messages on laptop 1280×600) rather than a page that scrolls.

## Player-visible changes
- Every end screen: the quests line, bigger XP number, bigger boss and pawn, the placement card.
- The 1 v 1 end page on computer and phone.
- The quick lines on the 1 v 1 end page.
