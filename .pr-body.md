## Moon's Ride: the star drawn once, behind the pawn (no more lines on the seat)

Eric, Nov 17 after #768: "Still weird lines on the star, how hard is this to fix Jesus".

**Cause:** the seat was a second, masked copy of the star drawn in front of the pawn. Every edge of that copy (its outline, rim light,
shading, the mask's edge) landed on the pawn and its items as a line. Four passes moved the mask; each left another seam.

**Fix (structure, not art):**
- The star, its seat's lip and the sparkler fizz are drawn once, **behind** the pawn. The ride's front layer draws nothing.
- New optional `RiderPose.hide` (kit.ts): a shape on the ride's square, per frame, where the rider (pawn and items, one group) is hidden.
  `RideLayers` (Items.tsx: home, profiles, locker) and the town's compositor (rides.ts) clip the rider by it in place of the straight `cut`
  (which still places the pawn). Other rides are unchanged.
- Its edge is the lip's own line (drawn behind), so the pawn looks sunk into the seat: one outline, no copy, no seams. All three views,
  every frame; the move's tilt and bob are followed.
- Deleted: the front star copy, its mask (`frontClipD`), `LipEnds`, the front views' copies.

**Call (decision file):** the brief said "outside the dip, don't clip", but at the ride's 0.75 size the pawn's base and most skins (suit,
coat, chimney, ninja gi) are wider than the dip below the lip (measured: x 10 to 83 vs the dip's 22.5 to 71.5); unclipped, the pawn's
base and a coat's hem stood on the star beside the seat. So beside the dip the hidden shape's edge is the fourth pass's curved shoulder,
now only a clip: a weapon hanging low beside the pawn (katana, tommy gun) ends there, as it did before. The fizz moved behind (its
sparks crossed the parrot's tail).

**Player-visible:** no star line, band or edge on the pawn or any item, in any frame or view. The look is otherwise the same.

**Checked:** Eric's look (suit, crown, parrot) through the town's own compositor, all 60 frames of the three views, at phone size and 4x;
the home/profile pawn (SVG) at 4x; katana, tommy gun, parrot, captain's coat. Stills: branch `stills/star-seat-one-layer`
(`star-seat-town-before-after.png`, `star-seat-avatar-4x-before-after.png`, `star-seat-looks-*.png`, `star-seat-every-frame-after.png`).
