## Birdie's Station inside: longer crossing arms, and 50 birds at the bar

Eric, Nov 26: the crossing arms "much longer ... up like high towards the ceiling"; "like 50 birds at the bar ... nibbling on bird feed ... the bartender keeps scooping up more bird feed ... if you run up to the bar, then they all fly away, and then they don't come back for a little bit".

**What changed**
- **Arms:** the art's barrier arm is 130 px instead of 42, with the same arm and stripes (`birdie-lobby-art.ts` `ARM_LEN`). Each angle's picture is now cut to its drawn pixels, so the longer arm doesn't draw a mostly empty 146 × 146 square every frame.
- **Birds** (`shops/birdie-birds.ts`, the room's `RoomFx` scene, wired in `Town.tsx roomFxOf`; every number is in `SHOP_FEEL.birds`):
  - One sheet, baked as the room loads and kept for the page (162 × 126): one bird in 9 poses (idle, look, 2 pecks, crouch, hop, 3 wingbeats), in 4 colors (2 mourning doves, 2 sparrows), facing both ways, plus 3 seed-pile sizes.
  - Each bird has a fixed, seeded loop made once (`poseAt` is pure): it sits and pecks in bouts, takes a small hop and back, flies up into the ceiling toward its side of the room, then flies back in. Stool birds hop up onto the bar first.
  - The bartender's existing 5.1 s scoop is the clock: each pour fills the next of the run's 4 seed piles, which empties over 16 s. A bird pecks while its pile has seed.
  - When you come within 2.2 squares of the bar, every bird flies off. None return until 25 s after you leave, and then they come back over 15 s.
  - The birds draw in one pass, as one figure (after the front stools, before the gates and you).
- The art's 5 static stool birds are left out of the room, because they would keep sitting while the rest of the flock flew off.

**What players see:** much taller crossing arms that lower right across the battle spot. About 40 of the 50 birds are on the bar, the stools and the floor at any moment, pecking, hopping, and a few always flying in or out. The bartender's piles fill and empty. Walking up to the bar sends the whole flock up and away.

- **The cost of 50, said plainly:** `__shopBench` (a resting draw, run synchronously so the canvas is rasterized on the main thread) goes from 0.2 to about 2.2 ms at 1x, and from about 1 to about 10 ms at 4x.
  - I split that cost apart. The flock's own code is 0.013 ms of step and 0.24 ms of draw calls. Taking the birds' scene out brings the bench back to 0.2 ms, with the longer arms kept.
  - The rest is rasterizing 50 scaled sprites at a 3x pixel ratio in software: about 1.1 ms per 50 sprites in a bare page, whether they come from one sheet or from separate canvases.
  - In the real frame loop, the room's frame times above are unchanged within run-to-run spread, and phones rasterize Canvas 2D on the GPU.
- **Proposal:** keep 50. If Eric's `?report` inside the room on his phone shows frames over budget, set `SHOP_FEEL.birds.count` to 30, which roughly halves the birds' raster cost. That is one number, with no other change.
