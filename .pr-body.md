## The town's piano solos: sometimes early, always within 3 minutes

Eric (Nov 16): "the piano solos are not firing in the town … fires the solos sometimes in the first minute 30 or sometimes later, not every
time early maybe like 33% of the time, but it should always play a solo before 5 minutes or so of looping". Cause: 3 passes before the
first solo (8:56), counted again each time the town's music restarts; the "3 keys" were the theme's own closing figure.

**What changed** (all numbers in `TOWN_MUSIC`, `packages/core/src/settings.ts`):
- Each start of the town's music rolls once. With `earlyChance` 0.33 the theme starts partway in, on a bar line (`earlyEntries`: bar 77 or
  bar 61, at random), fading in over `earlyFadeS` (1 s), and its first solo comes at 0:37 or 1:07; it is decoded at once.
- Otherwise from its top, the first solo after `firstPasses` 1 pass (2:59). Always within 3 minutes.
- After each solo, 1 or 2 passes at random (`passesAfterSolo`), so it isn't clockwork. (`passesPerSolo` is gone.)
- An early solo waiting on its decode, or on another solo being let go, now looks again every second (before, it had no timer left).
- `?townsolo` (a test switch, the usual gate: admins and test servers): every start is the early one at bar 77, its solo 37 s in
  (log `music:town:entry:141.395349`, then `music:town:solo:a`). Wired in Town.tsx.

**Player-visible:** the first piano solo within 3 minutes of the town's music starting (a third of the time within about a minute), then
one every 1 or 2 passes (about every 3.6 to 6.6 min).

**Lane:** targeted (music timing). Checks: typecheck; `packages/app/test/music.test.ts` (79 pass); `e2e/town-street.spec.ts` "the town's
first frame doesn't wait for the music" run locally twice, with the early roll forced and with it off (both pass).

**Changed checks (old → new):**
- music.test.ts "the solos by their turn …": `passesPerSolo` is 3 → `firstPasses` is 1 and `passesAfterSolo` is [1, 2].
- "after 3 passes the solo starts …" → "after 1 pass …" (solo at t0 + L, was t0 + 3L).
- "the solos take turns, a, b, a, each after 3 more passes" → first after 1 pass, then 1 or 2 as rolled (rolls pinned).
- "a solo not decoded in time", "a decode that lands late/in time", "stopped mid-solo …", "stopped as a solo was due …", "the town
  closes …", "no solo decoded until its turn nears", "once a solo has rung out …", "never more than the theme and one solo": the same
  checks at the new times (1 pass first, 2 after a solo; `Math.random` pinned to 0.99 in the block).
- New: "when the first solo comes (Nov 16)": the roll (chance, bar lines, ~1/3 over 3000 seeded rolls), the first solo's moment (0:37,
  1:07, 2:59; all under 3 min and inside the decode window), the early start (offset, fade-in, decoded at once, on the clock), its decode
  landing late (one more pass) and in time, and the `?townsolo` switch.
- e2e/town-street.spec.ts: "no solo decoded" → no solo decoded, or, when the start was early (`music:town:entry:` in the log), only the
  first solo (a) decoded.
