# End screens, round 2: every mode tighter; ranked 1 v 1's end page is its game screen; `npm run stills:end-screens`

Targeted lane (shared component: `EndScreen.tsx` runs the whole e2e suite). Decision: `docs/decisions/2026-11-25-end-screens-2.md`. Stills: `stills/end-screens-2`. `end-screens.png` is the sheet of every case at every size; the other files are its separate pictures, each named by case and screen size.

## The cause (and its fix)
End screens were built and checked one mode and one size at a time, so the gaps and duplicates only showed up on Eric's devices. `npm run stills:end-screens` (in `scripts/screen-stills/`) plays every case for real, at several sizes, and puts them all in one labeled sheet:
- **Cases:** Boss alone won (with XP and quests), lost and resigned; the 50 v 50 resigned; Squads resigned; a 1 v 1 placement game lost and won, a 1 v 1 won with XP, and a draw.
- **Sizes:** phone 390×844 (both the screen and the whole page), laptop 1536×730 and desktop 1920×960. The 1 v 1 also runs at 375×667 and at Eric's 1735×1035 window.

Look at the whole sheet before shipping any change to an end screen.

## Eric's spacing rule (round 2b)
- **One gap everywhere:** every end screen has one gap between its containers (12 px, 6 px inside a group), nothing stretched, and a short stack centered as one block.
- **No reserved room:** no fixed boxes hold blank lines, and the boss's box starts at his head instead of his frame's empty top.
- **Buttons:** stacked on every end screen.
- **Guard:** `endGaps` (e2e/helpers.ts) on the 1 v 1 end page (phone 390×844 and all four real screens) and on a boss battle resigned (phone 390×844 and laptop 1536×730); a gap off by more than 2 px or anything stretched fails.
- **Fixed a bug, the "XP EARNED +0" Eric saw:**
  - **What happened:** a tap before the XP arrived wrote "+0", and the "No XP" line that came next reused that element and kept it.
  - **Fix and guard:** the line is keyed now. The guard fails on the old build and passes on the fix.
- **1 v 1 end page chat:** small (about 4 lines of messages) under the buttons, on computer and phone.
- **Merged main** (#941): the only conflict was in the imports of `ranked-1v1.spec.ts`; both sides kept.

## Round 2d (Big Boy's picture over the XP bar)
- **Cause:** the end screen's box runs from his head to his feet, but his sprite element is his whole frame, which keeps rows under his feet (his shadow, a jump's landing). Those rows hang under the box, and the only room under the row was the rewards' 12 px gap. Big Boy's frame has 6 rows under his feet: 13.5 px at 2.25, so it ended 1.5 px into the XP bar. Ginger's 5 rows (11.25 px) fit, which is why only he failed of the three. It wasn't rounding or a stance offset.
- **Every boss with art, rows under the feet → px at 2.25:** Boingo, Jefferson, Sawyer, Wrangler, Chub & Chirp, Smudgy, Don Dough, Hidden Boss, Birdie 4 → 9. Ginger, Dash 5 → 11.25. Big Boy, Hollow, Gardener, Tiger, Folio, Chandy 6 → 13.5. Alfil, Moo, Timber 8 → 18. So 9 bosses crossed the bar. Some of them draw there too: Timber's defeat reaches 13.5 px under his feet, Moo's entrance 15.75.
- **Fix:** `ResultDuo` sets `--rd-below` (his frame's rows under his feet) on the row. On the end screen the row keeps what the gap doesn't cover under it: `max(0, rows × 2.25 − gap)`. `--rd-k` moved from `.rd-boss` to the row so that rule can use it (same values, the Recluse still 1.25). His feet stay on your pawn's base line. Bosses whose rows fit the gap (9 of them) don't move. The others' XP bar sits 1.5 to 6 px lower.
- **Checks changed:** none.
- **Specs run locally:** `boss-review.spec.ts` and `boss-result.spec.ts`, phone and desktop: 9 passed, 1 skipped by its project. Also the typecheck.
