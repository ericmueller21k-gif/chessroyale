# Battle Tower: pixel hearts, "lives left" on the clerk's card, hearts beside the board (Nov 21)

Light lane (numbers, copy and layout only). Eric, Nov 21: the hearts become drawn pixel hearts, the clerk's card says the lives left,
and in the match the hearts sit by the board instead of in the strip.

## What changed
- `components/TowerHearts.tsx`: one pixel heart on a 9 x 8 grid (`<rect>`s, `crispEdges`), drawn from Eric's map. Full: dark red border,
  red, highlight. A spent heart is hollow (border greyed at 0.6, its slot a faint shape via `--tw-slot`). Always two slots.
- `tower.css`: the pixel colors and the hollow state; `.tw-board-lives` (computer), `.tw-strip-lives` (phone).
- `town/TowerDesk.tsx`: the clerk's card's line is "2 lives left" / "1 life left" / "No lives left" (was the floors-lost recap or
  "No floors lost"). Before a run, the line is unchanged ("Two lives a climb").
- `screens/TowerBar.tsx`, `screens/Hud.tsx`, `screens/Boss.tsx`, `screens/Play.tsx`: the strip keeps "Battle Tower · Floor N" centered
  and no longer holds the hearts. On a computer (the board's layout, MOUSE_LAYOUT) the hearts are drawn over the board's wrap in its
  bottom rank (rank 1), just outside the right edge, stacked one above the other. On a phone they sit at the strip's right end.

## Player-visible
- Hearts are pixel art; a spent one is hollow, never hidden.
- Clerk's card: "2 lives left" (was "Two lives a climb" with a run's "No floors lost" beside the hearts).
- Computer: hearts beside the board, at rank 1, between the board and your pawn.
- Phone: hearts at the right end of "Battle Tower · Floor N".
