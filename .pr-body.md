**The stall skit** (once a battle, solo and online, groups too):
- On your move, at **20 s** of your clock (a bank: Boss alone, a party) or **50%** of the move clock (a raid / group battle: 10 s of 20), he **slams his clock for 5 s** (new `slam` loop, a hit and shake each slam) with a hurry-up line.
- At **30 s** / **75%**, still nobody done: **his fake second move** lands on the board (a seeded quiet move of his), he brags (`fakeSmug`), then "Nahh, jk bro" (`jkLaugh`, his new laugh sound) and it slides back. The board takes no move during the hold.
- **The clock stops for both beats, for everyone** (as for the King's strike: deadlines and bots moved back, the pauses left out of the time charged). Nothing in the position or clocks changes.
- Armed until the fake move comes; never with another moment of his on that move, a scramble, his Rush, the Last Stand's re-pick, in check, or after the King's strike that move (the strike is refused during a beat). Online the server times it (`stall` timer/message); a pick reaching it during the hold counts.

**The clock tap** (once a battle, screen only): on a seeded turn 3–9 (the first with nothing else of his), ~1 s into your move he zips down beside your time under the board, lies on his side tapping his own clock for 3 s (a tick each tap), then zips off; his spot is empty meanwhile; your clock keeps running. Never over the squares or the digits (phone and computer).
