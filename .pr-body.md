No charge-up (Eric): no swell under the slowdown, no rising charge under the shake. All made in code; the pieces are made ahead in the sound worker as a crate's page opens and as Open is tapped, and laid on the audio clock from `clickTimes` (`timeline.ts` `cues`, `sound.ts` `playCrateCues`). The strip waits under "Opening…" up to 1.5 s for its first pieces, so none is made on its frames. Everything moves by transform and opacity, and the card's visibility runs as its own animation so its fade stays on the compositor. Reduced motion drops the blur, shake and jolt, fades the box in and fades the beam up.
