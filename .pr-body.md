# The Stranger's storm, redrawn: a drawn fire-and-ice ring and a rift in the board (GBA style)

Eric, Nov 27, on the particle ring: "Does this look like... confetti"; "like a GIF basically"; then "a bit more Game Boy Advance
style, but still keep the details"; on the crack: "like somebody ripped open the board". Boss lane.

## What changed for players
- **The ring**: fire up the left of the board and across the top (cores, tongues, embers), frost wind, crystal shards, snow and
  icicles down the right and along the bottom, both turning the same way and meeting top and bottom. On a fire turn the fire leads
  (whole and doubled, the ice fainter); on an ice turn the ice does. Through the charge both double and play twice as fast.
- **The rift**: at the fire sword's slam the board tears open from the impact to the lower left (lava, flames, chunks of board
  bursting up); at the ice sword's, to the upper right (ice, crystals erupting). It stays under the pieces through the storm's three
  turns and heals a stage each turn, gone with the storm. It replaces the opening's line cracks; the swords' ripples stay.
- **See-through and a soft dim**: the storm is 88% opaque over what it overlaps (Moves, his figure, the strip still show), with a
  22% dim round the board. Along the bottom it stays low, so the line under the board and the clock read.
- No new text. No rule, timing or protocol change (STORM_SCENE and the storm's turns are as they were).

## How
- Drawn offline (`scripts/storm-art/`: `render.py` the ring, `rift.py` the rift and its opening, `pack.py`) into two sheets:
  `stranger-storm.webp` 378 KB (36 frames) and `stranger-rift.webp` 40 KB (3 stages x 4 frames + 19 opening frames), loaded as his
  battle shows (prewarmStranger), not at boot.
- At play time `StormRing` blits 2 to 6 cells a frame on one 250 px canvas (no particles); `StormRift` (a boss under-layer) redraws
  one 202 px canvas only when its frame changes; the dim is one still CSS shadow.
