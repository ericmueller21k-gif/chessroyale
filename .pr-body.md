# Tower shared floors: the split bar as an accordion

Targeted lane (a shared screen component and its animation). Brief: `notes/briefs/tower-accordion-bar.md`, "Build".

## What changed
- `screens/TowerBar.tsx`, `tower.css`: the approved mockup made shippable. Each boss's card is the lone bar's own markup (his bar prop
  included) laid out once at the open width; each part is a window onto its left side. The open part (the boss to move, or one tapped
  open until the turn changes or it's tapped again) gets 75%, the others share the rest; the windows slide in 300 ms (instant with reduced motion).
- A folded boss's speech bubble (phone) now draws over the bar beside his window (`.tw-say`), pointing at him, never cut by the window;
  his part doesn't open for it (Eric's pick).
- Fixes found while building: the open part's class was `tw-open`, which the party list already uses (`margin-bottom: 6px`), so it's now
  `tw-part-open`. Babur's pump lost its grey "balloons off" look inside the card, so the rule now covers the card. Enter/Space open a part too.
- `components/BossPowers.tsx` (`cardProp`) and `screens/Boss.tsx` (`BossNext` pulled out of the lone bar, which is otherwise unchanged): from the mockup.
- `speech.tsx`: the test-only `sayBoss(text, who?)` hook can name the boss who speaks.
- Docs: `docs/decisions/2026-11-21-tower-accordion-bar.md` (records this bar as Eric's named exception to "nothing shifts"),
  `docs/areas/battle-tower.md`.

## What players see
On a floor of 2 or 3 bosses, the boss to move gets a wide card (75%). The others show a narrow slice of their card's left side (the
figure). When the turn changes, the cards slide. Tapping a narrow card opens it until the turn changes or it's tapped again. The bar's
height and the board don't move. The floor count never shows.
