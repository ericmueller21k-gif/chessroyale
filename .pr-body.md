## Phone casters face the board: carrots and hoops leave the drawn paw

Follows #774 (Eric, Nov 17: "have all the bosses facing the right way"): its one leftover place, a phone's cast at the board's
top-left corner (`.pm-caster`).

**What changed**
- The corner cast turns by the same rule as everywhere else: `PLACE_FACING.caster` (the board's way) in kits.ts, mirrored about
  its feet (`.pm-mirror`), so the feet stay on the corner. Nine turn: Sawyer, Wrangler (his warning), Alfil, Chub & Chirp, Moo,
  Puddles, Babur, Folio, Timber. Front- and right-facing bosses are unchanged; Dash casts in place.
- One shared helper for projectiles from a hand: `casterPoint` (PowerParts.tsx), whose mirror is `figurePoint`'s (`drawnX`,
  BossCharacter.tsx). Only two casts throw from a hand: Chub's carrots (paw 15.6, 23.5, as before) and Babur's hoops (his paw
  is now a frame pixel, 24, 70 at `throw1`, instead of a board point). The rest start elsewhere (Moo's beams from above,
  Puddles' sweep across the board, Folio's ink, Alfil's glow, Sawyer's cut, Timber's teeth): unchanged.
- Room: every turned cast's mirrored drawing (each frame of its kit's animations, measured) reaches no further left than the
  unturned one did, within a point of the board (Wrangler's warning -10.6% against -11.3%).

**Player-visible:** on a phone those nine bosses face the board while casting at its corner; the carrot and hoop fly from the
paw that's drawn (they used to leave from behind them). Computers unchanged (they cast in place).
