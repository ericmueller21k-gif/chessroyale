## XP: a turn you called the God King to play counts as one of your moves

Eric, Nov 21: a Battle Tower floor won by mate on move 12, with God King calls, said "No XP: the game ended before move 10." A call is kept as an abstained turn (`move: null`), and both counts for XP (the device's for solo games, the match room's online) counted picks only, so 12 turns with 3 calls came out as 9.

### What changed
- **core `xp.ts`:** `ownMoves` / `tookTurn`: your own moves are the turns you took (a pick, a God King call, a turn Hollow's dark ended at your last wrong try), never a missed turn.
- **app:** `MoveRecord` keeps `abstained` (from `moveRecordFrom`) and `darkOut` (solo.ts, the dark's 5th wrong try). App.tsx sends `ownMoves(solo.moves)` to `/api/results` (Boss alone, solo raids, tower floors; a kept floor keeps the records, so a rejoin counts the same). The town challenge's "in N moves" uses it too.
- **server `lobby.ts` `lock`:** online, a call and the dark's end count as a pick does (`r.picked`, `pickedAt`). That one count feeds XP's minimum, the daily reward's 15, boss tokens' 10 and the Welcome Crate (raids, Boss Battle and tower parties).
- Unchanged: the 10-move rule and the trust rules (solo: the device's word can only lower XP; online: the room's own count).
- Calls (each in `docs/decisions/2026-11-21-xp-king-turns.md`): a call counts whether or not the King came. Hollow's dark ending your turn counts (it's still scored as a miss). Online XP time runs to your last turn taken. The Last Stand, Folio's Revision and the engine move were already counted. A timeout and Boingo's funhouse still don't count. The tower's free leave already counted the King's move (`crowdMoves`).

### Player-visible
- A game with God King calls now earns XP from your 10th turn, calls included (before, calls didn't count). The same goes for the daily reward, boss tokens and the Welcome Crate.
- FAQ, XP section: "Every turn you take is one of your moves, a turn you call the God King to play included; a turn you let run out isn't."
