## Flaky: Alfil's Slam after a queen take (the trigger pulled into a Slam already on its way)

Main's full run on f75947d (run 44832889384) went red on `e2e/alfil.spec.ts:247` (phone), which passed only on its retry:
"the triggered Slam landed within the time cap: its own banner, once · Expected: 2 · Received: 1".

### Cause (the test, not the game)
The failing attempt's log (shard 12) shows the banners **TUSKS! | TUSKS! | RAGE! | MAMMOTH SLAM! | QUEEN DOWN!**, then the battle
ran on to a draw (the error context shows "A draw with Alfil"). The test offered its queen on the turn his meter warned (RAGE!). His
meter's own Slam came at the start of his turn, and his move then took the queen. So the test pulled its trigger while that Slam was
under way, and the trigger is refused then: `triggerUltimate` never overlaps an ultimate. The test never checked whether the trigger
took, so it waited for a Slam that wasn't coming.

Reproduced locally (phone project, the container's Chromium) by holding the queen back until the first RAGE!: the trigger was refused
every time (`ultAt` set, `ultNext` false, 3 pieces up). Those runs **passed falsely**: the meter later brought another Slam, and the
test counted it as the trigger's. On CI the battle ended first, so the run went red. The game behaved as designed. Today's timing
merges (#856 Sawyer, #855 end screen) played no part.

### Fix
In that test only:
- It offers the queen only when no Slam is warned or under way (`powers.warned`, `powers.ultAt`). While one is, it plays a good move
  that leaves the queen unattacked (`goodMove(page, queenAttacked)`, falling back to `goodMove(page)` when no such move exists).
- When it pulls the trigger, it now states that the trigger took: `expect(powers.ultNext, "the trigger took as he took your queen").toBe(true)`.
  This makes the Slam it waits for the trigger's own, so a later meter Slam can't pass for it.

### Checks changed (old → new)
- `alfil.spec.ts` "Slam right after a queen is taken": the banner counts are unchanged. Added: "the trigger took as he took your queen"
  (new). Nothing loosened, skipped or removed.

### What I ran
- `npm run typecheck`: passes.
- The test on the phone project: 12 of 12 unforced (`--repeat-each 12`). Forced (queen held until RAGE!, temporary code): before the
  fix the trigger was refused every time; after it, 8 of 8 passed the way the test means (RAGE! | MAMMOTH SLAM! | QUEEN DOWN! |
  MAMMOTH SLAM!, trigger taken). In an earlier forced batch, 1 of 8 runs failed on the new check: he took the queen during the meter's
  Slam even though it wasn't attacked after the test's move (a lifted piece may have opened a line). Another forced run lost the battle
  before the queen was ever offered, which forcing causes by making the game longer. Both come from the forcing, which keeps a Slam
  pending for many turns; unforced runs rarely have one pending at all. If that case happens on CI now, it fails on the trigger check
  with a clear message instead of passing falsely.

Lane: targeted (one e2e spec, test-only). Lessons: the story at the end of `docs/history/lessons.md`, its rule in `.claude/LESSONS.md`.
