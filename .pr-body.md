### What changed
- **Server:** `TownHere.beat` now says whether a pawn has just come in (new, past its limit, or from another room). The live hub hands
  that to the chat room of the same name (`TownChat.arrive`), which posts **"Ann has entered the town."** (`sys: "enter"`, no account
  behind it). One line per visit. Coming back within **2 minutes** of your last beat (a reload, a trip home or to a game) posts nothing
  (`TOWN_CHAT.enterRejoinMs`). Arrivals within **6 s** of an "entered" line are held and go out as one line: "Ann and Bo entered the
  town.", "Ann, Bo and Cy entered the town.", "Ann, Bo and 3 others entered the town." (`enterBurstMs`). So a busy town gets at most one
  of these lines every 6 s, and the first arrival in a quiet town shows at once. The lines follow the room's rules (5 minutes, a
  newcomer gets the last 12). A device the age gate marked too young now reads these lines and nothing else (`enterOnly` in `/api/live`;
  before, it read nothing).
- **App:** the "entered" lines are a new log kind, `arrival`: the system lines' gray, upright (the line only you see stays italic), no
  name, no menu, so they can't be reported or muted, and a tap on one opens typing. Guests and players with the chat off still get them.
- **The hint:** while the box shows and you aren't typing, its foot strip shows "Tap here to type" (phone) / "Click here to type"
  (computer). It's thin (300) and faint gray (the system gray at 0.72, day and night), the same size as the messages, 8 px in so it
  lines up with the timestamps' "[". It hides the moment typing opens, comes back when typing closes, and fades and goes with the box
  (New messages' fade, Off). It's its own element, not a placeholder: the field still has no placeholder. Nothing moves or resizes.
