## Inside rooms: tap a door from anywhere, its card, then run to it

Eric, Nov 20: "you should be able to tap on the door, like no matter where you're standing, and then hit go in, and then it would just run you to the door." Then: "I'm referring to the rooms on the inside, not the outside of the building."

### What changed
- Inside every room, a tap on a door's picture (the way out, and every door to another room: the Battle Tower lobby's elevator, the floor room's elevator and way down, Rolling Dough's back door, the walk-up's stairs) opens that door's card at once, over the door, wherever you stand. A walk you're on stops at the end of its step.
- The card's button (Go out, Go up, Go in, Down to the lobby, Up to floor N…) runs you to the nearest square just inside the door at your usual pace (round the room, Jefferson's crew and Ginger's elves as any walk), then does its action. Already at the door: the action at once, as before.
- The picture counts where it's drawn, plus 10 px (`SHOP_FEEL.doorPad`); a picture smaller than a finger, its whole 44 px box (`doorMinPx`). Not the Shopkeeper over it, and not the floor just inside a door: that still walks you there and the card opens as you arrive (unchanged). A card open (the clerk's, say): a tap on a door's picture swaps to that door's card instead of only closing it.
- A tap elsewhere or an arrow key on the way cancels the run; nothing happens at the door.
- The street's buildings are unchanged.
- Code: `shops/room.ts` (`doorOf`, `atDoor`, `doorGoal`, `onDoorPic`, `opaqueNear`, pure), `shops/ShopRoom.tsx` (door pictures' boxes at tap time, `aimDoor`, `ShopCtl.go`, the card over the door; the walk-off shared as `setOff`), `Town.tsx` (the tower's own door buttons go through `go`), `feel.ts` (two numbers). Nothing new per frame except the open door card's place (one box) while it's open.

### Player-visible
- Tap the elevator (or any door inside) from across the room: its card pops up over it; Go up and your pawn runs there and rides up.
