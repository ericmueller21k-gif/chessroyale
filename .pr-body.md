## What changed

Auto chess players are bigger (Eric, Nov 9, from his phone). Each pawn is as tall as a bench tile (never under 44 px). Each player block (pawn, name, level, HP and XP) now fills its bench row's height, with bigger text and taller, wider bars. The top block matches the bottom one exactly.

The board, benches, action box, header and gear keep their size and place. The board's size still comes from `--pl-h`, which is unchanged.
