# Device trust: one seat per account, checked host boards, judges never one account

## What changed
- **One seat per account per lobby** (main lobby, Squads; a guest's own account too): a second tab or device takes over
  that seat as a reconnect does. Squads now refuses a seat token from another account (`SQUADS_OTHER_ACCOUNT`), as the
  main lobby does (a guest who signs in mid-lobby too; production refuses guests anyway); a refused Squads hello no
  longer marks the real seat's player gone.
- **Judges:** two judges of a job are never one account (`drawJudges` by owner). Claimed speed capped at
  `JUDGES.maxNps` 3M (was 50M), `speedWeight` 2 (was 3), and the second judge from the first's class (computer with
  computer), so a pair finishes together: at a lobby of 20 computers and 80 phones, jobs whose answer is used alone go
  from about 48% (linear) to 0; a two-account attacker gets both judges of a board 1.9% of the time
  (`2026-11-10-judge-pairing.md`). A judge whose partner is past the grace is still used alone, spot-checked 25%.
- **Host boards** are checked for shape (`hostBoardOk`: legal best move, chances 0 to 1, every pick and bot scored). A
  board that fails scores every pick the same (the "nobody could score it" rule); a left-out pick no longer "counts as
  the best". The values of a board that passes are still the host's word (ROADMAP). The engine server's re-check skips
  failed boards.
- **Squads' bot work:** never two devices of one party, except a second party device as a checker when no outside
  device is free (agreement counts as one answer, disagreement goes to the server). No answers shared between lobbies
  for now (`TOP_CACHE` stays empty): the server's verdict only scores the devices' moves (ROADMAP: its own top moves).
- **Message fields:** `bossMove.move` must be a string (≤ 128); `botPlan` keeps only this round's picking bots with
  allowed moves; `crossCheck` logs the first 20, cut to 200 characters; the lobby's Durable Object drops a message that
  isn't an object with a type, or `scores` without a list of board objects (`atTheDoor`).

Player-visible: none for honest players (a second tab now joins your own seat instead of a new one). No new text but
the Squads refusal "That seat belongs to another account." (same as the main lobby's).
