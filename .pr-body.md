Boss art made on first use, not on every page load

**What changed.** Every page load built all the bosses' art (poses and parts) before `load`. Now an animation is made the first time
it's read (`lazyAnim` in `characters/sprite.ts`, put around about 720 of them, mostly by codemods), a part the first time it's drawn
(`lazyParts`), derived figures map their frames lazily (`mapFrames`), and timings read from animations are computed when used.
Pig, Tiger, Gardener and Moo share rounded-pose caches (Moo also a counter) across frames, so `lazyAnimsTogether` makes their
animations in file order, up to the one read. The cost moves to quiet moments, in ~4 ms slices with 50 ms gaps (`prewarmAnims`):
the battle warms its boss and his power effects as it loads, then runs his own prewarm; home warms the tiles' portraits 1.5 s in;
Boss Battle warms the picked boss.

**Player-visible:** no new text; the art is unchanged. Every frame of all 1,902 characters hashes the same as main's, built in
forward, reverse and three shuffled orders. Pages open much sooner (prod build, iPhone 13, Playwright; medians of 4):
