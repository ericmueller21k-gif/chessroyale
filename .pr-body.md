## Battle Tower on a computer: the floor's title moves to the right column, above the Moves box

Eric, Nov 21: "We really don't want it to say Battle Tower Floor 4 right there ... we need all that space for the boss for the bar at the top ... put it to the right above the moves black container".

Branched from `claude/tower-accordion` (#871); `main` merged in. Targeted lane (layout on the boss screen that changes the board's size math on computers).

**What changed**
- `Hud.tsx`: on a computer, the tower's `.tw-strip-line` between the bar and the board is gone. `TowerStrip` is now the first row of `BossTools` (`.tw-col-title`).
- `tower.css`: on a tower floor the column's tools are two fixed rows: the title (20 px, centered over the Moves box, top level with the bar's top), then the tools in their usual 28 px row. The whole block is 52 px, which fits inside the shortest tower bar's row (a lone floor's 49 px bar plus its 8 px margin).
- `board-size.css`: the `:has(> .tw-strip-line)` room rule is removed, so `--bd-room` goes back from 151 to 123 px on tower floors.

**What players see (computer only; phone unchanged)**
- "Battle Tower · Floor N" sits at the top right, over the tools and the Moves box. The bar sits right above the board again (8 px gap).
- The board is 24 px bigger where the window's height limits it: 1366×768 goes from 616 to 640, 1880×1025 from 872 to 896. At 1280×800 the board is limited by width, so it stays 600.
- The tools sit 3 px lower on a floor of several bosses, and about 10 px lower on a lone floor. The Moves box keeps its size and place.
- No floor total is shown, only the current floor.

**Specs run locally:** `e2e/battle-tower.spec.ts` "a shared floor's moments" (includes the #869 no-scroll check at 1280×800, 1366×768, 1440×900, 1600×900 and 1880×1025) and "the split bar as an accordion", on phone and desktop. All 4 pass. Typecheck is clean.
