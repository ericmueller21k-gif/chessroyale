Town phase 0: a deploy no longer throws the saved town away; the chips' faces drawn once a place

Phase 0 of the town's structure (`DESIGN.md` on `claude/town-architecture`, finding 2 and "small ones"). No pixel of the town changes.

**What changed for players**
- **After an update, Chess Town opens as fast as on any later visit**, unless the update changed the town's art. The saved pictures were
  keyed by the build's commit id, so every deploy painted all 2,250 again. They're now keyed by a fingerprint of the town's art that the
  build takes: esbuild bundles the code the pictures come from with everything it uses (tree-shaken) and hashes it
  (`packages/app/town-art.ts` → `__TOWN_ART__`). Nobody keeps a list; comments, types and settings the pictures don't read don't count.
- **The edge chips:** each place's face is drawn once, on a canvas of its own, and a place keeps the chip it had. Before, a chip's one
  canvas was cleared and redrawn whenever the nearest places changed order.
- The first visit after this ships paints once more (the key's form changed). No new text.
