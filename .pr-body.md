## What

Puddles' watering now grows back the piece he'd lose by passing (Eric, Nov 26: "if he's going to lose like a big piece, then he
should just basically regrow that same piece").

- **Which piece** (`regrowKind`, pure, no engine): after the pass, the knight, bishop, rook or queen of his that the crowd nets the
  most from by the exchange check (`exchangeNet`, over the crowd's legal captures that no plant stops), if that's at least
  `passGuardPawns` (2) pawns of material. Never a pawn. The server names it in the ask (`water.regrow`); with `noAdd` there's none.
- **Whether** (the host's engine): the pass alone is searched (one search, only when something hangs). It regrows when the
  engine's best reply takes a piece of that kind, or when `passLoses` holds.
- **Where:** before the seeds, on the square that leaves his gain nearest 0 (under the band's top). The seeds are then placed on
  top as before, so the regrowth is counted once and his total stays near `waterTarget`.
- **The pass guard:** unchanged. It waits only if the watering still loses real material, e.g. under the full-set rule (all eight
  pawns and two knights: no third knight), up to `ultWaitMax`.
- **Online:** the answer's token is `g` plus the piece letter (e.g. `gnc4`). `waterCheck` accepts at most one, only of the named
  kind, under every hard guard. A host can't invent one.
- **Unchanged** when nothing hangs (the same code path).

Decision note: `docs/decisions/2026-11-26-puddles-regrow.md`. **One call differs from the brief:** the brief named `passLoses` as
the trigger. With real Stockfish (20,000 nodes), his knight lost to a pawn measured −1.8 when he was already a pawn down. That's
under the guard's 2, so it would never regrow. Hence "the engine's line takes it, or `passLoses`".

## Player-visible

- When a piece of his is hanging as he waters, a piece of the same kind buds for him among the outcomes: one more than his seeds,
  played like a grown piece, with his cheer.
- The watering then comes at once, where it used to wait up to 3 turns.
- FAQ, the Watering line: one new sentence on the regrowth; the waiting sentence now says "would still cost him".
