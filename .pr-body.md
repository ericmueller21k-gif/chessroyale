# Music a little under the game's sounds, and never two copies of one theme

Light lane for the level (one number); **targeted** for the doubled-music fix in `music.ts` (logic). Specs: `e2e/sound.spec.ts`
(sound and the audio context's return) plus music.ts's unit tests.

## What changed
- `MUSIC.volume` 0.25 → **0.4** (+4.1 dB). Sound effects unchanged.
- `music.ts`: a new copy of a theme cuts one still fading out at its end (a room left, a result, a splash gone: 1.5 s) to
  `MUSIC.handoffMs` (`endPlaying` / `cutEnding`).
- `music.ts`: the context handover (#697) now carries whatever is playing on an older context when the new one's clock runs, so music
  the battle took over from its splash mid-handover goes along too (before, it stayed on the old context and went silent as that
  closed). A newer context made since leaves it to its own handover.

## Player-visible
- The boss music is clearly louder: about 3 dB under a move or the clock tick (it was 7 to 9 under).
- Walking straight back into a room, a rematch, a result into a new challenge's splash, or a called-off splash into the room no longer
  plays the theme twice over itself for up to 1.5 s.

## Measured (Chromium, the speakers' input: every node into the destination recorded; K-weighted, LUFS)
| | before (0.25) | after (0.4) |
|---|---|---|
| clown theme, short-term (3 s) median | -30.2 | **-26.2** |
| clown theme, beats (100 ms windows, 95th pct) | -27.2 | -23.1 |
| clown theme, sample peak | -18.1 dBFS | -14.0 dBFS |

Each sound's hit (its loudest 100 ms; sample peak), unchanged: move -23.2 (-4.7 dBFS), clock tick -23.3 (-8.1), capture -21.0 (-5.7),
Folio's voice -23.0 (-18.8), Big Boy's clack -18.6 (-12.0), menu open -14.1 (-5.6), menu select -5.7 (-4.0).
0.5 (the brief's guess, 0.5 to 0.7) measured -24.2 short-term with beats at -21.1: over a move's hit, and the hottest theme (Hidden Boss
phase 3, 1.7 dB hotter than clown) over a capture's. That breaks "never louder than the game", so 0.4.

## Doubled-music count (looping sources of one theme sounding, 450 ms after each step, i.e. past the 300 ms handover; real Chromium)
| step | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| room entered | 1 | 1 |
| room left, walked back in 0.3 s later | **2** | 1 |
| window blurred 2.5 s, focus, tap (new context) | 1 | 1 |
| splash from the room (carried) | 1 | 1 |
| battle starts from the splash | 1 | 1 |
| blurred 2.5 s in battle, focus, tap | 1 | 1 |
| result, rematch 0.4 s later | **2** | 1 |
| result, new challenge's splash 0.3 s later | **2** | 1 |
| splash called off, room entered 0.3 s later | **2** | 1 |
The #697 handover always stopped the old copy at the handover's end.

Sample rate: the device's (44100 Hz in the test browser); no context in the app sets one, so nothing to change.
