## Flake at its cause: ranked-1v1 "…both ranks move" (CI on #976)

**Cause.** The test released Ann's pawn, then waited from the test process for `.dv-overlay` "Timer: Blitz 3+2". That line
shows for `RANKED_1V1.overlayMs` (1.5 s), starting `voteBothPickedMs` (1 s) after both picks. On #976's busy runner the
test process stalled past that window: the line came and went unseen, the page went on to play (Blitz, correct: the
failure's page snapshot shows "Ann Duel's first move: 15 s" and both clocks at 3:00), and the 8 s wait found no element.
The app was right. The test read a 1.5 s moment at the wrong time.

**Fix (test only).** Ann's page now records two times: when her pawn's release lands (a capture `pointerup` listener) and
when "Timer: Blitz 3+2" first shows (a MutationObserver). The test waits for play, then checks that the line showed and
came less than `voteBothPickedMs + 4 s` after the release. It is the same bound as before, now timed on the page, so a
stalled test process can't move it. The existing `watchFor` "timer seen" check stays as it was. Decision:
`docs/decisions/2026-11-26-ranked-1v1-timer-line-flake.md`.

**Player-visible changes.** None.

**Checks changed (old → new).**
- `e2e/ranked-1v1.spec.ts` "…both ranks move":
  - Old: a live 8 s `toHaveText("Timer: Blitz 3+2")` on `.dv-overlay`, then the time from the test process (`Date.now() - both < voteBothPickedMs + 4000`).
  - New: the line was seen over Ann's board, and the time from her page's release to the line is under `voteBothPickedMs + 4000`.
  - No check removed or loosened.
- **Eric's rule:** this test guards "Tap a vote zone and your pawn goes there" (`docs/rules/eric.md`). That part (Bo taps
  Bullet, and his pawn stands in Bullet, seen by both) is unchanged.

**Proof.** `--repeat-each 15 --project phone -g "both ranks move" --retries 0` in `mcr.microsoft.com/playwright:v1.56.1-noble`, under the shared e2e lock, with a second container running the town specs (`town`, `town-street`, `town-chat`, 2 workers, still running when this run ended) for contention: **15 passed** (2.1 min).

Lane: targeted (one spec, test-only).
