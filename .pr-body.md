## Fix main's red: panel.spec:282, the phone's 50 v 50 scoreboard at three rows

**Lane:** targeted (a shared component's logic: `pickRows`, used by the phone's scoreboard and the boss battle's panel). No CSS change; `claude/crowd-frame` touches neither file.

### Cause
`pickRows` (packages/app/src/pick-rows.ts) adds rows most-important-first and **stopped at the first one that didn't fit**. At 390 × 750 (five vote rows) the scoreboard's body is 137 px every run (measured; the box, `--slots` and the font were the same in passing and failing runs). When you're last on your team, the order is you (#50), the leader (#1), the last safe row (#47), then the first row past the cut (#48). That row adds a second "⋯" gap and the cut line: 4 rows = 18 + 88 + 26.4 + 10 = 142.4 px > 137, so the list ended at three, though your neighbor (#49, no cut line: 132.4 px) fit. It depends on where you rank, which is random, so the test was red in 2 runs out of 20 and green on the retry.

### Fix
A row that doesn't fit is skipped and the next one that fits is shown (one line). The box, its thresholds and the vote box are unchanged; nothing moves. With any 137 px body, a 4th row with no new gap (a neighbor of you or of the leader) always fits.
