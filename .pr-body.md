## Rooms: walking smooth (the back through WebGL), and `?report` counting inside

**What changed**
- **`?report` in rooms:** the pill's "● Recording N s" was set by the town's frame loop alone, so it stood still inside a room (the
  frames were recorded all along). Now the recorder calls it each new second (`FrameRec.onSec`).
- **Walking in a room:** the room's back (ceiling picture, cave rock, the day outside, the floor and its still plans) is drawn by WebGL
  into one opaque picture (`shops/back-gl.ts`, about 300 lines) and copied onto the room's canvas in one `drawImage`. Before, the floor
  was 156 to 360 clipped triangles a frame on the 2D canvas. Pools, figures and darks still blend with it in the same canvas. With no
  WebGL or a lost context, the back is drawn the 2D way as before.
- **`?back=2d`** (anyone's, read once like `?report`): draws the back the 2D way, so Eric can compare both on his phone with the
  same build. The report's device line then says "(rooms 2D)".

**Player-visible:** the same picture. Drawn both ways at one view, tile edges sit about a pixel apart, mostly far off (the 2D way
stretched one triangle over two squares there): 1 to 6% of pixels differ by more than 48, mean difference 2 to 13.

**Fix round: ghosts and dark patches in Rolling Dough (Eric, Nov 11, iPhone, on main)**
- **Where:** on main and on this PR alike. Not #638's kept back and not WebGL: reproduced headless on main, on this PR, and with `?back=2d`.
- **Cause:** the back room's floor tiles were named `bfloor-*` and `rug-*`, but rooms bake only `floor-*` names into the floor (room.ts
  `isFloor`). So all 196 tiles stood up as pieces every frame (130 on screen), and each went see-through when you walked behind it:
  the dark squares. The front room's two door mats did the same. The cave's see-through rule also reaches any room with a floor's shape,
  so at the table's end (the Challenge spot) the whole table and board ghosted.
- **Fix:** the tiles are renamed `floor-back-*`, `floor-rug-*` and `floor-mat-*`. Rolling Dough now lists the only pieces that may go
  see-through (`seeThru`: its doors and near walls), so the table stays solid and you're never hidden at a door. The rug now lies in the
  floor's perspective (it was stepped). The back room draws 23 pieces a frame instead of 130.
- **`?report` starting in a room:** on this PR, pressing Again inside a room and then walking starts it ("Recording 2 s"). On main it
  stays on "walk or turn to start": Eric's "won't turn on when I go in".
- **Before, Eric's phone on main:** rooms 620 frames at a 45 fps median, "Outside our code" 21 ms a frame against ours at about 0.4 ms.
  That's the 2D floor this PR moves to WebGL.
