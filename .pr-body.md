## 50 v 50: the vote results box as a standing skeleton (Eric, Nov 25)

**What changed**
- The 50 v 50's vote box always shows its full cap of rows (`slots`, as before). Rows a round doesn't fill are gray skeleton rows (a soft bar where the move goes and the bar's gray track; light and dark; no shimmer, so nothing redraws each frame). A turn you only watched gets a gray result line in its fixed spot.
- **Computer** (the 206 px slot above the chat): the skeleton rows now sit there between reveals too, on your turn and the other team's (`Play`, `WatchScreen`), and the status line ("Move in. Waiting…", "White is voting…") moves to the slot's foot, where the result line goes. The reveal fills the same rows in place, top down: no box appearing and vanishing.
- **Phone** (the 104 px slot): between reveals the slot still holds the power-up row and status line (there's no room for both), so the box appears at the reveal, now always at its full height, with the unused rows as skeleton instead of empty space.
- 50 v 50 only: Squads and Classic don't use this box.

**Nothing shifts (measured):** computer 1536×730: rows at 96/121/146/171/196/221 px (22 px each) before and after results; the slot is 96–302 px in every phase; the board and chat don't move. Phone 390×844: the box is 438–542 px in every phase (six 13 px rows plus the 20 px line), with the board and scoreboard unchanged.

**Lane:** light/targeted (UI: markup, CSS and a small shared component). Specs affected: `e2e/crowd.spec.ts`, plus `crowd-play.spec.ts` and `desktop-board.spec.ts`, which measure this slot.
