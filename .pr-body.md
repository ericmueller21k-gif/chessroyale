## What changed

The approved crate-opening mockup, built into the game (brief: `notes/briefs/crate-open-build.md`; Eric's picks, Nov 24).

- **Every crate opens the new way:** a crate's page in the Crates tab (in the shop page or the inventory layer), the Welcome Crate, a boss crate's "Open now" in its shop, and Boingo's claw machine. The strip spins 4 s (`CRATE_OPEN.spinMs`, one setting) with a cubic slowdown and a blurred copy that fades as it slows. It stops just past the winning tile's near edge, runs a few pixels over and settles back. The winning box then drops in, shakes and bursts open with a beam in the item's tier color, and the item rises into the reveal card. Legendary and Mythic get more light, a flash and a jolt. Fischer Random shows its banner, then a second strip whose winner's beam comes up without a box.
- **Its own layer (Eric, Nov 24):** on a phone the whole screen, on a computer the middle column (between the side menu and the right column), top to bottom, on the blue-black background. The screen it was opened from stays mounted and unmoved under it, so Done returns you exactly where you were. A short window scales the stage down whole rather than cutting it off.
- **The sound, Eric's mix (Nov 24, ~18:55):** the spin is sound C, the slot reel (a heavier metal tick at each tile edge, the reel's hum, a low swell under the slowdown, a clunk as it locks); the ending is sound A (the box's thud and shake, then the glassy chord that climbs with the tier, A's bigger ending for Legendary and Mythic). All made in code; the pieces are made ahead in the sound worker as a crate's page opens and as Open is tapped, and laid on the audio clock from `clickTimes` (`timeline.ts` `cues`, `sound.ts` `playCrateCues`). The strip waits under "Opening…" up to 1.5 s for its first pieces, so none is made on its frames. The old reel's marimba is gone from crates only (Settings' "Reveal sound" still voices the move reveal).
- **Frame rate:** the Web Animations are set up while "Opening…" waits for the server and start together two frames after the answer. Everything moves by transform and opacity, and the card's visibility runs as its own animation so its fade stays on the compositor. Reduced motion drops the blur, shake and jolt, fades the box in and fades the beam up.
- **Unchanged:** rolls, odds, server calls and what's shown as won.
- Also: the mockup's dev page is now the opening's preview (`/crate-open-preview.html`, the real `CrateOpening` with a made-up roll). `scripts/crate-open-frames.ts` makes stills, every frame, and an MP4 with sound; `scripts/crate-open-perf.mjs` measures frame times at 4x. The Hidden Boss's light gives its shapes as masks (`lightMasks`). The dead `.shop-opening` and `.claw-opening` styles are gone.
