## Hollow's bulbs: your ten bigger, his strip six counting every move (Eric, Nov 12)

Lane: targeted (boss presentation). Eric's words and my calls: `docs/decisions/2026-11-12-hollow-bulbs-big.md`; `docs/areas/bosses.md` updated.

### What changed
- **Your ten bulbs (Boss alone)**: a computer's strand is now 190 px wide (it was 110). That's 1.73x, and exactly 2x the 95 x 15 drawing, so every pixel is 2 x 2 and stays crisp. A phone's is 114 px (it was 95, so 1.2x). It stays right-aligned under "Move N / N moves left" and is never wider than the bar's free room (`min(…, 100%)`), so it can't run over the name, the level, the status or the edge. Same drawing, wire and fizz.
- **His strip under the rage meter: six bulbs, one out per move, yours and his** (`stripLit` in `components/Hollow.tsx`).
  - The rules don't change (`darkEvery` stays 3). The count is twice the rules' `bulbs`, less one once this turn's crowd move is on the board.
  - It starts at 2 lit before his first cover (your first move and his, which brings the cover). From then on it reads 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 and relights as each cover's darkness lands.
  - Raids and online work the same way: the crowd's revealed move puts one out (checked live in a solo raid).
  - On his move a bulb goes out with its pop. On yours it goes out quietly, because your move already knocks.
- **As wide as the meter, drawn pixel for pixel**: `bulbStrand` is 44 x 11 (a phone's meter is 44 px) and the new `bulbStrandWide` is 88 x 11 (a computer's meter is 88 px, with only 11 px of room under it). The strip uses the battle colors of his strand's first six bulbs. Its row keeps its 11.25 px, so the bar and the board don't move (measured from 360 to 428 px phones and 1280 to 2560 px computers: identical bar and board boxes).
- `WipPreview.tsx`'s strip preview now uses the six-bulb drawings.
