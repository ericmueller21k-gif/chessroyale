## What changed

- **Boss Battle select: the picked boss stands bigger on the stage.** One setting, `STAGE_SCALE` (1.5) in `characters/boss-select.ts`. The figure is capped by the room above its feet (less the speech bubble's tail), so every boss stays on its pad and inside the stage. Real growth is about 1.1x to 1.45x in figure height (the room binds for every boss but Wrangler, whose lasso caps him by width), not the full 1.5x; see the decision note.
- **The figure keeps its proportions.** `.bs-spot .bs-sprite` takes its height from its width. Before, the canvas was stretched to the stage's height.
- **Don Dough's tile shows his portrait** (it showed a 🦓 icon: `zebra` was missing from `SELECT_FRAMING` and `SELECT_TINT`). His framing and tint are added.

## Player-visible changes

- The picked boss on the stage is bigger, and tall bosses are held to the stage's height.
- Don Dough's tile in the grid shows his portrait, in a pink tint.

## Checks changed (old → new)

- Added: `has a framing and a tint for every boss in the roster with a sprite` (packages/app/test/boss-select.test.ts). It fails if a sprited boss lacks either entry, and the Hidden Boss is exempt from the tint.
- Unchanged: the other eight boss-select tests (all pass, including the figure-framing and tint-contrast checks, which now cover Don Dough).
- Changed: none removed.
