# Outfit slot and the Banana Suit

**Lane: targeted** (no migration: equips are stored per slot, so `outfit` is just a new row). Effort high.

## What changed for players
- A new item slot, **Outfit**. With an outfit on, your pawn shows only the outfit, plus goggles or shades (eyewear) over its face hole.
  Everything else stays equipped underneath and comes back when you take the outfit off.
- **Banana Suit** (Epic, outfit): "Fully committed to the bit. Opponents keep slipping up." Your pawn's head shows in the face hole.
  It does a slow two-frame bounce standing, a side-on sprint running left or right, and front and back runs going down or up. The peel takes the item's color.
- It's in both regular crates, Beta and Winter, as a fifth Epic. Each Epic item there drops 2.8% of the time now (was 3.5%). It's not in boss crates.
- It's drawn everywhere a dressed pawn is: the town, home's pawn and ring, the locker, profiles, crate tiles and reveals, and match screens. It shows on match screens even though rides don't.
- The locker: an **Outfit** tab (the tabs are now an even 4 + 3 grid on a phone and one row on a computer). While an outfit is on, every other item
  except eyewear shows greyed with "Hidden by your outfit", and you can still wear it. Profile rows and the Equipped Cosmetics list show only what's visible.
- The FAQ's shop line mentions outfits.

## How it works
- In core: `ItemSlot` gets `"outfit"`; `eyewear: true` on Ski Goggles and Dark Shades; `shownLook`, `mountOf` and `hiddenByOutfit`; and `OUTFIT.eyewear`
  in settings (set it to false to hide eyewear too).
- The outfit is drawn with the rides' kit (`components/rides/banana-suit.tsx`, its cells measured into `ride-cells.ts`), so the town and `Avatar` use the code they already have.
- `scripts/measure-rides.ts` and `preview-items.mjs` now accept `PW_CHROMIUM` (a browser path), as the other scripts already do.
