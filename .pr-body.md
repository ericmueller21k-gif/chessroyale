## Cause
`e2e/fairplay.spec.ts`, "a ban means you can't sign in", passed only on its retry since Nov 14 (both projects). Its "fresh browser" step cleared the cookies and storage of the tab still open on the ban screen, about 50 ms after the ban screen drew. That tab still had requests on their way. The answer to one sent before the clear (the ban answer: it sets the browser's `hc_banned` mark and the app writes `brc.banned`) landed after it, so `/` loaded in a browser marked banned again. The server was right: the request came from the banned browser. CI's retry records a trace, which slows the page enough that every answer is in before the clear, so the retry always passed.

## Fix
The fresh browser is a browser of its own (`browser.newContext({ ...test.info().project.use })`, the pattern from friends and board-tools), and the open tab is closed first. The rest of the test (the refused sign-in, `/town`, the lifted ban signing in) runs in that browser.
