Fixes main's two retry-passes after #942 (run 45584074647), each at its cause. Targeted lane: test-only changes, no app code.

## 1. [phone] xp.spec "a solo battle left mid-game" (the reload's card never came)

- **#942 didn't cause it, and it's not a player bug.** The failing try's page after the reload was home, with the profile loaded and
  no card. The test plays the engine's best move against Ginger, and that can mate her: in the CI retry's trace the engine's expected
  score was 1.0 (won) by move 10. A battle that's decided is rightly not kept (`SoloMatch.noteEnded` clears it; under 10 moves nothing
  is recorded), so the reload, about 10 ms after the 9th move, had nothing to offer. A player who reloads a battle still going always
  gets the card. 37 local runs on CI's Chromium 141 (4 workers, CPU stress, the whole spec in CI's order) never failed, which fits a
  rare winning line rather than a reload race.
- **Fix:** `playOwnMoves` plays the engine's best move that doesn't end the game (mate or stalemate, by chess.js), else pickMove's quiet
  one among the moves that don't. Its comment already said "the game must still be going after n"; now the picker makes sure of it.

## 2. [phone] crowd-play.spec "the scoreboard box is built once" (`done(b.seq)` false)

- **Cause (the test's):** the tracker's first sample waited a frame (`requestAnimationFrame(tick)`), and the loop read "play" and
  picked 5 ms after it was set up. The pick ended the turn inside that frame, so the record began "scoring" with no "play" before
  the first cut. `done` only looks at the first cut, so it never passed, though every later turn was fine (the seq shows them).
- **Fix:** the tracker samples at once, and the loop samples again right before each pick (in the evaluate that reads the board).
