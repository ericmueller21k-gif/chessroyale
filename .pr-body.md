## Moo's moonrise: no moon before the land

Eric, Nov 20, live on his phone: "The first few frames you can see the moon behind the land because the moon loaded first."

**Cause:** #848 drew the rising moon from its own picture (`moo-moon.png`, 2 KB, small enough for the build to inline) and the field's sky and front from another one (`moo-field-layers.png`, 27 KB). On a cold load the moon arrived first, so it showed before the land.

**Fix (at the cause):**
- **One picture.** The moon is now the layers' ninth frame (`scripts/lift-moo-field.py`). The sky, the moon and the front all come from one image, so they can't arrive apart. The eight existing frames are pixel-identical to before, and the moon's frame is pixel-identical to the old sprite. `moo-moon.png` and `scripts/lift-moo-moon.py` are deleted.
- **Drawn only once it's decoded.** `prewarmCinematic()` (called when his battle starts) decodes the picture. The layered rise is used only if it has decoded before the field's first frame. If not (a cold load on a slow network), that Full Moon shows the field's own single picture for its whole rise, with the moon already in place behind the hills. The choice is made once per Full Moon and never changes mid-rise. If the decode fails, the fallback is used every time.
- Timing is unchanged: every beat and the 13.38 s total are the same, and there's no wait.

**Player-visible:** on a cold load the moon never shows without the land. If the picture is late, that one Full Moon shows the moon already risen (the original field) instead of the rise. Nothing else changes.
