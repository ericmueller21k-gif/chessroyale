## A boss's words beside the board stay put on a computer (Eric, Nov 15)

**The bug.** On a computer, Don Dough's speech bubble showed correctly, then grew and slid under the left menu rail. Every turn draws a new game screen (yours, then his), and the boss's figure and bubble are drawn again with it. The figures' sizes (`--side-s`, `--side-d`, …) were set on each new screen by `fitSides`, in a layout effect that runs after the child bubble's own layout effect. So the new bubble fitted itself at the default size and place. Then the figure grew and moved left, and the box went with it at its full 9 px, unshifted. That put it 19 px under the menu at 1611 × 843. Every boss was exposed to this; the God King's `--king-room` had the same timing.

**What changed.**
- `fitSides` (BossCharacter.tsx) now sets the figures' sizes on the `.arena`, which stays the same element across the battle's screens. Each new screen draws its figures and words in place from its first frame.
- New `--side-room`: the space from the boss's box's right edge, where his bubble opens, to the side menu (or his column's left edge), less 8 px. The bubble's `max-width` is `min(220px, var(--side-room))` (styles.css, board-size.css). A long line wraps inside that space at 9 px, so the box never needs moving into view.

**What players see.** On a computer, a boss's bubble keeps its size and place for the whole line. It is never cut off by the menu, and long lines wrap into more rows. Phones are unchanged; the bar bubble is untouched.

**Lane:** targeted (layout + timing). Specs touched: `e2e/boss-side.spec.ts`.

**Checks: old → new.**
- Added to `e2e/boss-side.spec.ts`: "Eric's rule (Nov 15)". Don Dough's longest line, sampled every frame from first to last through your move and his, at 1611 × 843 and 1280 × 800. The box's rectangle must stay the same on every screen, and stay right of the menu and inside the window. It fails on main (144.5 → 125.5 px left, under the menu at 144) and passes here.
- No checks changed or removed.

**Proof.** Typecheck passes. `boss-side.spec.ts` (computer) passes 27/27. `boss-bar.spec.ts` (phone) passes 30, with 1 computer-only test skipped. A local sweep ran all 18 bosses at 1280 × 800, 1440 × 900, 1611 × 843 and 1920 × 1080, each with its own longest line and a 67-character line, through a move. Every box kept its place and stayed on screen, and none was nudged or shrunk. The one exception is below.

**For the director.** The Hidden Boss's bubble can shift a few px vertically mid-line (3.5 px at 1611). That is his own Nov 14 rule: his words stand on the head of the pose shown now (`bc-heads`, `--bc-head`). It is unrelated to this bug and never goes off screen, but it does conflict with "nothing shifts". Left as is: whether to keep it is Eric's call.
