## Battle Tower PR 2: held pieces onto the board layer

**What moved.** Ginger's, Boingo's, Moo's, Folio's and Chandy's held pieces (the freeze, the blizzard's and Super Freeze's ice, the pie's
freeze, the moonbeam's stun, the fold, rapid fire's dizzy pieces, the beam's held pieces) left each boss's `held` list for the board layer:
`BossRules.layer` (piece marks built by `heldBy`, `held`, binding the crowd). The dispatcher's `limitCrowd` (so `crowdAllowed` and
`boardAltered`) reads them through `layerStop`, then the boss's `crowdFilter`, then the same lift as before. `heldIn` now lives in
`board-layer.ts` on `movePaths`; the old one and every `held` hook are deleted (one path).

**Behavior change: none.** The layer's held rule differs from the old `heldIn` only for a Chess960 castling rook, and boss battles never
start from Chess960. Calls: `docs/decisions/2026-11-18-held-pieces-on-the-layer.md`. Left for later PRs: `bossAllowed`, `stopReason` and
`icedSquares` reading the layer; Chandy's beam walls (PR 3).

**The guard (twin).** New `packages/chess/test/held-layer-twin.test.ts`: today's `heldIn`/`limitCrowd`/`crowdAllowed`/`boardAltered`
frozen in the test, against the dispatcher, on every crowd turn of seeded battles for all five (the kit's battles at two seeds, plus each
holding power switched on: freeze, blizzard, superfreeze, pie, moonbeam, fullmoon, draft, rapidfire, beam; crowd White and Black): no
difference. It reached held pieces that stop moves on 40 to 221 turns a boss, castling with a held rook for Ginger, Moo and Chandy.
Before the old hooks were deleted it also matched their squares, turn by turn.
