## Cause

Folio's Draft marks only a **defended** piece of yours that Folio attacks with **nothing cheaper** (`chooseDraft`). The smoke's crowd plays "a knight to an empty square": Na3, Nb1, Na3, … with every other piece at home. So the only target it ever offers is the knight on a3, and only when Folio has opened its f8 bishop's diagonal (…e5/…e6) while the knight stands there, which is every other turn at best. Measured locally: the Draft came at turn 4 in most runs, and at 8 workers on 4 cores 5 of 16 runs failed (one with no Draft at all; four where the Draft came so late that the ultimate, which waits for the passive or 4 moves, missed the deadline). On a busy CI runner only about 4 crowd turns fit in 70 s.

It isn't a timing race in `watchPowers` (the watcher starts before your first move, and the Draft can't come before turn 2), and it isn't a Folio bug: the Draft did exactly what its rule says.

A crowd line (`SMOKE_FIRST`, as Chandy got on Nov 16) can't fix this for sure. An exhaustive search over Folio's replies showed no line of yours forces the Draft's condition from the usual start, because Folio can keep its own pieces home. A d4/Nc3/e4 line, tried in the real game, still took until turn 7 in 2 of 12 runs.

## Fix

`?power=draft` (Folio's passive test switch) now starts from **BALLOON_TEST_MOVES** (1.e4 d5 2.exd5 Qxd5: its queen on d5, already on your a2, d2 and g2). Don Dough's `?power=dough` already does exactly this, and Smudgy's `?power=repaint` uses a start of its own. After the smoke's first move (Na3), **all 47** of Folio's replies leave a mark, so the Draft comes as turn 2 begins, whatever the engine plays. A new unit test checks every reply. The smoke spec and its assertion are unchanged.

No player-visible change: only the test link's start position moves.
