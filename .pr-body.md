# Chess Town: the menu's pages open over the town (computer and phone), and Play is Home

Eric, Nov 22: "you should be able to just click play with friends, daily quests, shop in crates, and profile without exiting out of Chesttown ... instead of making it say play, we should just change it to ... home." Builds on #875 (now on main, merged in here).

## What changed
- #875's `InventoryLayer` is generalized into `PageLayer` (`components/InventoryLayer.tsx` → `components/PageLayer.tsx`; `InventoryLayer` is now one use of it, unchanged for the player; `inventoryTakesPop` → `layerTakesPop`).
- In a computer's town, **Play with friends, Daily Quests, Shop & crates and Profile** open in a `PageLayer` over the town's middle column only (`App.tsx` `townLayer`, through `TownPage` → `FrontFrame`'s new `layer` slot). Each is the page's own component: `ShopScreen`, `PlayerProfileScreen`, home's quests card (`QuestsLayer`) and home's Play with friends sheet (`FriendsLayer`, exported from Home.tsx around its own `FriendsSheet`).
- The side menu's **Play is Home** (`TOWN_BACK_TO` too: "You'll head back to Home.").
- **A phone's town bar** (Eric, Nov 22: "I don't think we need the word Chesstown here ... they should be the same as the main menu"): back, then the map, the chat, home's **Quests, Shop & crates and Friends**, and the gear, all in home's top bar buttons; the title is for screen readers only. Quests, Shop & crates and Friends (Play with friends' sheet) open the same layers, over the whole screen. Inside rooms too.
- QuestsOverlay, Play with friends' sheet and the public profile's sheet take their own Escape in the capture phase (as #875 did for its three pop-ups).

## Player-visible
- **Phone:** the town bar loses "Town square" / the room's name and gains Quests, Shop & crates and Friends (Play with friends), home's icons and look; all seven buttons fit from 320 px at 40 px or more (44 px from 375 px; home's margins and gaps from 390 px, tighter below). The chat icon keeps showing the chat's mode; the gear is still Town settings. Each page opens over the town and closes back to it.

**Computer:**
- The four pages open over the town: the side menu and the right column (TOWN tools, chat) stay where they are and work; the open page's item is lit; another of the four switches the layer in place; Back, Escape, the back gesture and Chess Town close it. Your pawn, the chat and a party stay as they were; no key reaches the town under it.
- Making or joining a lobby from Play with friends, and your profile's Settings and Friends, leave the town, so they ask "Leave Chess Town?" first. Home, Boss Battle and Settings leave as before (asked first). **Settings is unchanged** (its page links on to the FAQ and Auto chess, which leave anyway).
- The walkthrough's PLAY slide lights only home's PLAY button (not the side menu's Home).
- Stills: branch `stills/town-desktop-layers`: `town-shop-desktop.png`, `town-profile-desktop.png` (1280 × 800); `town-bar-390-phone.png`, `town-bar-320-phone.png`, `town-shop-390-phone.png`. The shop in the layer is pixel for pixel the /shop page's column.
