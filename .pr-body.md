## Each timed power's beats written once, in its timeline (follow-up to #885: F1, F2)

#885's last review (delta 3, F1) found that Dash's Scramble Rush was kept only as his sweep began. So a crash, or a second window's
Resume, after the sweep had shown played the whole sweep again. Lights out's drop had the same shape. The cause, as the review named it:
each power's "what the screen shows by the clock" was written twice, in its screen and in `SoloMatch.moment()`, and the walk test copied
`moment()`'s list. This fixes that cause.

**What changed**
- `packages/chess/src/bosses/dash.ts`: the Rush's sweep (`rushRuns`: his copies' passes, when each swept piece goes and lands) moved
  here, unchanged, from `DashRush.tsx`. New `rushBeats(rush)`: the sweep's start, each piece gone and landed, the sweep's end, the ten
  seconds, time's up.
- `chandy.ts`: `dodgeTimeline().beats` (the same list `moment()` had). `hollow.ts`: `lightsOutTimeline().beats`, which adds the drop's
  end.
- `solo.ts`: `moment().reveals` reads those lists. `momentNow` keeps the last beat due within the 100 ms lead, not the first, so two beats
  under 100 ms apart (the sweep's can be) are both kept. F2: the stale `resumeTurnMs` comment.
- `DashRush.tsx` draws the sweep from `rushRuns` in chess.
- Docs: `docs/decisions/2026-11-22-timeline-beats-once.md` (the director's call on the sweep, mine on the drop and `momentNow`),
  `.claude/LESSONS.md` and `docs/areas/bosses.md` (a new timed power exports its beats beside its timeline).

**Player-visible changes:** none. `rushRuns` gives the same sweep as `main`'s on 798 rushes. I recorded the Rush frame by frame on a
phone, on `main` and on this branch (copies from about 0.73 s, place at 9.03 s, done at 19.05 s in both). Every board state matched what
`main`'s sweep draws, each change two or three frames after its time. Only the keeps change: a rejoin now opens the Rush at or after the
last swept piece shown, and after the sweep never replays it.

**Lane:** targeted, plus the boss kit (the brief's call: boss powers' timing and the kept battle).

**Checks changed (old → new)** (all in `packages/app/test/solo-resume.test.ts`)
- The walk, "keep before show" (6 cases): `beats()` was its own copy of each list (for the Rush, `scatterAt` and `placeAt` only). It now
  reads `dodgeTimeline().beats`, `rushBeats(...)` and `lightsOutTimeline(...).beats`, so it checks keeps at every swept piece, the
  sweep's end, time's up and the drop's end. On the old reveals, or the old `momentNow`, both of its Rush cases fail.
- New, F1 flipped (from `review-885-delta-3.test.ts`, 2 cases): the page goes with no hide event, after his sweep or midway through it.
  The rejoin shows the board that was on screen, and no piece is swept again. Both fail on the old reveals.
- New: the Rush's on-screen board and stage (`rushShownFen`, `rushStage`), taken every ms, change only at its beats and at every one. So
  the list is the screen's own, not a copy.
- No check removed.
