## Crowd on a computer: the status line centered and bigger; board, scoreboard and chat on one bottom line

Eric (Nov 25, a 50 v 50 at 2000 px): center the move line and make it bigger; "the board and the two boxes should all be the same lower band"; move the chat up.

Light lane (layout: CSS, plus one small measuring component).

### What changed
- **Status line** (`Move 5 · cut in 10 · P38/50 · −0.8 · CUT +0.2`), computer only: centered on the board's squares, 22 px (1.4 × 16) wherever the board is wide enough. On a narrower board it's as big as the width allows: about 18 px on the 504 px board at 1280 × 800. If a line is ever too long, its "Move" part ends in "…" (the phone already does this), so it never runs under the gear. Still one line.
- **One bottom line:** the scoreboard (left) and the chat (right) end exactly where the board ends, including its frame (the clock's bar under it). Their content scrolls inside. CSS can't know where the board ends, because its size comes from the window's height or its column's width, whichever is smaller. So `packages/app/src/board-foot.ts` (`<BoardFoot />` in the arena, App.tsx) measures the board's squares and writes `--side-foot`, `--chat-foot` and `--board-shift` on the arena, and `board-size.css` uses them on a computer only.
- **The chat higher:** the empty gap above the chat was holding room for the reveal's vote results (six bars, the result and its note: 259 px). To keep the rule that nothing shifts, that space stays as one fixed slot, but the bars are thinner on a computer, so the slot is now 206 px and the chat starts 56 px higher (324 instead of 380). The chat can't start right under the "Black is voting…" card without the reveal then pushing it down or covering it.
- **Other modes:** in a Boss Battle raid, the scoreboard now also ends on the board's line. Its right column is a different layout (the move list and the God King) and is unchanged, as is the boss chat layout (screens with a boss dock are excluded). Squads has its own screens and standings panel (`.sq-arena`): not changed.
- **Phone and tablet:** unchanged. The new rules need a window at least 1100 × 600 with a mouse.
