## What changed and why
- **boss-bar:204 (app bug).** The match's screens are rebuilt at every phase, which puts the boss bar back in the page twice a move. Each time, the lifted level badge replayed its gold pop: about 9 times in one Full Moon. Now a badge that is drawn again carries on from when the lift began (`levelSince`, a negative `animation-delay`), so the pop plays once. The red slash for the King's strike works the same way.
- **town-leave:148 (app bug).** A Back or Go out tapped while a room was still fading in (up to about 1.3 s after it is drawn) was dropped, and you stayed inside. Now the tap waits and goes out once the room is ready (`outNext`).
- **global-chat:116 (test timing).** The test chat allowed one line a second with a 2 s line. That meant the fourth message was "busy" only if it reached the server within 1 s of the first rush, and on CI it arrived later. The test chat now allows one line every 3 s with a 6 s line. The young-device check (:141) waits up to 10 s for its own line, because that line can queue behind Ann's (up to 3 s) and then arrives with the next live poll (every 5 s).
