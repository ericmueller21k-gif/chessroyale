## The account not loading: say why, never "Loading…" for good

Eric (Nov 20, the installed app on his laptop): Profile "just sitting loading", Friends empty, chat off, quests greyed, "Where's our sign in?"

**Cause (confirmed in code).** When `/api/me` or `/api/auth/config` failed, `loadAccount` swallowed it as "no accounts here" (local dev's path), and your own Profile waited for an account id that never came, so it said "Loading…" for good. Why it failed: hunchess.com is behind Cloudflare Access before launch. Once its sign-in ran out, every `/api/*` got a 302 to `*.cloudflareaccess.com`. A `fetch` that follows a cross-origin redirect fails exactly like being offline, so nothing knew why. The brief's guess about the service worker was close but not quite: its `navigate` branch already passes a redirect on (a page's request keeps redirect mode "manual", so the worker gets an `opaqueredirect` and the browser follows it). It falls back to the saved shell only when the fetch throws. The shell was running from an open app window, or from a load that got no answer at all.

**Fix.**
- `api()` (account.ts) asks with `redirect: "manual"` and reads the gate's answer for what it is (`site-gate.ts`, `gateAnswered`): an `opaqueredirect`, or a 401/403 with `www-authenticate: Cloudflare-Access` (what Access answers a script-looking request; checked with curl). Our API never redirects a script's request; the only 302s are Google's sign-in trips, which are navigations.
- `loadAccount` records why it failed in `account().failed`: `offline`, `server` or `gate`. Each request has a 20 s limit (`FRONT_DOOR.accountTimeoutMs`), so no load waits for good. `retryAccount()` runs it again. The account also loads by itself when you come back online, and when the gate lets the page in again.
- The minute's `/api/status` poll also notices the gate both ways (`redirect: "manual"`).
- The service worker: no behavior change. Its comment now says an answered navigation (the gate's redirect, an error page) is always passed on and the saved app is only for no answer. New tests hold this.

**What players see.**
- Your Profile, when the account didn't load: the reason in plain words and **Try again**; when signed out of the site's gate, **Sign in again** too (a reload, which reaches Access's login). It never sits on "Loading…" after a failure. If the server has no accounts at all (local dev), it says "Profiles aren't available here." If your profile's page itself fails to load, you also get Try again.
- Settings: an ACCOUNT section with the same reason and buttons (before, ACCOUNT was hidden entirely).
- Signed out of the gate: one notice floats over the app (the `.fd-notice` pop-up, per Eric's Nov 16 rule: it sits on top and pushes nothing) with **Sign in again** and ✕. It goes away once a request gets through.
- The guest's Sign in: checked, no change. The desktop Profile shows KEEP YOUR PROFILE with its Sign in in view at 1280×800, and Settings' ACCOUNT shows it too, once the account loads as a guest. Eric didn't see it because his account never loaded.

**Calls made** (`docs/decisions/2026-11-20-account-load-fail.md`):
- "Sign in again" is `location.reload()`. No cache-busting address or worker bypass is needed, since the worker passes the redirect through.
- The saved app shell is still served whenever the page fetch throws, not only when `navigator.onLine` is false. A laptop on Wi-Fi with no internet says it's online and would otherwise get the browser's error page instead of solo play.

**Checks changed (old → new):** no guard tests in `docs/rules/eric.md` were touched, and no checks were removed.
- `packages/app/test/account-load.test.ts`: + 6 cases: the gate's redirect (asked with `redirect: "manual"`, not retried, noticed app-wide), the gate's 401, Try again after being let in (the notice goes), offline, a server error, and the timeout. + an `afterEach` in that block that lets a load's leftover second try finish inside its own test.
- `packages/app/test/sw-versions.test.ts`: the harness's network can now fail like offline (`net` may return an Error), plus an `open()` page helper. + 3 cases: the gate's redirect passed on, an error page passed on, the saved app when there's no answer.

A 302 to an Access-like URL → "Sign in again" on the card and in one app-wide notice, whose button reloads the page.

**Lane:** targeted (accounts' client side; no server or stored-data change). Note: `sw-template.js` is a shared foundation, so this PR's e2e run is the whole suite.
