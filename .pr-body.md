and 2 short piano inserts" that "seamlessly work into the main theme every few play throughs".

### What changed
- `public/music/`: `town-loop.mp3`, `town-solo-a.mp3`, `town-solo-b.mp3` (from `town-music/` on `stills/trailer-v2`, commit 296f7e0c,
  still the newest), and a `town` entry in `themes.json` (`loop`, `loopSeconds`, each solo's `id`, `file`, `seconds`).
- `music.ts`, `townMusic(open)`:
  - The theme loops the same way a boss loop does: from `pad`, between `pad` and `pad + loopSeconds`.
  - After every `TOWN_MUSIC.passesPerSolo` passes, the solo whose turn it is starts exactly at the end of a pass. That pass stops on
    the same moment. The theme starts again from its top `seconds` later, and the solo's file rings out over it.
  - Every join is a Web Audio start or stop time. A timer only puts the next solo and the theme's return on the clock
    `TOWN_MUSIC.aheadS` before they play.
  - It uses one gain at `MUSIC.volume`.
  - When an audio context is rebuilt, the town's music moves to the new one at the same place (`handOverTown`, which shares the boss
    music's clock shift, now `clockShift`).
- `stopPlaying` never lets a source play past a stop it was already given. Without this, fading the town out just before a solo would
  let the last pass ring over the solo's first second.
- `preloadMusic` order: the town's theme first (decoded and played), then its solos, then the bosses' themes (fetched only).
- `Town.tsx`: `townMusic(true)` runs when the bosses' fetching starts, 2 s after the first frame. `townMusic(false)` runs when the town
  goes and when the Hidden Boss's summoning starts, so the town's theme doesn't come back while his fight opens.

### What players hear
- In the street town, and in buildings with no theme of their own (shops, the walk-up's hall and landing): the town theme. About every
  9.5 minutes a piano solo plays in place of a pass, solo a then solo b in turn.
- A boss's room (walked in), a challenge's splash or a battle takes over. The town's theme fades out over 1.5 s, the same way a room
  theme does.
- Walking out of a room, or calling off a challenge: the town's theme comes back from its top.
- Music off: nothing is fetched or played, same as before.

### Settings
`TOWN_MUSIC = { passesPerSolo: 3, order: ["a", "b"], aheadS: 120 }` in `packages/core/src/settings.ts`.

### Calls made
These are in `docs/decisions/2026-11-15-town-music.md`:
- **Challenges:** the walk-in and intro keep the town's theme until the splash takes over.
- **Late solo:** if a solo isn't decoded in time, the theme plays one more pass first.
- **Solo turns:** a solo already scheduled but not yet heard is still next. The order resets only when the town closes.
- **Memory:** all three pieces stay decoded while the town is open (about 100 MB at 48 kHz). They are let go when the town closes.
