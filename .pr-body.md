# Battle Tower: its music

Eric approved the tower's theme (Nov 19: "this is the one, nice work"), then corrected the floors' battles: "the battle tower one is just for when you're idling in the tower, or you're on the floors ... during the game ... it should just pick one ... it doesn't reuse the same one for, 5 or 6 rounds."

**What changed**
- `tower-loop.mp3` (from `tower-music/` on `stills/trailer-v2`, re-encoded 192 → 128 kbps like every theme; same length, aligned at 0, `pad`, `pad + loopSeconds`) and its `tower` entry in `themes.json` (no intro, 160 s). No `.ogg`.
- The tower's lobby and floor room play `tower` (shops.ts: their home's theme); back from a floor's battle it starts again as the room shows (Town.tsx `towerBack`).
- A floor's battle plays ONE of its bosses' themes (tower.ts `pickFloorTheme`, `FloorPlay.music`): random among the floor's bosses whose theme isn't one of the last 5 floors' on this device (`brc.tower.music`), else the least recently played. Picked as the battle first plays, kept in the kept floor (`KeptFloor.music`, `brc.tower.solo`): the same for the whole match whoever is to move (no fading on each turn change) and after a reload.
- `MUSIC.towerRecent: 5` in settings.ts.
- Docs: music README, battle-tower.md, decision note `docs/decisions/2026-11-19-tower-music.md`.

**Player-visible:** the tower's music in its lobby and floor room; a floor's battle plays one boss theme start to finish, varied floor to floor. Normal music volume and mute apply. About 2.6 MB more fetched during the town's loading.

**Checks:** added to `tower-floor.test.ts` ("the tower's music": the rooms pick `tower`; the floor pick is stable across calls and a reload, avoids the recent ones, falls back to the least recent, keeps 5). None changed or removed. Typecheck and the app's tower/music unit tests pass locally.

Lane: targeted (an asset plus a small pick rule in the tower's kept floor).
