## Ranked 1 v 1: colors in turn, not a coin flip (+ a Handbook entry)

Eric, Nov 25: "Color balance seems good, and add this to the handbook". No White edge in the rating (not asked; `expectedScore` still ignores color).

**How White was chosen until now:** a coin flip. `seatDuel` (`packages/server/src/matchmaker.ts`) drew one random bit per pair. Not queue order, not a fixed seat.

**Now** (core's `firstGetsWhite`, every number in `RANKED_1V1.colors`):
1. Nobody gets one color more than `maxInRow` (2) games in a row when the other player can take it. If both are stuck on the same color, the longer run switches first.
2. Otherwise White goes to the larger (Black − White) over each player's last `recentGames` (10) ranked games.
3. A tie: whoever had Black last (no games ranks between "Black last" and "White last").
4. Still tied: a coin flip.

The colors are read once, with the ticket (`recentColors` in `duelEntrant`, alongside its other reads, on the existing `white_id`/`black_id` indexes), so pairing reads nothing. A failed read means no history, so a coin flip. Bot and town games are unchanged and don't count.

Sim (40 seeds × 12 players × 300 random pairings): over any 10 games in a row, Whites and Blacks were at most 4 apart (coin flip: 10). The longest run was 3 (coin flip: 16). Runs of 3 or more were 12× rarer.
