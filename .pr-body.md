Town phase 0: a deploy no longer throws the saved town away; the chips' faces drawn once a place

Phase 0 of the town's structure (`DESIGN.md` on `claude/town-architecture`). No pixel of the town changes; no new text.

**What changed for players**
- **After an update, Chess Town opens as fast as on any later visit**, unless the update changed the town's art. The saved pictures were
  keyed by the build's commit id, so every deploy painted all of them again. Now the key is a fingerprint of the town's art, taken by
  the build: esbuild bundles the code the pictures come from with all it uses (tree-shaken) and hashes it (`packages/app/town-art.ts`
  → `__TOWN_ART__`). Nobody keeps a list; comments, types and settings the pictures don't read don't count.
- **The edge chips:** each place's face is drawn once, on a canvas of its own, and a place keeps the chip it had.
- The first visit after this ships paints once more (the key's form changed).
