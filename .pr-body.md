## End screen sounds: the steel pan level-up, a soft last note, pings that rise with the XP

Eric, Nov 20: "the pan is actually better than the bells ... let's make that the official one for now ... the other ones sound good, the different XP ones." Lane: light/targeted (a sound change). Decision: `docs/decisions/2026-11-20-end-screen-sounds.md`.

### What changed
- **Level-up**: one shared steel pan sound (`levelUp` in `sound.ts`, numbers in `END_SCREEN.levelUp`): G5 C6 E6 G6 60 ms apart, then a C6 E6 G6 C7 chord at 0.3 s. Replaces `autochess("level")` in all three places: the end screen's level-up, auto chess's `acLevel`, the quests overlay's `questDone` (`QUEST_SOUND_FILES` still overrides).
- **`xpDing`**: one soft pan G6 (`END_SCREEN.ding`) instead of the two-note bell.
- **`xpPing`**: pitch by the XP counted so far (`xpPingHz` in core's `xp.ts`, `END_SCREEN.ping`): C6 up 9 semitones, eased `1-(1-r)^2`, the top reached with about 1 s of a 500 XP fill left; the count-up's an octave down. `END_SCREEN.fillMs.max` 2200 → 2800.
- **Removed**: auto chess's `level` chime (its sound, its level trim, its `MAKE` entries), the pentatonic scale.

### Player-visible
- A new level-up sound on the end screen, in auto chess and on a finished quest; a softer note when the XP bar stops; the fill's pings glide up instead of stepping.
- A big gain's bar fill is a little longer (a full level: 2.8 s, was 2.2 s; 100 XP: 1.6 s, was 1.48 s). RP's fill uses the same setting.

### Calls for the director
- **Loudness**: at the demo's levels the chord's four notes struck together peaked above a move (0.62 vs 0.57). `levelUp.gain` 0.42 brings the peak to 0.26 (under half a move's, the rule every synthesized sound follows; the reveal's three dings: 0.28). Its loudness (RMS 0.027) is under the old chime's (0.057), so it is quieter than before. One number to raise if Eric wants it louder (up to about 0.45 keeps the rule).
- **The last note**: level 0.35 (peak 0.08), not the brief's ~0.6: against a trimmed level-up, 0.6 would have been louder per note than the level-up's run.
- **A 500 XP game**: one game pays at most 350 XP (30 min + a win) without a daily reward or a quest, so the real end screen demo is 350 XP; the 500 XP fill is the same code replayed.
- RP's pings count a full RP bar as a level of XP.

### Checks changed (old → new)
- `packages/app/test/autochess-sounds.test.ts`: the pack's names: 11 with `level` → 10 without; removed `m.level.sounds > m.xp.sounds * 2` (the sound is gone); two test titles no longer name the level-up's chime. Every other check unchanged.
- `packages/core/test/xp.test.ts`: **new** test for `xpPingHz` (starts at C6, never down, no step of 0.1 semitone between one XP and the next, 100 XP climbs 3 to 5.5 semitones, the top at 500 XP and held, about 1 s of a top fill left at the top, the count-up an octave down).
- `e2e/xp.spec.ts`, "a counted game with a level-up": **added** its sounds: more than 10 pings, one `acLevel`, one `xpDing` after it; the level-up's peak under half a move's and its RMS under a move's; the last note's peak under the level-up's.
