## Big Boy: a tower turn alters the board only when a block takes a move away (Eric's (b), Nov 18)

Lane: full for fair play (the director runs the review). Decision: `docs/decisions/2026-11-18-bigboy-powerturn.md`.

### What changed
- **Fair play:** a turn with Big Boy's tower or scattered blocks up is a power turn only when a block takes one of the
  crowd's legal moves away (`toysTakeMove`). Before, every turn with his toys up was one, so his battles gave fair play almost no signal turns.
- **The Last Stand:** his toys keep the God King off only when they take away the reply the save would replay (the judge's
  feared answer), checked against his toys as they'll stand for it (`toysTakeReply` via `toysAfterCrowd`: a topple's
  scattered blocks in, an expiring scatter gone, the tower open to him on his rare turns). An unknown reply keeps the save off as before.
  Before, the save was effectively off for the whole battle.
- **Interface:** `BossRules.powerTurn(boss, fen?)` (other bosses ignore the position) and a new optional hook
  `standAltered(boss, fen, played, reply)` that replaces `boardAltered`'s general rule. Only Big Boy has it, so every other boss
  is unchanged (boss kit and the `AUDIT` table pass as before). The runner passes the position, move and reply; solo and
  online both resolve rounds there.
- `afterCrowdMove` now calls the pure `toysAfterCrowd` (same behavior). Docs: `bosses.md`, `fairplay.md`, `god-king.md`, one FAQ line.

### Player-visible
- The God King's Last Stand can now come in a Big Boy battle (unless a block stops the boss's punishing reply).
  FAQ: "Big Boy's toy blocks are the exception: they keep him out only when a block stops the boss's reply that would punish your move."
- No screen changes.

### Changed checks (old → new)
- `bigboy.test.ts`, "his tower: a block from the 2nd crowd turn…": `powerTurn(b)` is true → `powerTurn(b, fen)` is true
  exactly when the crowd's moves are limited (always for a 1-tall tower); `powerTurn(b)` without the position is still true.
- `king-save-altered.test.ts`: a comment on the `bigboy` AUDIT entry only (assertions unchanged).
- New: 5 tests in `bigboy.test.ts` (fair play and the Last Stand, pure and in the match: tower that takes nothing → counts and
  saves; that takes a move and the reply → neither; a move but not the reply → no signal, save comes; a topple's seeded
  scatter; reply unknown), 1 in `server/test/boss-powers-lobby.test.ts` (online fair `power` = the round's allowed list).
- No guard test from `docs/rules/eric.md` touched.
