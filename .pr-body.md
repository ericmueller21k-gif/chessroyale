**Battle Tower: on a floor of three bosses, each boss ults at most once** (Eric, Nov 22: "when there's 3 bosses, they should each be maxed out at 1 ult ... They only ult once each at most"; Smudgy had ulted twice). Boss lane.

## What changed

- `BATTLE_TOWER.ultsOnFloorOf: { 3: 1 }` (settings.ts). As a floor of three starts, the runner puts `ultMax: 1` on every boss's powers (`towerUltMax`, beside `towerNoUlt`/`towerNoAdd`). Solo and the party's room run the same runner, a restored battle keeps it, and the view sends it to the screens.
- One gate for every boss in the dispatcher (`ultCapped`, bosses/base.ts), on top of each boss's own cap (the lower wins). Once his one ultimate has come and its effect is over: its step never runs again, his meter stays empty (no gain, and the God King's strike adds none), Chandy's "can't wait" path refuses, and so does the admins' trigger. Folio is never asked for a Crease line.
- App: his meter stays on the bar, empty and dimmed, titled "Rage: no more ultimates on this floor", and the admin's ⚡ goes, as it does for a spent boss. The FAQ's rage-meter section explains the rule in a line.
- Calls made (decision note `docs/decisions/2026-11-22-tower-three-ult-cap.md`): only the ultimate counts. Ginger's Super Freeze and Dash's Scramble Rush keep their own caps. The Rush runs off a half-full rage meter, so it can't come after Dash's one Time Scramble; in play it nearly always comes first. If Eric counts the Rush's "RAGE!" as an ult, that's one more line. Every boss's meter stays shown after his one, Smudgy's and Chub & Chirp's included (alone, theirs disappear after their last).

## What players see

On a floor of three, each boss's ultimate comes at most once. After it, his rage meter stays empty and greyed for the rest of the floor. Floors of one and two are unchanged.
