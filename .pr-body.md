Dash's stall skit follow-up: the round never locks over "Nahh, jk bro"; the watchers' clocks need nothing (the review of #770, findings 4 and 5)

**Lane:** targeted (server round timing). God-king lane. Brief: `notes/briefs/dash-watchers-pause.md`.

### What changed
- **Finding 5, fixed** (`packages/server/src/lobby.ts`, `lock()`): a round done during Dash's fake move (the last pick landing in
  the hold, the last person it waits for dropping, leaving or kicked, a party's King call) now locks as the hold ends
  (`setTimer("lock", stall.fake.until)`), not at once. The fix sits in `lock()` itself, so every early-lock path is covered in one
  place. The pick still counts as before; the deadline is always later (moved back by the hold), and think time and the party's bank
  already leave the hold out. Solo needs nothing: it refuses moves in the hold, so its move can't end there.
- **Finding 4, no code** (my call: `docs/decisions/2026-11-17-dash-pause-watchers.md`): nobody can watch Dash's stall or the King's
  strike from outside the round. Both happen only in a boss battle. A boss battle is only a raid or the Crowd's boss final, and
  `deal()` puts everyone left into its round. A player who is out watches the live board only with `spectatorsWatch()` (the 50 v 50
  rework, not a raid), and those matches never reach a final (`isFinal()` is false with `crowdCuts`), so they never meet a boss.
  Players out of a raid get `spectate`, which has no clock. A comment at both sends (`dashLobby.stallBeat`, `callStrike`) says what to
  do if a boss ever meets a watching team.

### Protocol
No message or field changed. Older apps are unaffected.

### Player-visible
Online, the fake move's "Nahh, jk bro" always plays to its end before the reveal, even when the move's last pick lands during it
(lag) or the last player still thinking drops or is kicked. Nothing else.
