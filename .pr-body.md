## Rooms' frame rate, and going in and out (Eric's iPhone, Nov 11)

Builds on #636 (`/town?report`). Targeted lane: the room's draw loop and the report. Specs this touches: `e2e/shop.spec.ts` (rooms) and `e2e/town-street.spec.ts` (the report).

### What Eric saw, and what it was
- **"Inside shop rooms 42-44 fps standing still, never climbs" and "the 3rd time it stayed at 60":** a room had no frame counter, so the `?fps` pill stayed frozen on the town's last reading from the door zoom (42-44, or 60 that time).
- **"Exiting ANY building drops to 2-4 fps for a full second":** also the pill. Its two half-second windows covered the whole visit, so it divided the frames from before you went in by the seconds spent inside. I reproduced it headless (1 fps, then 3 fps); a separate page frame counter saw steady frames the whole time.

### What changed
- **The `?fps` pill** (`FpsCount` in `frame-report.ts`): the town and a room tick one shared counter, so inside it reads the room. A hidden tab restarts it; the black between the town and a room doesn't (that wait is real).
- **The report covers rooms** (a): room frames go into the same run under the town's part names, marked "in a room". Frames from Go in until the black lifts are marked "going in", and from Go out until the town's zoom ends "going out". The panel gains two columns, **Room** and **In/out**, and still fits a 360, 390 and 428 px phone: the frames·fps row is tighter, and the table is a size smaller under 380 px.
- **A room's real cost was its floor** (c), laid every frame as hundreds of clipped triangles. Headless with the CPU slowed 4×: 20 fps with the floor, 60 without. Now, while the camera rests, the ceiling, the outside, the floor and the still plans are kept as one picture, copied pixel for pixel, and drawn with one copy. The follow camera now settles exactly so a resting view stays the same. A moving view draws as before.
- (b) The exit "hitch" was the pill, so there was nothing to keep alive. The town already keeps its renderer and caches through a visit.
- (d) Not done. A first visit inside already runs like a later one: no painting or uploads in either visit's room frames. Its one extra cost is painting the room's art behind the black as you go in. Doing that earlier would drop the town's frames during the walk to the door (eric.md, Oct 27). See the decision file.
