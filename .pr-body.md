## 50 v 50 opening vote: cards fill upward; the hint moves under the zones

Light lane (CSS and a small component change). Eric, Nov 26.

**What changed**
- `Vote.tsx` / `styles.css`: the vote cards' left-to-right bar is gone. Each card has one `.vote-card-fill` that rises from its foot to votes / all votes of its height (the bar's color at 35%, 40% in dark mode, with a 2 px top edge; a 300 ms `translateY` transition, not `scaleY`, so the edge stays 2 px; none with reduced motion; a halo of the card's color on its text where the edge crosses it). The cards keep their size and place; the count stays at the foot. Votes / total (not / the leader) so it reads as each option's share, like the old bar.
- The first-time hint "Drag your pawn into a zone to vote" sits just under the zones, pointing up, instead of above them (where it covered the middle zone's name tag, "Majority"). Phone and computer, Crowd and Squads.

**Player-visible**: the cards fill like glasses as votes land (computer and phone, light and dark); the hint no longer hides a zone's name.

**Stills**: branch `stills/vote-card-fill` (`mock/` the mockup, `mock/*-v2-*` the director's stronger fill as built; root the first build at 1536x730, 1920x960, phone 390x844, light and dark).

**Checks changed (old → new)**
- `e2e/vote-hint.spec.ts` `aboveZones` (hint above the zones) → `underZones` (hint under the zones, inside the board); used by the Crowd and Squads hint tests (the Squads title says "under").
- Added to `e2e/vote-hint.spec.ts`, both projects: "50 v 50's vote (Eric, Nov 26)": the hint overlaps no zone name tag or count; each card's fill is votes / total of its height from its foot; the cards' sizes don't change.

Typecheck passes; `vote-hint.spec.ts` and `opening-vote.spec.ts` pass on phone and desktop locally.
