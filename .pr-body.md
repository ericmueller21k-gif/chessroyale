## Tower room: a floor arrow to the next door, a solid table, the clerk's card only in front of his counter

Eric, Nov 21 (brief `notes/briefs/tower-room-arrow-board.md`). Targeted lane (town / battle tower).

### What changed
- **Floor arrow:** after a floor, a blinking gold arrow lies on the floor right in front of the next door and points at it. That's the
  elevator after a win or a spent life, and the way down once the run is over. Code-built from the floor art's palette
  (`SHOP_TOWER_FLOOR_ARROW_PALETTE`). The logic is in `tower-floor.ts` (`floorArrow`, `arrowBlink`, sizes in `TOWER_FLOOR_FEEL`) and
  `tower-floor-scene.ts` draws it under every figure. With reduced motion it stays fully lit and doesn't blink.
- **Solid table:** you can't walk onto the table's four squares (`SHOP_TOWER_FLOOR_TABLE_SQUARES`, the room art's new `round`). A tap beyond
  it walks you round. Holding an arrow key that would step onto it walks you round its nearer side and keeps going (`room.ts stepRound`).
- **Clerk's card:** it opens only from the three squares under his counter (`talkAt` in `shops.ts`), not from anywhere within 3 squares of
  him. A tap on him or the counter walks you there and opens it. I checked the other rooms: Shopkeeper taps only count on the Shopkeeper's
  own picture, and door taps on the door's picture plus 10 px. The other Shopkeepers' small "Talk" card still shows within 3 squares, as
  designed on Nov 2. If Eric wants the clerk's rule everywhere, it's one `talkAt` per shop (noted in the decision).

### What players see
- After a floor, a blinking arrow on the floor in front of the open elevator (or in front of the way down when the run is over).
- You can't run through the table in the middle of a floor room any more. Holding up from your side runs you round it.
- In the lobby, the clerk's panel no longer pops up from a tap on the floor near him.

### Checks run
- `npm run typecheck`; `vitest` for `packages/app`: all 2776 tests pass.
- `e2e/battle-tower.spec.ts` "the clerk's Start…" on phone and desktop. This is where the focused check was added (the arrow, the
  table). Also "the elevator tapped from across the lobby" on both.
- `e2e/tower-party.spec.ts` (both tests) and `e2e/town-street.spec.ts` "the Battle Tower", on phone, because they use the clerk's
  card (the "Go to the tower" chat button, taps on him).
- Frame rate (phone, CPU slowed 4x, 5 s in the floor room after a win): with the arrow, 205 frames, p50 16.7 ms, p95 33.4 ms. With the
  arrow turned off, 211 frames, p50 16.7 ms, p95 33.4 ms. No measurable difference.
- The town `@perf` spec ("Chess Town on a slow phone") misses its load budget in this container (18 to 20 s against 8 s). `main` at
  c0fcaddc misses by the same amount (17.8 s and 19.7 s), so it's this machine, not this change. This PR doesn't touch the town's load.
