# Device trust: one seat per account, checked host scores, server-only shared answers

A device's word now counts only when it's checked (outside review: findings 1 and 5, "Squads and matchmaking", part 2
rules items 3 and 6). Lane: **full** (fair play, protocol core, online sync): needs the `full-e2e` label and a review.

## What changed
- **One seat per account per lobby** (main lobby, Squads; a guest's own account too): a second tab or device takes over
  that seat as a reconnect does. Squads now refuses a seat token from another account (`SQUADS_OTHER_ACCOUNT`), as the
  main lobby does; a refused Squads hello no longer marks the real seat's player gone.
- **Judges:** never two of one account (`drawJudges` by owner); Squads' bot work never two devices of one party.
  Claimed speed capped at `JUDGES.maxNps` (5M, was 50M), `speedWeight` 1 (was 3) until speed is measured (ROADMAP line).
- **Host scores** are checked as a judge's are (`hostBoardOk`: legal best move, chances 0 to 1, every pick and bot
  scored). A board that fails scores every pick the same (the "nobody could score it" rule); a left-out pick no longer
  "counts as the best". The engine server's re-check skips such boards.
- **Squads' shared cache** (`TOP_CACHE`) holds only the engine server's answers.
- **Message fields:** `bossMove.move` must be a string (≤ 128); `botPlan` keeps only this round's picking bots with
  allowed moves; `crossCheck` logs the first 20, cut to 200 characters; the lobby's Durable Object drops a message that
  isn't an object with a type, or `scores` without a list of boards.

Player-visible: none for honest players (a second tab now joins your own seat instead of a new one). No new text but
the Squads refusal "That seat belongs to another account." (same as the main lobby's).
