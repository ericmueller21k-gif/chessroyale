## Dash's lying-down clock tap: bigger, over the board's edge, and redrawn

Eric, Nov 26: "could we scale him up maybe like 3 times where he's actually hanging over the board a bit ... you can still click on your own tiles that he's blocking ... he shouldn't move", and the pose "kind of wonky ... your feet are up in the air ... resting one hand on his head".

**What changed**
- `characters/dash-lie.ts` redrawn: on his belly, his cheek on the near paw, the far paw tapping the clock (same two taps a loop and the same sounds), lower legs up behind him with the sneakers' soles to the sky, kicking lazily, tail curling. Drawn at twice his standing sprite's detail, from his own shapes (head, cap, ears, jacket, sneakers) and palette. The zip in and out are redrawn to match.
- `DashStall.tsx` `placeLie`: `DASH_TAP.lieScale` (3) times his old size, capped so he reaches at most `DASH_TAP.overhang` (0.8) of a square over the board's bottom edge (never a whole square) and fits beside your time. His belly sits on the dock box's bottom and his clock stays against your time. Taps still go through him (`pointer-events: none`), and he's placed once, so he never moves while you pick.
- Settings: `DASH_TAP.lieScale: 3`, `DASH_TAP.overhang: 0.8`. Decision: `docs/decisions/2026-11-26-dash-tap-bigger.md`. `docs/areas/bosses.md` updated.

**What players see**
- He's about 2.1x his old size on a phone (320 and 390 px) and 2.7x on a computer (1280 x 800, 1536 x 730), hanging over your back rank. A true 3x would reach 1.4 squares up on a phone, so the overhang cap decides the size there. Raise `overhang` toward 1 to make him bigger.
- Pieces under him can be tapped and moved; he keeps lying and tapping until your move is in.

**Checks changed (old → new)**
- `e2e/dash.spec.ts`, "the clock tap (&power=clocktap)…":
  - Overlap check: was "his cap's top never over the board's squares". Now "his top over the board's edge, by at most `overhang` of a square". This follows eric.md's whole-on-screen rule ("over the board ... is fine").
  - Words check: was "his words' bottom within 80 px of the board's bottom". Now "within 40 px over his top".
  - Move 1 is now e2e3, so the pieces on your back rank under him have moves.
  - The move that gets him up was `submitMove`. It's now real taps on a piece under him: the piece is selected and shows its moves, he stays (same box, still tapping), then the move goes in and he zips off.
- `packages/app/test/dash-wired.test.ts`:
  - The palette check's effect keys are now dust and shadow only. The speed and tap lines use his own colors.
  - New test: his top and floor rows are where `placeLie` reads them, his sneakers are up in the air, and he taps twice a loop.

Ran locally: typecheck; app and core unit tests (3451 passed); `dash.spec.ts`'s clock tap tests on phone and desktop (5 passed). Lane: characters (light/targeted) plus Dash's spec.
