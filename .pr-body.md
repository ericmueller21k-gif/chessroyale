## Town: a tap on an arrow key walks one square, not two

Eric, Nov 17: "when you tap on keys u only move 1 square in the town, not 2".

**What changed:** `Walker.begin` still asks the keys for the next square as a step begins, so a held key runs on with no stop
between squares. Now that square is dropped if the key is let go before the step ends, and the step eases into its stop from
that moment (`ease` gets a `from`: the place and speed carry on unbroken, and it ends at the same speed as a whole last step;
with `from` 0 it is the old curve exactly). Shared by the town and the shop rooms, by the arrows, WASD and the on-screen keys.
Bots and bosses are unchanged: no runner, so they never get a `holding` check.

**Player-visible:** a tap walks one square. A held key runs on as before, and now stops after the step it's on when let go.
Before, it took one square more.

**Checks:** targeted lane. `packages/app/test/town-arrows.test.ts` gets new tests (taps of 16/48/80 ms → one square, eased,
no jump; held across two step-times → no stop between squares, then stops after the step it's on; `ease` matches the old
curve at `from` 0 and keeps place and speed at a late `from`). All five fail on the old code. No checks changed or removed.
No e2e edits: `e2e/town-controls.spec.ts` `arrowStep` (it lets go once you leave your square, and reads only the direction)
and `e2e/town.spec.ts`'s held-arrow test (≥ 3 squares) still describe the new behavior. A real-key capture in the built app
(desktop): ArrowUp/Down at 20, 50 and 80 ms, W/S and the on-screen arrows each walked exactly 1 square, and a 250 ms hold
walked 3. No guard in `docs/rules/eric.md` covers this.

Known edge, left as before: a press and release inside one frame walks no square.
