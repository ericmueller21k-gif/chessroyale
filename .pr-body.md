# Residents, PR 1: the server's side (the flag, the route, the kill switch, fair play, engine and host rules)

**Full lane**: add the `full-e2e` label (accounts, stored data, fair play, the server). Effort xhigh. Design: `notes/residents-design.md`, PR 1.

## What
- **`users.resident`** (a column, as the schema adds them; index `users_resident`). Residents are not `test`: they count in "Top N%", 1 v 1's top places and name search.
- **`/api/admin/residents`** (`residents.ts`, a copy of the test accounts' route). Callers: an admin, the e2e's key, Access with `RESIDENTS_CLIENT_ID`'s token (`""` in wrangler.jsonc until the token exists), or `TEST_ACCOUNTS_CLIENT_ID`'s for tonight's runs.
  - `POST {name, look?}`: makes `<slug>@resident.hunchess` (`cleanName` + the filter). A new one gets its look and 0-3 common locker items, one ledger op `resident-seed:<ledger id>`. Signs the program in: its own live cookie is kept, otherwise a new session that ends the resident's others.
  - `GET`: the residents, today's numbers (results by mode, 1 v 1 games, those against people), "would have" counts, the last heartbeat.
  - `POST /beat`: the program's heartbeat (JSON object, max 16 KB), the latest kept in `meta`.
  - The POSTs are refused while paused; GET still answers.
- **`PAUSE_RESIDENTS`**: a resident's session gets 503 "Residents are paused." on every `/api/*` route (sockets too). Lobby, DuelGame, SquadsLobby and SquadsParty refuse a resident's hello and close its socket on its next message (a disconnect, so its seat goes to a bot under the usual leaving rules). It's the server's own switch: never in `/api/status`.
- **Fair play**: residents' matches are recorded as anyone's, plus `fairplay_matches.would` (what detection would have done). Detection, reports, evasion and the automated reviewer never open or move a resident's case; a person still can. Residents' own reports are dropped. A real player's report of a resident is kept, with no case. Residents are never pooled, and the deep re-check skips them.
- **Engine server**: in a Crowd, raid or Classic lobby whose connected people are all residents, it's never asked (`residentsOnly`). A resident's hello never wakes it, and a real person connecting turns it back on.
- **Host rule**: `pickHost`/`hostNow` order hosts as trusted people, then trusted residents, then anyone.
