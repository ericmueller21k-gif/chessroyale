**chat.spec (Eric's guard): the wait leaves time for the two taps**

**What changed:** `untilMyTurn` in `e2e/chat.spec.ts` now waits for at least 3 s left on the phone's move, at all three of its
calls. With less, it waits for the phone's next turn. Eric's OK: Nov 17, "yes" (this is his guard test, `docs/rules/eric.md`).

**Cause of the flake** (main, Nov 16, twice, `chat.spec.ts:283` on desktop): the helper only checked that the turn had begun.
It could return inside the 300 ms late grace past the deadline, while the client still says "play". The round then locked
between the two taps, the second tap rightly did nothing, and `__picked` stayed `[]`. Both CI snapshots show the phone's team
turn ending without its pick (49 of 50 votes). Reproduced 4 of 4 locally; fixed, 0 of 4 under the same timing. Not an app bug:
no tap was lost while the turn was open.

**Changed checks:** only the helper's wait. No assertion changed or removed. Player-visible changes: none.

**Tested:** `e2e/chat.spec.ts` on desktop (2 passed) and phone (skipped by design: one desktop run plays both devices).

Decision: `docs/decisions/2026-11-17-chat-guard-wait.md`.
