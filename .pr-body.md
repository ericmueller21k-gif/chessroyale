# Chandy's art, drawn ahead of his rules

Eric approved Chandy's design on Nov 16 ("I like how you have it done"), with one change: no aim lines while Dodge's vote runs. This PR is the boss lane's art job. His rules come in a separate job (`docs/ideas/chandy-wiring.md`, with `DESIGN.md` on `stills/boss-chandy` as the spec).

## What changed
- **His figure** (`characters/chandy.ts`): an olive alligator standing upright, with war paint, an eye strap with a cyan scope lens, and a sling. The energy rifle is his full length and grows over 6 stages. Every animation (idle, point, fire, aim, stow) comes at every stage (`chandyAnim`). The bust and select-tile framing are in the same file.
- **His clones**: the red-green look `CHANDY_CLONE_PALETTE` (kept from round 1's scrambled GIF), aiming level or down, with a teleport blink, an appearance and a fading afterimage.
- **Board art** (`characters/effects/chandy.ts`, 11 `chandy…` EFFECTS on the shared board-effects canvas):
  - the crosshair, with pips counting the turns left
  - scatter shot's burst and blocked squares
  - the opaque beam
  - bubble shields
  - dizzy stars
  - the queen lock's aim, round, trail and shatter
  - Dodge's line flash and blasts
- **Components**: `ChandyUpgrade.tsx` (the upgrade window in the command-window look, 2 s) and `ChandyDodge.tsx` (scope-in, slide, blank board, edge clones with teleports and no lines during the vote, flash, blast, result). Both take their state as props.
- **His kit** is in `ART_KITS` (kits.ts), not `BOSS_KITS`. He isn't in `CHARACTERS` either. Both lists are the roster's: their tests walk every playable boss, and his wide frame would shrink every boss's side figure.
- **Sounds**: placeholders from other bosses' packs (`CHANDY_SOUNDS`). `CHANDY_CUES` lists the sound each cue should get.
- **Admin preview** at `/chandy` (admins only, in no menu: `main.tsx`). In dev it's `chandy-preview.html`.
- **Tools**: `frames:character` reads `ART_KITS` (`kit=Chandy`) and marks the welcome walkthrough as seen (it was blocking the taps). BossEffect's shared frame loop is now exported.
