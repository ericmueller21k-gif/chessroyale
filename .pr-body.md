## Facing fix

**Cause.** Birdie's town figure (the one the room, his challenge and the town square draw) has the id `birdie-town`
(`birdie-figure.ts` BIRDIE_TOWN). `artFacing` (`characters/sprites.ts`) only knew `birdie: "left"`, so for `birdie-town` it returned
null and every caller fell back to "the art faces right". On his mark (`bossFace: 1`, east) the room drew him unmirrored, so his
left-facing train and face pointed west, away from the table, through every beat; walking in the town square he faced backward too.

**Fix.** `artFacing` reads a boss's `<id>-town` figure as its character (one place). In his challenge the test hook now reports
`looks: 1, mirrored: true` from the face beat through the table beat; the other bosses' facing is unchanged (none of their town
figures uses a `-town` id; Chandy's `chandy-town` still has no known facing, as before).

**Checks (changed old → new).**
- `packages/app/test/boss-challenge.test.ts`: new check, "Birdie faces the table on his mark" (his marks face east, his town
  figure's art reads as left so it's mirrored, and every boss's town figure faces as its character's art does). Fails without the fix.
- `e2e/birdie-home.spec.ts`, his challenge: new check, his figure faces east (`looks` 1) in every beat from the marks on.
- Nothing removed.

Proved: typecheck; `boss-challenge`, `shop-room`, `shop-birdie` unit tests; `e2e/birdie-home.spec.ts` challenge (phone) and
`e2e/boss-challenge.spec.ts` Babur's challenge (phone), both pass. Stills retaken on `stills/birdie-lobby-4`: `gates-lowering-phone.png`,
`gates-down-phone.png`, `gates-table-phone.png`.
