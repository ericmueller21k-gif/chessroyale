# Boss alone and a raid on a computer: the shared game frame

The shared game frame's third job (the director's brief, Nov 26): Boss alone and a raid (solo and online) on the 50 v 50's frame,
reused, with Eric's two-line boss bar kept ("I like the boss bar bigger"). Targeted lane plus the boss lane's kit (screens and a
shared frame; no rules, server or protocol).

## What changed
- `board-size.css`: the 50 v 50's frame rule (`--cf-*` sizes, gap, drawing step) now takes two knobs, `--cf-left` (the left column's
  width: the scoreboard's 340 px, or Boss alone's boss column) and `--cf-side-most` (the column's most: 300, or 340 for Boss alone's pawn
  and God King). A new `bf-frame` block puts Boss alone and a raid on it from 1100 px wide with a mouse: the left column (a raid's
  scoreboard; Boss alone's boss column), the board with its strips (the boss bar over it, the dock's row under it) and the column beside it,
  centered as one block, 28 px either side of the board (Boss alone's boss column takes half the room the block leaves, so Babur's pump
  keeps its room beside him: centered, it had none at 1920 × 1080 and his board fell to the taller bar's 832). The gear row without its old shift. The Moves panel from the board's top line down
  to 12 px over the God King's frame; an online raid's chat as before (under the moves, down to the God King's room).
- A raid's left column mirrors the right one (the director's review of the mock): the scoreboard's table from the board's top down to over
  the boss (his frame and room for five lines of his words, measured in `board-foot.ts`), its rows scrolling; the boss under it on the
  board's foot, covering nothing. A raid's block is centered: with the menu's labels it keeps at least 40 px either side, else icons.
- `game.ts` `bossFramed`: the one test for which battles take the frame (Boss alone and raids; not a Battle Tower floor, not the Hidden
  Boss's fight, whose phases keep phase 1's boxes). Its class goes on every screen of the battle: Play, Boss, Reveal, so nothing moves
  between your move and the boss's.
- At 125% and 150% the board's box is on its drawing step now (the frame's), so the bar and the dock's row are the drawn board's width
  (they were 5.4 px wider at 1536 × 730).

  other sizes the equal gaps moved the board 12 to 14 px right, so their room changed a little: your pawn and the God King 1.2 → 1.15 at
  1280 × 800 and 1.6 → 1.55 at 1920 × 1080; the boss 3.25 → 3.4 at 1440 × 900. A raid's figures are unchanged.

## Round 2: one figure size in every fight
CI (shard 4) failed `e2e/side-same-size.spec.ts:16` [desktop]: "1280x800: your pawn in the hidden boss's fight 201.6 as tall as in
tiger's 193.2".

- **Cause:** the hidden boss's fight was left out of the frame (`bossFramed`), so it kept the old layout. Measured on this branch, the
  framed board stands 12 px right of the old place at 1280 × 800, 1440 × 900, 1536 × 864, 1920 × 960 and 1920 × 1080 (14 at 1100 × 800,
  11 left at 1366 × 768: the old layout centered the board in its column), and the figures are sized from the room either side of the board.
  So his fight's figures came out bigger or smaller than every other boss's: your pawn and the God King 1.2 v 1.15 at 1280 × 800, 1.55 v 1.5
  at 1536 × 864, 1.6 v 1.55 at 1920 × 1080; the boss 3.25 v 3.4 at 1440 × 900.
- **Fix, option (b): the hidden boss's whole fight takes the frame too.** `bossFramed` no longer leaves him out, and his phase change and
  phase 2 screens (`Recluse2.tsx`) carry `bf-frame` the same way. They are phase 1's skeleton (the four boards inside phase 1's board
  square, the same dock and Moves panel, no computer layout of their own), so they keep phase 1's boxes in the frame as they did outside it
  (`hidden-boss-room.spec.ts` checks the boxes stay put through his fight: it passes). Why not (a): keeping the old scale inside the frame
  would mean sizing the figures from where the old layout *would* have put the board, a second copy of its sums kept in step by hand.
  With (b) every boss fight uses one layout, so one size follows from it. 3 lines of code.
- **What a player sees:** on a computer from 1100 px wide, the hidden boss's fight (phase 1, his phase change, phase 2) is in the shared
  frame like every other boss: 28 px either side of the board, the taller Moves panel, and his figures at every other boss's size. The board
  is the same size. A phone, a tablet and under 1100 px: unchanged. (This replaces the line above that said his fight is unchanged.)
- **Checks changed (old → new):** none. The spec's rule untouched.
- **Run locally (desktop):** `side-same-size.spec.ts`, `desktop-board.spec.ts` (all, Eric's column rule included), `board-layout.spec.ts`,
  `boss-side.spec.ts`, `hidden-boss-room.spec.ts`, and `recluse.spec.ts`'s computer tests (the board's size to the split, his words, phase 2
