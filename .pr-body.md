# Battle Tower: its music

Eric approved the tower's theme (Nov 19: "this is the one, nice work"; "keep the sound more muted overall, it's the background battle tower music").

**What changed**
- `packages/app/public/music/tower-loop.mp3` (from `tower-music/` on `stills/trailer-v2`, re-encoded 192 → 128 kbps as every theme since Nov 16; same length, aligned at 0, `pad`, `pad + loopSeconds`) and its `tower` entry in `themes.json` (a loop with no intro, 160 s). No `.ogg` (no other theme ships one).
- Rooms: the tower's lobby and floor room drop `music: null`, so they play their home's theme, `tower` (shops.ts).
- Battles: a tower floor's battle plays `tower` in place of its bosses' own (App.tsx `musicBattle` → music.ts `towerBattle`; its `boss` is the tower's key, so a floor's next boss doesn't restart it).
- Back from a floor's battle, the room's theme loads and plays as the room shows (Town.tsx `towerBack`).
- Docs: music README section, battle-tower.md one line, decision note `docs/decisions/2026-11-19-tower-music.md`.

**Player-visible:** the tower's own music in its lobby, its floor room and every floor's battle; the usual music volume and mute apply. It's fetched with the other themes during the town's loading (about 2.6 MB more).

**Checks:** one added (`tower-floor.test.ts`, "the tower's theme": both rooms and a floor's battle pick `tower`, and its plan from themes.json). None changed or removed. Typecheck, `tower*`, `music` and `recluse-building` unit tests pass locally.

Lane: light (an approved asset and data, wired as the boss themes are).
