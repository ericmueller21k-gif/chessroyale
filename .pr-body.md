## Battle Tower PR 6: several bosses in the match runner

**Lane: full** (the runner's core; add `full-e2e`). Design: `docs/decisions/2026-11-18-battle-tower-design.md` §2; calls made:
`docs/decisions/2026-11-18-several-bosses.md`. No screens (PR 10), no tower rules (PR 8): `settings.bossIds` (2-3 ids) starts a raid with them.

**What changed**
- Core: `MatchState.bosses` (each boss's own part, `BossOwn`), `focus`, `moved`; `BATTLE_TOWER.rageScale` (1, 0.75, 0.6) and `apart` (Hollow, Smudgy); `settings.bossIds`.
- Runner: `state.boss` is always the boss in focus in full (so every boss file runs as alone); the deal (`startSide`), turn order in the order
  dealt, out of turn via `BossRules.outOfTurn` (Wrangler's duel reply, Folio's move again after Revision), Hollow's second move keeps his focus;
  every boss's `prepareTurn` each crowd turn, each ultimate at his own turn change, one a turn change (`prepareTurn`'s `hold`); the moments queue
  (the view's `events`: every boss's, the one in focus first); the race brain, Dash's clock, Hollow's dark, the judge, fair play, the Last Stand
  and `afterCrowdMove` over the whole side.
- Dispatcher: `side*` functions (`sideCrowdAllowed`: every boss's marks merged, every filter, one lift; `sideBossAllowed`: the side's guards, but
  Sawyer's first move binds only his: `ownStops`), `sideRates`. A side of one calls today's functions.
- Call made: the design's "the second waits a turn" became "each ultimate at its own boss's turn change" (after his move for `triggerAtHisTurn`,
  before it otherwise): a first try left Wrangler's line-up made but its showdown held, and he moved twice.

**The 1-boss guard** (no existing assertion edited): the whole chess suite (1516 tests: the boss kit, every boss's tests, the four layer twins)
and the server's boss, lobby and parity tests (250) pass; typecheck clean. One boss: `bosses` unset, every changed runner line branches on
`several()` or calls a `side*` function that delegates to today's for a side of one.

**Pairs and threes** (`boss-kit-side.test.ts`, crowd White from the start, every ultimate triggered, the kit's invariants after every
step): 152 pairs (all but Hollow + Smudgy) and 12 seeded threes on CI's seed; by hand 3 seeds a pair (456 battles). Two pairs break,
reported (not fixed) in `PAIR_BREAKS` (still asserted to break, `it.fails`; the director's call):
- **Smudgy + Alfil** (seeds 1-3): Repaint paints his pawn the crowd's while Alfil's Slam holds crowd pawns in the air: 9 crowd pawns as
  they land. **Proposed:** Smudgy | Repaint | IN, WITH A RULE: never while another boss's power holds pieces off the board (Alfil's
  Slam): it waits, its warning kept. (Or the pair joins `apart`.)
- **Alfil + Jefferson** (seed 2): the Slam lifted the crowd's c1 bishop in front of a boss pawn on c2 (queens next move). **Proposed:**
  Alfil | Mammoth Slam | IN, WITH A RULE: never lifts a crowd piece that stands in a boss pawn's way to promoting (likely Alfil's alone too).
- Parity (`boss-kit-parity.test.ts`, new describe): every tower boss in a pair plus two threes, solo and online agree (11 of 11). Two harness
  limits, true with one boss too: Timber goes first with his ultimate's switch (his scraps run on the server's clock online, none solo), and the
  side's cap is 16 (a moment after the cap's last crowd move is read solo, never sent online).
- `several-bosses.test.ts`: the deal and `apart`, the turn order, the merged layer (the side's moves within each boss's), one ultimate a turn
  change, `rageScale`, Hollow's second move, Folio's Revision, Wrangler's reply out of turn.

**Changed checks (old → new)**
- `boss-kit-lib.ts`: `kitSettings` takes an optional `bosses` (sets `bossIds`, `bossNoStrikes`); `runBattle`'s "the runner met" check compares
  the side's ids (one boss: his id, as before); `seen()` reads every boss's events (one boss: his, as before).
- `boss-kit-parity.test.ts`: `online()` takes an optional settings patch (default none: the one-boss checks unchanged).
- None removed.

**For the auditor (after this PR):** the focus swap (`asBoss`, `setFocus`), `prepareSide`'s ultimate gate, the piece-following of one boss's
pieces moved by another (each boss's `afterBossMove` runs on his own moves only: the kit found no break from it, but it's the likeliest next).
