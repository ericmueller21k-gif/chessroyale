## Fix main: Don Dough's splash music decoded late wasn't logged as carried

Targeted lane (the music's late path, one spec). Fixes main's red at f0e89d7: `[desktop] e2e/zebra-home.spec.ts:273` (run 45447937791, shard 4).

**Cause.** The failing log, `["music:splash:zebra"]` with no `music:carry`, `music:splashEnd` or `music:start`, comes from only one place: `splashMusic`'s late branch. That branch runs when the theme finishes decoding after the battle has registered (`startSolo` sets the match while the splash covers the screen). It hands the music to the battle correctly (one start, no restart) but never logged the carry. The CI trace shows the decode's read at 17:44:06.15 and the battle starting at 08.09, with three Stockfish workers and Maia loading at the same moment, so a decode of about 2 s is plausible on that runner.
**Not #920.** f0e89d7 passes locally. With every decode delayed by 0.7 s (a temporary `decodeAudioData` wrapper), **8423926 fails the same way**, with the same log, as f0e89d7 did. It's a latent race that a busy runner exposed.

**Fix.** `packages/app/src/music.ts` `splashMusic`: the late branch logs `music:carry:<boss>` after `music:splash:<boss>`, as `bossMusic` does. What players hear is unchanged.
