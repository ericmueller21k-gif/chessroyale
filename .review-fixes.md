# PR #835 (Battle Tower party play): the review's findings, fixed

The review: `REVIEW.md` on `review/tower-party`. Each finding was checked against the code first; all twelve held up. The decision
note (`docs/decisions/2026-11-20-tower-party.md`, calls 2, 11 and the new 13-17) and the area pages (`docs/areas/battle-tower.md`,
`docs/areas/chat.md`) say what changed. Eric's guard tests in `docs/rules/eric.md` are untouched.

## The findings

1. **Blocker, fixed.** Each copy keeps the floor its party reached, `members.party`. Only the party's own writes set it: a START's commit
   (the floor begun) and the room's write (the floor after). The alone routes never move it. `partyPlan` takes the lowest of
   `min(copy.floor, copy.party ?? copy.floor)`, and the copies above it go back to it. The run's deal on that floor: when a copy's first
   floor alone begins, the deal it stands on (still its party floor then) is kept beside it (`members.partyMet`), so a party whose members
   all played on alone still climbs on from the party's floor with the party's own deal. The review's appendix test is in, as written,
   plus the commit, the crate and the party best after it, and a second case (one member alone, the other not).
2. **Fixed.** One party line an account: the town's room keeps each account's line, and a newer one replaces the older. A new one comes at
   most every `TOWER_PARTY.openGapMs` (60 s, new setting); too soon, the leader is told "You can open a party to the town once a minute.
   Try again in N s." The party's Durable Object runs the town chat's checks for the leader first (`townPartyPoster`: banned, a moderator's
   chat ban, the chat paused; their words as townPostResponse's), and the line goes out under the account's name (filtered as a typed
   line's). Sign-in isn't asked again (the party takes only accounts where signing in is set up; locally the e2e's guests open parties).
   A deleted account's party line, or one after a moderator's name reset, goes with their other lines.
3. **Fixed.** The open line lapses at `open.at + TOWN_CHAT.lineMs` (`at` is reset when it goes up), in `takeLapsedOpen`, with that time
   in `nextAlarm`, so Open party comes back live. The `openLineMinutes` sentence in the `TOWER_PARTY` comment is gone (rewritten for the
   new rules). **Found on the way (decision call 17):** a Boss Battle party stays "placed" after its battle, so after a tower party's
   first floor an Open party went up and was taken out at once. The line now lapses on a floor under way (START to the room's end).
4. **Fixed.** `townRemoveParty(n, code)` (the hub) / `TownChat.removeParty` remove a line only while it's that party's
   (`sys === "party" && party === code`).
5. **Fixed.** `preparePartyFloor` refuses a seat whose account is gone (`{ gone }`: "Bo's account has been deleted. Kick them to climb
   on."), and a new run's inserts take `user_id` from `(SELECT id FROM users WHERE id = ?)`, so an account deleted between the plan and
   the commit makes the whole batch fail (NOT NULL): nobody's copy written, the id in nobody's `members`.
6. **Fixed (the director's call, decision call 13).** The server refuses Kick from START until the room says the floor is over
   (`towerFloorOn`), and the view carries `tower.floor`. The clerk's panel had no Kick at all, so there was nothing to grey: each other
   member's seat now has a small Kick, the leader's (asked first, "Kick Bo?"), greyed for the others and during a floor.
7. **Fixed.** `writeTowerEnds` (lobby-do.ts) goes past an entry that fails instead of stopping (each member has one end a floor). A
   crate's grant that keeps failing stops holding its floor up `MATCH_SAVE.strictMinutes` after the entry's first failed try
   (`failedAt`): the floor is written without its crate, logged (`towerCrate for …`, `lobby.towerCrateFailed`).
8. **Fixed.** The merge rebuilds `uids` with `json_each` (the guest's id becomes the account's, skipped where the account's is already
   there), and the statement is in the batch only when the guest holds a party copy (`holdsPartyCopy`, one indexed read before the
   batch; never in production, where guests can't sit in a tower party).
9. **Fixed.** `?win=1` only where `env.TEST_SWITCHES === "1"`, never an admin on the live site.
10. **Fixed (the director's call, decision call 14).** A continue plan carries `back` (each copy above the party's floor and its floor):
    "Your climb together goes on: floor 5. Ann's climb goes back from floor 30, Bo's from floor 12." READY asks first ("Back to floor
    5?") when it's yours, and the leader's START needs their confirm (the server checks, as for ends). The floor room's up button's words
    come from the plan ("Up to floor 5", "New climb: floor 1", "START" while it's read).
11. **Fixed (the director's call, decision call 15).** The clerk's panel is one layout in both states, nothing swapped: the party's four
    seats, the line, READY or START and Leave party, then the solo climb (Continue or Start, Start over, Alone). Outside a party the
    party's half shows greyed; inside one, the solo half. The line keeps room for three lines. The town line's Join reads "Joined" only on
    the party's `welcome` (`joinTowerPartyAnswer`); turned away, the party's words show in your chat as a line only you see (and Join is
    live again; a closed party reads "Closed"). The pill is as wide as its widest label (every label laid in one grid cell, the others
    unseen), so a change never widens it. The "see the clerk" hint moved to that private line on joining. **Then superseded by the
    director's delta below:** the line's button is "Go to the tower" (one label), and Join (with the same welcome-or-words answer and the
    same fixed-size pill) is on the clerk's list of open parties. **Found in the stills:** the
    seats' two columns took their width from the names ("Ann Open (you) ★" widened its half as someone joined); they're two equal
    halves now (`minmax(0, 1fr)`), a long name cut with "…".
12. **Tests added:** (a) the appendix's test and more (server `tower-party.test.ts`); (b) the party's Durable Object on a fake runtime with
    a real in-memory database (new `tower-party-do.test.ts`): START with the plan everyone readied for, a plan changed between READY and
    START, the room made but the commit refused, the raid kill switch, and the open line's checks, its one line an account and its lapse;
    (c) `writeTowerEnds`: a failing member passed over, the crate's lenient fallback after `strictMinutes`; (d) the open line's expiry
    (the party core, and the Durable Object's alarm); (e) one `expect` in `e2e/tower-party.spec.ts` that the Join press left `.tc-form`
    closed (`data-open="0"`), with the pill's width the same before and after.
