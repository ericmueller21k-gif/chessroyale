# Review fixes: PR #837 (Jefferson's Roman candle: char first, then gone)

Each finding was checked in the code before anything changed. All five were real.

## 1. Important: the crowd's reveal drew the char marks as they'd be after the fire: fixed

- **Cause.** `CrowdReveal` drew the marks from `match.boss`, and by then that state already counts the move and the fire after it.
- **Fix.** `battleDecor` now takes an options object. Its new `reveal` option (`{ move, fenBefore, played }`) gives the reveal its own view of the marks, and it covers all three cases in the review:
  - **Before the fire** (`backBeforeFire` in `BossPowers.tsx`): a mark this turn's fire added or added to is one less, and dropped when that leaves none. A charred piece this fire burns (`hits > 1`) gets its mark back.
    - Case (a): a fresh char stays plain until the boss screen's flames.
    - Case (b): a burning charred piece stays charred and doesn't flicker plain.
  - **Before the move is played** (case c): marks go back to where they stood. This uses the new `unfollowSplats` in `packages/chess/src/bosses/boingo.ts`, which is `followSplats` run in reverse: the moved piece's mark returns to its square (a promoted pawn's too), and so does castling's rook's.
- **Also fixed for Boingo.** His splats were drawn on the destination square before the move landed, the same way. They now go through the same path.
- **One case left as it was.** During a Last Stand the move is undone, so the reveal keeps the state's marks there, as before.
- **Watched.** Every frame of the reveal is sampled by the new e2e (below).
  - With the fix: passes.
  - With `Crowd.tsx` put back to `battleDecor(match.boss)`: fails with "f2, charred by turn 10's fire, drawn charred on its reveal". So the check catches the bug.

## 2. A newly charred piece wore the look from the burn's first frame: fixed

- **Fix.** `grexDecor` now leaves out a first-time char (`charred` with `hits <= 1`) while `now < since + BURN.goneAt`. That's the same window that keeps a burning piece's look, mirrored. A piece reached before keeps its look throughout (at `candleHitsToBurn` 3).
- **Watched frame by frame** with `scripts/frames-grex-char.mjs` on an iPhone 13:
  - First hit: the boss screen starts at 67 ms, plain, and the pawn turns charred at 154 ms. `BURN.goneAt` is 80 ms: the burn's flare, just as the tile's `burnOut` drops from its full flames to dying ones.
  - Second hit: the burning charred pawn keeps its look until it comes off. The newly hit h2 pawn waits for its flare.
  - The slide: the look moves with the piece. No lost frames.

## 3. On a shared floor, another boss's power that moves a charred piece left the mark behind: fixed

- **Fix.** `GREX.followBoard` drops a mark whose square no longer holds a crowd piece of its kind, immediately, as a capture would, the way Sawyer and Alfil do. It shares a new `marksStanding` helper with `wearOff`.
- **Boingo too.** He had the same gap, so `BOINGO.followBoard` now does the same for his splats. It's one shared function, so the two can't drift apart.
- **Limit, recorded in `docs/areas/bosses.md`.** Marks follow squares, not piece identities. If the same power puts another piece of the same kind on the square, that piece still wears the mark.

## 4. "Your king is fireproof." when the tile fizzled over a pinned piece: fixed

- `fireLine(burnt, fen?, toBurn?)` now checks the board after the fire (`boss.board.fen`). If the king is on a fizzled square, the line is "Your king is fireproof." as before.
- Otherwise it says "Fizzled: it shields a king" ("they shield" for several). This covers both fizzle cases: a crowd piece pinned to its own king, and one whose loss would put the boss's king in check.
- With no fen it falls back to the king's line.
