# The delta review's findings on PR #835: what was done

The delta review is `REVIEW.md` on `review/tower-party-delta`, section "New findings". I checked each finding against the code before
changing anything, and all nine held. The calls are in `docs/decisions/2026-11-20-tower-party.md`, items 20 to 26.

## The findings

1. **Fixed (important): `GET /api/tower/parties` no longer fans out per reader.**
   - Each Worker instance makes the list at most once every `TOWER_PARTY.listCacheMs` (3 s, a new setting) per chat room, and every reader
     on that instance shares it, including while it's being made (one promise). See `townOpenParties` in `town-chat-api.ts`.
   - The codes come from the instance's own copy of the town's room, the one `townSlice` already keeps for `TOWN_CHAT.readCacheMs`, so
     while the town's readers keep that copy fresh, the list costs no hub call. When it's stale, it costs one `townRead`.
   - The hub's per-request `townParties` is gone, and so is `TownChat.partyCodes`. The pure `partyCodesOf(snapshot)` replaces them.
   - The codes are deduped, newest first, and filtered before the cap: up to 2 × `listMax` are asked, and at most `listMax` (12) are
     kept. So per instance per 3 s it's at most one hub read and 24 party calls.
   - The list only goes to whoever could join a party. That's an account (a cached session lookup, `cachedUserId`), and a signed-in one
     where signing in is set up (`signedInReader`, cached a minute: the party's own rule). Anyone else gets `{ parties: [], message:
     "Sign in to climb with a party." }`, the card shows those words in the list's box, and it stops polling.
   - `listPollMs` and `listMax` stay as settings.
   - Not done: a banned account still gets the list. Checking for a ban would cost D1 reads on every poll, and their Join is refused
     with the ban's words anyway.
2. **Fixed: the "floor under way" flag now clears when a floor's room ends without results.**
   - While the flag is up, the party asks its floor's room every `TOWER_PARTY.floorCheckMs` (a minute, a new setting) whether the battle
     from its START is over there (`PartyCore.takeFloorCheck`, set by `placedIn`, on the party's alarm).
   - The room answers with its new `partyBattleDone(code, start)`: true when it has closed, is another battle's room now, or has reached
     its results.
   - When it's over, the party runs `battleOver` itself. Kick, Open party and the list come back, and the plan is read again.
3. **Fixed (Eric's "nothing shifts"): the plan line no longer grows.**
   - `.tw-plan` now has a fixed `height` of three lines, not a `min-height`. There's no `overflow: hidden`: the words are written to fit
     instead (LESSONS: a hidden overflow fails no test).
   - The words moved to a pure module, `packages/app/src/tower-plan.ts`. They name your own climb with its floor and count the others:
     "Your climb together goes on: floor 5. Yours goes back from floor 12, and 2 more." / "… 2 party climbs go back to it." / "A new
     climb from floor 1. It ends yours on floor 6, and 2 more." / "It ends 3 party climbs."
   - The leader's "Waiting for Bo, Cy." is now a count, "2 not ready yet." The seats' ✓ show who. I kept a count so a greyed START
     still says why.
   - Everyone is still asked about their own climb as they READY.
   - The e2e measures the longest words in the card's own font, at 92% of its width (an iPhone's font runs wider). Those are a party of
     four's worst plan plus "3 not ready yet.", and the longest refusals: `needTwo`, `openWait`, a 16-character name's "account has been
     deleted", the ban line and the raid pause. It checks that each fits and that the box stays three lines high.
4. **Fixed: the list honors the age gate and the town chat's pause.** It answers an empty list on a device the age gate marked too young
   and while the town's chat is paused, the same cases where the town shows no party lines.
5. **Fixed: the list box's 3 px overflow.** The last row has `margin-bottom: 0`, so three rows fit exactly, and `.tw-open-list` has
   `scrollbar-gutter: stable`. The e2e checks that three rows fit without scrolling.
6. **Fixed, both parts.**
   - "Go to the tower" no longer carries over to the next visit. The clerk flag now rides with the walk: the controller's
     `goIn(i, clerk)` keeps it beside `goingIn` and passes it to `onGoIn`. Any walk elsewhere (`walkOff`) or a challenge drops it with
     the walk, so a later way in by the door is the door's. The `toClerk` ref is gone.
   - On the street the pill is greyed while a boss's challenge plays. `noChallenge` mirrors `flow` as state, and `onGoTower` is passed
     only then.
7. **Not changed, recorded instead (decision 25).** Refusing a closed party's newcomers at `connect` would also turn away a friend the
   leader invited and the run's own members coming back, since all of them come in by the same code. A stranger can only get there
   from a list entry a few seconds old. A newcomer only turns the plan into a new climb, which asks the run's members first, and the
   leader can kick.
8. **Fixed: Mute now hides the party line.**
   - The party line carries the leader's account as `from`; `name` stays "".
   - The town chat's own filter already drops lines from muted and blocked accounts, both as they arrive and when you mute later, because
     the app's log keeps that id as the line's `uid`.
   - A report of the line by its number names the leader (`reportedLine` checks `from`).
   - Partly done: the line has no name to tap (it's still a server line, `kind: "server"`), so it has no menu of its own. The leader is
     muted from any of their own lines and reported from their profile.
9. **Fixed: no more 3 s rechecks for a deleted member.** `readTowerPlan` asks for no recheck when the refusal is `gone`. Only a kick or
   a leave changes that, and both read the plan again.
