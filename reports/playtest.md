# Battle Royale Chess: playtest report (bots)

200 full matches of 32 bots with Stockfish at 100,000 nodes (phase A), plus 1,000 matches per variant replayed from the 33,600 positions those matches produced (phase B). Generated by `packages/sim/scripts/simulate.ts` in 45.2 minutes.

Bots: 32 skill settings from T = 0.25 (strongest) to T = 32 (weakest), evenly spaced on a log scale. A bot picks among the engine's top 8 moves with probability proportional to exp(-loss / T), and plays a random legal move 2% of the time. Seats are shuffled every match.

## Answers

### 1. Does final placement track bot skill?

Yes. Mean Spearman rank correlation between skill rank and final placement: **0.67** in the full matches (1.0 would be perfect order, 0 no relation).

### 2. Do the best bots survive?

| Measure | Full matches |
| --- | --- |
| A top-4 bot knocked out in stage 1 | 25.5% |
| Strongest bot reaches the duel | 35.5% |
| Strongest bot wins | 21.5% |
| Strongest bot's average placement | 8.3 |

### 5. Dead rounds

**43.4%** of group-rounds were dead (every pick within 1 point of the others). Median loss of a pick: 0.0 points; 75th percentile 1.0; 90th 3.8.

### 6. Board retirements

| Reason | Per match |
| --- | --- |
| Expected score reached 0.90 | 4.74 |
| Game over | 0.01 |

### 7. Engine budget

On 120 positions, scoring the top moves at 100,000 nodes and at 20 times that: median difference in a move's loss 1.05 points, 90th percentile 15.15. Same best move in 73.3% of positions. In random groups of 4 picks, the small search's group winner is also the big search's winner (within 0.5 points) 87.0% of the time.

### Timing

An engine-scored round took 1.66 s for 8 boards on one engine in Node (1.04 searches per board).
