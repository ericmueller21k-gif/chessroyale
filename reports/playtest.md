# Battle Royale Chess: playtest report (bots)

4 full matches of 32 bots with Stockfish at 100,000 nodes (phase A), plus 50 matches per variant replayed from the 672 positions those matches produced (phase B). Generated by `packages/sim/scripts/simulate.ts` in 1.2 minutes.

Bots: 32 skill settings from T = 0.25 (strongest) to T = 32 (weakest), evenly spaced on a log scale. A bot picks among the engine's top 8 moves with probability proportional to exp(-loss / T), and plays a random legal move 2% of the time. Seats are shuffled every match.

## Answers

### 1. Does final placement track bot skill?

Yes. Mean Spearman rank correlation between skill rank and final placement: **0.62** in the full matches (1.0 would be perfect order, 0 no relation).

### 2. Do the best bots survive?

| Measure | Full matches |

| Strongest bot reaches the duel | 50.0% |

| Strongest bot's average placement | 7.0 |

### 5. Dead rounds

**46.3%** of group-rounds were dead (every pick within 1 point of the others). Median loss of a pick: 0.0 points; 75th percentile 1.0; 90th 3.6.

### 7. Engine budget

On 4 positions, scoring the top moves at 100,000 nodes and at 20 times that: median difference in a move's loss 2.15 points, 90th percentile 6.90. Same best move in 50.0% of positions. In random groups of 4 picks, the small search's group winner is also the big search's winner (within 0.5 points) 68.8% of the time.

### Timing

An engine-scored round took 1.57 s for 8 boards on one engine in Node (1.04 searches per board).
