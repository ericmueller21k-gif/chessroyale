# Ranked 1 v 1: ratings, ranks and pairing on synthetic players

Generated by `packages/sim/scripts/ranked-1v1-sim.ts` (seeded; `npx tsx packages/sim/scripts/ranked-1v1-sim.ts 1500 400`), through core's own `rankedGame`, `pairUp` and `rankView` with `RANKED_1V1` as it stands: Glicko-1 from 1500 (or the account's rating from other modes), RD 350 → 60; rank points 2 × 20 × (result − expected result) plus 0.05 × (hidden − ladder rating) within ±10; 5 placements, placed at most Diamond 4 0; pairing within 100 + 10/s waited + 1 × the larger deviation, at most 1000.

**The players:** 1500 with a true strength drawn from a normal curve (mean 1500, spread 350, kept within 500-2900) on the Elo scale; 60% start from an account rating that reads their strength give or take 250, the rest at 1500. A game's result comes from the two true strengths (Elo's expected score, 8% draws between equals). Each round, 15% of them (243) queue at once and are paired second by second for up to 90 s; a round is a tenth of a day. 400 rounds.

**The scale.** The ranks keep the players' order (Spearman's rank correlation of rank with true strength: 0.98), but pairing by the closest rating stretches the scale: the strong are rated above their true number and the weak below, more so the longer they play (results between near-equals say little about the scale). ## Gaming it

**Sandbagging:** 60 pairs of twins (the same true strength, 1500-1900, and the same account rating) in the same population; one loses all five placements on purpose, then plays honestly. The sandbagger's ladder minus the honest twin's, after the same number of games (100 = one division):

| Games | Pairs | Sandbagger − honest twin (median ladder points) | Sandbagger ahead |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 6 | 58 | -1018 | 0% |
| 10 | 51 | -951 | 0% |
| 20 | 40 | -783 | 0% |
| 40 | 8 | -436 | 0% |
| 80 | 0 | – | – |

**Trading wins:** two new accounts of equal strength, alone in a quiet queue, A always beating B for as long as the pairing range lets them meet (the range at its widest).
