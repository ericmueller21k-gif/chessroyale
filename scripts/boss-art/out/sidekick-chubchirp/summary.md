**Choices the owner might want to change:**
- **Scarf placement:** the knot and hanging tail sit at the back of the neck, so it reads as scarf-on-back. Moving the knot to the front chest is possible.
- **Palette:** the fur ramp leans lighter than Chub's, so the baby reads as a paler brown. The scarf is Chub's blue-green, not the grasshopper's yellow-green.
- **Defeat:** it ends in a belly-flop, with a slump first. I tried a quarter-turn onto its back, but the rotated shading looked muddled.
- **Pawless props:** the grasshopper and scarf are its only props, so "prop lowered" in sit is simply the paws resting on the table.
