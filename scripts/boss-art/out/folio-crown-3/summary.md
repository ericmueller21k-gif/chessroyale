## Its notes

I redrew the crane on Folio's Paper Crown (legendary head item, id folio-crown-3, attach 25) as one big paper bird sitting on top of the crown. At 60 px it now reads as a bird because the head and beak show clearly. The crown is only a weak second read: the crane hides its points. The wings' V is also smaller than I wanted.

**The crane**
- It's about twice as wide as before (x 12.5–81.5) and drawn in warm cream, with one tan face per wing. Every part has a heavy 2.2 outline.
- The parts: a pointed boat-shaped body with a tan underside, a slim neck rising up-left to a chunky head with a beak bent forward and a black eye, a short tail point up-right, and two big triangular wings. The front wing has a thin gold edge, which is the only gold on the crane.
- It sits clearly lighter than the crown and holds up on Yellow, Opal and Pearl because of the outline.

**The crown**
I kept the band, the handwriting squiggles, the gold diamond and the two-tone faces as they were. I lowered and flattened the points (tips at y 12.5–14.5) to make room. The crane's body now covers most of them, so only the band and the edges of the crown show.

**Motion** (3.5 s loop, 10 frames)
1. Wings rest folded along the body, pointing back (1.3 s).
2. They lift quickly into a tall V over the back (0.5 s).
3. They hold the V (1.1 s).
4. They fold back down (0.6 s).

The change is visible at 60 px, but it's not big.

**Why it's shorter than the brief allowed**
In the preview pictures, anything above about y 0 was cut off. A try that went up to y −4 lost the crane's head and the wing tips. So I kept everything at y 1.2 or below and made the crane wider instead of taller. That's why the V isn't as tall as it could be.

**Choices you might change**
- If the real avatar does show up to y −9, the wings' V (and the whole crane) could rise about 3 units. That would make the V bigger and uncover the crown's points.
- The rest pose is folded along the body. A drooped-wing rest pose would make the swing larger.
- The description is carried over from the last try: "Folded from a page of royal decrees. The crane on top has read them all and remains unimpressed."
- The tile frame is [11, 0, 72, 29].
