## Its notes

Jefferson's Firework (epic weapon, 1.12 s loop of 8 frames at 140 ms): a Roman candle held upright at the pawn's right side, tilted 8°, standing well above the head. It reads at 60 px on white and black pawns and in every color scheme; in the last render no frame spilled past its box.

What it is: a cream cardboard tube wound with diagonal bands. The bands are the painted part (a candy-stripe look like Jefferson's candle). Fixed colors: tan cardboard rims top and bottom, a gold cone cap, a short dark fuse curling up and left, and a white highlight down the tube.

Description: "A Roman candle the size of a pawn. The fuse is already lit, so maybe make your move quickly."

Motion: the fuse tip has a small glow (white core, yellow, faint orange halo) that throbs in size every frame. 8 tiny four-point sparks are each born at their own frame and live 3 frames: they pop out white, turn yellow as they fly out, then fade to small orange and drop a little. 2 or 3 are always in the air at different stages, so it spits all the time but never turns into a flame.

Choices to change:
- Opal and Pearl bands are pale on the cream tube and read weakest; a slightly darker cream would fix that.
- The fuse leans left, over the tube, to keep the sparks inside the square, which puts them close above the pawn's head.
- I left out the reference candle's blue bands so the painted color stays the only stripe.
- The tube is 16 units wide; a fatter tube would look bolder but hide more of the pawn.
