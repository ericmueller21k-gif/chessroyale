## Its notes

I drew Birdie's idle only, as the brief asked: 28 frames, about 5.9 s, looping. File: scripts/boss-art/out/boss-birdie-a/boss-birdie-a.ts, exported as `BIRDIE` with id "boss-birdie-a" and name "Birdie". Every idle frame has been rendered and checked at 3 to 8x and at phone size beside the tiger and Chub & Chirp. The render still lists entrance, thinking, move, capture, hurt, check, smug, rattled, defeat and victory as missing, which is expected for now.

**How he looks:** a mourning dove seen from the side, facing our left. He has a small round head with a buff face, a big dark eye in a pale ring, a slim dark beak and a small dark cheek mark. His body is plump with a buff chest. The wing has rows of scalloped feathers (dark ends with pale edges, one black dove spot) and dark flight feathers at the back. The long tapered tail has a dark band and a pale tip and sticks out past the back of the train. He wears a navy conductor's cap with a gold band and a short visor, tipped back a little, and a small burgundy tie. His coral feet grip the rim of an open hatch in the train's roof.

**The train:** a yellow toy diesel with a rounded nose to our left. It has a dark windshield, a side window, two stacked headlights, a dark roof with fan hints, a thin red stripe that sweeps down at the nose, two portholes, panel seams, and silver wheels and trucks with a fuel tank between them. It's about as long as he is. There are no letters, numbers or markings anywhere.

**Idle, beat by beat:**
1. Perched and breathing: his chest swells and his head rises a pixel.
2. A dove's head bob: his head pokes forward and down, then back.
3. One more breath, then a blink (half-shut, shut, half-shut).
4. He bobs down slightly, then ducks into the cab over three quick frames, pulling his head back over the middle of the hatch. He sinks a pixel too far and settles, so only his head and cap show above the rim.
5. He peeks left, blinks as he turns, looks right (head and cap mirrored) with a little lift, blinks back and looks left again.
6. He pops back up over two quick frames onto his feet. His cap jumps 2 pixels and settles, then he breathes and the loop starts again.

**Choices you might want to change:**
- **Size:** the frame is 100 × 96, a bit bigger than Chub & Chirp (84 × 84). With the train he stands taller and wider than they do at phone size, about as tall as the tiger with his balloons. If he should be smaller, the train is the easiest thing to shorten.
- **Head:** I made it a little bigger than a real dove's so the face reads at phone size.
- **Body while he ducks:** you see it sinking for a couple of frames. At the lowest point I don't draw it at all, because it would otherwise show under the train.
- **Plumage:** soft grey-brown. It could go darker and browner to match the photo more closely.
- **Cap:** sits straight-ish and is tipped back by only one pixel. I can tilt it more if you want it jauntier.
- **Hatch:** a dark opening with a silver lip at each end. The near rim, where his toes hook over, is lit silver.
