## Its notes

Jefferson's sidekick (id "sidekick-jefferson-opus-high") is finished. Every animation has been rendered and looked at, the idle at large scale and at phone size beside Jefferson (grex). The latest renders gave no edge warnings.

The look: a baby giraffe, pale yellow with a few round spots. It has a big round head with shiny eyes (white glints), a pink blush, a cream muzzle with a small smile, stubby knobbed ossicones with no flames, a short neck with a plain brown mane, a round body with a cream belly, knobbly knees, and a plain tail tuft. It holds one small sparkler in its near hoof, and the tip spits a few sparks, different every frame. It faces our right, with light from the top left, the k outline and a faint halo. It stands about 54 px tall, about 60% of Jefferson. The frame is 64x69 with the feet at (26, 66), leaving 5 px above the head for hops and sparks.

Animations, beat by beat:
- idle (3 s, loops): a one-pixel breathing bob, the tail swishing, the sparkler fizzing, one blink.
- walk (0.6 s, loops): a trot on the spot. The planted foot slides back while the other swings forward, the head bobs, the sparkler stays up, and a "step" sound cue fires twice per loop.
- sit (3.2 s, loops): on a small wooden stool facing right, legs dangling and swinging. The near hoof rests on its lap with the sparkler lowered and fizzing faintly. It looks at the board, rolls its eyes up to think, gives a slow blink, then a small nod down.
- wave (1.2 s, once): a dip, the sparkler goes up, two arcs out to the right with happy closed eyes and an open smile, then it settles back to idle.
- entrance: crouched with the sparkler unlit, it fizzes alight, a gasp, a hop for joy with both hooves up, then it lands and holds the sparkler up proudly.
- thinking (loops): sparkler by its chin, eyes rolled up, a foot tapping, three thought dots popping up one by one, a blink.
- move: a little hop toward the board, then it points the sparkler at the board with a gasp and a bigger fizz.
- capture: two bounces with both hooves up and big sparks, then a proud pose.
- hurt: a white flash and a flinch back with squeezed eyes as the sparkler sputters, a pout with a tear, then it's fine again.
- check: two excited jabs of the sparkler at the board, each with a gasp, then a proud happy pose.
- smug (loops): it copies Jefferson. Chin up, half-closed eyes, a raised brow and a smirk, sparkler held high, a slow nod.
- rattled (loops): worried brow and small pupils, a wobble, sweat drops, the sparkler sputtering on and off.
- defeat: a bump, the sparkler fizzles out in a curl of smoke, and it plops onto its bottom with its legs out and a tear rolling. The last frame holds.
- victory (loops): it jumps for joy with both hooves up and its biggest sparks.

Choices you might want to change:
- It stands on two legs like Jefferson, so it can hold the sparkler up, wave and sit on a stool. A four-legged baby giraffe would be more natural, but it couldn't hold the sparkler.
- Sit draws only the stool, not the chess table, because I assumed the town scene draws the table. The sparkler rests low in its lap rather than being put away.
- Defeat is a sad sit-down with a tear rather than a fall, since it's a kid who never fights.
- The sound cue names are "fizz", "step", "poof" and "thud". They need sounds hooked up in its kit.
- The worried and smug brows are only a few pixels and barely show at phone size. The eyes and mouth carry those moods.
