## Its notes

I've drawn Birdie's idle. It's a 21-frame loop that runs about 5.6 s, and I checked every frame at 3–10x and at phone size beside the tiger. The other ten animations are still to draw, as the brief asked.

**How he looks:** He stands tall in an open hatch in the train's roof, facing our left. His coral feet grip the hatch edge, and the near side of the train is drawn in front of him so he can sink into the hatch. He has a round grey-brown head with a buff face and a small grey beak. The eye is big and dark with a pale ring and a glint at its top left, plus the small dark spot under it. His wing has staggered scalloped feathers with pale tips and two dark spots. The breast is buff with faint flecks, and a long tail lies across the roof with a pale tip. He wears a navy conductor's cap with a gold band and a black brim, tipped back a little, and a burgundy tie at his throat.

**The train:** A yellow toy diesel seen from the side, nose to the left, with a rounded nose and a sloped windshield. It has a dark roof with the hatch, a cab window and door line, two small square windows on the side (the reference's are round) and a thin red stripe that sweeps down the nose. There are two headlights, a small pilot under the nose, silver trucks and four wheels. There are no letters, numbers or markings.

**Idle, beat by beat:**
1. Perched, then a breath (body and head sink 1 px while the feet stay put), then a small forward head bob, a blink, another breath, and a head cock upward.
2. He ducks into the hatch in two quick frames: a squat, then half down (the body drops behind the train's side).
3. Only his head and cap show above the edge, centred in the hatch. He peeks left, then turns to look right (the head and cap flip), blinks, and lifts his head a touch.
4. He turns back left and pops up with a 1 px hop above his perch.
5. He settles with a breath and a head bob, and the loop starts again.

**Choices you might want to change:**
- **Size:** he's about the tiger's width but shorter overall (74 × 78 frame). The train is about 1.4 times his length, at the upper end of "a little more". It could be shortened to make him bigger in the frame.
- **The hatch:** it's in the middle of the roof, not at the front cab, so he sits balanced over the train.
- **Crouched head:** it sits 2 px higher than fully down so his beak clears the dark roof. Lower than that, the beak disappeared against the roof.
- **Looking right:** done by mirroring the head and cap, so for that moment the light falls from the top right.
- **Mid-duck tail:** while he's partly sunk, his tail isn't drawn, so its tip doesn't poke out past the back of the train.
- **Cap:** the jaunty touch is subtle, just the crown leaning back.
- **Id:** the file exports `BIRDIE` with id "boss-birdie-b" as you asked; the brief's final id is "boss-birdie", so it needs renaming once this variant is picked.
