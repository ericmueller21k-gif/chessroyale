# Puddles' Hose (a weapon, Boss Series Crate #1)

Draw **Puddles' Hose**, a crate weapon your pawn carries at its side. Puddles is the game's hippo gardener boss (his
pictures are the references): he waters his plants with a garden hose, deep teal green with a brass nozzle.

- A garden hose **coiled round** in a neat loop or two, held at the pawn's right side, its brass spray nozzle sticking out
  of the coil. **Not fancy**: a plain, friendly garden hose, chunky and readable.
- The painted part (the item's color) is the hose itself; the brass nozzle and the water are fixed colors.
- **Its motion:** at rest most of the time; **now and then a little squirt of water** from the nozzle (a short spurt of
  a few drops that arcs out and fades), then rest again. Long rest, short squirt (about 3 to 4 seconds a loop).
- It should feel like "yeah, this thing is sweet": crisp and satisfying at 60 px, charming at 4x.
- Tier: rare. Name it "Puddles' Hose", id "puddles-hose".
