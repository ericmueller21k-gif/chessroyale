# Moon's Ride (a ride, Boss Series Crate #1)

Draw **Moon's Ride**, a hyper-rare ride from the boss Moo & Moon (the references: Moo the cow hugs his friend the Moon, a
soft yellow crescent moon with a darker rim and a sleepy, friendly face).

- Your pawn **sits on the crescent moon**, the moon lying on its back like a hammock or a little boat, its two horns
  pointing up; the pawn sits in the hollow of the crescent, its lower part hidden behind the moon's near edge (the way a
  pawn sits on a park bench). The moon keeps its face (a calm, happy eye on its outer curve, a smile). It floats: it never
  touches the ground; a soft shadow below it.
- The moon is the painted part (the item's color: a red moon, a cobalt moon...), with a lighter rim highlight and a
  darker rim; its face is fixed (dark).
- **Idle:** it floats in place with a slow bob up and down; a few **little stars sparkle out behind it** (tiny four-point
  stars that twinkle and fade).
- **Town move:** a slow **glide** across the square with a gentle bob, the little stars trailing out behind it as it
  goes, fading (one square, dx 0 to 100; its last frame leads into the next move's first).
- It must read at town size (a pawn about 45 px) and look great at 4x. Dreamy and sweet.
- Tier: mythic (rides are the rarest). Name it "Moon's Ride", id "moon-ride".
