# Moon's Ride (a ride, Boss Series Crate #1): redrawn, Oct 31 (Eric's design)

Draw **Moon's Ride**, a hyper-rare ride from the boss Moo & Moon (the references: Moo the cow hugs his friend the Moon, a
soft yellow crescent moon with a darker rim and a sleepy, friendly face). This replaces an earlier drawing Eric turned
down (a moon lying on its back like a hammock, the pawn sunk in its curve): **don't do that**.

- **Eric's design:** a **flat crescent moon lying level, like a flying board** (a hoverboard or a magic carpet), floating
  a little above the ground with a soft shadow below it. Side view: a long, low, gently curved crescent, its two thin
  tips at the ends (front tip to our right), thicker in the middle. **The pawn sits on top of it**, upright, its bottom
  just behind the moon's top edge (the way a pawn sits on a park bench: only the very bottom of the pawn hidden). The
  pawn must read as sitting *on* the moon, never cut in half or sunk inside it.
- **Its face** on the front edge (the side facing us, toward the front tip): a sleepy, friendly closed eye (or calm half
  lid), a small smile, a little blush. Dark, fixed colors.
- **The painted part** (the item's color): the moon itself (a red moon, a cobalt moon...), with a lighter rim highlight
  on top and a darker rim underneath. The face stays fixed.
- **Idle:** a slow float bob up and down; a few tiny four-point **stars twinkle** around it (twinkle and fade).
- **Town move:** a **glide** across the square with a slight forward lean (nose a touch down, then level), and a
  **short sparkle trail** behind it that fades. One square per move, dx 0 to 100; its last frame leads into the next
  move's first.
- **Size (Eric: "be cognizant of the horizontal size"):** the whole ride, every frame, stays within **1.25 squares
  wide**: at most 125 units across, e.g. x from about -12 to 113 on the pawn's 100-unit square, sparkles and trail
  included in the idle; in the move the trail may reach a little further back but keep it short.
- It must read at town size (a pawn about 45 px) and look great at 4x. Dreamy and sweet. The item style (bold dark
  outlines, flat fills, a highlight or two). Never draw letters or numbers.
- Tier: mythic. Name it "Moon's Ride", id "moon-ride". Say in your notes the ride's width in units.
