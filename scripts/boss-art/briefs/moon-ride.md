# Moon's Ride (a ride, Boss Series Crate #1): redrawn, Oct 31 (Eric's design), second try

Draw **Moon's Ride**, a hyper-rare ride from the boss Moo & Moon (the references: Moo the cow hugs his friend the Moon, a
soft yellow crescent moon with a darker rim and a sleepy, friendly face). Two earlier drawings were turned down: a moon
lying on its back like a hammock with the pawn sunk in its curve, and a **bowl or boat** (the horns curving up so it
looks like a banana boat). **Don't do either.**

- **Eric's design:** a **flat crescent moon lying level, like a flying board** (a hoverboard, a surfboard or a magic
  carpet), floating a little above the ground with a soft shadow below it. Side view: think of a crescent moon laid
  **face-up and flat**, seen edge-on: a long, **thin, low** shape whose **top is a flat (very nearly straight) deck**
  and whose **underside is the moon's outer curve** (a shallow belly). The two thin tips are at the front and back
  ends and stay **low and level** (at most a tiny upturn at the very tips, like a skateboard's kick, never a bowl).
  Its thickness is small: a slab about 12 to 18 units tall at the middle, tapering to the tips.
- **The pawn sits on top of it**, upright, on the flat deck: the pawn's base shows resting on the deck (at most its
  bottom few units hidden behind the near edge). It must read as riding *on* the moon like a board, never in it.
- **Its face** on the front of the moon (the side facing us, toward the front tip, on the thick edge): a sleepy,
  friendly closed eye (or calm half lid), a small smile, a little blush. Dark, fixed colors (a pale edge on dark colors).
- **The painted part** (the item's color): the moon itself, with a lighter rim highlight on the deck edge and a darker
  shade on the belly. The face stays fixed. A few faint craters are welcome.
- **Idle:** a slow float bob up and down; a few tiny four-point **stars twinkle** around it (twinkle and fade).
- **Town move:** a **glide** across the square with a slight forward lean (nose a touch down, then level), and a
  **short sparkle trail** behind that fades. One square per move, dx 0 to 100; its last frame leads into the next
  move's first.
- **Size (Eric: "be cognizant of the horizontal size"):** the whole ride, every frame, stays within **1.25 squares
  wide**: at most 125 units across, e.g. x from about -12 to 113, stars included in the idle; in the move the trail may
  reach a little further back but keep it short (about 20 units).
- It must read at town size (a pawn about 45 px) and look great at 4x. Dreamy and sweet. The item style (bold dark
  outlines, flat fills, a highlight or two). Never draw letters or numbers.
- Tier: mythic. Name it "Moon's Ride", id "moon-ride". Say in your notes the ride's width in units.
