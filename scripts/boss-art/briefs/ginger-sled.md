# Candy Cane Sled (a ride, Boss Series)

Draw **Candy Cane Sled**, a hyper-rare ride from the boss Ginger: the Freeze boss (an angry gingerbread man with white icing trim, a green bow tie, red gumdrop buttons and a big candy cane he holds like a weapon) and his sidekick, a little gingerbread kid with a candy-cane scarf (the references show the boss and the sidekick).

- Your pawn rides **a gingerbread sled**: a small sled whose body is gingerbread with white icing piping along its edges, its runners two curled red-and-white **candy canes**, gumdrops along the rail. Side view, facing our right. The pawn sits in it, its lower part hidden behind the sled's side.
- The item style (bold dark outlines, flat fills, a highlight or two), like Riding Chirp and Moon's Ride.
- **The painted part** (the item's color): the sled body (the gingerbread panel: the item's color in place of the brown); the icing, candy-cane runners and gumdrops stay fixed. Say in your notes what you chose.
- **Idle:** alive and calm: a tiny settle on the runners, a sparkle of frost on the icing now and then, a single snowflake drifting by.
- **Town move:** it **slides** across the square on its runners, a little puff of snow kicked up behind, easing to a stop with a small forward lurch. One square per move, dx 0 to 100; its last frame leads into the next move's first.
- It must read at town size (a pawn about 45 px) and look great at 4x. Cute and charming, never a copy of the boss himself.
- Never draw letters or numbers; nothing based on a real person.
- Tier: mythic (rides are the rarest). Name it "Candy Cane Sled", id "ginger-sled".
