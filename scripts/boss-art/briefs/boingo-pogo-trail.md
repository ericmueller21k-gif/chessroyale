# Trail: Boingo's Pogo (`boingo-pogo`)

Give **Boingo's Pogo** (`boingo-pogo`), the finished ride in `scripts/boss-art/out/boingo-pogo/boingo-pogo.tsx`, its **trail
mark**: what it leaves on the squares behind it as it runs around the town, in place of the town's white droplets. It's
drawn only while it runs, never while it stands.

- **The mark:** one round bounce mark per hop: a small ring with a darker middle where the rubber foot landed, and two tiny dust flecks. About about 16 x 10 units (at most 40 x 30; a square is 69).
- **Its color:** fixed: a dark rubber grey with the flecks a pale tan.
- **How it fades:** 3 frames, about 500 ms: sharp, ring spreads lighter, gone. One on every square it leaves.
- **Which way:** the same every way.
- **Take out of its frames:** none known (check every view). Keep everything it does now.
- Change nothing else about the ride.
- Cap: $1.50, effort medium.
