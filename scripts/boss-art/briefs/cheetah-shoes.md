# Cheetah Shoes (a ride, Blitz's special ride)

Draw **Cheetah Shoes**, a ride from the boss **Blitz**, the speed-chess cheetah (the references: a lanky spotted cheetah
in a backwards cap, a teal-and-orange track jacket and white sneakers). Eric: "his thing will be that your guy runs twice
as fast… you're just having fun running around town faster than everyone else."

- **Not a vehicle:** a pair of **cheetah-print running shoes** on the pawn's base. The pawn stands in them: its **whole
  body shows** (no cut, or a cut only at the very bottom of its base, where the shoes' tops wrap round it). Seen from
  the side, facing our right: two chunky running shoes, toes pointing right, one a little ahead of the other, tan with
  black rosette spots, a white midsole.
- **The painted part** (the item's color): the **soles and the laces** (and a little heel tab if it helps the color
  read). The cheetah print and the white midsole stay fixed.
- **Idle:** impatient: a toe-tap of the front shoe, a little bounce of the pawn, as if it can't wait to go.
- **Town move:** a fast, blurry **run cycle**: the shoes stride (heel up, kick back, reach forward), the pawn leans
  forward and bobs, a few **speed lines** and a **kick of dust** at the back foot that go along with it (nothing left
  behind on the ground: that's the trail, later). One square per move, dx 0 to 100. The game will play it at double
  speed, so keep each frame's pose clear.
- **Width within 125 units** in every frame (shoes, speed lines and dust included; a square is about 69 units). It must
  read at town size (a pawn about 45 px) and look great at 4x. Fun, sporty, a bit cocky, like Blitz.
- Tier: mythic (rides are the rarest). Name it "Cheetah Shoes", id "cheetah-shoes".
