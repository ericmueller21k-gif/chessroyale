# Trail: Balloon Pup (`balloon-pup`)

Give **Balloon Pup** (`balloon-pup`), the finished ride in `scripts/boss-art/out/balloon-pup/balloon-pup.tsx`, its **trail
mark**: what it leaves on the squares behind it as it runs around the town, in place of the town's white droplets. It's
drawn only while it runs, never while it stands.

- **The mark:** little balloon-dog paw prints: a pair of small round prints, each a pad and three toe dots, rounded like balloons. About about 18 x 12 units (at most 40 x 30; a square is 69).
- **Its color:** the item's color, a little darker, with a thin dark edge.
- **How it fades:** 3 frames, about 550 ms: fresh, lighter, faint, gone. One on every square it leaves.
- **Which way:** point the way it ran; left and right in turn.
- **Take out of its frames:** none known (check every view). Keep the white sparkle glinting on its body.
- Change nothing else about the ride.
- Cap: $1.50, effort medium.
