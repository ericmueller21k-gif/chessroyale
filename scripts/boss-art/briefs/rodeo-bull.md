# Rodeo Bull (a ride, Boss Series)

Draw **Rodeo Bull**, a hyper-rare ride from the boss Wrangler: the cowboy pig boss (a pink pig in a brown cowboy hat, a gold star, a fringed vest, a big belt buckle, boots with spurs and a spinning lasso) and his sidekick, a piglet in a cowboy hat (the references show the boss and the sidekick).

- Your pawn rides **a stocky little rodeo bull**: chunky, cute, brown with a cream muzzle, short curved horns, a nose ring, a tuft on its head, a rope tail. A western saddle with a horn and a fringed saddle blanket; the pawn sits in it, its lower part hidden behind the saddle. Side view, facing our right. Make it clearly a bull, not Moo the cow.
- The item style (bold dark outlines, flat fills, a highlight or two), like Riding Chirp and Moon's Ride.
- **The painted part** (the item's color): the saddle blanket (its fringe a darker shade) and the saddle; the bull, horns and a small gold star concho stay fixed. Say in your notes what you chose.
- **Idle:** alive and calm: a snort with a tiny steam puff from the nostrils, a hoof paws the ground, the tail flicks.
- **Town move:** a **buck-hop**: back legs kick up, it springs forward in a short bucking arc (the pawn tilts with it), lands on its front then back hooves on the next square with a small dust puff. One square per move, dx 0 to 100; its last frame leads into the next move's first.
- It must read at town size (a pawn about 45 px) and look great at 4x. Cute and charming, never a copy of the boss himself.
- Never draw letters or numbers; nothing based on a real person.
- Tier: mythic (rides are the rarest). Name it "Rodeo Bull", id "rodeo-bull".
