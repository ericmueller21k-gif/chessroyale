# Birdie's Train (a ride, Boss Series)

Draw **Birdie's Train**, a ride from the boss Birdie: his little toy diesel train, the same train he rides on the board. The pawn is the rider, not Birdie: no bird in the cab.

**The shape is that train, and it is long and low.** Match the reference pictures' shape and proportions; not a steam locomotive. The reference pictures (`refs/birdie-train/`, the board train seen from above and the game's own screenshot) show: a long body, wider than it is tall, with a flat or barely curved low roof (no tall cab, no arched roof, no smokestack-and-boiler shape, no steam-engine silhouette). The cab is a low open hatch in the roof, sitting in the middle of the body. The nose is rounded, with round headlights set at the front and a grille. Grey wheel trucks, with the wheels visible under the body.

- Your pawn rides **in the open cab of that little yellow train**, seen from the side, facing our right. The pawn sits in the cab where Birdie sits, its lower part hidden behind the cab's side. A dark roof over the open cab, a sloped windshield at the front of the cab, and a thin red stripe running along the body and sweeping round the nose.
- Colors as drawn in the reference pictures: bright yellow body, dark roof, thin red stripe, round headlights, grey wheel trucks.
- At the rides' level of detail: simpler than the board drawing, matching the other rides. No letters or numbers anywhere, on the train or elsewhere.
- The item style (bold dark outlines, flat fills, a highlight or two), like Riding Chirp and Moon's Ride.
- **The painted part** (the item's color): the yellow body and the cab's yellow, so the Yellow color matches his train exactly. The dark roof, the red stripe, the headlights and the grey trucks stay fixed. Say in your notes what you chose.
- **Idle:** alive and calm: a slight rock on its springs, the headlights glow, a small puff from the stack at the back of the roof now and then (a puff that drifts up and fades near the train is fine). Wheels still.
- **Town move:** it **rolls** along: the wheels turn (the spokes or tread marks roll), a little rock on the rails, a small puff from the stack as it sets off, coming to a soft stop. One square per move, dx 0 to 100; its last frame leads into the next move's first.
- It must read at town size (a pawn about 45 px) and look great at 4x. Cute and charming, simpler than Birdie's boss drawing.
- Never draw letters or numbers; nothing based on a real person.
- Name it "Birdie's Train", id "birdie-train-ride". (The board art's id, "birdie-train", is already taken.) No tier set here: the director decides it.
