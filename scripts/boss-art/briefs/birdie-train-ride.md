# Birdie's Train (a ride, Boss Series)

Draw **Birdie's Train**, a ride from the boss Birdie: his little yellow toy diesel train, the same train he rides on the board. The pawn is the rider, not Birdie: no bird in the cab.

- Your pawn rides **in the open cab of a little yellow toy diesel train**, seen from the side, facing our right: a rounded nose with two round headlights, a sloped windshield, a dark roof over the open cab, and the cab's open side where the pawn sits, its lower part hidden behind the cab's side. The look and colors of Birdie's train, which you can't read here, so here they are: bright yellow body, a dark roof, a thin red stripe running along the body and sweeping down the nose, round headlights, grey wheel trucks with four wheels. At the rides' level of detail: simpler than the boss drawing, matching the other rides. No letters or numbers anywhere.
- The item style (bold dark outlines, flat fills, a highlight or two), like Riding Chirp and Moon's Ride.
- **The painted part** (the item's color): the yellow body and the cab's yellow. The dark roof, the red stripe, the headlights and the grey trucks stay fixed. Say in your notes what you chose.
- **Idle:** alive and calm: a slight rock on its springs, the headlights glow, a faint puff from the roof now and then (a puff that drifts up and fades near the train is fine). Wheels still.
- **Town move:** it **rolls** along: the wheels turn (the spokes or tread marks roll), a little rock on the rails, a puff from the roof as it sets off, coming to a soft stop. One square per move, dx 0 to 100; its last frame leads into the next move's first.
- It must read at town size (a pawn about 45 px) and look great at 4x. Cute and charming, simpler than Birdie's boss drawing.
- Never draw letters or numbers; nothing based on a real person.
- Name it "Birdie's Train", id "birdie-train-ride". (The board art's id, "birdie-train", is already taken.) No tier set here: the director decides it.
