# Boingo's Pogo (a ride, Boss Series)

Draw **Boingo's Pogo**, a hyper-rare ride from the boss Boingo: the clown boss (a grinning harlequin with red eyes, always bouncing on a pogo stick) and his sidekick, a little clown kid with a squirting flower (the references show the boss and the sidekick).

- Your pawn rides **a clown's pogo stick with a little saddle seat**: a bright pole with a spring at the bottom and a foot, handle bars on top, and a small round clown seat (with a ruffle) where the pawn sits, its lower part hidden behind the seat's ruffle. Harlequin diamonds on the pole, a pom-pom on each handle end. Side view, the handles toward our right.
- The item style (bold dark outlines, flat fills, a highlight or two), like Riding Chirp and Moon's Ride.
- **The painted part** (the item's color): the pole and the seat (harlequin diamonds in a darker shade of the color); the spring (silver), the ruffle (white) and the pom-poms stay fixed. Say in your notes what you chose.
- **Idle:** alive and calm: never quite still: small in-place bounces on the spring (squash and stretch of the coil), a pom-pom jiggles.
- **Town move:** **one big BOING** per square: compress the spring, launch in a tall arc (the pawn rides along, a little lag), land on the next square with a squashed spring and a rebound. One square per move, dx 0 to 100; its last frame leads into the next move's first.
- It must read at town size (a pawn about 45 px) and look great at 4x. Cute and charming, never a copy of the boss himself.
- Never draw letters or numbers; nothing based on a real person.
- Tier: mythic (rides are the rarest). Name it "Boingo's Pogo", id "boingo-pogo".
