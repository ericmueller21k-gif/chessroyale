# Trail: Jefferson's Giraffe (`jefferson-giraffe`)

Give **Jefferson's Giraffe** (`jefferson-giraffe`), the finished ride in `scripts/boss-art/out/jefferson-giraffe/jefferson-giraffe.tsx`, its **trail
mark**: what it leaves on the squares behind it as it runs around the town, in place of the town's white droplets. It's
drawn only while it runs, never while it stands.

- **The mark:** two-toed hoofprints: a small pair of split (two-toed) hoof marks. About about 18 x 12 units (at most 40 x 30; a square is 69).
- **Its color:** fixed: a dark earthy brown.
- **How it fades:** 3 frames, about 600 ms: pressed, lighter, faint, gone. One on every square it leaves.
- **Which way:** point the way it ran; left and right in turn.
- **Take out of its frames:** none known (check every view). Keep everything it does now, the mane flames included.
- Change nothing else about the ride.
- Cap: $1.50, effort medium.
