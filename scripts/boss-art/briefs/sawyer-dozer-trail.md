# Trail: Sawyer's Dozer (`sawyer-dozer`)

Give **Sawyer's Dozer** (`sawyer-dozer`), the finished ride in `scripts/boss-art/out/sawyer-dozer/sawyer-dozer.tsx`, its **trail
mark**: what it leaves on the squares behind it as it runs around the town, in place of the town's white droplets. It's
drawn only while it runs, never while it stands.

- **The mark:** tread marks: two bands of short parallel cleat lines, like a crawler's tracks pressed into the ground. About about 34 x 16 units (at most 40 x 30; a square is 69).
- **Its color:** fixed: a dark earthy brown.
- **How it fades:** 3 frames, about 700 ms: crisp, lighter, broken, gone. One on every square it leaves.
- **Which way:** along the way it ran (cleats across the band).
- **Take out of its frames:** any dirt crumbs left behind the rear track and any exhaust puffs that stay where they were left behind it, every view. Keep the puff popping out of the stack while it stays with the dozer, crumbs at the blade going along with it.
- Change nothing else about the ride.
- Cap: $1.50, effort medium.
