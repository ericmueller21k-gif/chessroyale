# Puddles's Mower (a ride, Boss Series)

Draw **Puddles's Mower**, a hyper-rare ride from the boss Puddles: the hippo gardener boss (an upright hippo with a heavy barrel body, a big square muzzle, pinkish-grey skin, holding a garden hose with a brass-and-green nozzle) and his sidekick, a baby hippo with a watering can (the references show the boss and the sidekick).

- Your pawn rides **a little riding lawnmower**: a chunky body with a hood, a seat, a small steering wheel, big back wheels and small front wheels, a grass catcher bag behind; a flower or a sprout tucked in a holder as a nod to the gardener. Side view, facing our right. The pawn sits in the seat, its lower part hidden behind the seat back and body.
- The item style (bold dark outlines, flat fills, a highlight or two), like Riding Chirp and Moon's Ride.
- **The painted part** (the item's color): the mower's body and hood; the wheels, seat, steering wheel and grass bag stay fixed. Say in your notes what you chose.
- **Idle:** alive and calm: the engine idles: a small chug-shake, a tiny exhaust puff now and then, the flower nods.
- **Town move:** it **drives** across the square: wheels turn, a spray of green grass clippings flies out the side and settles, a chug of exhaust. One square per move, dx 0 to 100; its last frame leads into the next move's first.
- It must read at town size (a pawn about 45 px) and look great at 4x. Cute and charming, never a copy of the boss himself.
- Never draw letters or numbers; nothing based on a real person.
- Tier: mythic (rides are the rarest). Name it "Puddles's Mower", id "puddles-mower".
