# Trail: Ginger's Sled (`ginger-sled`)

Give **Ginger's Sled** (`ginger-sled`), the finished ride in `scripts/boss-art/out/ginger-sled/ginger-sled.tsx`, its **trail
mark**: what it leaves on the squares behind it as it runs around the town, in place of the town's white droplets. It's
drawn only while it runs, never while it stands.

- **The mark:** two runner tracks with a few candy-colored specks: two parallel short pressed lines and three or four tiny red, white and green specks. About about 32 x 16 units (at most 40 x 30; a square is 69).
- **Its color:** fixed: a pale snowy white-grey for the tracks with a darker edge, the specks candy red, white and mint.
- **How it fades:** 4 frames, about 650 ms: crisp, lighter, specks fade, gone. One on every square it leaves.
- **Which way:** along the way it ran, as wheel tracks would.
- **Take out of its frames:** the snow puffs kicked up behind the runners in the move, the ones that stay where they landed and trail behind, every view. Keep the frost sparkles flashing on the icing; a kick of snow that goes along with the runners is fine if it stays with the sled.
- Change nothing else about the ride.
- Cap: $1.50, effort medium.
