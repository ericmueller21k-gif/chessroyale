# Trail: Moon Ride (`moon-ride`)

Give **Moon Ride** (`moon-ride`), the finished ride in `scripts/boss-art/out/moon-ride/moon-ride.tsx`, its **trail
mark**: what it leaves on the squares behind it as it runs around the town, in place of the town's white droplets. It's
drawn only while it runs, never while it stands.

- **The mark:** a little dust of fading stars: two or three tiny four-point stars and a couple of specks, scattered in the box. About about 24 x 14 units (at most 40 x 30; a square is 69).
- **Its color:** fixed: pale gold and white.
- **How it fades:** 4 frames, about 650 ms: bright, twinkle smaller, specks only, gone. One on every square it leaves.
- **Which way:** the same every way (a slight scatter difference by n is welcome).
- **Take out of its frames:** the sparkle trail drawn into its move frames, in every view: the sparkle stars and pale streaks that drift back behind the tail on the side, the sparkles drifting behind the pawn on the front, and the sparkles dropping from the tips on the back. Keep the twinkling stars round it at rest, if they stay with the ride.
- Change nothing else about the ride.
- Cap: $1.50, effort medium.
