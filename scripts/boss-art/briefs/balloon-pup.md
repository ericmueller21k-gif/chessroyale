# Balloon Pup (a ride, Boss Series)

Draw **Balloon Pup**, a hyper-rare ride from the boss Babur: the circus tiger who wanted to make balloon animals instead of jumping through hoops (orange with black stripes, a small black top hat with a red band, a red and gold ringmaster's vest, holding the strings of chess-piece balloons) and his sidekick, a tiger cub with a big purple balloon (the references show the boss and the sidekick).

- Your pawn rides **a giant balloon-animal dog** (the classic twisted-balloon poodle: round sausage segments for the legs, body, neck, head with a snout, two ear loops, a tail knot), seen from the side, facing our right, with knot twists where segments meet. The pawn sits on its back between the neck and tail, its lower part hidden behind the near body segment. A small tiger-striped ribbon bow on its neck as a nod to Babur.
- The item style (bold dark outlines, flat fills, a highlight or two), like Riding Chirp and Moon's Ride.
- **The painted part** (the item's color): the balloon (all segments: shiny rubber with a bright curved highlight on each); the bow stays fixed. Say in your notes what you chose.
- **Idle:** alive and calm: a soft squeaky bob (the segments squash and stretch a hair), the tail knot wiggles, a shine glints across a segment now and then.
- **Town move:** a **springy bounce**: squash down, boing up in a low arc (segments stretch), land with a rubbery squash and wobble, one square per move. Bouncy and silly. One square per move, dx 0 to 100; its last frame leads into the next move's first.
- It must read at town size (a pawn about 45 px) and look great at 4x. Cute and charming, never a copy of the boss himself.
- Never draw letters or numbers; nothing based on a real person.
- Tier: mythic (rides are the rarest). Name it "Balloon Pup", id "balloon-pup".
