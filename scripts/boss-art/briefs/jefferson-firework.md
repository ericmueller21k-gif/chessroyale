# Jefferson's Firework (a weapon, Boss Series Crate #1)

Draw **Jefferson's Firework**, a crate weapon your pawn carries at its side. Jefferson is the game's fire boss, a cocky
giraffe (the references), whose big move is a huge Roman candle: a cardboard tube banded in color, which he lights.

- **A big firework**, a rocket or a Roman candle (pick the one that reads best at 60 px), held upright at the pawn's right
  side, slightly tilted, taller than the pawn's head: a chunky tube with bold bands, a pointed or capped top, a fuse.
- The tube's main bands are the painted part (the item's color); the other bands, the cap and the fuse are fixed colors
  (cream or white bands, a dark fuse; take Jefferson's flame colors for the fire).
- **Its tip is a subtle sparkler:** the fuse at the top fizzes with a few tiny sparks that flicker and spit, small and
  quick, never a big flame (about 1 to 1.5 seconds a loop, 6 to 10 frames).
- "Yeah, this thing is sweet": bold, a little dangerous, fun.
- Tier: epic. Name it "Jefferson's Firework", id "jefferson-firework".
