# Trail: Alfil's Howdah (`alfil-howdah`)

Give **Alfil's Howdah** (`alfil-howdah`), the finished ride in `scripts/boss-art/out/alfil-howdah/alfil-howdah.tsx`, its **trail
mark**: what it leaves on the squares behind it as it runs around the town, in place of the town's white droplets. It's
drawn only while it runs, never while it stands.

- **The mark:** big round mammoth footprints: one or two wide round prints with a few toenail dents along the front edge. About about 30 x 16 units (at most 40 x 30; a square is 69).
- **Its color:** fixed: a dark earthy brown.
- **How it fades:** 3 frames, about 700 ms: pressed, lighter, faint, gone. One on every square it leaves.
- **Which way:** the toenail dents face the way it ran; left and right prints may take turns.
- **Take out of its frames:** none known (check every view). Keep everything it does now.
- Change nothing else about the ride.
- Cap: $1.50, effort medium.
