# The beaver builder: a new boss's first still (idle only), Oct 31 (Eric's design)

A **new raid boss**, approved by Eric: draw **only the `idle` animation** (it loops, alive but calm, about 3 to 5 s).
Ignore the other animations the system lists; the rest is drawn later. No name yet: id "boss-beaver".

His power: he's a builder who loves wood; through the battle he builds chess pieces from scrap wood in his workshop
and drops them on the board; his ultimate: he gnaws one of your pawns into scraps. He sits among the other bosses (a
circus tiger, a gingerbread man, a hippo gardener, a chameleon painter, a cow and the moon, a paper crane, a raccoon
with a saw): just as charming and readable at phone size, a big clear silhouette, **facing a little to our left
(toward the board)**, light from the top left. In the style of the other bosses (see Sawyer the raccoon in the
references for the look and finish; and Eric's own reference picture, `eric-reference.jpg`, for the beaver).

## Eric's design (follow it exactly)
- **A brown beaver standing upright**, stout and friendly, **big buck teeth**, whiskers, small round ears, a dark nose.
- A **crosshatched, flat paddle tail** (dark brown-grey with a crosshatch pattern) showing behind him.
- An **orange hard hat** and an **orange safety vest** (not yellow: Sawyer the raccoon wears a yellow hard hat).
- A **yellow tool belt** (and harness) with a **hammer with a red handle**, a **tape measure**, a **wrench** and
  **pouches**.
- **Two crossed wooden planks strapped on his back** (their ends showing over his shoulders).
- **No pencil** anywhere.
- Never draw letters or numbers; nothing based on a real person.

## Idle
Breathing (a gentle chest rise), a blink, and maybe a little tap of the hammer on his palm or the tail giving a small
pat. Proud and cheerful, a craftsman pleased with his work.
