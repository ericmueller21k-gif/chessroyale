# Paper Crane (a ride, Boss Series)

Draw **Paper Crane**, a hyper-rare ride from the boss Folio: the paper crane boss (an origami crane folded from a page of handwriting: cream paper with faint blue-grey ink lines, crisp folds lit from the top left, a small red seal) and its sidekick, an origami frog (the references show the boss and the sidekick).

- Your pawn rides **a big origami crane**, seen from the side, facing our right: crisp straight folds, flat faces in two or three tones, a long neck to a pointed head, a pointed tail, two broad wings raised. The pawn sits on its back between the wings, its lower part hidden behind the near wing's root. It hovers just above the ground with a soft shadow.
- The item style (bold dark outlines, flat fills, a highlight or two), like Riding Chirp and Moon's Ride.
- **The painted part** (the item's color): the paper itself (colored origami paper: lit faces lighter, shaded faces darker, crease lines darker still), with the faint ink-line squiggles on top; the small red seal stays fixed. Say in your notes what you chose.
- **Idle:** alive and calm: it hovers with a slow bob, wings lifting and settling a little, the head dipping once.
- **Town move:** a **glide with one wingbeat**: wings sweep down and up as it sails across the square in a gentle swoop, landing back into its hover. One square per move, dx 0 to 100; its last frame leads into the next move's first.
- It must read at town size (a pawn about 45 px) and look great at 4x. Cute and charming, never a copy of the boss himself.
- Never draw letters or numbers; nothing based on a real person.
- Tier: mythic (rides are the rarest). Name it "Paper Crane", id "folio-crane".
