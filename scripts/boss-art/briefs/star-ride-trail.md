# Trail: Moon's Ride, the rounded star (`star-ride`)

Give **Moon's Ride** (`star-ride`), the finished ride in `scripts/boss-art/out/star-ride/star-ride.tsx`, its **trail
mark**: what it leaves on the squares behind it as it runs around the town, in place of the town's white droplets. It's
drawn only while it runs, never while it stands.

- **The mark:** a few sparkler sparks that fell from it: three or four tiny bright five-pointed star sparks and two or three
  specks, scattered in a little cluster, as if they dropped off its sparkler fizz. About 24 x 14 units (at most 40 x 30;
  a square is 69).
- **Its color:** fixed: pale gold and white (the fizz's), with a thin dark outline so they read on the light squares.
- **How it fades:** 4 frames, about 600 ms in all: bright and popping (a tiny glint), twinkle smaller, specks only, gone.
  One on every square it leaves.
- **Which way:** the same every way (a slight scatter difference by n is welcome).
- **Take out of its frames:** none: the sparkler fizz at its back point goes along with it; keep it as it is.
- Change nothing else about the ride.
- Cap: $1.50, effort medium.
