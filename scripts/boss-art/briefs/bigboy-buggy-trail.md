# Trail: Bigboy's Buggy (`bigboy-buggy`)

Give **Bigboy's Buggy** (`bigboy-buggy`), the finished ride in `scripts/boss-art/out/bigboy-buggy/bigboy-buggy.tsx`, its **trail
mark**: what it leaves on the squares behind it as it runs around the town, in place of the town's white droplets. It's
drawn only while it runs, never while it stands.

- **The mark:** two thin wheel tracks: two parallel short lines (a little dotted with the spokes' pattern) running the way it went. About about 32 x 16 units (at most 40 x 30; a square is 69).
- **Its color:** fixed: a dark grey-brown.
- **How it fades:** 3 frames, about 600 ms: crisp, lighter, broken, gone. One on every square it leaves.
- **Which way:** along the way it ran: across the square going left or right, up and down the screen going up or down (seen narrower, the two lines side by side).
- **Take out of its frames:** none known (check every view). Keep everything it does now.
- Change nothing else about the ride.
- Cap: $1.50, effort medium.
