# Riding Chirp (a ride, Boss Series Crate #1)

Draw **Riding Chirp**, a hyper-rare ride: your pawn rides Chirp, the grasshopper from the boss Chub & Chirp (the
references: Chub the rabbit rides Chirp, a green grasshopper with long legs, big dark eyes, two antennae and a saddle).

- Chirp seen from the side, facing our right, in the item style (bold dark outlines, flat fills), with a saddle on his
  back. Your pawn sits in the saddle where Chub sits: its lower part hidden behind the saddle and Chirp's body, the way a
  pawn sits on a park bench, the rest of it riding upright on top.
- The painted part (the item's color) is the saddle and its blanket (or Chirp's body if that reads better; say which you
  chose). Chirp's own greens and his eyes stay fixed if the saddle carries the color.
- **Idle:** alive and calm: antennae twitch, a little breathing bob, a leg shift now and then.
- **Town move:** he **hops** from square to square instead of walking: crouch, spring up in an arc (the pawn rides with
  him, a little lag or squash is welcome), land and settle on the next square. One square per move, dx 0 to 100.
- It must read at town size (a pawn about 45 px) and look great at 4x. Cute and sweet, like Chirp.
- Tier: mythic (rides are the rarest). Name it "Riding Chirp", id "riding-chirp".
