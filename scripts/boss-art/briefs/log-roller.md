# The Log Roller (a ride, Timber's ride)

Draw **The Log Roller**, a ride from the boss **Timber**, the beaver builder (the references: a brown beaver in an
orange hard hat, an orange safety vest and a yellow tool belt, holding crossed planks; a flat grey tail). Eric: "the
log roller is a great one."

- Seen from the side, facing our right: the pawn **balances on top of a rolling log**, lumberjack log-rolling style.
  The log lies across the way it goes, so from the side we see its **round end** face-on and the roll reads: a fat round log, its end showing tree rings, bark round the edge, a little stub of a branch or a notch so
  the turning shows. The pawn stands on top, its whole body showing (its base on the log's top, maybe cut a hair where
  it meets the bark).
- **The painted part** (the item's color): a **painted band** round the bark (like a lumberjack's paint mark), seen as a
  stripe across the log's end and bark. Keep the rings and bark wood-brown. If the end's rings read better painted, say
  so in your notes (pick one).
- **Idle:** a little **wobble and catch**: the log rocks a hair back and forth, the pawn tilts, leans and recovers.
- **Town move:** the log **rolls** under the pawn (the rings and the notch turn round as it goes) while the pawn takes
  quick **balancing steps** on top (little tilts, a bob). One square per move, dx 0 to 100. A puff of bark dust at the
  log's back may go along with it; nothing left behind on the ground (that's the trail, later).
- **Width within 125 units** in every frame (a square is about 69 units; a log about 60 to 80 units across is plenty).
  It must read at town size (a pawn about 45 px) and look great at 4x. Cheerful and handmade, like Timber.
- Tier: mythic (rides are the rarest). Name it "The Log Roller", id "log-roller".
