# Alfil's Howdah (a ride, Boss Series)

Draw **Alfil's Howdah**, a hyper-rare ride from the boss Alfil: the woolly mammoth boss (a shaggy brown mammoth with big curved ivory tusks, a red and blue Nettipattam on his brow, a caparison with gold trim and tassels) and his sidekick, a woolly baby mammoth (the references show the boss and the sidekick).

- Your pawn rides **a woolly baby mammoth** (Alfil's sidekick, seen from the side, facing our right): shaggy brown fur, little curled tusks, a trunk, small ears, short sturdy legs. On its back sits a small **howdah** (a little seat box with a gold rim and a tiny canopy or two corner posts with gold finials); the pawn sits in it, its lower part hidden behind the howdah's front wall.
- The item style (bold dark outlines, flat fills, a highlight or two), like Riding Chirp and Moon's Ride.
- **The painted part** (the item's color): the caparison (the blanket over the mammoth's back, with its gold trim and tassels fixed) and the howdah's canopy; the fur, tusks and gold stay fixed. Say in your notes what you chose.
- **Idle:** alive and calm: trunk sways and curls up a little, an ear flaps once, a slow breathing bob, a tassel swings.
- **Town move:** a heavy, happy **plod**: one full walk cycle across the square, legs stepping in turn, the body rocking a little side to side and the pawn swaying in the howdah, trunk swinging. Cute and weighty. One square per move, dx 0 to 100; its last frame leads into the next move's first.
- It must read at town size (a pawn about 45 px) and look great at 4x. Cute and charming, never a copy of the boss himself.
- Never draw letters or numbers; nothing based on a real person.
- Tier: mythic (rides are the rarest). Name it "Alfil's Howdah", id "alfil-howdah".
