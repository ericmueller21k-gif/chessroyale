# Giraffe Gallop (a ride, Boss Series)

Draw **Giraffe Gallop**, a hyper-rare ride from the boss Jefferson: the Fire boss (a cocky giraffe with flaming horn tips, a fiery mane, a cheeky grin and a sparkler in each hand) and his sidekick, a baby giraffe with a sparkler (the references show the boss and the sidekick).

- Your pawn rides **a young giraffe**, seen from the side, facing our right: spotted coat, long legs, a neck that rises to a head with ossicones whose tips flicker with little flames, a short flame-tipped mane. A saddle at the base of the neck; the pawn sits in it, its lower part hidden behind the saddle. Keep the neck short enough that the whole ride reads at town size (it may rise above the pawn; no more than about 40 units above the square).
- The item style (bold dark outlines, flat fills, a highlight or two), like Riding Chirp and Moon's Ride.
- **The painted part** (the item's color): the saddle and its blanket; the coat, spots and flames stay fixed. Say in your notes what you chose.
- **Idle:** alive and calm: the flames on the horn tips and mane flicker, an ear flicks, a slow chewing or blink, a small sway of the neck.
- **Town move:** a **long-legged gallop**: one stride cycle across the square, legs reaching, the neck pumping forward and back, the flames streaming. One square per move, dx 0 to 100; its last frame leads into the next move's first.
- It must read at town size (a pawn about 45 px) and look great at 4x. Cute and charming, never a copy of the boss himself.
- Never draw letters or numbers; nothing based on a real person.
- Tier: mythic (rides are the rarest). Name it "Giraffe Gallop", id "jefferson-giraffe".
