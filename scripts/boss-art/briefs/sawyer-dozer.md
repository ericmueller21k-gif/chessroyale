# Sawyer's Dozer (a ride, Boss Series)

Draw **Sawyer's Dozer**, a hyper-rare ride from the boss Sawyer: the raccoon boss (a yellow hard hat, a black bandit mask, white gloves, a striped tail and a red-and-black reciprocating saw) and his sidekick, a raccoon kit with a toy saw (the references show the boss and the sidekick).

- Your pawn rides **a little bulldozer**: a chunky cab-less body on two caterpillar tracks, a big front blade, a short exhaust stack, a hazard stripe, a raccoon-striped tail flag on a whip antenna as a nod to Sawyer. Side view, facing our right. The pawn sits in the driver's seat, its lower part hidden behind the body.
- The item style (bold dark outlines, flat fills, a highlight or two), like Riding Chirp and Moon's Ride.
- **The painted part** (the item's color): the body (construction yellow by default, so the color replaces it); the tracks, blade edge and hazard stripes stay fixed. Say in your notes what you chose.
- **Idle:** alive and calm: the engine idles: a little rumble-shake, a puff from the stack, the tail flag waves.
- **Town move:** it **trundles** across the square: tracks roll (their treads cycling), the blade dips and lifts, a puff of exhaust and a few dirt crumbs. One square per move, dx 0 to 100; its last frame leads into the next move's first.
- It must read at town size (a pawn about 45 px) and look great at 4x. Cute and charming, never a copy of the boss himself.
- Never draw letters or numbers; nothing based on a real person.
- Tier: mythic (rides are the rarest). Name it "Sawyer's Dozer", id "sawyer-dozer".
