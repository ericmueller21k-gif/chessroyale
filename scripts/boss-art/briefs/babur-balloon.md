# Babur's Balloon (a weapon, Boss Series Crate #1)

Draw **Babur's Balloon**, a crate weapon your pawn holds at its side. Babur is the game's circus tiger boss (the
references): he carries a bunch of shiny chess-piece balloons (a queen, a knight, a rook, a pawn), each round and glossy
with a big highlight, on strings.

- One balloon on a string, held at the pawn's right side (the string's end at about the pawn's hand height, x 70-80,
  y 55-65), the balloon floating up beside its head (it may rise to the cap at y -9, no higher). Make it a chess-piece
  balloon like Babur's, a **knight's head** shape that reads at 60 px (a round, puffy, balloon-animal knight: glossy, a
  knot at its base). If a knight won't read at 60 px, a round balloon with a knight on it is fine; say which.
- The balloon is the painted part (the item's color), with a white glossy highlight; the string is a thin fixed line
  (lw(1.2) or so), a little curl in it.
- **Its motion:** it **sways**: the balloon drifts gently side to side on its string, a slow pendulum with a little
  bob, the string bending with it (about 2.5 to 3.5 seconds a loop, 8 to 12 frames).
- "Yeah, this thing is sweet": joyful, shiny, circus-bright.
- Tier: rare. Name it "Babur's Balloon", id "babur-balloon".
