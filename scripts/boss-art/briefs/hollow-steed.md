# Hollow's Shade (a ride, Boss Series)

Draw **Hollow's Shade**, a hyper-rare ride from the boss Hollow: the Darkness boss (a lanky, shaggy creature of charcoal-black fur with glowing gold eyes, a swirling band of violet darkness through his middle, and a strand of Christmas lights) and his sidekick, a little shadow critter with one glowing bulb (the references show the boss and the sidekick).

- Your pawn rides **a shaggy shadow beast**: a low, friendly, four-legged creature of charcoal-black fur (shaggy edges, almost a walking rug), two glowing gold eyes, with a **strand of Christmas lights** draped over it as reins and trim. A saddle blanket on its back; the pawn sits on it, its lower part hidden behind the beast's shaggy back. Side view, facing our right. Spooky-cute, never scary.
- The item style (bold dark outlines, flat fills, a highlight or two), like Riding Chirp and Moon's Ride.
- **The painted part** (the item's color): the saddle blanket; the fur, the gold eyes and the lights (red, green, gold, blue bulbs) stay fixed. Say in your notes what you chose.
- **Idle:** alive and calm: the fur ripples at its edges, the eyes blink slowly, the bulbs twinkle one after another.
- **Town move:** a soft **slink** across the square (legs low and padding, the body gliding like smoke), a wisp of dark trailing and fading behind, the bulbs swinging. One square per move, dx 0 to 100; its last frame leads into the next move's first.
- It must read at town size (a pawn about 45 px) and look great at 4x. Cute and charming, never a copy of the boss himself.
- Never draw letters or numbers; nothing based on a real person.
- Tier: mythic (rides are the rarest). Name it "Hollow's Shade", id "hollow-steed".
