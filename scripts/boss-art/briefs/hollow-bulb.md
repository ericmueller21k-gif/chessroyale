# Hollow's Bulb (a ride, Boss Series): Eric's design, Oct 31

Draw **Hollow's Bulb**, a hyper-rare ride from the boss Hollow: the Darkness boss (a lanky, shaggy creature of
charcoal-black fur with glowing gold eyes, a swirling band of violet darkness through his middle, and a strand of
Christmas lights; the references show him). It replaces a rejected shadow-beast ride.

- **Eric's design:** a **chubby, round, old-style Christmas tree bulb** (the big fat vintage kind: a round-bellied glass
  body narrowing to a ridged metal screw cap), **lying on its side and floating** a little above the ground with a soft
  shadow (a gentle glow on the ground is welcome). Side view: the round glass belly toward our right, the **metal cap**
  at the back/top (our left). **The pawn sits on top of the bulb, beside the cap**, upright, only its very bottom hidden
  behind the bulb's top curve (like a pawn on a park bench). It must read as sitting *on* the bulb, never cut in half.
- **A short loop of cord** comes out of the cap and curls behind like a little tail (a dark green wire, one curl).
- **The painted part** (the item's color): **the glass**. **Inside the glass, a soft glow in a second color** (a warm
  lighter tint, e.g. a pale yellow-white core, or a lighter shade of the item's color: pick what reads in every color
  and say which) that **slowly dims and brightens**. A shiny glass highlight streak. The cap (silver or brass) and the
  cord are fixed colors.
- **Idle:** a gentle float bob, the glow pulsing slowly (dim to bright and back, about 2 to 3 s).
- **Town move:** it **bobs along** across the square, the cord swinging behind with the motion (lags, then catches
  up). One square per move, dx 0 to 100; its last frame leads into the next move's first.
- **Size (Eric: "be cognizant of the horizontal size"):** the whole ride, cord included, every frame, stays within
  **1.25 squares wide**: at most 125 units across, e.g. x from about -12 to 113 on the pawn's 100-unit square.
- It must read at town size (a pawn about 45 px) and look great at 4x. Cozy, spooky-cute, festive. The item style (bold
  dark outlines, flat fills, a highlight or two). Never draw letters or numbers.
- Tier: mythic. Name it "Hollow's Bulb", id "hollow-bulb". Say in your notes the ride's width in units.
