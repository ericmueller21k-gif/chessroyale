# Big Boy's Buggy (a ride, Boss Series)

Draw **Big Boy's Buggy**, a hyper-rare ride from the boss Big Boy: the baby boss (a chubby bald baby in a teal "BIG BOY" crop shirt and a light blue diaper, with a swirly rainbow lollipop, who throws toy blocks) and his sidekick, a toddler teddy bear with a toy block (the references show the boss and the sidekick).

- Your pawn rides **in a baby buggy (a pram)**, seen from the side, facing our right: a deep rounded basket body on a curved chassis with four spoked wheels, a folding hood at the back, a push handle, a dangling rattle or a swirly lollipop hung from the hood. The pawn sits in the basket, its lower part hidden behind the basket's side. No letters anywhere.
- The item style (bold dark outlines, flat fills, a highlight or two), like Riding Chirp and Moon's Ride.
- **The painted part** (the item's color): the pram's basket and hood; the wheels, chassis and the dangling toy stay fixed (the toy in Big Boy's colors: teal, light blue, rainbow swirl). Say in your notes what you chose.
- **Idle:** alive and calm: a gentle rock on its springs, the dangling toy swings, the hood's edge flutters.
- **Town move:** it **rolls** across the square: wheels turn (spokes rotate), a little bump and rattle mid-square, the toy swinging, coming to a soft stop. One square per move, dx 0 to 100; its last frame leads into the next move's first.
- It must read at town size (a pawn about 45 px) and look great at 4x. Cute and charming, never a copy of the boss himself.
- Never draw letters or numbers; nothing based on a real person.
- Tier: mythic (rides are the rarest). Name it "Big Boy's Buggy", id "bigboy-buggy".
