# Tock, the clockmaker badger: a new boss concept (idle only)

A **concept for a new raid boss**, one of three Eric will choose between. Draw **only the `idle` animation** (it loops,
alive but calm, about 3 to 5 s): this is a concept, so ignore the other animations the system lists; the chosen one is
drawn in full later.

The boss's power: **Time squeeze.** Its ultimate: your next 5 moves get only 1 second each. Its world is time, clocks
and speed. It sits among the other bosses (a circus tiger, a gingerbread man, a hippo gardener, a chameleon painter, a
cow and the moon, a paper crane, a raccoon with a saw): just as charming and readable at phone size, a big clear
silhouette, facing a little to our left (toward the board), light from the top left.

**Never base it on, name or hint at a real person** (no real player, streamer or celebrity); no letters or numbers on it
(a clock face shows ticks, never numerals).

## This one: Tock, the clockmaker badger
- **Tock**, an old **badger clockmaker**: a stout badger with the striped black-and-white face, a jeweler's
  **loupe** over one eye, a leather apron with pockets full of tiny gears and screwdrivers, rolled shirtsleeves, holding
  up a big brass **pocket watch** on a chain. Fussy, precise, a little smug about punctuality; cozy, not evil.
- Brass, walnut brown, cream and the badger's black and white; a gold glint on the watch.
- **Idle:** the watch's hand ticks round, he checks it and taps it, the loupe glints, a tiny gear spring pops out of a
  pocket and he tucks it back.
