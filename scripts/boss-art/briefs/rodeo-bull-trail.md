# Trail: Rodeo Bull (`rodeo-bull`)

Give **Rodeo Bull** (`rodeo-bull`), the finished ride in `scripts/boss-art/out/rodeo-bull/rodeo-bull.tsx`, its **trail
mark**: what it leaves on the squares behind it as it runs around the town, in place of the town's white droplets. It's
drawn only while it runs, never while it stands.

- **The mark:** hoofprints with a small dust puff: a pair of cloven hoofprints with a small flat tan dust puff on the ground beside them. About about 22 x 14 units (at most 40 x 30; a square is 69).
- **Its color:** fixed: dark brown prints, the dust a pale tan.
- **How it fades:** 4 frames, about 650 ms: prints and puff, the puff spreads and thins, prints lighten, gone. One on every square it leaves.
- **Which way:** the prints point the way it ran; left and right in turn.
- **Take out of its frames:** none known (check every view): the dust under its hooves as it lands stays with it and is fine. Keep everything it does now, the steam puffs and the buck included.
- Change nothing else about the ride.
- Cap: $1.50, effort medium.
