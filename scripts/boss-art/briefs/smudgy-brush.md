# Brush Ride (a ride, Boss Series)

Draw **Brush Ride**, a hyper-rare ride from the boss Smudgy: the chameleon painter boss (a lime-green chameleon with a striped casque, big amber turret eyes, a plum beret, a paint-splashed apron, a big dripping brush and a palette) and his sidekick, a baby chameleon with a paintbrush (the references show the boss and the sidekick).

- Your pawn rides **a giant paintbrush, flown like a broom**: a long wooden handle, a silver ferrule, fat bristles dipped in paint with a drip hanging, held level above the ground with a soft shadow, the bristles behind (our left). A little palette hung on the handle as a step. The pawn sits astride the handle near the middle, its lower part hidden behind a small seat-pad tied to the handle.
- The item style (bold dark outlines, flat fills, a highlight or two), like Riding Chirp and Moon's Ride.
- **The painted part** (the item's color): the paint on the bristles and the drips (and the stroke it leaves); the handle, ferrule and palette stay fixed. Say in your notes what you chose.
- **Idle:** alive and calm: it floats with a slow bob, a drip swells and falls from the bristles now and then.
- **Town move:** it **swoops** across the square, leaving a short **paint stroke** on the air behind it that fades, a drip flying off. One square per move, dx 0 to 100; its last frame leads into the next move's first.
- It must read at town size (a pawn about 45 px) and look great at 4x. Cute and charming, never a copy of the boss himself.
- Never draw letters or numbers; nothing based on a real person.
- Tier: mythic (rides are the rarest). Name it "Brush Ride", id "smudgy-brush".
