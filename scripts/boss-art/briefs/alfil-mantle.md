# Alfil's Mantle (a skin, Boss Series Crate #1)

Draw **Alfil's Mantle**, a crate skin. Alfil is the game's woolly mammoth boss (the references): over his back he wears a
decorated ceremonial blanket, a caparison: a red field with gold trim, a blue border inside a gold line, gold ornaments
and tassels (with a matching Nettipattam headdress on his brow).

- Your pawn wearing Alfil's blanket **like a robe or a cape**: draw the pawn (<Pawn side={side} />) and over it the mantle,
  draped from its shoulders (the collar, y about 36-40) down its body, gold-trimmed, with the blue border and a few gold
  ornaments and tassels along its hem. The head stays bare (so hats still fit; the pawn's anchors are its own).
- The mantle's field is the painted part (the item's color); the gold trim, the border and the ornaments are fixed
  colors (gold #f2c14e and its shades; the border a deep blue, or a darker shade of the field if blue fights a color:
  say which).
- **Its motion:** it **sways**: the hem and tassels swing gently, the cape's edge rippling a little (about 2.5 to 3
  seconds a loop, 8 to 10 frames). Subtle.
- It must read on a white and a black pawn, and feel regal: "yeah, this thing is sweet".
- Tier: epic. Name it "Alfil's Mantle", id "alfil-mantle".
