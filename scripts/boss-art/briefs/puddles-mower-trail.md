# Trail: Puddles' Mower (`puddles-mower`)

Give **Puddles' Mower** (`puddles-mower`), the finished ride in `scripts/boss-art/out/puddles-mower/puddles-mower.tsx`, its **trail
mark**: what it leaves on the squares behind it as it runs around the town, in place of the town's white droplets. It's
drawn only while it runs, never while it stands.

- **The mark:** a short strip of fresh-cut grass clippings: a narrow band of tiny lime and green blades lying on the ground. About about 34 x 12 units (at most 40 x 30; a square is 69).
- **Its color:** fixed: lime and leaf green with a few darker blades.
- **How it fades:** 4 frames, about 700 ms: fresh and bright, fewer, scattered, gone. One on every square it leaves.
- **Which way:** the strip lies along the way it ran.
- **Take out of its frames:** the lime clippings that spray up behind the rear wheel and stay on the ground as a trail, and the exhaust puffs that stay where they were left as the mower drives on, every view. Keep a puff at the exhaust pipe that goes along with the mower, the chug and the flower's nod.
- Change nothing else about the ride.
- Cap: $1.50, effort medium.
