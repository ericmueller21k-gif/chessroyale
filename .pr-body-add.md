**The independent review's fixes** (`REVIEW.md` on `claude/town-phase1b-review`: no blockers; S1 to S5, every nit, the precache's check).
Decision: `docs/decisions/2026-11-28-town-load-baked-fixes.md`. S6 (pages kept twice) and the `loadSheetWaitMs` caps: phase 1c.

**What changed for players:** no pixel, no text. Home no longer fetches the town's files while its queue shows (a boss battle's lobby, a
match loading, a 1 v 1's search), nor on `saveData`, `slow-2g`, `2g` or `3g`. A connection that hangs can't hold the town's loading card
up for good: with no byte arriving for 15 s (the page showing) the device paints, as with a file missing.

- **S1** home's fetch only while `queue` is empty; stopped (the file on its way too) as the queue shows or home closes; the town closing
  stops its own fetching and decoding. **S2** `TOWN_PICTURES.stallMs`: counted in bytes, so a slow load still moving is never cut off.
  **S3** a fallback says why once a page (`town-baked-fallback`: manifest, file, decode, size, list, stall). **S4** home's fetch waits
  until the worker answers the page. **S5** the old saved town (IndexedDB, `brc.townTops`) cleared after a baked load.
- **Nits:** `adopt` says whether it took the pages (else each picture is kept); the precache's town copy can't fail the install; the
  worker's `TOWN_FILE` is filled in from `BAKED_NAME`; home asks for each file once a page; home never reads the manifest (the build
  hands it the page names, `__TOWN_PAGES__`) and fetches it at low priority; the town's requests are its own; dev: the manifest read
  fresh each load, one bake at a time. **Pre-existing:** the precache keeps only a 200, and under `/assets/` never the app's page.
- **Measured** (slowed phone, worker blocked, fetch and decode of the files, 6 visits): 0.7 to 1.6 s with the byte count, 0.75 to 1.2 s
  without: within this machine's noise.
