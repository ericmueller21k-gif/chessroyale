## Findings

### 1. MEDIUM: Revision puts the board back, but the others' trackers already followed Folio's crossed-out move, so `followBoard` drops pieces that stand where they were

- **Where:** `packages/chess/src/runner.ts:1544` (`followOthers` runs every partner's `followMove` on Folio's move as it lands);
  `packages/chess/src/bosses/folio.ts:556` (`revise` puts the board back to `last.fen`, the position *before Folio's move*);
  `packages/chess/src/runner.ts:1581` (`followBoard` after the loop); `smudgy.ts:612`, `sawyer.ts:350`, `alfil.ts:582` (their
  `followBoard`).
- **Cause:** the fix for Finding 3 keeps the crowd's crossed-out reply away from the others. Revision also crosses out Folio's own
  move, though, and that move now reaches every partner's trackers (the new `followMove`). Revision winds back only the board and
  Folio's own state. The partners' trackers stay one move ahead, and `followBoard` then lets go of the tracked pieces as if they
  had been taken.
- **Scenarios (proven, `test: "revision"`, `triggerFrom` 99, `maxMoves` 24, crowd White, `KIT_OPENING`):**
  - `sawyer+folio` s12 (the first review's own Finding 3 seed): Folio's move `h4h3` moves Sawyer's split half, and the crowd replies
    `b2b3`. Revision puts the pawn back on h4, but Sawyer's half on h4 is gone from `split.halves` for good. This is the symptom
    Finding 3 reported, now reached by a new path. Also `folio+sawyer` s31 (`h5h4`).
  - `folio+smudgy` s5: Folio steps the hidden rook `h5h4` as a pawn (the shared disguise allows it), and the crowd plays `b5b6`.
    Revision puts the rook back on h5, but Smudgy's `camo` is `{square: null, reveal: {at: 6, square: "h4", how: "taken"}}`. His
    Camouflage is over, and the reveal names a square where nothing was taken. Also `folio+smudgy` s37 (`g7g5`, `h3g5`).
  - `folio+alfil` on 10 of 40 seeds (e.g. s2 `f5f4`, s13 `a7a5`, s18 `g7g5`; none with Alfil first): Folio moves a Tusks pawn, Revision comes, and both
    Tusks pawns are let go (the fusion fails), while both still stand on their squares.
  - `folio+zebra`: 80 Revisions over 80 battles, with no gold lost. Folio never moved a gold piece in those battles.
  - By the same cause, not checked: Chandy's lock (or aim) and Boingo's splat on a crowd piece Folio's crossed-out move took are
    dropped by their `followMove`, and the piece comes back unmarked.
- **Repro:** for each order and seed, `runBattle({boss: order[0], bosses: order, seed, side: "w", test: "revision", triggerFrom: 99,
  strikes: [], maxMoves: 24, opening: KIT_OPENING}, null, watch)`. At Folio's boss step, save `sideTracked(partner)`. At Folio's
  next boss step, with the Revision pending (`revision.at === crowdMoves && !revision.move`), each saved square that is no longer
  tracked holds the same piece in the restored position (`fen === saved fen`).
- **Why the PR's test passes:** `several-bosses.test.ts`, "Folio's Revision (with sawyer)", runs only seed 11, where Folio never
  moves a tracked piece. Its check (the partners' powers at the crowd's deal against their powers at the out-of-turn move) would
  catch this on s12.
- **Fix (either one):**
  - (a) **The simplest, a design call:** on a side, Revision never comes after a move of Folio's that reached another boss's
    trackers. In `followOthers`, when the mover's rules have `crossedOut` (Folio) and any other boss's state changed, mark Folio's
    `last` (for example `last.shared = true`, as `last.staggered` is marked). `revise` then returns false for it, like the strike's
    stagger.
  - (b) **Keep Revision's reach:** save each partner's tracked fields just before `followOthers` runs on Folio's move. When
    Revision crosses that move out, put them back with a `BossRules.rewind(b, saved)`, the mirror of `followMove`, for each tracker.
    That is more code and adds a hook per tracking boss.

  The decision note lists Revision among the powers that are "let go" (`followBoard`). That holds for a board Revision changed by
  itself. Here, though, the pieces never left their squares: only the tracker moved. Letting go there drops a live power.

### 2. MEDIUM: another boss's step of Smudgy's hidden rook is named as a rook's move ("Rd6"), so the rook is given away while it still counts as hidden

- **Where:** `packages/chess/src/runner.ts:1526` (`special` comes from the mover's rules only); `runner.ts:1538` (`bossLast.san =
  toSan(board.fen, m)` on the real board); `packages/chess/src/bosses/smudgy.ts:578` (`specialMove`: the masked name; only Smudgy's
  own moves reach it).
- **Scenario (proven; the PR's own test setup):** `runner(["gingerbread", "smudgy"], "", 5, { bossPaintTest: 1 })`. The crowd plays
  `a2a3`, then Ginger plays `applyBossMove("d7d6")`. `camo.square` is `d6`, still hidden, and the crowd's board masks it. But
  `bossView().lastMove` is `{"move":"d7d6","san":"Rd6"}`. With Smudgy alone, the same step is named `"d6"`. The name reaches every
  player (the view's `lastMove`, online too). By the same cause, a move of another boss's other rook to a square the hidden rook
  could also reach is named with its file ("Rhd8"), which Smudgy's `specialMove` exists to prevent.
- **Cause:** the fix made "another boss may step it as a pawn would" a rule, and it brought the tracker (`followRook`) but not the
  masking of the move's name. As with `bossStops` before `sideStops`, Smudgy's `specialMove` mixes two things: a move played as
  something else (Wrangler's pull: the mover's own) and a name masked for the whole side (Smudgy's disguise).
- **Fix:** on a side, take the move's name from any boss on the side whose `specialMove` renames this same move (`special.play ===
  m`, which is Smudgy's), and keep the play from the mover's rules. Or split the naming into its own side-wide hook, as `sideStops`
  was split out of `bossStops`. Add one check to the PR's test "another boss may step Smudgy's hidden rook…":
  `expect(r.bossView()?.lastMove?.san).toBe("d6")`. It fails today.
- This is Finding 2's Smudgy scenario ("the real rook on d6 is visible as a rook"), only partly fixed: the tracker follows, but the
  name still gives it away.

### 3. TEST GAP: invariant 8 can't fail for the bug it was added for; `followBoard` drops a stale tracked piece before any check reads it

- **Where:** `packages/chess/test/boss-kit-lib.ts:423` (invariant 8, `side()`); `runner.ts:2478` and `runner.ts:1581` (`followBoard`
  runs at the end of every boss turn and every crowd move, just before the kit's `position` check).
- **Proven:** I reverted Finding 2's fix in memory (`MatchRunner.prototype.followOthers = () => {}`) and ran the first review's own
  sweep: `[x, gingerbread]`, `[gingerbread, x]`, `[x, clown, grex]` for x in smudgy, sawyer, alfil, zebra (`triggerFrom` 2,
  `maxMoves` 24). Over seeds 1 to 6, invariant 8 fails **0 times**. Over seeds 1 to 3 (36 battles), `followBoard` silently lets go
  **25** tracked pieces: Smudgy's hidden rook (h5, h7), Sawyer's halves (e4, d4, h4, g4), Alfil's elephants (a7, e4, c6, …), and
  more. With the fixes, the same 36 battles let go **0** pieces. Only when `followBoard` is also reverted does invariant 8 fail (e.g.
  `gingerbread + smudgy` s2: "smudgy's hidden rook on h5: an empty square stands there"). So invariant 8 checks something
  `followBoard` guarantees by construction. A tracker that stops following another boss's move, which was Finding 2, turns into a
  silent loss of the boss's power that no kit check reports. Finding 1 above passes it too.
- **Invariant 9 does fail when its fix is reverted:** I set `hisTurn = () => true` and deleted `SMUDGY.sideStops`. Then
  `gingerbread + smudgy` s11 reports "gingerbread to move is held to smudgy's own forced move h7h5" (then `a8a6`),
  `smudgy + gingerbread` s11 reports `h8h6`, and `clown + smudgy` s11 reports `h7h5` and `a8a6`. Good.
- **Fix (cheap):** make the kit fail on a let-go that has no cause. In the kit (a wrapper, as in my repro) or in the runner
  (`followBoard` returning what it let go), record what `followBoard` drops after each step. Fail when it drops anything after a
  step whose board change is exactly the move (the board after equals `applyMove(before, move)`, no base). A drop there means a
  `followMove` missed it. With the fixes this count is 0 over the sweep, so the check is free of noise. Or run invariant 8 before
  `followBoard` lets go.
