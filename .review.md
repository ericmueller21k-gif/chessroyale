# Review: PR #808, several bosses on one side (branch `claude/tower-several-bosses`, head 49c1b12)

Independent read-only review (full lane, the match runner's core). Every finding below was proven with a throwaway vitest
file in `packages/chess/test/` (not committed: the key lines are quoted so they can be re-run). The PR's own
`several-bosses.test.ts` passes (9/9) at this head.

## 1. A one-boss battle: no findings

I checked every changed runner path. With `bossIds` unset or of length 1, each one either branches on `several()` or calls today's
function through a `side*` wrapper that hands a side of one to the one-boss function: `startRaid`, `preparePowers`, the judge
paths (`sideMustScore`, `sideRanked`, `sideOffers`, `sideEvaluation`; `[]` and `undefined` give the same answers as the old `null`),
`scoreRound`, the flag, `powerTurn`, the Last Stand's two tests, `stageComplete`, `finishBossBattle`, `bossCarrotWin`,
`bossBlackout`, `moveClock`, `bossOutOfTime`, `stallPlan`, `bossRace`, `raceJob`, `triggerUltimate`, `applyExtraMove`, and
`bossPassed`/`bossTurnOver`. `asBoss(-1, fn)` is just `fn()`. `rageOf(b, this.rageRates())` is `rageOf(b, BOSS_POWERS)`.
`prepareTurn`'s new `hold` defaults to false, and the `ultNext` expression is unchanged when it is false. The view memo's key gains
nothing for one boss. `MatchState` and `Settings` gain only optional fields, so nothing reaches the protocol, a reconnect, a rematch
or saved data for one boss.

## 2. Several bosses

### Finding 1: HIGH. Smudgy's Camouflage forced moves bind every boss on the side, and his Camouflage dies on the others' turns

- **Where:** `packages/chess/src/bosses/smudgy.ts:660` (`afterCrowdMove`: `camoPending(c) && b.bossToMove()` sets `due` after *every*
  crowd move); `packages/chess/src/bosses/smudgy.ts:550` (`bossStops`: `m !== due`); `packages/chess/src/boss-powers.ts:452`
  (`sideBossLimit` applies every non-`ownStops` boss's `bossStops` to the boss to move); `packages/chess/src/runner.ts:1558`
  (`afterCrowdMove` runs every boss's hook, and `b.bossToMove()` is true for every one of them).
- **Scenario (proven, seed 11, crowd White):** `[gingerbread, smudgy]`. After the crowd's first move, Smudgy's hook computes his forced
  push `h7h5` while **Ginger** is the boss to move. `bossAllowed()` is `["h7h5"]`, so Ginger plays Smudgy's forced move. Smudgy's
  `afterHisMove` never sees it (Ginger is in focus), so his `step` stays 0. Smudgy then plays `a7a5`, and Ginger is next forced into
  `a8a6` (Smudgy's rook lift). After that, every boss turn that isn't Smudgy's own counts as a turn he "missed" (`missed` 1, 2, 3),
  and Camouflage switches itself `off` by the crowd's 6th move. The same happens with `[smudgy, gingerbread]` and `[clown, smudgy]`.
  The design keeps only the *disguise* binding every boss ("his hidden rook's disguise (`keepsDisguise`) binds every boss's moves"),
  not his forced moves.
- **Repro:** `runBattle(battle(["gingerbread","smudgy"]), null, (r, fen, who) => { if (who === "boss") log(r.boss.id, r.bossAllowed(),
  smudgy.powers.camo) })`: Ginger's `allowed` is `["h7h5"]`, then `["a8a6"]`.
- **Fix:** two parts, both from one cause (a "his turn begins" hook firing for every boss):
  (a) A hook's "the bosses are to move" should mean "**this** boss is to move". Make the Battle bridge's `bossToMove()`
  (`runner.ts:1992`) answer true only for the boss who will be in focus to move, while `afterCrowdMove` runs each boss's hook. For
  example, work out `focusNow()` for the bosses' turn first and pass "is it him" down to the hooks. This also fixes the low finding
  4 below (Moo, Don Dough), and it needs a per-boss call on Ginger's Super Freeze, Babur's balloon placement and Chandy's queen shot,
  which use the same check and today fire at any boss's turn (the design says those run as alone).
  (b) Split Smudgy's `bossStops`. Make the forced move (`m !== due`) his own, like Sawyer's first move. Keep the disguise
  (`keepsDisguise`) for the whole side. `ownStops` is all-or-nothing per boss, so it needs a finer hook, e.g. `bossStops(boss, { own })`
  or a separate `sideStops`.

### Finding 2: HIGH. A boss's tracked pieces go stale when another boss moves them

- **Where:** every boss that tracks pieces of the bosses' side follows only the crowd's moves and his own:
  `afterCrowdMove`/`afterBossMove`, and `runner.ts:1541` calls only the mover's `afterBossMove`. That covers Smudgy's hidden rook
  (`camo.square`, `smudgy.ts` `afterHisMove`), Sawyer's split halves (`sawyer.ts:365` `track`), Alfil's elephants and Tusks pawns
  (`alfil.ts:821` `follow`) and Don Dough's gold pieces (`zebra.gold`).
- **Scenarios (proven):**
  - Smudgy, `bossPaintTest: 1`, `[gingerbread, smudgy]`. The crowd plays `a2a3`. Ginger may step the hidden rook as a pawn would
    (`bossAllowed` includes `d7d6`, since the shared disguise rule allows it) and does. Smudgy's `camo.square` stays `d7`, now
    empty. The view's `camo.square` is `d7`, and the crowd's filter (`crowdFilter`: `maskedFen(fen, "d7")`) masks an empty square.
    The real rook on d6 is visible as a rook with no `unmask` moment.
  - Over 6 seeds each, with `[x, gingerbread]`, `[gingerbread, x]` and `[x, clown, grex]` (`triggerFrom` 2, `maxMoves` 24),
    checked before every step: a tracked square that holds no piece of the bosses' side. Sawyer's half on e4 after Ginger's `e4e3`
    (`gingerbread+sawyer` s1). Alfil's elephant on a7 after Ginger's `a7b6` (`alfil+gingerbread` s1). Don Dough's gold queen
    "on d8" after Ginger's `d8f6`, stale for the rest of the battle (`zebra+gingerbread` s2). Don Dough's gold mark is a layer
    shield ("the crowd's pawns never take one"), so whatever later stands on d8 is shielded and his real gold queen isn't. That
    changes the crowd's allowed moves.
  - The control: the same check with each boss alone passes on every seed.
- **Fix:** a move on the board should reach every boss's trackers, not only the mover's. For example, add a `BossRules.followMove(b,
  move, fenBefore)` (or run each tracker under `asBoss`) for every boss on every move, the crowd's and every boss's, and keep
  `afterCrowdMove`/`afterBossMove` for the effects. `board-layer.ts`'s `followLayer` already does exactly this for piece marks.
  Smudgy's case also needs his unmask checks (`keepsDisguise`, `rookChecks`, `seenMoveLeft`) when another boss moves the rook. A
  simpler design call: another boss may not move the hidden rook at all.
  - Same cause, not proven here: one boss's power changes the board and another boss's marks on the crowd's pieces don't follow.
    Examples: Chandy's locks when Alfil's Slam lifts the locked piece or Jefferson's fire burns it.

### Finding 3: MEDIUM. Folio's Revision crosses out a crowd move that the other bosses have already acted on

- **Where:** `runner.ts:1558`. `forEachBoss` runs the boss in focus first, and Folio (who just moved, so not in focus) last. Folio's
  `revise` (`folio.ts:496`) then puts the board back to the position before its move, but every other boss's `afterCrowdMove` has
  already run on the reply it crosses out. Every boss's `judged` was also set from that move (`runner.ts`, the `updateSide` before
  `afterCrowdMove`).
- **Scenarios (proven, `test: "revision"`):**
  - `folio+zebra` s12 and `zebra+folio` s12: Don Dough's dough timer ticks for the crossed-out move (`left` 4→3, 12→11).
  - `folio+sawyer` s12: one of Sawyer's split halves is dropped for good. Folio had moved it (Finding 2), and Sawyer's tracker
    removed it on the crowd's reply. Revision then restored the board with the pawn back, but not the half.
  - Alone, Folio is the only boss, so nothing else ever sees the crossed-out move.
- **Fix:** on a side, run the hook of the boss whose move a power can cross out (`moved`) first. If it crossed the move out
  (`crossedOut`), skip the other bosses' `afterCrowdMove` for that move and leave their `judged` as it was.

### Finding 4: LOW. The admins' trigger fires Moo's Full Moon and Don Dough's Gold Plate at another boss's turn

- **Where:** `moo.ts:251` (`due = !!next.ultNext || warnAt <= crowdMoves`, then `b.bossToMove()`) and `zebra.ts:508` (`goldDue && b.bossToMove()`).
  Both ultimates come in `afterCrowdMove`, after `prepareSide`'s one-ultimate gate.
- **Scenario (proven, kit trigger from turn 2, seed 11):** `[gingerbread, moo]`: the Full Moon's `ultAt` is 11, and the boss to move
  after the crowd's 11th move is Ginger (`11:gingerbread`). `[gingerbread, zebra]`: gilds at 2 and 3, the second at the start of
  Ginger's turn. With the meter alone it comes right, because his warning is set only at his own turn change. The exception is an
  out-of-turn move (Wrangler's duel reply) after the warning, where the hook still sees "a boss to move".
- **Fix:** the same as Finding 1 (a): the hook's `bossToMove()` means him.

### Note: not proven, by reading. Hollow's second move holds the next boss's ultimate step for a round

After Hollow's move, with his extra move due, `focusNow` keeps Hollow in focus (`runner.ts:2374`), so `prepareSide`'s `f` is
Hollow. The boss after him has `mine` false (`runner.ts:2421`), so his ultimate step and any trigger are held at the turn change
before his own move. After the extra move `refocus` brings him in, but the turn is already set up. His ultimate waits a full
round more than the rule says. Suggested fix: in `prepareSide`, take "the boss to move next" from the order (`moved + 1`), not from
the focus, when Hollow's extra move holds the focus.

### Note: PR 10's view

`buildBossView` merges `iced`, `events` and `allowed` over the side, but the board's marks (`pie`, `pies`, `fire`, `burnt`, `candle`,
`funhouse`/`angle`, the boss's own `view` fields) come from the boss in focus only. For example, Boingo's pie disappears from the view
on Ginger's turn while it still stops moves. No screens read a side yet (`bossIds` is a runner option), so this is for PR 10.

## 3. Tests that pass with these bugs present

- `boss-kit-side.test.ts` passes every Smudgy pair (except the known `smudgy + alfil`), and every Sawyer, Alfil and Don Dough pair,
  with Findings 1 and 2 present. The kit's `Checker` never compares a boss's tracked squares with the board, and never checks that a
  stop of one boss's own turn leaves another boss free. Suggested invariant (cheap, after every step): each boss's tracked squares
  hold a piece of the bosses' side (the check I used), and `bossAllowed()` for the boss to move never contains only another boss's
  forced move.
- `several-bosses.test.ts:67` ("Sawyer's first move is his own: a pawn's") checks only the order of movers. It would pass if Sawyer's
  first move bound the others or weren't a pawn's.
- `several-bosses.test.ts:78` ("one ultimate a turn change") compares `ultAt` values as one unit. `prepareTurn` sets `ultAt` to the
  turn (crowdMoves + 1), but Moo and Don Dough set it to `crowdMoves` in `afterCrowdMove`, and the sample (Ginger, Boingo, G-REX)
  has no ultimate that comes outside `prepareTurn`. With `[gingerbread, moo]` or `[gingerbread, zebra]` the same check fails
  (Finding 4).
