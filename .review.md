# Review: PR #794, Big Boy's tower from above (boss lane, one pass)

Read: the diff against `origin/main`, the brief, the decision note, `BigBoy.tsx`'s tower and scatter wiring, the core rules for
where the tower and its blocks can land, and haptics. Ran `test/bigboy.test.ts` and `test/haptics.test.ts` (30 passed), and
rendered the tower, block and flight sheets with `preview:characters` (`stand1..10`, `add10`, `tip10`, `sit/land/poof1..10`,
`fly1..10`). I didn't run the in-game frames. What the sheets show: each top block carries the count; each lower block shows as
a strip; "10" fits on every size (the small font on the shrinking poof); the add's last frame matches `stand<N>`; the topple
collapses on the square and ends empty.

## Findings

### 1. Medium: a scattered block landing no longer vibrates the phone
- `packages/app/src/characters/effects/bigboy-tower.ts:530` (`id: "toy-block-top"`) vs `packages/core/src/settings.ts:4990`
  (`HAPTICS.cues`: `"toy-block clack"`).
- `cueHaptic` looks up `"<art id> <cue>"` (`haptics.ts:242`, called with `ch.id` from `BossEffect.tsx:245/331`). The new block
  sprite's id is `toy-block-top`, so `"toy-block-top clack"` matches nothing. Big Boy's toy-block buzz (`bigboyBlock`) is
  gone. It was the only one of his tower's beats that buzzed on main, and the PR doesn't mention removing it.
- The test that would catch this passes anyway: `packages/app/test/haptics.test.ts:158` calls `cueHaptic("toy-block", "landA",
  "clack")` with the old id and the old anim name, neither of which exists now.
- Fix: rename the cue key to `"toy-block-top clack"` (or keep the sprite id `toy-block`). Point the haptics test at the real
  id, ideally `cueHaptic(EFFECTS.toyBlock.ch.id, "land1", "clack")`, so a rename can't slip past it again. Adding
  `"bigboy-tower-top add1 clack"` would also make the first block's plop buzz. That's the director's call (main didn't buzz there).

### 2. Low: a tall tower on the crowd's back rank casts its shadow below the board
- `packages/app/src/styles.css:16898` (`.board-wrap > .bb-under { overflow-x: clip; }`), with `towerShadow` in
  `bigboy-tower.ts` (its far edge at frame y about 74 for N = 10, against the square's bottom at 63).
- The board shadow now falls down and to the right of the square. On the bottom rank on screen (the crowd's back rank:
  `chooseBlock` allows any empty square on the crowd's half that a crowd piece can reach), a 7-10 tower's shadow reaches about
  a third of a square below the board. It lands over the timer bar's gap and whatever is under the board. Only the sides are
  clipped. On main, the side-view tower's foot was the square's bottom, so nothing spilled downward.
- The tower only stands on the crowd's half, which the crowd sees at the bottom. It rises upward and never leaves the top of
  the board. So clipping both axes is safe.
- Fix: `overflow: clip` (both axes) on `.bb-under`, plus one line in the decision note.

### 3. Low: on an a- or h-file, the 10-tower's top block is cut flat at the board edge as it wobbles
- Same rule (`styles.css:16898`), with `SWAY[10]` (A 4.8) in `bigboy-tower.ts`.
- Block 10 is 32 px wide plus its outline, centered with a +1 jitter, so even at rest it reaches a pixel or two past its
  square. In the big wobble it reaches about 5 px (about a sixth of a square) past it. On an edge file the board's side clips
  it, so the top block looks sliced as it sways. The decision note mentions the falling block being clipped there for 80 ms,
  but not the standing wobble.
- Fix: probably accept it and add it to the note. If Eric minds, mirror `JX`/the sway toward the board on edge files (more
  code, so not recommended now).

### 4. Low (test hook): `data-state` on the scattered-block markers now reads `sit3` / `land10`, not `sit` / `land`
- `packages/app/src/components/BigBoy.tsx:539`: `b.anim!.replace(/[A-Z]$/, "")` stripped the old letter suffix. The new
  suffix is a number, so nothing is stripped. No spec reads it today, but the marker's meaning has changed without anyone
  noticing.
- Fix: `.replace(/\d+$/, "")`.

### 5. Low (copy/comments): leftovers of the side view
- `packages/app/src/characters/power-art.ts:579`: "one square's width and a half, four squares and a quarter tall; the square
  is its bottom 32 rows" is now wrong. The frame is 3 x 3 squares, with the square in the middle.
- `packages/app/src/characters/bigboy.ts:392`: the block in his hand is still the old lettered block ("a letter"). It's tossed
  and becomes block 1 ("1", also red) in the air. At 8 px in his hand you barely see it, so no art change is needed, but the
  comment's pointer ("characters/effects.ts toyBlock") is stale.
- The town shop's `toyBlock` in `town/shops/bigboy-art.ts` and the intro table's ABC blocks are their own drawings, not the
  removed sprites. Nothing there references the removed sprites, and they're unaffected.

### 6. Note for the director (design, not a bug): what the scattered blocks' numbers mean to a player
- Every scattered block now shows its tower number (`blockItem(..., i + 1)`: 1 nearest … k farthest). On the tower, the number
  means "this many blocks" (Eric's words). A lone block on a square reading "4" can just as easily read as "4 turns left" or
  "4 blocks". That was the director's call in the brief, and it's consistent and correct in the code (the flight and the
  landed block always match). Worth watching in Eric's playtest.
