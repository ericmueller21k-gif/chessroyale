Phone (iPhone 13, light), solo against Jefferson with `?power=candle&laststand=0`, moves by real taps
(`scripts/frames-grex-char.mjs` on `claude/grex-char`).

- `grex-char-1-charred-phone.png`: your move after the candle's fire reached the pawn on f2 for the first time: it stays,
  charred (charcoal, an ember edge).
- `grex-char-2-second-hit-phone.png`: a later turn, the charred pawn left on h2 burns (its second hit) while the pawn on
  b2 is charred (its first); the dock: "Pawn burnt, pawn charred".
